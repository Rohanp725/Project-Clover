## 0. The filming rig v2 (Unity, first)

As in the early notes:
- fresh bodies for every clip;
- one trigger;
- quiet enemies;
- no leftovers;
- self-checks that fail a clip on a second cast, or a hit after the effect ends.

It has to pass on 5 sample reactions before any re-film. This also fixes the tab-wide comment and Supercooled's recast.

## 2. Blender redesigns (strips to the owner first)

| Look | The owner's direction |
|---|---|
| Rime | Scrap the crystals blowing at enemies. Frost creeps across the ground to the others, and rime feathers grow where it reaches |
| Quickening | No jumpy seed effect. Plants with green stems and BLUE flowers on top. Healing is a green pulse, not green crystals flowing in |
| Diamond Ring | The portal sits a little off the enemy, light chains run into the portal, and there's no cursed after-effect |
| Shut in the Dark | One cloud inside the jar; when it breaks, no flat pool, just the Poison affliction on them |
| Carried Embers | One small, tight whirl of embers and flame curls down its lane, lighting only what it touches |
| Winter Seed | The falling drops read as ice (sleet and frost beads), not water |
| Smoulder | The flames that spread to others are black and purple, not normal fire |
| Thermal Shock | A better ice explosion (the frozen flames shatter) |
| Logan Stone | A balanced stone rises beside the enemy, rocks twice, and topples onto it; the enemy is knocked flat and gets up after |
| Chokedamp | The ground cracks, then a low, heavy dark-green damp seeps out and rolls around the legs; enemies cough and stagger as it rises to the waist, then it sinks |
| Breath Taken | Near-black wind curls with violet edges sweep round the enemy, pull a pale wisp of breath from its mouth and swallow it |
| Glint | Sharp gold-steel glints flash across the armour one at a time, then a reflected flash blinds; no standing beam |
| Kindle | The player catches fire: flames rise off the shoulders and arms while it lasts (original, not copied) |
| Static | Better-detailed lightning: branching, crawling arcs, not straight lines popping in and out |
| Overdrive | A charged body: violet-cyan arcs crawl over the arms and legs and snap between them, hair and cloth lift, and steps crackle |
| Gust | Reads more fully as wind; research what makes wind read, using curls only |
| Tailwind | Wind at your back: curls stream past from behind as you move and tug the cloth forward, and whip into a gust on a dash. No floor ring, no leaves |
| Wellspring | A spring bursts up at your feet, falls back as rain and splashes, and water streams off you while it lasts |
| Bedrock | A better earthen coat: natural plates and stone that follow the body, not a misaligned brick pattern. Remove the sparks |
| Flung Blade | A small forged blade flies spinning on a visible path, glints once and sticks in; no radial white slash |
| Regrowth | Leaves and small blossoms grow along the arms and shoulders, a soft green pulse rings out as you heal, and petals fall at the end |
| Marked | A warm-gold sigil burns onto the chest like a brand, pulsing, with motes drifting off. No beam |
| Glare | No floor shapes: a blinding flash at each enemy's face, they recoil shielding their eyes, with gold motes |
| Clarity | The lotus stays; gold light rises from the lotus around you instead of a beam |
| Fever | No pool: the skin flushes sickly green-grey, with heat shimmer and dark sweat drops, and dark veins pulse on the arms |
| Hunger | A shadow maw: your shadow grows black fangs that gnash at nearby feet, with dark wisps pulled to you when you hit |

**Saved for later:** Poison + Life, with roots growing out of the body (the owner's idea).
