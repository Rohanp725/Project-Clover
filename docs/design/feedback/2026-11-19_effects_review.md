# The owner's comments on the effects (2026-11-19)

The owner went through every GIF in the friend document ("The arsenal in detail") and left 114 comments, one per
picture. They are kept here word for word, grouped by picture, because the comments were taken off the page after
being read. The triage at the end says who acts on each.

## Themes across the comments

- **Weapons infused with an element are the biggest gap.** Today the element's pieces are stuck along the blade.
  Each weapon needs its own effect for its swings, made from a close look at the weapon and its animation, for every
  element: a trail or sheath that follows the blade's path through the swing. Metal adds only a glint. Life's thorns
  on the blade make sense but still need fitting per weapon.
- **Readability.** Too small, sitting at the enemy's feet, or inside the enemy. An effect must read from the game
  camera with size and height.
- **Speed.** Several reactions are too fast to judge. The beats need time to read.
- **Elements, not shapes.** Many looks read as "shapes chucked at the enemy". Each must visibly be its elements
  doing the idea, for example burning wind carrying fire, or poisoned wind carrying poison.
- **Area reactions must show their area.**
- **Things that grow come up out of the ground.** Rocks are shoved up and then fall; they never float. Ice grows out
  of the ground and is thick at the bottom. Rings that turn lie on the ground.
- **Metal's visual idea.** A blade or a needle stuck through the enemy. This suits Sundered, Rust, Quicksilver,
  Live Iron, Cold Short and Red Hot.
- **No mystery shapes.** The "quarter concentric circles", the white spider-web, the green hoop and the ground
  curve: anything that doesn't plainly belong goes.
- **The filming itself (the owner's general comment).** Every GIF was filmed on a training dummy, which hid some
  floor effects, never reacted, and stood alone, so multi-target effects were never shown. From now on, effects are
  filmed on real enemies (the castle soldiers) that react: they flinch, freeze, fall and get thrown. Wherever an
  effect reaches more than one target (chains, areas, carried afflictions, bolts that pass through), a group of 3 to
  5 enemies is spread out to show its reach. The camera and placement must keep the floor marks in view.
- **The 18 god moves (the gods' weapons):** the owner likes the concept and the visualisation of all of them
  (2026-11-19, said in chat). Keep their ideas and looks as they are when they are built in Phase 5.
- **Praised; these set the bar:** Diamond Ring, Iron Thorns, Sleet Charge, Thermal Shock (concept), Firebloom,
  Earthed, Lightning's whole set, Gust, Stone Rise's shove, Riptide's wave, Water's landing splash, Struck Plate's
  after effect, Light's Marked, Glare and Ray Along the Edge, Dark's Grasp and blade blows.

## Added for the end of Phase 3 (the owner, 2026-11-19, in chat)

- **The element braziers must contain their element, not show flames of its colour.** This applies to every brazier
  that stands for an element (the Proving Ground's altars and any other). Each holds its element in the pass-2
  language, animated and alive:
  - Fire: flames.
  - Lightning: a crackling caged storm.
  - Wind: a small turning vortex with leaves.
  - Ice: frost crystals and cold mist.
  - Water: a turning basin of water.
  - Life: a living plant that sways and blooms.
  - Earth: shifting stones.
  - Metal: a molten glow and an ingot.
  - Light: a radiant core and rays.
  - Dark: a black hole that drinks light.
  - Poison: a bubbling green brew and fumes.

  Design them in Blender, then build them in Unity.
