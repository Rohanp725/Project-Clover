# The owner's comments on the effects, third pass (2026-11-20)

The owner went through the friend document ("Project Clover: The Lands, the Story and the Gods") again on 2026-11-20
and left 120 open comment threads, all their own: 53 on the Reactions tab and 67 on "The elements' own effects". No
one else commented, no thread has a reply, every other tab has no open thread, and the companion document "Weapon
swings on film" has no comments at all. They are kept here word for word, grouped by tab and then by picture, because
the threads were taken off the page (resolved) after this file was written.

Times are local (the document's UTC times minus six hours). The comments were written between 10:54 and 12:15. Every
Reactions picture was replaced at about 12:25, after its comment was first written, and nine of them (Thunder Gust,
Quickening, Glint, Winter Seed, The Grudge, Red Hot, Breath Taken, Earthlight, Golden Feathers) again at about 14:00.
On "The elements' own effects" ten pictures and the Lightning brazier were replaced at about 14:00; each is marked
below. Between 15:08 and 15:27 the owner rechecked and added newer notes inside 19 of the Reactions comments.

**How each comment is split.** Inside a comment the owner wrote the word "edit" before a newer note. The text before
"edit" is about the earlier film and the text after it is about the current film; a comment without "edit" applies to
both. No comment has "edit" more than once. Comments the coordinator is checking with the owner are marked
"(ambiguous: see report)".

## Themes across the comments

### What the current films still need

- **The second cast after a reaction.** "When it ends it insta casts another set of both afflictions on the person
  that dont react ... its all of them other than flashover." It may be a game bug or the film maker casting again.
  Named on Supercooled, Sleet Charge, The Ground Gives, Black Chain, Nerve Fire, Rust and Earthlight; Rime's
  "refreezes" and Supercooled's "why it freezes again" may be the same thing.
- **Flame turns downward before it vanishes.** Red Hot ("really dont know why fire keeps turning down"), Firebloom
  ("again the fire turns down"), Fire Medicine and Smoulder ("the flames keep rotating downward before
  dissapearing"). Flashover has one flame cone that fades far too late.
- **Tornadoes must blow themselves out.** Thunder Gust "just pauses and then fades, instead of blowing into nothing";
  Carried Embers' pillar "just freezes and disappears", should lose its random cones of fire, and its hits must read
  as hits.
- **Films still show the old element looks.** On "The elements' own effects" the owner marked many spell,
  infused-strike and katana-swing films "old effects" (old afflictions inside them): Hoarfrost, Stone Rise, Flung
  Blade, Grasp, Lash, Struck Plate, Venom Flick, Gust ("static lines through the enemy"), and the "swung into three
  soldiers" films for Wind, Ice, Water, Earth, Metal, Life, Dark and Poison. Seven more "old effects" comments sit on
  pictures replaced at about 14:00 (Frost Bite, Riptide, Briar Patch, Grown Into It, Broken Ground, Taken, Miasma);
  see the report on whether they still apply.
- **The katana swing films need their colour and finish.** The "soldiers stood back" swing is greyscaled for every
  element but Fire. Water swings bluer and more watery (inspired by Demon Slayer's water, less detailed); the Dark
  trail almost black; the Light trail the brightest, as if the weapon gives off glare; Metal the only one that reads
  grey, with its final ground pattern reworked; Poison's trail poisonous. Wind loses the fading semicircle slash at
  the end, Light its final full semicircle on the ground, and Earth keeps only the forward trail of stones (no
  diagonal one).
- **Beams of light read as several beams.** Marked ("looks like 3 beams") and Clarity ("reads as 4 beams") should be
  one beam coming down. Clarity's light lotus on the ground is good.
- **Wind and dark shapes must read as what they are.** Breath Taken's black curls "now look like random black
  explosions and not wind"; Cursed's dark rotating shapes look like flames (research how others draw it); Hunger is
  better but does not give the feeling of hunger.
- **Reactions that are two effects stacked, not one reaction:** Thermal Shock (maybe the ice cracking with the heat)
  and Chokedamp. The owner is unsure how to rework either.
- **Per element.** Lightning: Static's later bolts look like they chain to others; Overdrive's bolts are a poor
  colour, so it looks greyed out. Wind: Tailwind upside down, narrow end of the spiral at the feet. Ice: Glaze must
  not drift toward metal's colour; Stolen Warmth's ice darker with a slight purple hue. Water: Wellspring far more
  detailed ("super low quality"); the Water brazier a drop falling from the sky into the water, a ripple, then a loop
  back. Earth: Stone Rise without the ring of three stones, just the massive stones rising and knocking the enemy up;
  Broken Ground's straight line of stones chunkier, not thin plates, and no diagonal line; a rework idea for Bedrock,
  earthen armour growing over your skin; the Earth brazier's rocks churning in and out of the ground instead of
  floating. Metal: Sundered's blade (its size is good) sticks in from the enemy's back, through the torso
  horizontally and out of the chest, instead of floating and rotating; the Metal brazier's middle bar steel, not
  gold. Life: Seeded's brown seeds and sprouts do not connect and hurt readability (the rotating seeds are
  fantastic); Briar Patch without flowers, just spiky plants hurting; Regrowth not in the cel-shaded style of the
  others. Light: the Light brazier's sun orb smaller and its rays less transparent. Dark: the Dark brazier has dark
  hands reaching up to grab instead of random lines. Poison: Fever with only the inner ring of clouds; Burning Sap
  gives off water vibes, not poison; Fish Poison could be clearer; Ill Wind should lay a lane covered by poison clouds.
- **Per reaction.** Split Rock: the roots are better but still need work, and the floating seeds do not look good.
  Anvil Stone: swords on other enemies sit oddly and stack up on the first enemy, and the anvil passes through the
  enemy instead of pushing it up. Quickening: the green-brown circle underneath goes; blue is right for the flower
  only (stems stay green). Winter Seed: the ice lotus is one flat colour with no detail, and the thorns are in the
  ground and do not read as thorns. Iron Thorns: the flung thorns need better animation. Windthrow: the briars that
  root enemies are jumpy, and there is no torn pit with seeds. Sickle Wind: the weapons lie flat for a moment; fling
  them into the ground at the end. Golden Feathers: smaller still, and landing on an enemy instead of through it. The
  Grudge: the bolt looks like purple smoke and lingers far too long. Quicksilver: the sword turns yellowish, not
  poison green. Diamond Ring: drop the stacked light ground mark and the cursed mark. Shut in the Dark: black-purple
  ribs, not root-like ones. Smoulder: drop the orange circle; bring back the earlier flames' shading. Steam: the enemy
  staggers before the steam appears. Seized: the pool should show it is charged, not only the enemy. Frost Heave: the
  ice shoves the enemy up from the ground instead of floating at random. Turned to Stone: remove the shaking. Cold
  Short: freeze outward from the weapon. Caught Light: the net must look like a net made of light. Girdled: roots
  spread up the body from every side, not a single coil. The Bolt Runs True: where is the horizontal beam through the
  enemies; rework it visually. Mock Suns: the bouncing is great, the mock sun visual is not. Capsize: maybe
  wind-driven rain and droplets that knock the enemy over, instead of a wave from you. Glint: a pass after the metal
  and light fixes.
