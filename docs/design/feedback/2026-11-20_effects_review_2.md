# The owner's comments on the effects, second pass (2026-11-20)

The owner went through the friend document ("Project Clover: The Lands, the Story and the Gods") again on 2026-11-20,
mostly between 05:05 and 06:13 UTC, and left 57 open comment threads across three tabs. All 57 are the owner's own;
no friend commented, and the companion document "Weapon swings on film" has no comments. They are kept here word for
word, grouped by tab and then by picture, because the comments were taken off the page (resolved) after being read.
Where the owner edited a comment, the final wording is quoted and the edit is noted.

## Themes across the comments

### Changes to the overall approach

- **Reactions are bespoke, not overlays.** "We have the parts from each element and then just simply overlaying them
  in different ways to try and make the combo." Each reaction needs its own effect made for it, breaking away from
  copying and pasting the two elements' pieces together.
- **Fixing an element fixes its reactions.** The comments on the 11 base elements apply to the reactions too, since
  most reactions are built from the elements' looks. Several reactions are judged as "the concept is good, the metal
  (or poison) rework will help", so the element reworks come first.
- **Earlier comments must be acted on before a look is shown again.** Overdrive ("I gave my comments on this last
  time and nothing has changed"), Wellspring ("not my old comment"), Seeded ("I believe I commentated on this
  earlier") and Flashover ("you didnt change the think I wanted") were shown again without the change. Check each new
  film against the earlier review before it goes back in the document.
- **Research real references.** Wind needs a study of wind effects in other games; Thunder Gust should look like
  lightning tornadoes from fiction; Carried Embers should look like a real fire tornado (the owner linked a photo).

### Per element, which the reactions inherit

- **Wind is the weakest element.** Today it is a line through the enemy with no identity. An idea: wind blowing
  around the enemy. The random white shapes that pop up in many wind reactions must go. Wind needs a detailed
  close-up pass. Seed Storm is the first look that actually feels like wind.
- **Water is one mega drop.** A single big drop falls on the enemy's head, hits and splashes, then small droplets
  follow. This replaces the many small falling drops (Soaked, Supercooled, Quickening, Drowned Weight). Wellspring is a
  pool of water under the player, not floating water.
- **Fire is real, flickering flame.** Not the stacked "staircase" of flame tongues. The flickering purple flames of
  Smoulder (not its orange stacked ones) are the model for how flame should move, coloured as real fire. Flashover's
  flame is all purple, popping up for a second and going away.
- **Ice builds slowly and holds.** It crystallises, then stays, instead of changing rapidly; it covers the whole torso;
  Rime builds up instead of appearing all at once.
- **Metal is a small, well-forged blade.** The affliction is a small dagger in the enemy, not odd-shaped armour
  inside it. The big curvy sword in many reactions becomes a well-forged small sword that sticks through the enemy,
  visible but not filling the screen, in a single steel shine rather than many colours. Temper turns you metallic.
  Rust must be rust coloured.
- **Poison is a cloud.** Clouds of miasma, with small puffs of poison coming off an afflicted enemy as the sign. The
  current look reads as "some tree thing". Fever, Chokedamp, Rot Roots and Quicksilver wait on this rework.
- **Life's Seeded is seeds and spores** on the enemy that then bloom into the various reactions. Regrowth keeps green
  and pink, not white and grey.
- **Dark is darker.** Cursed: dark energy sucked down into the black hole at the bottom, not purple marks dropping.
  Hunger's middle is near-black purple, not bright purple.
- **Lightning** needs a detail pass on Static (bolts arcing off the enemy, not only around or inside it). Overdrive
  still needs last time's changes. Seized loses its circles and flickers less so it does not hurt players' eyes.
- **Earth's Tremor:** the shaking is good; the flashing ring of stones goes.
- **Light is mostly fine.** Clarity's ground pattern may need reworking.
- **Flowers take their element's colour:** an ice flower with ice thorns for Winter Seed, blue-purple lightning
  flowers for Lightning Flower, bluer water plants for Quickening.

### Praised (these set the bar)

Turned to Stone ("Fantastic!"), Shining Herb, Ill Wind, Frost Heave, Earthlight, Black Chain, Venom Rime, Mock Suns,
Root Snare, Sun-Sap, Cold Short's concept and visuals, Seized (once its circles go), Sickle Wind ("decent").
