# Fix plan for the owner's second effects review (2026-11-20)

Source: `2026-11-20_effects_review_2.md` (57 comments) and the still-open items of `2026-11-19_effects_review.md`.
Every look below is checked against BOTH files before it is filmed for the owner again. A look that goes back into the
document without the change the owner asked for is the failure this plan exists to stop (Overdrive, Wellspring,
Seeded and Flashover went back unchanged last time).

## Order

1. **The eleven base elements first.** The reactions inherit them, so fixing an element fixes part of every reaction
   that uses it.
2. **Then every reaction as its own bespoke effect,** designed for that pair, not the two elements' pieces overlaid.
   The praised ones keep their idea and get only the named fixes.
3. **Then the in-game build and filming** on real enemies in groups, each film checked against the checklist line.

## Research first (feeds Wind, Thunder Gust, Carried Embers)

- How stylised action games draw wind: around and through a body, carried debris, motion lines that curve, what
  gives wind an identity without white shapes popping up.
- Lightning tornadoes in fiction (Thunder Gust).
- Real fire tornadoes, the owner's photo: a tall twisting column of flame (Carried Embers).
- Written to `docs/design/research/2026-11-20_effect_references.md`.

## Who does what

- **Research agent:** the references above.
- **Blender:** new art and timelines for every element look in the table, then the bespoke reactions. Work as before:
  stand-in enemies in groups of 3 to 5, game camera, GIFs, a recipe with motion, and the kit. Water first, metal,
  poison, life, dark, ice; then earth, lightning, light and wind after the research.
- **Unity:** what lives in code or shaders. The Burn flame (reuse the flicker that Smoulder's purple flame uses, in
  fire colours), Temper's metallic sheen on the player, Seized's circles and flicker, Hunger's centre, Regrowth's
  colours and Rust's colour where they are set in data. Then building the new Blender looks and filming them.
- **Before anything is filmed for the owner:** check it against its line in this file and the 2026-11-19 file, and say
  so in the report.
