# Combat Playtest 0.4 (unreleased)

## What this playtest is

A testbed for the combat. It grows with each update as the fighting is reworked. Everything is open: try anything, and tell us what felt good, what felt wrong and what you did not understand.

## New in 0.4 (phase 3: reactions)

- **Reactions, all 55.** When an affliction lands on an enemy that already carries another element's, the two react, and every pair of elements does something of its own: **Flashover** (Fire and Lightning: the Static discharges through the flame), **Steam** (Fire and Water: a scalding puff), **Thermal Shock** (Fire and Ice: it cracks, and your next heavy splits it), **Capsize** (Wind and Water: it goes down), **Smoulder** (Dark and Fire: the fire sinks in, deepens, then flares), **Fish Poison** (Poison and Water: the heavily dosed float up stunned) and 49 more. The guide to effects has an entry for each, with its numbers.
- **Many ask for your weapon.** A reaction often leaves a state on the enemy for a few seconds: a crust of rime your heavy blows out of its far side, a crack your heavy splits, a stone that rocks until your third blow throws it down, coils your blows draw tighter. Some answer its death (Smoulder flares, Shut in the Dark's jar breaks, Drowned Weight bursts).
- **Where you stand matters.** Several throw things away from you, past the enemy (Carried Embers, Rime, Red Hot, Golden Feathers, Thunder Gust): put the enemy between you and the others.
- **How they come.** A reaction comes once, then rests on that enemy for a few seconds (most 4 s, the pairs three apart 6 s, a few their own: Steam 3 s, Shut in the Dark and The Grudge 8 s). What a reaction lays starts no reaction of its own. Anything can set one off: your weapon, spells, marks, the charged blade, strikes, even an enemy wet from the river. The order never matters, but Seized (Lightning and Water) and The Grudge (Dark and Lightning) need two Static or more on the enemy.
- **The great resist.** What holds or slows an enemy takes half on an elite and nothing on a god; some reactions give elites and gods their own numbers instead (each entry says).
- **You see the name.** A reaction's name rises over the enemy for a moment. Each reaction's own look comes later: for now there is a puff in its two colours and whatever it does (a discharge, a burst, fires, a slick).
- **Weave to react.** Carry two elements: your weapon lays the first and your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike lay the second.
- **Not yet:** the duo branches' shapes that change a reaction come in a later update. Many reactions are simpler than their full design, and each entry in the guide says what is still to come under "Not yet": knocked down (Capsize and Logan Stone hold the enemy where it stands for now), the side a blow comes from (Rime, Mock Suns), things to pick up (Shining Herb, Firebloom), standing stones and trunks (Anvil Stone, Frost Heave, Girdled), The Grudge's cloud answering the enemy's blow, Black Chain's shared wounds, and others.

## New in 0.3 (phase 2: two elements and the Hand)

- **Two elements at once.** Carry a first and a second element (the same one twice is all in). Each spell is cast in its own element, lays its own affliction and is drawn in its own colour and stuff.
- **The altars set both.** Carrying one element, use another altar to carry its element as your second; use the altar of the element you carry to go all in. Carrying two (or all in), an altar starts again with its element alone. Before you press, the altar's prompt says which it will do ("Carry Lightning as second", "Carry Fire all in", "Carry Ice alone"). You arrive carrying Fire.
- **Your weapon lays the first element only.** Weapon blows lay the first element's affliction, even with the second's root mark learned. The second comes from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike, so two afflictions meet on an enemy when you weave weapon and spell.
- **The charged blade takes the landed spell's element.** Flame Wave charges it with Fire, Thunderclap with Lightning; only that cast's own first landing counts. A duo spell charges it with the first of its pair.
- **Strikes for each element.** L2 with R1 (RB) is the first element's strike, L2 with R2 (RT) the second's, and neither costs mana. Carrying one element, or all in, R2 is the deep strike for 20 mana as before.
- **The rest menu.** A rest fire now opens a menu: Set the Hand, The chart, Leave the fire. Closing the chart brings you back to it.
- **The Hand.** Your four spells in a diamond laid out like the buttons, the surge in the middle. Choose a slot to see only what can go there, grouped by element with each spell's role and one line; the weapon skill button shows the numbers. Pick the surge from either element. Suggested Hand fills it for you; three saved Hands keep a Hand for each kind of fight (use wears one, the heavy attack keeps yours there). Once you set a Hand the slots stop filling themselves. The Hand never changes which elements you carry.
- **The spell diamond** colours each slot by its own spell's element and names the R2 strike for the second element.
- **Duo branches** (Plasma and the other mixes) are in force only while both of their elements are carried; a duo spell sits in a slot like any other.
- **Slots open as you go, outside the playtest.** In the full game you start with two slots (West and North) and the surge; East opens when the Champion of the Gate falls and South when the Fire god falls, and they stay open from then on. The Hand shows a shut slot and says what opens it. In the playtest every slot is open.
- **Not yet:** in the full game the second element is still chosen nowhere (the hub's braziers give one element); it comes when the hub's choice is reworked. Duo surges, shapes and the designed starter Hands for each element come with the reshaped trees.

## New in 0.2 (phase 1: spells on cooldowns, mana for big moves, the charged blade, infused strikes and the spell diamond)

- **The charged blade.** A spell from one of your four slots that lands charges your weapon with its element. Your next three blows that land carry it: each hits 20% harder and lays the element's affliction, and the third does one thing more (Fire leaves the ground burning, Light throws a ray, Poison makes the poison bite at once, and so on). You see it on the weapon: flame and embers along the edge for Fire, lightning crackling along it, frost for Ice. Three pips beside the spell diamond count the blows left. A new spell that lands gives three fresh blows.
- **Infused strikes.** Hold L2 (LT) and press R1 (RB): your weapon's own move with your element on it, shaped by the weapon (the Katana's drawing cut throws Burning Edge along its arc, the Hammer's drop lays it in a circle, the Spear's thrust in a line). L2 with R2 (RT) is the deep strike, the heavier version, for 20 mana. Each button waits 8 s, and fighting with the weapon shortens the wait as it does a spell's. A strike counts as a weapon blow (mana, shorter spell waits) but never charges the blade. On the keyboard: hold Left Ctrl and click the left or right mouse button. Every element has its own pair; the guide to effects lists them.
- **The spell diamond** in the bottom-right corner replaces the spell line: your four slots laid out like the face buttons, each with its picture, its button and a shade that sweeps back as its wait runs out, the surge in the middle, the two strikes under it. It is small at all times and grows while L2 is held, with each spell's name. Buttons are named for the pad or keys you use last, and follow your rebinding.
- **Hunger** (Dark's surge, paid in health) now waits 25 s after it ends before it can be lit again.

- **Four spell slots on the L2 layer.** Hold L2 (LT) and press Square, Triangle, Circle or Cross (X, Y, B, A) to cast the spell in that slot. On the keyboard, 1, 2, 3 and 4 cast them directly. While L2 is held the face buttons are spells only: no flask, skill, dodge or use.
- **The surge is a tap of L2.** Tap it and let go quickly. On the keyboard it stays on F.
- **The weapon skill has its own button:** Triangle (Y), or Q. Holding the heavy button now gives the charged heavy only.
- **Basic spells cost no mana.** Each slot waits 6 to 12 s after a cast instead. Fighting with the weapon brings spells back sooner: a light blow that lands takes 0.3 s off every waiting slot, a heavy blow 0.6 s, a perfect guard 2 s, a perfect dodge 1.5 s, a finisher 3 s.
- **Mana is for the big moves:** the surge, the weapon skill and each element's one larger spell (Meteor Leap, Seven Flashes, Sky Dance, Winter Wall, Poured Blade, Elder Tree, Grave of Swords, Daybreak, One by One; the built duo spells too). You enter each area at half. Out of a fight mana rises to half and stops there; above half it is kept. A fight always starts with at least half.
- **Filling the slots.** Your element's own spell starts in West, and each spell you learn fills the next empty slot. At a rest fire, choose a learned spell on the chart and press use to put it in the next slot round. The Hand screen comes in the next update.
- **Controls:** the pause menu's controls page has rows for the spell layer and the four slots, on the pad and on the keys.
- **Not yet:** marks that only made the base spell cheaper do nothing for now, and some spell details on the chart still describe the old way of casting; the trees are reshaped later. Two elements at once and the Hand screen come in the next update.

## New in 0.1

- **The Proving Ground**, where the playtest begins: a rest fire, a rack of every weapon (the Storm Katana and the Sun-Spear among them), altars for every element, training dummies (one still, one that fights back with red and pale blows, and one carrying each element's affliction), pens to let out the castle's soldiers and the Fire land's people, gates to Narukami and Khepharu, and doors to the Castle, the Summit, Sun City and the Sun Court.
- **Playtest rules.** Resting gives unlimited Shards, so every mark can be bought, the gods' gifts included. The chart can be walked into every tree and branch. A fall puts you back at the last fire with nothing lost.
- **Back to the Proving Ground** from any level: pause, then Return to the Proving Ground.
- **The guide to effects**, from the pause menu and from the chart: every affliction, state and resource, with its numbers, what sets it off, how to use it and how it looks.
- **Details for the high-level marks** on the chart: what a mark does, when, the numbers, what it works with and an example. Written in full for the Fire and Lightning trees and their mixes; the other trees show what their data says for now.
- **Not built yet**: empty slots on the rack and on the chart show where the gods' weapons and boughs still to come will be.
