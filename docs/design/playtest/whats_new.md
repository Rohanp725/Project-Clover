# Combat Playtest 0.1 (unreleased)

## What this playtest is

A testbed for the combat. It grows with each update as the fighting is reworked. Everything is open: try anything, and tell us what felt good, what felt wrong and what you did not understand.

## New in 0.1

- **The Proving Ground**, where the playtest begins: a rest fire, a rack of every weapon (the Storm Katana and the Sun-Spear among them), altars for every element, training dummies (one still, one that fights back with red and pale blows, and one carrying each element's affliction), pens to let out the castle's soldiers and the Fire land's people, gates to Narukami and Khepharu, and doors to the Castle, the Summit, Sun City and the Sun Court.
- **Playtest rules.** Resting gives unlimited Shards, so every mark can be bought, the gods' gifts included. The chart can be walked into every tree and branch. A fall puts you back at the last fire with nothing lost.
- **Back to the Proving Ground** from any level: pause, then Return to the Proving Ground.
- **The guide to effects**, from the pause menu and from the chart: every affliction, state and resource, with its numbers, what sets it off, how to use it and how it looks.
- **Details for the high-level marks** on the chart: what a mark does, when, the numbers, what it works with and an example. Written in full for the Fire and Lightning trees and their mixes; the other trees show what their data says for now.
- **Not built yet**: empty slots on the rack and on the chart show where the gods' weapons and boughs still to come will be.
- This title, with the version and what is new.
