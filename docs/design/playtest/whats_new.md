# Combat Playtest 0.5 (unreleased)

## What this playtest is

A testbed for the combat. It grows with each update as the fighting is reworked. Everything is open: try anything, and tell us what felt good, what felt wrong and what you did not understand.

## New in 0.5 (phase 4: keystones, pairs and shapes)

- **Six new keystones.** A keystone is a big mark at the top of a tree. It changes how the whole tree plays.
  - **Into the Fire.** Dodge toward the enemy you face and you cannot be hurt for the whole roll. You roll through bodies and set them burning. A dodge away costs twice the stamina.
  - **The Blade Drinks Fire.** Your Fire weapon no longer lays Burn, and burns do not hurt. Instead, each blow on a burning enemy drinks its Burn and hits harder for it.
  - **High Water, Low Water.** A tide turns every 6 seconds. At high water your spells hit harder and your weapon hits softer. At low water it is the other way round. A perfect guard or a perfect dodge turns the tide at once.
  - **Drawn Back to Break.** Each dodge away stores a wave, up to three. Your next blow breaks them all in a cone. The cone soaks the enemy and throws it 2 metres back. With no wave stored, your weapon hits 20% softer.
  - **Shared Roots.** A third of each wound you deal spreads to Seeded enemies within 8 metres. Blows on Seeded enemies no longer heal you.
  - **In Its Season.** Leave a seed alone for 6 seconds and it ripens. A ripe seed bursts three times as hard and grows a thornbush. An unripe one bursts at half strength.
- **Four old keystones are back.** The Closed Ring, All Blades Home, Two Suns and Plague Bearer stand at the top of their trees again. Two Suns now makes your spells wait a fifth longer instead of costing mana.
- **A branch for every pair.** Carry two elements and a small diamond of marks opens in the middle of the chart. It is named for the pair and its reaction. Every pair of elements has one now. Its marks work only while you carry both elements.
- **Pair marks change their reaction.** Many pair marks change the numbers of their own reaction: bigger, longer or harder. The guide shows the new numbers when the mark is yours.
- **A surge for every pair.** Each pair has a surge of its own. Choose it at a rest fire while you carry both elements. It goes in the Hand's surge slot and uses its own mana and time.
- **Devotion.** Carry one element twice (all in) and you can buy its Devotion, a strong mark no pair can have. The deep strike (L2 with R2, all in) now needs the Devotion. Until you buy it, R2 all in is the plain infused strike and costs no mana.
- **Shapes at the rest fire.** A new Shapes entry, after the Hand, lists each spell and reaction that has a Shape mark. Pick which shape it wears.
- **Spells that move you.** Some pair spells now take more than one press of their button. Three Weasels rushes in and stops just in front of the enemy, then the next press cuts straight through. Under the Black Water swaps places with the enemy. Elites and gods stay put, and you come up behind them. Its pool curses, and your next blow hits half again as hard.
- **Dodges remember the enemy.** A run of dodges keeps counting "toward" and "away" against the first enemy. It lets go when that enemy dies, when you get 16 metres away, or after 3 seconds with no dodge.
- **New effects.** 32 reactions are redrawn under their game names. 13 element looks are new, among them Glaze's frost and Bedrock's earth armour on your body. Gust and Stone Rise are now looks. Nothing is drawn on your blade any more: the slashes and their colours stay. Bright white flashes are gone. A finished enemy gets a pale gold cross, and the ring that grows as an enemy staggers is gold too. A Burn set off and an ice shatter each burst in their own colour. A soldier's guard arc hides while its guard is held open.
- **Ice swings like ice.** Ice's swing trail is now pale glacier ice with a steel-blue edge. It no longer looks like Water or Metal.
- **Not yet:** some new marks still use a stand-in look or none (the tide, the waves behind you, a ripe seed, the blade drinking fire, Shared Roots' roots, Into the Fire's lunge). Pair surges wear their first element's look. A few pair spells still wait for their extra presses, and the guide says which under "Not yet".

## New in 0.4 (phase 3: reactions)

- **Reactions, all 55.** When an affliction lands on an enemy that already has another element's affliction, the two react, and every pair of elements does something different: **Flashover** (Fire and Lightning: the Static goes off through the flame), **Steam** (Fire and Water: a scalding puff), **Thermal Shock** (Fire and Ice: the enemy cracks, and your next heavy blow splits it), **Capsize** (Wind and Water: the enemy falls down), **Smoulder** (Dark and Fire: the fire sinks in, grows stronger, then flares up), **Fish Poison** (Poison and Water: enemies with lots of poison float up, stunned), and 49 more. The guide to effects has an entry for each one, with its numbers.
- **Many need your weapon.** A reaction often leaves something on the enemy for a few seconds: a crust of ice that your heavy blow blows out of its far side, a crack your heavy blow splits, a stone that rocks until your third blow knocks it down, or coils of roots your blows pull tighter. Some go off when the enemy dies (Smoulder flares up, Shut in the Dark's jar breaks, Drowned Weight bursts).
- **Where you stand matters.** Several reactions throw things away from you, past the enemy (Carried Embers, Rime, Red Hot, Golden Feathers, Thunder Gust). Get the enemy between you and the others.
- **How they work.** A reaction happens once, then has to wait a few seconds before it can happen again on that enemy (most wait 4 seconds, the pairs three apart on the ring wait 6, and a few have their own wait: Steam 3 seconds, Shut in the Dark and The Grudge 8 seconds). Afflictions that a reaction adds don't start a reaction of their own. Anything can set one off: your weapon, spells, marks, the charged blade, strikes, even an enemy wet from the river. The order never matters, but Seized (Lightning and Water) and The Grudge (Dark and Lightning) need two or more Static on the enemy.
- **Big enemies resist.** Anything that holds or slows an enemy works half as well on an elite and not at all on a god. Some reactions give elites and gods their own numbers instead (each entry says).
- **You see the name.** A reaction's name rises over the enemy for a moment. Each reaction's own look comes later. For now, there is a puff in its two colours, plus whatever it does (a burst of lightning, a burst, fires, a slick).
- **Switch between weapon and spells to react.** Carry two elements. Your weapon adds the first element's affliction, and your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike add the second's.
- **Not yet:** the duo branches' shapes that change a reaction will come in a later update. Many reactions are simpler than their full plan, and each entry in the guide says what is still to come under "Not yet": the side a blow comes from (Rime, Mock Suns), things to pick up (Shining Herb, Firebloom), standing stones and tree trunks (Anvil Stone, Frost Heave, Girdled), The Grudge's cloud answering the enemy's blow, Black Chain's shared wounds, and others.

## New in 0.3 (phase 2: two elements and the Hand)

- **Two elements at once.** Carry a first and a second element (carrying the same one twice is called all in). Each spell is cast in its own element, adds its own affliction, and looks like its own element, in its own colour.
- **The altars set both.** If you carry one element, use another altar to make its element your second. Use the altar of the element you carry to go all in. If you carry two (or are all in), an altar starts you over with only its element. Before you press, the altar's prompt tells you what it will do ("Carry Lightning as second", "Carry Fire all in", "Carry Ice alone"). You arrive carrying Fire.
- **Your weapon only adds the first element.** Weapon blows add the first element's affliction, even if you've learned the second element's root mark. A mixed branch's own marks are the exception: Biting Wind, Wet Seed and the rest add both of theirs. The second element's affliction comes from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike, so two afflictions meet on an enemy when you switch between weapon and spells.
- **The charged blade takes the element of the spell that landed.** Flame Wave charges it with Fire, and Thunderclap with Lightning. Only the first thing that cast hits counts. A duo spell charges it with the first element of its pair.
- **Strikes for each element.** L2 with R1 (RB) is the first element's strike, and L2 with R2 (RT) is the second's. Neither costs mana. If you carry one element, or are all in, R2 is the deep strike for 20 mana, like before.
- **The rest menu.** A rest fire now opens a menu: Set the Hand, The chart, and Leave the fire. Closing the chart brings you back to it.
- **The Hand.** Your four spells in a diamond laid out like the buttons, with the surge in the middle. Choose a slot to see only what can go there, grouped by element, with each spell's role and a short description. The weapon skill button shows the numbers. Pick the surge from either element. Suggested Hand fills it for you. Three saved Hands keep a Hand for each kind of fight (use puts one on, and heavy attack saves the one you have on there). Once you set a Hand, the slots stop filling themselves. The Hand never changes which elements you carry.
- **The spell diamond** colours each slot by its own spell's element, and names the R2 strike for the second element.
- **Duo branches** (Plasma and the other mixes) only work while you carry both of their elements. A duo spell sits in a slot like any other.
- **Slots open as you go, outside the playtest.** In the full game, you start with two slots (West and North) and the surge. East opens when the Champion of the Gate falls, and South when the Fire god falls, and they stay open from then on. The Hand shows a closed slot and tells you what opens it. In the playtest, every slot is open.
- **Not yet:** in the full game, there's still nowhere to choose the second element (the hub's braziers only give one element). That comes when the hub's choice is reworked. Duo surges, shapes, and the planned starting Hands for each element come with the reshaped trees.

## New in 0.2 (phase 1: spells on cooldowns, mana for big moves, the charged blade, infused strikes and the spell diamond)

- **The charged blade.** A spell from one of your four slots that lands charges your weapon with its element. Your next three blows that land carry it: each hits 20% harder and adds the element's affliction, and the third does one extra thing (Fire leaves the ground burning, Light shoots a ray, Poison makes the poison bite right away, and so on). You can see it on the weapon: flame and embers along the edge for Fire, lightning crackling along it, frost for Ice. Three dots next to the spell diamond count the blows left. A new spell that lands gives you three fresh blows.
- **Infused strikes.** Hold L2 (LT) and press R1 (RB): one of your weapon's own moves, with your element on it, shaped by the weapon (the Katana's drawing cut sends Burning Edge along its swing, the Hammer's drop puts it in a circle, the Spear's thrust in a line). L2 with R2 (RT) is the deep strike, a heavier version, for 20 mana. Each button has an 8-second wait, and fighting with your weapon shortens the wait, like a spell's. A strike counts as a weapon blow (it gives mana and shortens spell waits) but never charges the blade. On the keyboard, hold Left Ctrl and click the left or right mouse button. Every element has its own pair of strikes; the guide to effects lists them.
- **The spell diamond** in the bottom-right corner replaces the old line of spells. It shows your four slots laid out like the face buttons, each with its picture, its button, and shading that sweeps back as its wait runs out, plus the surge in the middle and the two strikes under it. It is always small, and grows while L2 is held, showing each spell's name. The buttons are named for the controller or keys you used last, and follow any buttons you've changed.
- **Hunger** (Dark's surge, paid for with health) now has a 25-second wait after it ends before you can light it again.

- **Four spell slots on the L2 button.** Hold L2 (LT) and press Square, Triangle, Circle or Cross (X, Y, B, A) to cast the spell in that slot. On the keyboard, 1, 2, 3 and 4 cast them directly. While L2 is held, the face buttons only cast spells: no flask, skill, dodge or use.
- **The surge is a tap of L2.** Tap it and let go quickly. On the keyboard, it stays on F.
- **The weapon skill has its own button:** Triangle (Y), or Q. Holding the heavy button now only gives the charged heavy blow.
- **Basic spells cost no mana.** Instead, each slot has a 6- to 12-second wait after a cast. Fighting with your weapon brings spells back sooner: a light blow that lands takes 0.3 seconds off every waiting slot, a heavy blow 0.6 seconds, a perfect guard 2 seconds, a perfect dodge 1.5 seconds, and a finisher 3 seconds.
- **Mana is for the big moves:** the surge, the weapon skill, and each element's one larger spell (Meteor Leap, Seven Flashes, Sky Dance, Winter Wall, Poured Blade, Elder Tree, Grave of Swords, Daybreak, One by One; the duo spells built so far too). You enter each area with half your mana. Out of a fight, mana rises to half and stops there; anything above half is kept. A fight always starts with at least half.
- **Filling the slots.** Your element's own spell starts in West, and each spell you learn fills the next empty slot. At a rest fire, choose a learned spell on the chart and press use to put it in the next slot. The Hand screen comes in the next update.
- **Controls:** the pause menu's controls page has rows for the spell button and the four slots, for both the controller and the keyboard.
- **Not yet:** marks that only made the basic spell cheaper do nothing for now, and some spell details on the chart still describe the old way of casting; the trees will be reshaped later. Two elements at once and the Hand screen come in the next update.

## New in 0.1

- **The Proving Ground**, where the playtest begins: a rest fire, a rack with every weapon (the Storm Katana and the Sun-Spear among them), altars for every element, training dummies (one that stands still, one that fights back with red and pale blows, and one with each element's affliction on it), pens where you can let out the castle's soldiers and the Fire land's people, gates to Narukami and Khepharu, and doors to the Castle, the Summit, Sun City and the Sun Court.
- **Playtest rules.** Resting gives you unlimited Shards, so you can buy every mark, the gods' gifts included. You can walk the chart into every tree and branch. Falling in battle puts you back at the last fire with nothing lost.
- **Back to the Proving Ground** from any level: pause, then choose Return to the Proving Ground.
- **The guide to effects**, from the pause menu and from the chart: every affliction, state and resource, with its numbers, what sets it off, how to use it, and how it looks.
- **Details for the high-level marks** on the chart: what a mark does, when, the numbers, what it works with, and an example. Written in full for the Fire and Lightning trees and their mixes; the other trees show what their data says for now.
- **Not built yet**: empty slots on the rack and on the chart show where the gods' weapons and boughs still to come will go.
