# Combat Playtest 0.2 (unreleased)

## What this playtest is

A testbed for the combat. It grows with each update as the fighting is reworked. Everything is open: try anything, and tell us what felt good, what felt wrong and what you did not understand.

## New in 0.2 (phase 1, first half: spells on cooldowns and mana for big moves)

- **Four spell slots on the L2 layer.** Hold L2 (LT) and press Square, Triangle, Circle or Cross (X, Y, B, A) to cast the spell in that slot. On the keyboard, 1, 2, 3 and 4 cast them directly. While L2 is held the face buttons are spells only: no flask, skill, dodge or use.
- **The surge is a tap of L2.** Tap it and let go quickly. On the keyboard it stays on F.
- **The weapon skill has its own button:** Triangle (Y), or Q. Holding the heavy button now gives the charged heavy only.
- **Basic spells cost no mana.** Each slot waits 6 to 12 s after a cast instead. Fighting with the weapon brings spells back sooner: a light blow that lands takes 0.3 s off every waiting slot, a heavy blow 0.6 s, a perfect guard 2 s, a perfect dodge 1.5 s, a finisher 3 s.
- **Mana is for the big moves:** the surge, the weapon skill and each element's one larger spell (Meteor Leap, Seven Flashes, Sky Dance, Winter Wall, Poured Blade, Elder Tree, Grave of Swords, Daybreak, One by One; the built duo spells too). You enter each area at half. Out of a fight mana rises to half and stops there; above half it is kept. A fight always starts with at least half.
- **Filling the slots.** Your element's own spell starts in West, and each spell you learn fills the next empty slot. At a rest fire, choose a learned spell on the chart and press use to put it in the next slot round. The Hand screen comes in the next update.
- **The spell line** under your weapon's name shows each slot's button, its spell, and the seconds it still waits or its mana.
- **Controls:** the pause menu's controls page has rows for the spell layer and the four slots, on the pad and on the keys.
- **Not yet:** the charged blade, the infused strikes (L2 with R1 or R2) and the spell diamond on the screen come in the second half of this phase. Marks that only made the base spell cheaper do nothing for now; the trees are reshaped later.

## New in 0.1

- **The Proving Ground**, where the playtest begins: a rest fire, a rack of every weapon (the Storm Katana and the Sun-Spear among them), altars for every element, training dummies (one still, one that fights back with red and pale blows, and one carrying each element's affliction), pens to let out the castle's soldiers and the Fire land's people, gates to Narukami and Khepharu, and doors to the Castle, the Summit, Sun City and the Sun Court.
- **Playtest rules.** Resting gives unlimited Shards, so every mark can be bought, the gods' gifts included. The chart can be walked into every tree and branch. A fall puts you back at the last fire with nothing lost.
- **Back to the Proving Ground** from any level: pause, then Return to the Proving Ground.
- **The guide to effects**, from the pause menu and from the chart: every affliction, state and resource, with its numbers, what sets it off, how to use it and how it looks.
- **Details for the high-level marks** on the chart: what a mark does, when, the numbers, what it works with and an example. Written in full for the Fire and Lightning trees and their mixes; the other trees show what their data says for now.
- **Not built yet**: empty slots on the rack and on the chart show where the gods' weapons and boughs still to come will be.
