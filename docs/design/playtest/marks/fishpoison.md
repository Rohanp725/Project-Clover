# Fish Poison: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Poison and Water share, named for its reaction,
Fish Poison (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Poison and Water"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Poison and
Water are both carried.

## fishpoison.shape
**What it does.** Bad Water: opens Fish Poison's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Fish Poison wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Rock Pool**: a small slick that floats its enemy at only two doses: one enemy, often. **The Ebb Takes It**: the fouled water runs away from you as a stream, soaking all along it, and floats what carries four doses.

**When it happens.** Every time Fish Poison fires (poison landing on a Soaked enemy, or water on a poisoned one) while Poison and Water are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Fish Poison runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Rock Pool: a slick 1.5 metres across for 5 seconds that soaks what stands in it; dosed two deep or more, it floats up stunned. The Ebb Takes It: four patches of slick running 6 metres on from it away from you, each 1.5 metres across, for 5 seconds; dosed four deep, it floats. The float as plain: lifted and held 1.5 seconds (elites 0.5; gods take 30 stagger instead), and your next weapon blow on it is critical and spends two doses for 6 damage each. Fish Poison rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** The Tide Brings It, Wide Slick, Fast Water, Poison the Lagoon.

**Example.** A duel with one brute: Rock Pool, and float it again and again. A line of soldiers: The Ebb Takes It, and soak the whole line.

**Not built yet.** Only the enemy that reacts can float: another that comes to the doses in the slick or along the stream does not float yet.

## fishpoison.tide
**What it does.** Riptide's wave, crossing a fouled slick, carries it: everything the wave carries takes two doses.

**When it happens.** As built, each spell of yours that strikes an enemy both Soaked and poisoned, while Poison and Water are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Two doses, at most every half second.

**Works with.** Fish Poison (more doses, more floats), Fast Water, Water's Riptide.

**Example.** Wash a fouled pack toward you and each comes in sicker.

**Not built yet.** Riptide does not yet pick up the slick it crosses: any spell striking a soaked, poisoned enemy lays the two doses.

## fishpoison.wideslick
**What it does.** Fish Poison's slick is 4 metres across and lasts 2 seconds longer.

**When it happens.** Each time Fish Poison fires.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. The slick is 4 metres across, not 3, and stands 7 seconds, not 5. In Rock Pool the pool is 2 metres across and stands 7 seconds; in The Ebb Takes It the stream stands 7 seconds.

**Works with.** Fish Poison and its shapes, The Tide Brings It.

**Example.** The slick reaches the soldier beside it.

## fishpoison.fastwater
**What it does.** Poison on a Soaked enemy bites twice as often and fades twice as fast.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Poison and Water are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Poison usually bites every 2 seconds and lasts 6. On a Soaked enemy it bites every second and lasts 3. Each enemy keeps its own pace, so one enemy getting wet does not change the others.

**Works with.** Fish Poison, The Tide Brings It, Poison the Lagoon.

**Example.** Soak a poisoned pack and the bites come fast.

## fishpoison.lagoon
**What it does.** You crush the fish-poison seed on the floor ahead: a slick spreads in rings to 6 metres across and stands 6 seconds, soaking all in it. Each weapon blow on an enemy in it stirs the water, a dose to every other in it, and any that reaches four doses floats up as Fish Poison does.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Poison and Water are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Poison.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 7 metres away, for 6 seconds: once a second 1 damage, a dose and a Soaked to every enemy in it; one dosed four deep is made to react at once, past its rest, at most once a second.

**Works with.** Fish Poison (everything in it reacts), Fast Water, The Tide Brings It.

**Example.** Drop it under a pack, then work the slick with your blade.

**Not built yet.** Your weapon blows do not yet stir the water to dose the others in it, and the forced reaction comes at most once a second for all, not once for each enemy.
