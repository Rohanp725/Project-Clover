# Fire: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Fire tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## fire.cinder
**What it does.** Every blow of your weapon that lands sets the enemy burning and lays one stack of Burn. Each further blow adds another stack, up to five. A burning enemy takes damage every second, more for each stack.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every weapon blow that lands, light or heavy.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One stack a blow, five at most. A burn deals 5 damage a second and each stack adds 30%, so 12.5 a second at five. Each blow sets its clock back to 4 seconds; when it runs out the stacks go with it. A burn set off by another mark deals 10 damage and 10 more for each stack: 60 at five.

**Works with.** Heat, Burn and Detonate, Catching Fire, Searing Edge, Slow Fuse, Dry Tinder, and Kindle, which sets every blow burning.

**Example.** Five quick light blows on a soldier leave it five deep, burning for 12.5 a second while you step back and raise your guard.

## fire.heat
**What it does.** Landing blows without being struck builds Heat, a level at a time, up to five. Each level makes your weapon hit harder. A blow that lands on you puts it out, and standing off too long cools it a level at a time.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. Every weapon blow that lands counts; lighting Kindle with the surge button fills it at once.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Three blows a level, five levels. Each level adds 6% weapon damage, 30% at five. Six seconds without landing a blow loses a level. A blow that lands on you drops it to nothing.

**Works with.** Stoked (two blows a level), Banked Coals (nine seconds, not six), White Heat, Wildfire Rush (a level for each enemy passed), Kindle, and Backdraft.

**Example.** Fifteen blows in, every cut is 30% heavier. One careless swing from a brute and you are back to nothing.

## fire.catch
**What it does.** When a burning enemy dies, its burn is thrown onto everyone standing near it, as deep as it was.

**When it happens.** The moment a burning enemy dies, whatever killed it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Every enemy within 4 metres of the one that died takes as many stacks of Burn as it carried, up to five. It can happen at most five times a second.

**Works with.** Cinder to build the depth first, Searing Edge and Slow Fuse to make the passed burns count, Wildfire (the ground burns where it fell as well), and Burn and Detonate or Open Furnace to set the new burns off.

**Example.** Kill a soldier at five Burn in the middle of a pack, and the two beside it are five deep at once without a blow from you.

## fire.crescent
**What it does.** The Iaido Slash throws a slow arc of flame ahead of you that travels on after the cut, striking and burning what it passes through.

**When it happens.** Each time you perform the Iaido Slash, the Katana's weapon skill (hold the heavy attack to full). It needs the Katana.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The arc starts 1.4 metres ahead of you, 1.5 metres in radius, and travels at 8 metres a second for 1.1 seconds, about 9 metres. It strikes each enemy once for 18 damage and 12 stagger and sets it burning for 3 seconds. The skill costs its usual mana.

**Works with.** Cinder and Heat on the cut itself, Catching Fire, and Burn and Detonate on what the arc leaves burning.

**Example.** Against a line of spearmen, the slash cuts the first and the flame rolls on to burn the two behind it.

## fire.rush
**What it does.** You become a point of fire and drive straight through every enemy in your path, coming out the far side facing back. Each enemy passed is struck, set burning and raises your Heat a level. Then a second cut lands on everyone on the line, and the line burns. Nothing can strike you while you drive; a wall, or something too big to shove, stops you.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 35 mana. Up to 7 metres, along a lane 2 metres wide. Passing: 14 damage, 20 stagger and two stacks of Burn. The cut after: 14 damage, 20 stagger and two stacks more. The line burns 2.6 seconds, biting for 4 every half second. One Heat level for each enemy passed.

**Works with.** Heat and White Heat, Cinder, Catching Fire, Burn and Detonate, Hot Coals.

**Example.** Drive through three soldiers in a row: Heat goes from none to three, and each is four deep in Burn.

## fire.whiteheat
**What it does.** Heat can rise to eight. Every level above five also quickens your swing. But struck while above five, you catch fire yourself for a while.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The speed comes with each level above five; the burn comes when a blow lands on you up there.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Heat to eight, 48% weapon damage at the top. Each level above five adds 5% swing speed, 15% at eight. Struck above five, you burn for 4 seconds at 4 damage a second; it never takes your last point. The blow still puts your Heat out, as always.

**Works with.** Heat, Stoked, Banked Coals, Wildfire Rush, and Kindle, which now fills Heat to eight at once.

**Example.** Light Kindle at the start of a fight: you are at eight Heat straight away, swinging 15% faster for 48% more, as long as nothing touches you.

## fire.backdraft
**What it does.** A perfect guard throws a cone of fire back at the one who struck. You turn to face it; everything in the cone is struck and set burning, and the attacker takes two stacks of Burn besides.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A cone 4.5 metres long and 100 degrees wide. 16 damage and 18 stagger to all in it, and each burns for 3 seconds. Two stacks of Burn on the attacker.

**Works with.** Furnace Breath (55% more stagger), Wide Hearth (25% further), Struck Flint, Kindling, Burn and Detonate (the cone sets off every burn it touches), Kiln Stance and Open Furnace.

**Example.** Against a soldier's thrust, guard just before it lands: the fire throws it off balance and leaves it burning two deep before you have swung.

## fire.detonate
**What it does.** A perfect guard sets off the burn on burning enemies: all of it goes at once as a single blow, and the burn ends. With Backdraft, which it grows from, every burning enemy in Backdraft's cone is set off, not only the one who struck.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard while the enemies in front of you are burning.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A burn set off deals 10 damage and 10 more for each stack: 60 at five. Backdraft's fire then lights it again for 3 seconds with no depth, and the attacker takes its two stacks. Wide Hearth: 25% further.

**Works with.** Cinder, Searing Edge, Backdraft, Kiln Stance (its caught blow counts as a perfect guard), and Arc Fire on the Plasma branch. Open Furnace replaces it with a wider version.

**Example.** Lay five Burn on a brute, then perfect-guard its next swing: 60 damage lands at once on top of Backdraft's cone.

## fire.trail
**What it does.** Your dodge leaves a line of fire on the ground behind it that burns whatever walks through.

**When it happens.** Every dodge.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Four patches along the dodge's path, each 0.95 metres in radius, laid as you roll. Each burns for 2.6 seconds and bites for 4 damage every half second, setting what it touches burning for 2 seconds.

**Works with.** Long Embers (54% longer), Hot Footing (50% harder), Flashpoint, Scorched Earth (the line lasts twice as long, and standing in it feeds you), and Wildfire Rush for a second line.

**Example.** Roll back from a charging brute: it runs through all four patches to reach you and arrives burning.

## fire.kiln
**What it does.** You stand ready in flame. The first blow that reaches you, from any side, red attack or not, is turned for nothing and answered with a pillar of fire under the one who struck. The caught blow counts as a perfect guard, so it gives that mana and sets off every mark that waits on one. If nothing comes, the stance goes out and the mana is lost. A dodge lets it go.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button, then any blow in the next 1.2 seconds.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 30 mana. Ready for 1.2 seconds. The pillar is 1.7 metres in radius: 30 damage, 50 stagger, a heavy blow, three stacks of Burn.

**Works with.** Backdraft, Burn and Detonate, Open Furnace, Struck Flint, and Twin Break on the Plasma branch.

**Example.** A brute winds up a red overhead you cannot guard: cast just before it falls, and the pillar answers it.

## fire.flash
**What it does.** After a perfect dodge, the next swing you land within three seconds lays two more stacks than usual, so with Cinder it sets a burn three deep at once.

**When it happens.** A perfect dodge: a blow passes through the very start of your roll. The gift waits up to three seconds for a swing that lands, and that swing spends it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Two extra stacks, for 3 seconds or one swing that lands, whichever comes first.

**Works with.** Cinder, Flame Trail, Burn and Detonate and Open Furnace (a deep burn to set off), Heat.

**Example.** Roll through a soldier's swing at the last moment and cut it on the way out: it is three deep in Burn from one blow.

## fire.furnace
**What it does.** A perfect guard sets off the burn of every burning enemy near you, each where it stands, not only the one who struck, and leaves each still alight. The price: a plain guard, one that is not perfect, lets more of a blow through as grey health.

**When it happens.** Always on: the blast with every perfect guard, the price with every plain one.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Reaches 8 metres round you. Each enemy's burn is set off on it alone, 10 damage and 10 more for each stack, and it keeps two stacks afterward. A plain guard lets 60% of a blow through as grey health, where it lets 35%.

**Works with.** Cinder, Catching Fire, Searing Edge, Backdraft and Kiln Stance (both count as perfect guards), and Arc Fire on the Plasma branch.

**Example.** Four soldiers burning round you, five deep: one perfect guard sets off all four for 60 each, and they are still burning.

## fire.firebomb
**What it does.** Holding the flask button throws the flask instead of drinking it. It heals nothing: it bursts in fire where it lands, leaves flames on the ground there, and lays three stacks of Burn on everything near. A short press still drinks.

**When it happens.** Hold the flask button. Each throw uses a flask.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Hold 0.3 seconds to throw. It flies at the enemy ahead within 9 metres, or 5 metres ahead, and lands 0.4 seconds later. The burst is 2.6 metres in radius: 36 damage, 30 stagger, three stacks of Burn and 4 seconds of burning. The flames left are 2.2 metres in radius and bite for 5 every half second for 3.5 seconds.

**Works with.** Burn and Detonate, Catching Fire, Wildfire, Scorched Earth.

**Example.** Three soldiers bunch at a gate: one flask leaves all three three deep and standing in fire.

## fire.meteor
**What it does.** You leap out of the fight and come down beside the enemy you face as a heavy blow, leaving a burning crater. Nothing can strike you in the air.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 50 mana. It finds an enemy faced within 9 metres and lands you 1 metre short of it; with none, 5 metres ahead. The leap takes 0.62 seconds. The landing strikes everything within 3.2 metres for 46 damage and 55 stagger. The crater is about 2.6 metres in radius, burns for 4 seconds and bites for 5 every half second, keeping what stands in it alight.

**Works with.** Scorched Earth (the crater lasts twice as long), Hot Coals, Burn and Detonate, Catching Fire.

**Example.** An archer stands behind two shield-bearers: leap over them and land on it.

## fire.sunspot
**What it does.** You brand the enemy you face with a spot of fire. Each light blow you land on it feeds the brand, up to six. A heavy blow sets it off in one burst, bigger for every blow fed, which also lays the branded enemy's own Burn on all it reaches. Left alone, it bursts at half when its time runs out. With no enemy faced the mana is lost.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button. Then light blows feed it and a heavy blow on it sets it off.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 35 mana. An enemy within 6 metres; the brand lasts 7 seconds. The burst is 3 metres in radius: 12 damage and 10 stagger, plus 9 damage and 8 stagger for each blow fed (66 and 58 at six), and one stack of Burn.

**Works with.** Cinder (the branded enemy's depth is passed on), Heat, Hot Coals, Catching Fire.

**Example.** Brand a brute, land six light blows, then a heavy: its Burn spreads to all near.

## fire.scorched
**What it does.** Fire you leave on the ground burns twice as long. While you stand in it, Kindle does not run down and your blows give more mana. The price: your spells cost more.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The Kindle and mana gifts hold for as long as you stand in fire you left.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Twice as long for every fire you lay on the ground. Standing in it: Kindle's clock stops, and weapon blows give 50% more mana. Every worn spell costs 20% more.

**Works with.** Flame Trail, Meteor Leap's crater, Firebomb Flask, Wildfire, Wildfire Rush, Falling Sun, Kindle, Long Kindle.

**Example.** Throw the flask at your feet, light Kindle and fight inside the flames: Kindle never ends while you hold the ground.

## fire.wildfire
**What it does.** Where an enemy dies, the ground burns for a while, biting and burning whatever stands there.

**When it happens.** Whenever an enemy dies.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A patch 2.2 metres in radius where it fell, for 4 seconds. It bites for 5 damage every half second and lays one stack of Burn with each bite.

**Works with.** Catching Fire, Scorched Earth (the patch lasts twice as long), Burn and Detonate, Cinder.

**Example.** Kill the first of a pack where they are crowding in, and the rest must walk through fire to reach you.

## fire.fan
**What it does.** You throw fire in a wide fan close in front of you. What it touches burns two deep.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 30 mana. A cone 4.5 metres long and 140 degrees wide. 22 damage, 20 stagger and two stacks of Burn to everything in it.

**Works with.** Hot Coals, Cinder, Burn and Detonate, Catching Fire.

**Example.** Surrounded on three sides: one fan sets the whole front half of the ring burning.

## fire.sun
**What it does.** Lighting Kindle starts the sun on its road. At dawn it is rolled to the enemy you face, and the ground it crosses burns. At noon it hangs over that enemy, and burns near you deepen by themselves. At dusk it sets on the enemy below, heavier for every burn it finds. A blow on you sends it under, to rise again.

**When it happens.** Light Kindle with the surge button. It sets when Kindle ends, or when you press the surge button again.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Dawn: up to 9 metres; the first enemy met takes 14 damage, 20 stagger, two Burn. Noon: each second, a stack of Burn on all within 6 metres of you. Dusk: 60 damage, 80 stagger in 4 metres, 8% more a Burn stack found, up to double; half if called while rolling. Sent under, it rises in 1.5 seconds if Kindle has 3 left.

**Works with.** Cinder, Heat, Long Kindle.

**Example.** Press again over a crowd at five Burn.
