# Burning Sap: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Light and Poison share, named for its reaction,
Burning Sap (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Light and Poison"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Light and
Poison are both carried.

## burningsap.shape
**What it does.** Lit Veins: opens Burning Sap's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Burning Sap wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **The Light Finds Them**: when it reacts, the enemies across the room are Marked as well, their veins lit. **Blistered**: the weals do not wait for the end; each bursts at the blow that raises it.

**When it happens.** Every time Burning Sap fires (poison landing on a marked enemy, or a Mark on a poisoned one) while Light and Poison are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Burning Sap runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The Light Finds Them: the sap wakes for 3 seconds as before; every enemy within 8 metres is Marked; at the end the poison bites, and each weal bursts, two doses on all within 2.5 metres. Blistered: for 3 seconds each of your weapon blows on it splashes two doses on everything within 2.5 metres; at the end the poison bites. Burning Sap rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Glare and Miasma to bring the two, Seen Through, Nectar After Poison, Bared to the Sun.

**Example.** A poisoned pack: The Light Finds Them marks them all at once for your criticals. A duel: Blistered, and three quick blows splash the poison onto whatever comes to help.

**Not built yet.** The Light Finds Them marks every enemy within 8 metres, not only the poisoned; Blistered's weals come with every weapon blow, not only criticals.

## burningsap.nectar
**What it does.** An enemy that dies carrying both a Mark and poison gives up health and mana.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy carrying both dies, while Light and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. You heal 6 and gain 8 mana.

**Works with.** Burning Sap, Bared to the Sun, Daybreak and One by One to spend the mana.

**Example.** Clear a marked, poisoned pack and walk out with your health and mana up.

## burningsap.seenthrough
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a poisoned enemy Marks it and makes all its poison bite at once.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against a poisoned enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A Mark, and every dose it carries bites now.

**Works with.** Burning Sap (the Mark on the poisoned starts it), Bright Venom, Five Needles.

**Example.** A poisoned brute swings: guard it perfectly and it is marked, bitten and its sap is waking.

## burningsap.brightvenom
**What it does.** The held bites strike 15% harder when they land.

**When it happens.** Each time Burning Sap fires, while Light and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. As built: one more dose of poison as Burning Sap fires.

**Works with.** Seen Through, which it grows from, Burning Sap.

**Example.** Each sap that wakes carries one dose more into its bite.

**Not built yet.** The held bites do not strike harder yet; a dose more stands in.

## burningsap.bared
**What it does.** You tear the shade off a place: a hard-edged shaft of sun falls on the aim point for 5 seconds. Every enemy in it is Marked, and the poisoned start Burning Sap at once, whatever its rest.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Light and Poison are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Light.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 13 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 8 metres ahead, 2.5 metres in radius, for 5 seconds: once a second, 2 damage and a Mark to all in it; a poisoned one starts Burning Sap (at most once a second in all).

**Works with.** Burning Sap and its shapes, Miasma (poison them first), Nectar After Poison.

**Example.** Poison a pack, then lay the sun over it.

**Not built yet.** It forces Burning Sap at most once a second for all of them, not once on each; your weapon blows inside do not yet raise more weals.
