# The Ground Gives: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Water and Earth share, named for its reaction, The
Ground Gives (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Water and Earth"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Water and
Earth are both carried.

## thegroundgives.shape
**What it does.** Wet Ground: opens The Ground Gives' two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your The Ground Gives wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Quicksand**: the sand drags what stands near it into its middle, so enemies end up together. **Sand Boil**: the pool lasts only a moment, then erupts in a fountain of mud and water.

**When it happens.** Every time The Ground Gives fires (Tremor landing on a soaked enemy, or Soaked on a shaken one) while Water and Earth are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain The Ground Gives runs (sunk to the knees for 4 seconds, slowed by half).

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Quicksand: sunk for 4 seconds, slowed by half; once a second everything within 1.5 metres is drawn 1 metre toward it; a heavy blow holds it 0.5 seconds. Sand Boil: sunk for 1.2 seconds, then lifted for 1 second, and everything within 3 metres takes 12 damage, a Soaked and a Tremor. The Ground Gives rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Riptide and Stone Rise to bring the two, Driven Down, Given to the Bog, The Island Breathes.

**Example.** Quicksand in a pack: the soldiers round the sunk one are drawn into a knot for one sweep. Sand Boil before a heavy combo: it comes up into your blows.

**Not built yet.** The pool itself is not drawn as a place: Quicksand draws from the sunk enemy outward, 1.5 metres.

## thegroundgives.bog
**What it does.** An enemy that dies sunk is swallowed and the bog gives back: a quarter of a flask.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy dies carrying both Soaked and Tremor, while Water and Earth are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A quarter of a flask, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives and its shapes, Drawn From the Drowned.

**Example.** Kill three sunk soldiers and a flask is nearly full again.

**Not built yet.** It answers any death soaked and shaken, not only one that was sunk.

## thegroundgives.drivendown
**What it does.** Heavy blows on a sunk enemy drive it deeper and hold it.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy carrying both Soaked and Tremor.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Held still for 0.5 seconds.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives, Quicksand, Mountain Stance.

**Example.** A sunk brute cannot answer: each heavy blow pins it again.

**Not built yet.** The heavy blow does not yet pass its guard, and it answers any soaked, shaken enemy, not only a sunk one.

## thegroundgives.landwassea
**What it does.** Your dodges are slides: each leaves a line of wet sand behind it and pays back its stamina.

**When it happens.** At the end of every dodge, while Water and Earth are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Three patches 0.8 metres across along 3 metres, for 3 seconds, soaking what stands in them every half second. 15 stamina back.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives (shake what you soak), Quicksand, Wake.

**Example.** Roll round a pack and leave it standing in wet sand for your Stone Rise.

**Not built yet.** It is meant only for a dodge through wet sand; as built every dodge slides.

## thegroundgives.island
**What it does.** The ground at the aim point breathes in: it heaves up like a back, and every enemy on it is lifted, shaken twice and soaked, so it sinks as The Ground Gives when it comes down.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Water and Earth are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Water.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 15 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to five pillars of ground under the enemies near the aim point (up to 8 metres), 0.9 seconds after the cast: 10 damage, 30 stagger, a Soaked and two Tremor each, and each is thrown up.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives and its shapes, Driven Down, Given to the Bog.

**Example.** Breathe in under a pack and fight them sunk.

**Not built yet.** The breath out (the place slumping into wet sand for 4 seconds) is The Ground Gives on each enemy rather than a place of its own, and blows on the lifted do not yet hold them up.
