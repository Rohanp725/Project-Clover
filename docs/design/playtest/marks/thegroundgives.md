# The Ground Gives: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Water and Earth share, named for its reaction, The
Ground Gives (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Water and Earth"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Water and
Earth are both carried.

## thegroundgives.shape
**What it does.** Wet Ground unlocks two new shapes for The Ground Gives. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your The Ground Gives uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Quicksand**: the sand pulls whatever stands near it into its middle, so enemies end up bunched together. **Sand Boil**: the pool only lasts a moment, then erupts in a fountain of mud and water.

**When it happens.** Every time The Ground Gives goes off while you carry both Water and Earth. The Ground Gives goes off when Tremor lands on a soaked enemy, or when Soaked lands on a shaken one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain The Ground Gives. The plain one sinks the enemy to its knees for 4 seconds and slows it by half.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Quicksand: the enemy sinks for 4 seconds and is slowed by half. Once a second, everything within 1.5 metres is pulled 1 metre toward it. A heavy blow holds it for 0.5 seconds. Sand Boil: the enemy sinks for 1.2 seconds, then is lifted up for 1 second, and everything within 3 metres takes 12 damage, a Soaked and a Tremor. After The Ground Gives goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Riptide and Stone Rise to set it up, Driven Down, Given to the Bog, The Island Breathes.

**Example.** Use Quicksand in a pack. The soldiers around the sunken one are pulled into a tight group for one big sweep. Use Sand Boil before a string of heavy blows: the enemy comes up right into your blows.

**Not built yet.** The pool itself isn't shown as a spot on the ground yet. For now, Quicksand pulls in toward the sunken enemy, from 1.5 metres away.

## thegroundgives.bog
**What it does.** An enemy that dies while sunk is swallowed up, and the bog gives something back: a quarter of a flask.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy dies while it is sunk (by The Ground Gives or either of its shapes), while you carry both Water and Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A quarter of a flask, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives and its shapes, Drawn From the Drowned.

**Example.** Kill three sunken soldiers, and a flask is almost full again.

## thegroundgives.drivendown
**What it does.** Heavy blows on a sunken enemy go through its guard and hold it in place.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy while it is sunk (by The Ground Gives or either of its shapes).

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Held still for 0.5 seconds more. Its guard lets heavy blows through for as long as it is sunk; light blows are still blocked.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives, Quicksand, Mountain Stance.

**Example.** A sunken brute can't fight back. Each heavy blow pins it again.

## thegroundgives.landwassea
**What it does.** Your dodges become slides. Each one leaves a line of wet sand behind it and gives back its stamina.

**When it happens.** At the end of every dodge, while you carry both Water and Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Three patches, each 0.8 metres across, along 3 metres, lasting 3 seconds. They soak whatever stands in them every half second. You get 15 stamina back.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives (shake the enemies you soak), Quicksand, Wake.

**Example.** Roll around a pack and leave it standing in wet sand, ready for your Stone Rise.

**Not built yet.** It is meant to work only on a dodge through wet sand. For now, every dodge slides.

## thegroundgives.island
**What it does.** The ground where you aim breathes in: it heaves up like the back of a huge animal, and every enemy on it is lifted up, shaken twice and soaked, so it sinks with The Ground Gives when it comes down.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Water and Earth. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Water.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 15 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. Up to five pillars of ground rise under the enemies near where you aim (up to 8 metres away), 0.9 seconds after you cast it. Each deals 10 damage, 30 stagger, a Soaked and two Tremor, and throws its enemy up.

**Works with.** The Ground Gives and its shapes, Driven Down, Given to the Bog.

**Example.** Make the ground breathe in under a pack, then fight them while they are sunk.

**Not built yet.** The ground breathing out (the spot slumping into wet sand for 4 seconds) is The Ground Gives on each enemy for now, not a spot of its own. Blows on lifted enemies don't keep them up in the air yet.
