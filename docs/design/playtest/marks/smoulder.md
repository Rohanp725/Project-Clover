# Smoulder: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Dark and Fire share, named for its reaction,
Smoulder (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Dark and Fire"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Dark and Fire are
both carried.

## smoulder.shape
**What it does.** Banked Coals: opens Smoulder's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Smoulder wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Night Sun**: the fire sinks in as before, and while it smoulders each of your blows on it heals you. **Coal Seam**: the fire goes down into the floor where it reacted, a patch of smouldering ground; any burning enemy that stands on it is cursed, and so smoulders.

**When it happens.** Every time Smoulder fires (a Curse landing on a burning enemy, or Burn on a cursed one) while Dark and Fire are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Smoulder runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Night Sun: as the plain Smoulder (its Burn deepens by one every 2 seconds while the curse lasts, and flares for 10 a stack within 3 metres when the curse ends or it dies), and each of your blows on it heals you 3, at most five times a second. Coal Seam: a patch 2.5 metres across for 8 seconds that Curses what stands on it every 2 seconds; on purpose, that Curse may start Smoulder. Smoulder rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Grasp and Cinder to bring the two, Third Eye, The Sun Goes Under, The Burning Lake.

**Example.** Night Sun on a brute you are trading blows with. Coal Seam at a doorway, then fight the burning ones across it.

**Not built yet.** Night Sun heals 3 a blow rather than 1 for each Burn stack; Coal Seam's patch does not yet flare at its end and set off every smoulder on it.

## smoulder.thirdeye
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a burning, cursed enemy opens a line of black-edged fire from you through it.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy carrying both Burn and a Curse.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A line 6 metres long ahead of you: 20 damage, 2 Burn and a Curse to all on it.

**Works with.** Smoulder, Deep Coal, Breath of the Lioness.

**Example.** A smouldering soldier swings: guard it perfectly and burn a line through it and the two behind.

## smoulder.deepcoal
**What it does.** Burn on a smouldering enemy can lie two deeper.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Dark and Fire are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards.

**Works with.** Smoulder (it deepens by itself while the curse lasts), Fire Without End.

**Example.** A smouldering brute reaches seven Burn instead of five before it flares.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: a deeper affliction on one enemy is still to come.

## smoulder.sungoesunder
**What it does.** While your health is below half, every Burn you lay smoulders, curse or no curse.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Dark and Fire are both carried and your health is below half.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: below half health, each blow that lands on a burning enemy Curses it, at most once a second, so Smoulder begins.

**Works with.** Pawned Heart and the Dark marks paid in health, Smoulder and its shapes, Third Eye.

**Example.** Wounded, every enemy you burn begins to smoulder.

**Not built yet.** It curses the burning enemy rather than making the Burn itself smoulder.

## smoulder.lake
**What it does.** A lake opens at the aim point: a still black surface, flame licking at its rim, embers rising. Every enemy on it is Cursed and takes a Burn each second, so all of them smoulder.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Dark and Fire are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Dark.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 16 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 8 metres ahead, 4 metres across, for 7 seconds: once a second, 2 damage, a Curse and a Burn to all on it.

**Works with.** Smoulder and its shapes, The Sun Goes Under, Third Eye.

**Example.** Open it under a pack and let them smoulder while you hold the edge.

**Not built yet.** It does not yet slow what stands on it, grow with each enemy killed on it, or flare every smoulder at once on a finisher.
