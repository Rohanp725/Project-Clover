# Wind: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Wind tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## wind.unsteady
**What it does.** Every blow of your weapon that lands knocks the enemy Unsteady, one stack at a time, up to five. An Unsteady enemy loses its balance sooner, is thrown further and turns more slowly, so you can circle behind it.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every weapon blow that lands, light or heavy, while Wind is your first element. With Wind as your second element the weapon lays the first element's affliction, and Unsteady comes from Gust, the charged blade and your R2 strike.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One stack a blow, five at most, lasting 6 seconds. Each stack means 6% more stagger taken, a 15% longer shove and 8% slower turning: 30%, 75% and 40% at five. During Tailwind every blow lays one more.

**Works with.** Rushing Wake (a dodge-then-blow lays two more), Rising Cut (five deep throws it into the air), Nothing Beneath, Off Balance (Unsteady lasts half again as long), and the Carried Embers reaction when a burning enemy is knocked Unsteady.

**Example.** Four light blows and a heavy on a soldier: five deep, it takes 30% more stagger from the heavy and is often broken by it.

## wind.slip
**What it does.** After a dodge, the next swing you land strikes harder and leaves its enemy two deeper in Unsteady.

**When it happens.** Every dodge, as it ends. The gift waits 1.2 seconds for a swing that lands, and that swing spends it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 30% more weapon damage and two extra stacks of Unsteady, for 1.2 seconds or one swing that lands, whichever comes first. With Following Draft it waits 2 seconds.

**Works with.** Unsteady, Long Stride (dodges 20% further), Thin Air, Passing Cut, Leaf on the Wind (more dodges), Tailwind.

**Example.** Roll past a spearman's thrust and cut it on the way out: the cut lands 30% harder and the spearman goes three deep at once.

## wind.passing
**What it does.** Where a dodge ends, a cut of wind strikes everything close to you and leaves it Unsteady. As built the cut comes where you stop, not along the path you passed through.

**When it happens.** At the end of every dodge, at most twice a second.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Everything within 2.2 metres of where you stop takes 10 damage, 8 stagger and one stack of Unsteady.

**Works with.** Rushing Wake, Clean Pass (8% more damage of every kind on an enemy carrying any affliction), Long Stride, Empty Air, Leaf on the Wind, Tailwind's longer dodge.

**Example.** Roll into the middle of three soldiers: all three are cut and Unsteady before you swing.

## wind.emptyair
**What it does.** A perfect dodge gives back the stamina the dodge cost and leaves the one who struck three deeper in Unsteady.

**When it happens.** A perfect dodge: a blow passes through the very start of your roll. When the blow came from afar, the Unsteady goes on the enemy you face.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 25 stamina back, the dodge's usual cost. Three stacks of Unsteady.

**Works with.** Thin Air (a longer safe moment), Light Feet, Rushing Wake, Rising Cut (three of the five it needs), Closing Circle, Leaf on the Wind.

**Example.** Roll through a duellist's lunge at the last moment: your stamina is full again and the duellist is three deep, two light blows from Rising Cut.

## wind.circle
**What it does.** You run a full ring round the enemy you face. When the ring closes, the wind inside it rises: everything within is struck, knocked five deep in Unsteady and thrown into the air. You end where you began, facing in, and nothing can strike you while you run.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It needs an enemy faced within 5 metres; with none, it is not cast and nothing is spent.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The ring is as wide as the gap to the enemy, 2 to 3 metres in radius. The run takes 0.7 seconds and the ring snaps shut 0.2 seconds later. All inside take 6 damage, 10 stagger and five stacks of Unsteady, and hang in the air for 1.2 seconds. What cannot be lifted reels instead.

**Works with.** Hammer Down, Open Air (20% harder on enemies in the air), Hang Time, Nothing Beneath, Empty Air, the charged blade (Wind's shoves).

**Example.** Two soldiers flank a brute: circle the brute and all three go up five deep, ready for a heavy blow each.

## wind.leaf
**What it does.** The dodge costs no stamina. It spends a breath of wind instead: you hold three, and time and blows win them back. With no breath left you cannot dodge. The price: you take more damage.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. Every dodge spends a breath; the breaths come back as you fight.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Three breaths at most, shown on the screen as Breath. One comes back every 2 seconds while you hold fewer than three, and one for every three blows you land. You take 20% more damage from every blow.

**Works with.** Rushing Wake, Passing Cut, Empty Air (its stamina is still given), Closing Circle, Tailwind's longer, safer dodge.

**Example.** Against a fast duellist, dodge three times in a row, then land three blows: a breath is back before the next swing comes.

## wind.rising
**What it does.** A heavy blow on an enemy five deep in Unsteady throws it into the air. It hangs there helpless, and each blow you land on it up there holds it up a little longer, up to three. The throw spends the Unsteady.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy carrying five Unsteady, at most twice a second.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. In the air for 1 second (elites and gods for 0.6 of it). Each of the first three blows on it up there adds 0.45 seconds. What is too heavy to lift reels for a second instead and takes 40 stagger. Hang Time adds 0.4 seconds.

**Works with.** Unsteady, Rushing Wake, Empty Air, Hammer Down, Open Air, Hang Time, Nothing Beneath, the deep strike Whirl Cut, and Thunder Lift and The Sky Finds Them on the Tempest branch.

**Example.** Five deep, a soldier takes your heavy blow and goes up: two quick light blows keep it there while you set up the third.

## wind.hammer
**What it does.** A heavy blow on an enemy in the air drives it straight into the ground. Where it lands the wind bursts, striking everything near and throwing it back.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy in the air, or one reeling because it was too heavy to lift.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The burst is 3 metres in radius round the enemy: 14 damage and 30 stagger, and everything in it is pushed 2 metres. The heavy blow itself deals its usual damage.

**Works with.** Rising Cut, Closing Circle, Lifting Wind, Sky Dance, Open Air (the heavy blow is 20% harder up there), Strong Arm (pushes 30% further).

**Example.** Lift a soldier in a pack with Lifting Wind and drive it down among them: every one is struck and thrown back.

## wind.lifting
**What it does.** A column of wind bursts up under the enemy nearest you, striking it as a heavy blow and throwing it into the air. Whatever its footing, it goes up; what is too heavy to lift reels instead.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). A ring closes under the enemy first, following it. With no enemy within reach the column comes up 5 metres ahead of you, and the slot still waits.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The nearest enemy within 5 metres. The column rises 0.35 seconds after the cast, 2 metres in radius: 8 damage, 25 stagger, a heavy blow and one stack of Unsteady to all in it, and 0.8 seconds in the air.

**Works with.** Hammer Down, Open Air, Hang Time, Nothing Beneath, Sky Dance, and the charged blade: it lands, so your next three blows carry Wind.

**Example.** An archer behind a shield: lift the archer, step in, and bring it down with a heavy blow.

## wind.skydance
**What it does.** You throw the enemy you face into the air and go up beside it. Five rings close on it, one after another: press the light attack as each closes for a cut in time, half again as hard. Then a lane follows the stick (or the cursor) for a moment, and you throw the enemy down along it. It bursts where it lands, harder for each cut in time. Nothing can strike you from the leap to the landing.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It needs an enemy faced within 6 metres that is not still reeling from the last dance; otherwise it is not cast and no mana is spent. The heavy attack throws at once.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 45 mana. A ring every 0.34 seconds; a press in the last part of its closing is in time. Each cut deals 10 damage, 15 in time; the first lays a stack of Unsteady. The throw goes 6 metres. The landing deals 20 damage, and 6 more for each cut in time (50 at five), with 60 stagger as a heavy blow, to the one thrown and to everything within 3 metres, which is pushed 3 metres away. What cannot be lifted is danced with on the ground.

**Works with.** Hammer Down, Open Air, Nothing Beneath, Against the Wall (each cut is a spell striking), Unsteady.

**Example.** Dance a brute up, land all five cuts in time, and throw it down into the soldiers behind it: 50 to each.

## wind.nothing
**What it does.** An enemy in the air stays up as long as you keep striking it, each blow there gives double mana, and your blows no longer drop it after three. The price: your weapon is weaker against an enemy that is not Unsteady.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The longer hang and the mana come with each blow that lands on an enemy in the air.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Up to 23 blows hold an enemy in the air, not three; the first adds 0.45 seconds and each after a little less, about 3 seconds more in all. Blows on an enemy in the air give twice their mana. Weapon damage is 15% lower, back to full against an Unsteady enemy.

**Works with.** Rising Cut, Closing Circle, Lifting Wind, Sky Dance, Open Air, Hang Time, Unsteady.

**Example.** Lift a soldier and keep cutting: it hangs for four seconds while your mana bar fills twice as fast.

## wind.wall
**What it does.** Each enemy your spells strike is hit by a small burst of wind that shakes it and anything beside it, and leaves them Unsteady. As built the burst comes wherever a spell strikes; it does not yet wait for a pushed enemy to meet a wall.

**When it happens.** Every time a spell of yours strikes an enemy: Gust, any spell from your slots, and the spells your marks throw.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A burst 1.6 metres in radius on each enemy struck: 6 damage, 15 stagger and one stack of Unsteady.

**Works with.** Gust, Wide Gust, Storm Front (a free Gust with every heavy blow), Lifting Wind, Indrawn Breath, Sky Dance, The Wind Remembers (each falling cut is a spell).

**Example.** Gust three soldiers in a row: each takes the gust and a burst, and they shake each other with theirs.

## wind.turned
**What it does.** A perfect guard throws everyone near you back and leaves the attacker three deeper in Unsteady.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Every enemy within 4 metres is pushed 3 metres away. Three stacks of Unsteady on the attacker.

**Works with.** Strong Arm (30% further), Rising Cut, Empty Air, Indrawn Breath, Drawing Wind.

**Example.** Ringed by four soldiers, perfect-guard the first swing: the ring is thrown back and the one who swung is three deep.

## wind.indrawn
**What it does.** You draw in your breath, and everything near is dragged into one place before you. Then the breath bursts out of that place, striking all of them as a heavy blow.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 11 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The point is 2 metres ahead, or on an enemy that close. For 0.5 seconds everything within 7 metres of it is dragged most of the way in. Then the burst, 7 metres in radius: 10 damage, 25 stagger, a heavy blow and two stacks of Unsteady to each.

**Works with.** Against the Wall, Turned Aside, Storm Front, Hammer Down, and Burn and Detonate or Catching Fire with Fire carried, since it bunches a crowd.

**Example.** Archers spread round a room: draw them all to one spot in front of you, then cut into the heap.

## wind.front
**What it does.** Every heavy blow that lands throws a Gust with it at no cost. The price: your blows give less mana.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, the Iaido Slash among them, at most once every 0.4 seconds.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. The free Gust is your usual Gust, as Wide Gust makes it: a cone 5 metres long and 70 degrees wide, 8 damage, 20 stagger and two stacks of Unsteady, throwing what it reaches down the wind. It does not start Gust's wait and never charges the blade. Weapon blows give 25% less mana.

**Works with.** Unsteady, Rising Cut, Hammer Down, Against the Wall, Wide Gust (43% wider, 20% further), Strong Arm.

**Example.** Every heavy blow in a crowd also blows the ones beside your target back two deep in Unsteady.

## wind.drawing
**What it does.** When the Iaido Slash lands, everything within six metres of you is pulled toward you and left Unsteady. It needs the Katana. As built the pull comes from all round you once the slash lands, not from the cone ahead as you draw.

**When it happens.** Each time the Iaido Slash, the Katana's weapon skill, strikes at least one enemy, at most once a second.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Everything within 6 metres is pulled 2.5 metres toward you and takes two stacks of Unsteady. The Unsteady is laid only while Wind is your first element. The skill costs its usual 30 mana.

**Works with.** Turned Aside, Rising Cut, Storm Front (the slash is a heavy blow, so it throws a Gust too), Strong Arm.

**Example.** Slash the spearman in front of you and the two archers behind it are dragged into your next swing.

## wind.remembers
**What it does.** While your surge lasts, every weapon blow you land leaves its cut hanging in the air where it fell, up to sixteen. When the surge ends, time slows and all of them fall again, one on the heels of the next, each half as hard as the blow it remembers.

**When it happens.** Light the surge with the surge button (a tap of L2, or F). The cuts fall the moment it ends.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Sixteen cuts at most; past that, each new blow is added to the lightest cut hanging. Each falls 1.4 metres round where it was struck for half the blow's damage, 10 stagger and one stack of Unsteady. Tailwind lasts 10 seconds for 35 mana.

**Works with.** Tailwind, Running Start, Fair Wind, Against the Wall (each falling cut is a spell striking), Nothing Beneath, Rising Cut.

**Example.** Light Tailwind and cut a pack sixteen times: as it ends, sixteen cuts fall on them again at half strength.
