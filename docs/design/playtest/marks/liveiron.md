# Live Iron: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Metal and Lightning share, named for its
reaction, Live Iron (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Metal and Lightning"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while
Metal and Lightning are both carried.

## liveiron.shape
**What it does.** Open Plates unlocks two new shapes for Live Iron. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Live Iron uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Drawn Iron**: the arcs of lightning turn magnetic, and each enemy they reach is pulled toward the enemy you hit, so a crowd folds in onto your blade. **Forged Bolt**: no arcs. Instead, the enemy's charge is hammered into spikes, and your heavy blow tears them out as one thunderbolt thrown in a line.

**When it happens.** Every time Live Iron goes off while you carry both Metal and Lightning. Live Iron goes off when Static lands on a notched enemy, or when a notch lands on a charged one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Live Iron.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Drawn Iron: for 6 seconds, each weapon blow on the enemy arcs to the nearest notched enemy within 8 metres for 35% of the blow's damage and notches it, up to two jumps. It also pulls everything within 8 metres 1.5 metres toward it. At most once every 0.3 seconds. Forged Bolt: for 6 seconds, the enemy's Static doesn't fade and blows don't use it up. Your first heavy blow on it takes up to three Static and throws a bolt 10 metres past it, away from you. Everything on the line takes 12 damage for each Static taken, 20 stagger and two Static. After Live Iron goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Three Smiths, Bare Wire, Iron Calls the Storm, Lightning Seeks Iron.

**Example.** A ring of armoured soldiers: use Drawn Iron, and fold them in onto one. A line of them: use Forged Bolt, charge the first one, and send it down the line with a heavy blow.

**Not built yet.** Drawn Iron pulls every enemy within 8 metres, not only the ones the arcs reach. Forged Bolt's spikes don't show up yet, one for each blow. For now, the heavy blow tears out up to three of the Static the enemy has.

## liveiron.threesmiths
**What it does.** Every third weapon blow on an enemy with Live Iron is answered from the sky: a bolt that deals 12 damage, 15 stagger and a Static.

**When it happens.** Weapon blows on an enemy that is both notched and charged, while you carry both Metal and Lightning.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Every third blow on the enemy calls a bolt down on it 0.3 seconds later. Each enemy keeps its own count. The bolt deals 12 damage, 15 stagger and a Static within 1 metre, at most once every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Live Iron and its shapes, Bare Wire, Lightning Seeks Iron.

**Example.** Keep hitting a notched, charged brute, and the sky joins in.

**Not built yet.** For now, it counts blows on any enemy that is both notched and charged, not only on an enemy with Live Iron.

## liveiron.barewire
**What it does.** Live Iron's arcs carry 45% of the blow's damage.

**When it happens.** Each time Live Iron's arcs jump.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor mark, 1 Shard. Each arc carries 45% of the blow that set it off, instead of 35%. It's the same with Drawn Iron.

**Works with.** Live Iron and Drawn Iron, Three Smiths.

**Example.** The wire bites almost as hard as the blade.

## liveiron.callsstorm
**What it does.** Every blade you throw (Flung Blade and the rest) turns in the air toward the nearest enemy with Static, and lands with two more Static.

**When it happens.** For now, each time one of your spells hits a charged enemy, while you carry both Metal and Lightning.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Two more Static, at most once every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Live Iron (more charge, longer wires), Three Smiths, Metal's Flung Blade.

**Example.** Throw your blades into the charged pack, and the storm grows stronger.

**Not built yet.** Blades don't turn in the air toward the nearest charged enemy yet. For now, any spell that hits a charged enemy adds the two Static, not only thrown blades.

## liveiron.seeksiron
**What it does.** You turn into a bolt of lightning. In a quarter of a second, while nothing can touch you, you travel to the farthest notched enemy within 10 metres. Every enemy on the line is cut for 10 damage, 15 stagger and two Static. When you arrive, you deal 16 damage and two Static.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Metal and Lightning. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Metal.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, movement, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 10 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. The line is 10 metres long. Each enemy on it takes 10 damage, 15 stagger, a notch and two Static.

**Works with.** Live Iron (each enemy cut gets both afflictions), Three Smiths, Iron Calls the Storm.

**Example.** Shoot through a line of soldiers as a bolt, and every one of them is live.

**Not built yet.** For now, it goes toward the enemy you face, not the farthest notched one, and it doesn't hit with its own blow when you arrive. A perfect guard in the 2 seconds before you cast it doesn't yet send you on to a second notched enemy.
