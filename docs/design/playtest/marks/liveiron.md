# Live Iron: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Metal and Lightning share, named for its
reaction, Live Iron (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Metal and Lightning"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while
Metal and Lightning are both carried.

## liveiron.shape
**What it does.** Open Plates: opens Live Iron's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Live Iron wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Drawn Iron**: the arcs magnetise, and each enemy they reach is drawn toward the one struck, so a crowd folds in onto your blade. **Forged Bolt**: no arcs; its charge is hammered into spikes that your heavy blow tears out as one thunderbolt thrown in a line.

**When it happens.** Every time Live Iron fires (Static landing on a notched enemy, or a notch on a charged one) while Metal and Lightning are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Live Iron runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Drawn Iron: for 6 seconds each weapon blow on it arcs to the nearest notched enemy within 8 metres for 35% of the blow and notches it, two leaps at most, and draws everything within 8 metres 1.5 metres toward it; at most every 0.3 seconds. Forged Bolt: for 6 seconds its Static neither fades nor is spent by blows; your first heavy blow on it takes up to three and throws a bolt 10 metres on from it, away from you, 12 damage for each one taken, 20 stagger and two Static to everything on the line. Live Iron rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Three Smiths, Bare Wire, Iron Calls the Storm, Lightning Seeks Iron.

**Example.** A ring of armoured soldiers: Drawn Iron, and fold them onto one. A line of them: Forged Bolt, charge the first, and heavy it down the line.

**Not built yet.** Drawn Iron draws every enemy within 8 metres, not only those the arcs reach. Forged Bolt's spikes are not yet seen standing in it, one for each blow: the heavy blow tears out up to three of the Static it carries.

## liveiron.threesmiths
**What it does.** Every third weapon blow on a Live Iron enemy is answered from the sky: a bolt for 12, 15 stagger and a Static.

**When it happens.** Weapon blows on an enemy both notched and charged, while Metal and Lightning are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. As built: one blow in three, by chance, calls a bolt down on it 0.3 seconds later, 12 damage, 15 stagger and a Static in 1 metre, at most every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Live Iron and its shapes, Bare Wire, Lightning Seeks Iron.

**Example.** Keep at a notched, charged brute and the sky joins in.

**Not built yet.** It is one blow in three by chance on any enemy both notched and charged, not every third blow counted on a Live Iron enemy.

## liveiron.barewire
**What it does.** Live Iron's arcs carry 45% of the blow.

**When it happens.** Each time Live Iron's arcs leap.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** Live Iron and Drawn Iron, Three Smiths.

**Example.** The wire bites almost as hard as the blade.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: a mark that changes the reaction's own numbers is still to come.

## liveiron.callsstorm
**What it does.** Every blade you throw (Flung Blade and the rest) turns in flight to the nearest enemy carrying Static and lands with two more.

**When it happens.** As built, each spell of yours that strikes a charged enemy, while Metal and Lightning are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Two more Static, at most every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Live Iron (more charge, longer wires), Three Smiths, Metal's Flung Blade.

**Example.** Throw your blades into the charged pack and the storm deepens.

**Not built yet.** Blades do not yet turn in flight to the nearest charged enemy, and any spell striking a charged enemy lays the two, not only thrown blades.

## liveiron.seeksiron
**What it does.** You become a bolt and in a quarter of a second, untouchable, go to the farthest notched enemy within 10 metres, cutting every enemy on the line for 10, 15 stagger and two Static; arriving, 16 and two Static.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Metal and Lightning are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Metal.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, movement, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. A line 10 metres long: 10 damage, 15 stagger, a notch and two Static to each enemy on it.

**Works with.** Live Iron (each enemy cut carries both), Three Smiths, Iron Calls the Storm.

**Example.** Bolt through a line of soldiers and every one of them is live.

**Not built yet.** It goes toward the enemy you face, not the farthest notched one, and lands no arrival blow of its own; a perfect guard in the 2 seconds before the cast does not yet send it on to a second notched enemy.
