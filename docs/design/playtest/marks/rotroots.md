# Rot Roots: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Life and Poison share, named for its reaction,
Rot Roots (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Life and Poison"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Life and Poison
are both carried.

## rotroots.shape
**What it does.** Bad Ground unlocks two new shapes for Rot Roots. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Rot Roots uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Gathered In**: the roots drink poison instead of giving it, pulling the crowd's poison into the one enemy they grow from, ready for you to set off. **Summer Grass**: no roots. Instead, the fungus waits inside the enemy, and when it dies, a stalk grows from its head and bursts in a cloud of spores.

**When it happens.** Every time Rot Roots goes off while you carry both Life and Poison. Rot Roots goes off when poison lands on a Seeded enemy, or when a seed lands on a poisoned one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Rot Roots.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Gathered In: roots reach three enemies within 5 metres for 6 seconds, and each one is Seeded once. Each time poison bites one of them, one of its doses is drawn along the roots into the enemy they grow from, up to eight. If that enemy is already full, the dose stays where it is. Summer Grass: lasts 20 seconds. If the enemy dies, 1.5 seconds later a puff of spores 4 metres across gives three doses and a Seed to everything in it, and you heal 6. After Rot Roots goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Tasting, Deep Threads, The Host, The Stalks Rise (use up what Gathered In pulled together).

**Example.** A crowd: use Gathered In on the toughest one, then set it off. A pack you will cut down one at a time: use Summer Grass, so each death Seeds the rest.

**Not built yet.** Summer Grass heals you wherever you stand, not only inside the spores (at most once every 2 seconds).

## rotroots.tasting
**What it does.** While you stand on a root, each poison bite on an enemy joined by the roots heals you 1.

**When it happens.** Each poison bite, while you carry both Life and Poison.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. For now: 1 health for each bite on a Seeded enemy, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Rot Roots (every bite runs down the roots), Deep Threads, The Host.

**Example.** Spread roots through a pack and let the poison heal you.

**Not built yet.** For now, it heals you wherever you stand, and for any Seeded enemy bitten, not only one joined by roots.

## rotroots.deepthreads
**What it does.** Rot Roots' roots reach 7 metres and join one more enemy.

**When it happens.** Each time Rot Roots goes off.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor mark, 1 Shard. The roots reach 7 metres instead of 5, and join four enemies instead of three. With Gathered In, the doses still go along the roots to the enemy they grow from, however far.

**Works with.** Rot Roots and its shapes, Tasting.

**Example.** The roots find the archers at the back.

## rotroots.host
**What it does.** Whatever you grow feeds on poisoned enemies. When a poisoned enemy dies within 3 metres of something you grew, that thing stays 3 seconds longer and adds a dose of poison with every hit.

**When it happens.** Each poisoned enemy that dies, while you carry both Life and Poison.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. The thing you grew nearest the body, within 3 metres, stays 3 seconds longer, and from then on each of its hits also gives one dose of poison to everything it hits.

**Works with.** Rot Roots, the things Life grows (Briar Patch and the rest), The Stalks Rise.

**Example.** Fight next to your briars, and each poisoned death spreads its rot.

## rotroots.stalks
**What it does.** Every enemy within 9 metres that is both Seeded and Poisoned grows a dark stalk from its head. After 1.2 seconds, each stalk bursts, using up both: it deals 5 damage per dose and 8 per seed within 2.5 metres, and the spores give two doses to everything nearby.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Life and Poison. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Life.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 14 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. On each enemy that has both: 5 damage for each dose and 8 for each seed to everything within 2.5 metres, plus 15 stagger, and two doses on each.

**Works with.** Rot Roots and Gathered In (gather the doses first), Tasting, The Host.

**Example.** Spread the roots through the pack, let the poison gather, then raise the stalks.

**Not built yet.** For now, the seeds are used up, but the doses are only counted and stay on the enemy. A weapon blow on an enemy while its stalk stands doesn't double its spores yet.
