# Rot Roots: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Life and Poison share, named for its reaction,
Rot Roots (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Life and Poison"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Life and Poison
are both carried.

## rotroots.shape
**What it does.** Bad Ground: opens Rot Roots' two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Rot Roots wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Gathered In**: the roots drink instead of giving, drawing the crowd's poison into the one host for your detonation. **Summer Grass**: no roots; the fungus waits in the host, and when it dies a stalk grows from its head and bursts in spores.

**When it happens.** Every time Rot Roots fires (poison landing on a Seeded enemy, or a seed on a poisoned one) while Life and Poison are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Rot Roots runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Gathered In: roots to three enemies within 5 metres for 6 seconds, each Seeded once; each time poison bites one of them, one of its doses is taken and passed to the nearest other enemy within 5 metres. Summer Grass: for 20 seconds; if it dies, 1.5 seconds later a puff of spores 4 metres across lays three doses and a Seed on everything in it, and you heal 6. Rot Roots rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Tasting, Deep Threads, The Host, The Stalks Rise (spend what Gathered In drew together).

**Example.** A crowd: Gathered In on the toughest, then detonate it. A pack you will cut down one by one: Summer Grass, so each death seeds the rest.

**Not built yet.** Gathered In's doses go to the nearest other enemy, most often the host, rather than to the host itself, and are not held to eight. Summer Grass heals you wherever you stand, not only inside the spores (at most once in 2 seconds).

## rotroots.tasting
**What it does.** While you stand on a root, each poison bite on a rooted enemy heals you 1.

**When it happens.** Each poison bite, while Life and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. As built: 1 health for each bite on a Seeded enemy, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Rot Roots (every bite runs down the roots), Deep Threads, The Host.

**Example.** Root a pack and let the poison feed you.

**Not built yet.** It heals wherever you stand, and for any Seeded enemy bitten, not only one joined by roots.

## rotroots.deepthreads
**What it does.** Rot Roots' roots reach 7 metres and join one more enemy.

**When it happens.** Each time Rot Roots fires.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** Rot Roots and its shapes, Tasting.

**Example.** The roots find the archers at the back.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: a mark that changes the reaction's own numbers is still to come.

## rotroots.host
**What it does.** Whatever you grow feeds on the poisoned: a poisoned enemy that dies within 3 metres of something grown makes it stand 3 seconds longer and lay a dose of poison with every bite.

**When it happens.** Each poisoned enemy that dies, while Life and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: while something you grew stands within 4 metres of you, a poisoned enemy that dies doses everything within 3 metres of where it fell, at most once a second.

**Works with.** Rot Roots, Life's grown things (Briar Patch and the rest), The Stalks Rise.

**Example.** Fight beside your briars and each poisoned death spreads its rot.

**Not built yet.** The grown thing near the death does not yet stand longer or start dosing with its bites: the dose goes to those near where it fell, and only while something you grew stands near you.

## rotroots.stalks
**What it does.** Every enemy within 9 metres that is both Seeded and Poisoned grows a dark stalk from its head; after 1.2 seconds each stalk bursts, spending both: 5 a dose and 8 a seed in 2.5 metres, the spores laying two doses on what is near.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Life and Poison are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Life.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 14 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. On each enemy carrying both: 5 damage for each dose and 8 for each seed to everything within 2.5 metres, 15 stagger, and two doses on each.

**Works with.** Rot Roots and Gathered In (gather the doses first), Tasting, The Host.

**Example.** Root the pack, let the poison gather, then raise the stalks.

**Not built yet.** The seeds are spent but the doses are only counted and stay on the enemy; a weapon blow on an enemy while its stalk stands does not yet double its spores.
