# Dark: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Dark tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## dark.curse
**What it does.** Every blow of your weapon that lands curses the enemy. A Cursed enemy hits you more weakly, and killing it heals you.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every weapon blow that lands, light or heavy, while Dark is your first element. Weapon blows lay only the first element's affliction; carried second, Dark curses through Grasp, its other spells, the charged blade and its strike.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. Cursed is one deep and lasts 8 seconds; each blow sets its clock back. A Cursed enemy deals 15% less damage, and killing it heals you 6.

**Works with.** Long Curse (it lasts half again as long), Heavy Curse (they hit weaker still), Feast, Dread, Fat of the Kill, Devouring Maw, Hunger (every blow curses while it burns), and Wither on the Blight branch.

**Example.** Cut the brute first: while it is Cursed every swing of its takes 15% less of your health, and when it falls you get 6 back.

## dark.bloodprice
**What it does.** When a spell asks more mana than you hold, the rest is paid in health. That health is not lost at once: it turns grey, and dealing damage wins it back like any grey health.

**When it happens.** Each time you cast a spell paid in mana with too little mana for it: an element's larger spell or a duo spell. Basic spells run on cooldowns and cost no mana, and Dark's own spells cost none, so it matters for the spells of your other element and for duo spells.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Each point of mana you lack costs half a point of health, taken as grey health. It never takes your last point: if the health is not there, the spell is not cast.

**Works with.** Borrowed Strength (the grey health it leaves makes you hit harder), Slow Collector (grey health lingers longer), Small Cuts (health prices 20% smaller), Unpaid, and Wasting Grip on the Blight branch.

**Example.** At 10 mana, cast Wasting Grip for 40: it goes off, 15 health turns grey, and the blows that follow win it back.

## dark.borrowed
**What it does.** The more grey health you carry, the harder your weapon strikes.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, for as long as any grey health remains: from blocked blows, Blood Price, Pawned Heart or anything else that leaves health owed.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Each point of grey health adds 0.75% weapon damage, up to 30% at 40 points.

**Works with.** Blood Price, Slow Collector and Unpaid (the grey stays longer), Reckless Arm (swings cost 15% less stamina while any is owed), and Pawned Heart (its stopped heart leaves grey health behind).

**Example.** Block a brute's three-blow chain and carry 20 grey health: your answer strikes 15% harder, and each cut wins some of the grey back.

## dark.debtscalled
**What it does.** Everything near is dragged to a point just in front of you, and when it gets there a burst of shadow strikes it all at once as a heavy blow.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The point is 1 metre ahead of you. For half a second everything within 4.5 metres of it is drawn most of the way in; then everything within 4.5 metres takes 18 damage, 35 stagger and a Curse. As built the burst strikes but throws nothing back, and it does not yet grow as your health falls.

**Works with.** Curse, Dread, Feast, Easy Prey, Grasp, and the charged blade (it charges your weapon with Dark when it lands).

**Example.** Four soldiers spread round you: one cast draws them into a knot at your sword's point, all Cursed, ready for a wide swing.

## dark.pawnedheart
**What it does.** You tear out your heart and set it down behind you. Your health drops to a single point, and while the heart beats your weapon strikes far harder and swings cost no stamina. Walk away from the heart and then back onto it, and all the health you pawned comes back with a share of the damage you dealt meanwhile. If its ring closes first, the heart stops: you keep one point, and half of what you pawned comes back only as grey health.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It cannot be cast at one point of health.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: the price is health, and it waits 40 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The heart lies 1.8 metres behind you and beats for 10 seconds. While it beats: 60% more weapon damage and swings free of stamina. You must step 2.6 metres from it, then come within 1 metre of it again: back come all the health pawned and 30% of the damage you dealt. A blow that lands on you while it beats can kill you.

**Works with.** Borrowed Strength (the grey health a stopped heart leaves), Unpaid, Near the Grave (you are below a third the whole time), Hunger, and Small Cuts.

**Example.** Pawn the heart beside a staggered brute, cut it down in five free swings, then step back onto the heart and stand up with more health than you pawned.

## dark.unpaid
**What it does.** Grey health lingers twice as long before it fades, and every blow you land heals you a little. The price: flasks heal half as much.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The healing comes with every weapon blow that lands.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Grey health fades at half its usual speed, 5 a second where it is 10. Each blow that lands heals 2, at most twice a second. A flask heals 22.5 where it healed 45. As built nothing is yet left wholly grey: every wound still takes health as it always did.

**Works with.** Borrowed Strength (the grey stays and keeps you strong), Blood Price, Slow Collector, Pawned Heart, Hunger.

**Example.** Block a long chain and keep cutting: the grey health waits longer for you to win it back, and each cut adds 2 besides.

## dark.feast
**What it does.** A finisher on a Cursed enemy feeds you: it heals, and heals more while Hunger burns.

**When it happens.** Every finisher (a heavy blow on a staggered enemy) that lands on a Cursed enemy.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Heals 12, and 6 more while Hunger lasts: 18 in all.

**Works with.** Curse, Grasp and Debts Called (to curse first), Hunger, Long Hunger, Never Sated (20 more on any finisher), and The Reaping on the Blight branch.

**Example.** Stagger a Cursed brute with Hunger lit and finish it: 18 health back, and 6 more when it dies.

## dark.glut
**What it does.** Drinking a flask wraps you in a shade that turns aside one blow. So does a kill, though not more than once in ten seconds.

**When it happens.** Every flask you drink, and any kill at most once every ten seconds.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One ward a flask or a kill, three at most. A kill gives one at most once in 10 seconds. Each ward takes a blow you did not guard, a red attack among them. As built it gives wards; health over a full bar is not yet kept.

**Works with.** Near the Grave (kills give wards below a third), Dread, Fat of the Kill (a Cursed kill heals 3 more). Never Sated takes your flasks, so only kills feed it then.

**Example.** Drink between two packs and the next soldier's thrust breaks on the shade instead of on you.

## dark.dread
**What it does.** When a Cursed enemy dies, every enemy near where it fell loses heart, and its balance buckles.

**When it happens.** Each time a Cursed enemy dies, whatever killed it; at most once a second.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 30 stagger to every enemy within 8 metres of where it fell. No damage.

**Works with.** Curse, Debts Called (curse a knot of them), Easy Prey, Feast, Carrion Flies and One Death Feeds on the Blight branch, Unsteady (more stagger from each).

**Example.** Kill a Cursed soldier at the front of a pack and the four behind it are 30 nearer a stagger before they reach you.

## dark.maw
**What it does.** A great jaw of shadow rises at your shoulder and lunges down a lane before you, striking and carrying what it passes, then closes at the end of its reach. Each Cursed enemy it bites feeds you.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 11 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It forms for 0.3 seconds and runs 5 metres along a lane 3.2 metres wide. What it passes takes 15 damage, 15 stagger and a Curse, and is shoved along with it. The jaws close 5 metres ahead: everything within 2.4 metres takes 30 damage and 30 stagger as a heavy blow, and a Curse. Each time it strikes an enemy that is Cursed you heal 5.

**Works with.** Curse and Grasp (curse them first, so every strike feeds), Long Shadows (15% harder), Feast, Dread, Hunger, and the charged blade.

**Example.** A row of Cursed soldiers in a corridor: the jaw drags them to its end and closes on all of them, and you heal for each.

## dark.neversated
**What it does.** You carry no flasks at all. Instead every blow you land feeds you a little, every Cursed kill a great deal, and every finisher more.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: with every weapon blow that lands, every Cursed enemy killed and every finisher.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. No flasks, and none are earned back. Each blow heals 6% of its damage. A Cursed kill heals 12, on top of Cursed's own 6. A finisher heals 20.

**Works with.** Curse, Feast (a Cursed finisher heals 32, 38 in Hunger), Hunger (its 30% and this 6% together), Fat of the Kill, Grave Appetite.

**Example.** Against a long pack, never stop: each kill and finisher keeps you standing where a flask would have.

## dark.nearthegrave
**What it does.** Below a third of your health you strike harder, and each kill wraps you in a shade that takes the next blow.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while your health is below a third. The shade comes with each kill made down there.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Below a third: 20% more weapon damage. Each kill below a third gives one ward, three at most.

**Works with.** Hard to Bury (12% less damage taken down there), Pawned Heart (it puts you there), Hollow Strength, Glut, A Life for a Life.

**Example.** At 25 health in a pack: cut harder, and each soldier that falls leaves you a shade against the next one's blow.

## dark.hollowstrength
**What it does.** While you are Risen, you strike harder and no blow makes you flinch. Each kill stills the draining of your health for a few breaths.

**When it happens.** While Risen: after A Life for a Life stands you up, or for good with Dead Already.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Risen: 25% more weapon damage, and blows that land do not stop you. Each kill stops the drain for 3 seconds.

**Works with.** A Life for a Life, Dead Already, Slow Rot (the drain runs at 70%), Grave Appetite (each blow heals 1 more while Risen), Near the Grave.

**Example.** Risen with a sliver of health: swing straight through the soldiers' blows, and each one you kill gives you three seconds without the drain.

## dark.secondshadow
**What it does.** Your shadow stands at your shoulder for a while. Every weapon blow you land, it lands again a beat later, for half the damage, and curses.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then every weapon blow that lands is echoed.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It stands for 8 seconds. Each echo lands 0.2 seconds after your blow, for 50% of its damage with no stagger, and lays a Curse. One shadow at a time.

**Works with.** Curse, Pawned Heart (half of a much bigger blow), Borrowed Strength, Near the Grave, Hollow Strength, Hunger.

**Example.** Cast it as a brute kneels staggered: every cut of the finisher's opening lands twice.

## dark.deadstand
**What it does.** Three shades rise beside you. Each runs at an enemy, a different one while there are enough, and jabs it for a while before going out. With no enemy near they keep at your side.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Three shades for 5 seconds. Each finds an enemy within 16 metres; in reach, it jabs about half a second later and then every 1.6 seconds, for 12 damage, 12 stagger and a Curse each time. As built they rise beside you, not from the dead.

**Works with.** Curse, Dread and Feast (the shades keep the pack Cursed), Long Shadows (15% harder), and the charged blade.

**Example.** Call them on three archers at the back while you hold the front: each archer is Cursed and pestered until you get there.

## dark.deadalready
**What it does.** You do not wait to die: you are Risen from now on. In every fight your health drains toward its last point, and your blows feed you. You strike far harder. There is no second rising.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The drain runs while an enemy is within 12 metres of you.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. 25% more weapon damage, and 30% of each blow's damage comes back as health. The drain takes 2 health a second, never the last point. Every mark that asks for you to be Risen is in force at all times. A Life for a Life can no longer stand you up.

**Works with.** Hollow Strength (another 25%, no flinch, and kills still the drain), Slow Rot (drain 70%), Grave Appetite, Hunger (another 30% back), Never Sated.

**Example.** Fight without pause: every cut gives back far more than the drain takes, and the moment you stop to wait, it bleeds you down.

## dark.lifeforalife
**What it does.** Once, the blow that would kill you does not. You stand again with half your health, and it drains away. A sigil hangs over the enemy that killed you: land a finisher on it and you live again, health returns, and you may rise once more.

**When it happens.** The moment a blow would kill you, if you have not risen yet. The finisher on the killer, or on anyone if the killer is already gone, ends it.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. You rise at half your full health. It drains 4 a second, never the last point. The finisher gives back half your full health and readies the rising again. Without it you stay Risen, draining to one point, and a blow that kills you then is a fall; after a fall you may rise again. Dead Already turns it off.

**Works with.** Hollow Strength, Slow Rot, Grave Appetite, Near the Grave, Feast.

**Example.** A brute's red overhead kills you: rise, break its balance, finish it, and stand with half your health and your rising ready again.
