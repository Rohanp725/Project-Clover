# Earthlight: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Earth and Light share, named for its reaction,
Earthlight (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Earth and Light"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Earth and Light
are both carried.

## earthlight.shape
**What it does.** Lit Stone unlocks two new shapes for Earthlight. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Earthlight uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Down the Passage**: the light keeps going through the ground in a straight line from the enemy, away from you. **Lit Seam**: the cracks of light stay lit, and each heavy blow on the enemy flashes them again.

**When it happens.** Every time Earthlight goes off while you carry both Earth and Light. Earthlight goes off when a Mark lands on a shaken enemy, or when Tremor lands on a Marked one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Earthlight.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both shapes put a fresh Mark on the enemy. Down the Passage: half a second later, a line 8 metres long and 1 metre wide past the enemy deals 12 damage and a Mark to everything on it. Lit Seam: the light breaks out along a star of six seams like the plain one (14 damage, 25 stagger and a Mark to everyone standing on a seam, each seam 3 metres long). Then for 5 seconds, each heavy blow on the enemy flashes a star of seams around it for 10 damage and 15 stagger on everything standing on them. After Earthlight goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Stone Rise and Glare to set it up, Keeping the Hour, Quartz Vein, Dawn Through the Door.

**Example.** A line of archers stands behind a shaken soldier. Mark the soldier from the front, and Down the Passage lights up the whole line.

**Not built yet.** The cracks aren't drawn as a star on the floor yet. Lit Seam's flash runs along a star around where the enemy stands now, not along the first seams if it has moved off them.

## earthlight.hour
**What it does.** Every 6 seconds during a fight, your next heavy blow is right on the hour: it is a critical hit and shakes the enemy twice. A small dial at your feet fills up to show when the hour comes.

**When it happens.** During a fight, the dial fills over 6 seconds. Once it is full, it waits for your next heavy blow or finisher that lands. Then it empties and starts again. Out of a fight it stays empty.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The heavy blow on the hour is a critical hit and adds two stacks of Tremor.

**Works with.** Earthlight (gives the Tremor to start it), Quartz Vein, The Stone Keeps the Hour.

**Example.** Keep your heavy blows six seconds apart, and each one shakes and lights up a Marked soldier.

## earthlight.dawn
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a Marked enemy blinds the attacker. It gets Marked again, and its Tremor is filled all the way.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy that has a Mark.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A Mark, and Tremor filled all the way.

**Works with.** Earthlight (both afflictions at once start it), Glare, Mountain Stance.

**Example.** A Marked brute swings. Guard it perfectly, and Earthlight goes off under it.

**Not built yet.** It's meant to work when you make a perfect guard while standing on the glowing cracks. For now, it works against any Marked attacker.

## earthlight.quartz
**What it does.** Earthlight's star of light is 0.5 metres wider.

**When it happens.** Each time Earthlight goes off, plain or with a shape.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor mark, 1 Shard. The star reaches 3.5 metres instead of 3, and so does Lit Seam's. With Down the Passage, the line is half a metre wider.

**Works with.** Keeping the Hour, which it comes after on the chart, Earthlight.

**Example.** The soldier standing just outside the light gets Marked too.

## earthlight.stone
**What it does.** A standing stone rises in front of you, and a line of sunlight sweeps around it like the shadow on a sundial. Every enemy it crosses is hit, shaken and Marked. It never hurts you.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Earth and Light. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 16 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. The stone stands 3 metres ahead of you for 9 seconds. Once a second, everything within 6 metres takes 10 damage, 15 stagger, a Tremor and a Mark.

**Works with.** Earthlight (every enemy it hits gets both afflictions), Keeping the Hour, Hammer and Anvil.

**Example.** Plant it in a doorway and fight around it.

**Not built yet.** The light doesn't sweep around the stone as a line yet. The stone doesn't block the way, and it doesn't count as stone behind an enemy. A blow timed to the line isn't made a critical hit yet.
