# Earthlight: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Earth and Light share, named for its reaction,
Earthlight (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Earth and Light"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Earth and Light
are both carried.

## earthlight.shape
**What it does.** Lit Stone: opens Earthlight's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Earthlight wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Down the Passage**: the light runs on through the ground in a straight line from the enemy, away from you. **Lit Seam**: the seams stay lit, and each heavy blow on the enemy flashes them again.

**When it happens.** Every time Earthlight fires (Marked landing on a shaken enemy, or Tremor on a marked one) while Earth and Light are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Earthlight runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both renew its Mark. Down the Passage: a line 8 metres long and 1 metre wide beyond it, half a second on: 12 damage and a Mark on all on it. Lit Seam: the light breaks up as before (14 damage, 25 stagger and a Mark within 3 metres); for 5 seconds each heavy blow on it flashes 10 damage and 15 stagger on all within 3 metres. Earthlight rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Stone Rise and Glare to bring the two, Keeping the Hour, Quartz Vein, Dawn Through the Door.

**Example.** A line of archers behind a shaken soldier: Mark it from in front and Down the Passage lights the whole line.

**Not built yet.** The seams are not drawn as a star of cracks: the light strikes all within the ring, and Lit Seam flashes round the enemy struck, not only on those standing on the seams.

## earthlight.hour
**What it does.** Every 6 seconds in a fight, the next heavy blow is on the hour: it strikes harder and shakes the enemy twice.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow or finisher that lands, at most once every 6 seconds.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Two stacks of Tremor and 10 more damage.

**Works with.** Earthlight (the Tremor to start it), Quartz Vein, The Stone Keeps the Hour.

**Example.** Keep your heavy blows six seconds apart and each one shakes and lights a marked soldier.

**Not built yet.** No dial at your feet shows the hour yet, and the blow strikes 10 harder rather than being made critical.

## earthlight.dawn
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a marked enemy blinds the attacker: it is Marked again and shaken to its full depth.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy carrying a Mark.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A Mark, and Tremor to full depth.

**Works with.** Earthlight (both at once start it), Glare, Mountain Stance.

**Example.** A marked brute swings: guard it perfectly and Earthlight goes off under it.

**Not built yet.** It is meant for a perfect guard made on the glowing seams; as built it answers any marked attacker.

## earthlight.quartz
**What it does.** Earthlight's star is 0.5 metres wider.

**When it happens.** Each time Earthlight fires, plain or shaped.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. As built: a Mark on everything within 3.5 metres as it fires.

**Works with.** Keeping the Hour, which it grows from, Earthlight.

**Example.** The soldier just outside the light is marked as well.

**Not built yet.** The star itself does not grow; a ring of Marks half a metre wider stands in.

## earthlight.stone
**What it does.** A standing stone rises ahead of you, and a line of sunlight wheels round it like a dial's shadow: every enemy it crosses is struck, shaken and Marked. It never harms you.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Earth and Light are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 16 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The stone stands 3 metres ahead for 9 seconds; once a second everything within 6 metres takes 10 damage, 15 stagger, a Tremor and a Mark.

**Works with.** Earthlight (every enemy it strikes carries both), Keeping the Hour, Hammer and Anvil.

**Example.** Plant it in a doorway and fight round it.

**Not built yet.** The light does not yet sweep round the stone as a line, the stone does not block the way or count as stone at an enemy's back, and a blow timed to the line is not made critical.
