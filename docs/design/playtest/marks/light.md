# Light: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Light tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## light.mark
**What it does.** The last blow of a chain, and every perfect guard, leave a mark of light on the enemy: it is Marked. Your next three weapon blows on a Marked enemy are critical and each gives more mana; then the mark is spent.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The last light blow of a chain, or the heavy blow that ends one, marks the enemy it lands on; a perfect guard marks the one who struck. It marks whether Light is your first element or your second, since it is the mark's own doing and not the weapon's.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. A mark lasts 8 seconds or three weapon blows, whichever ends first. Each of those blows is critical, one and a half times its damage, and gives 2 more mana. Marking again refills the three blows.

**Works with.** Glare, which marks what its ray strikes; Clarity, which marks with every blow; Lasting Mark (a fourth blow), Sharp Light (critical blows 15% harder), Plain Guilt, Rich Mark, Weighed and Sentence, and the charged blade, whose third Light blow throws a ray that marks.

**Example.** Three light blows on a soldier mark it with the third; a heavy blow, then two more light blows, all land critical.

## light.justcut
**What it does.** A blow that lands may flash. For a moment after a flash every blow you land is critical. A blow that lands may also give you a little stamina back.

**When it happens.** Any weapon blow that lands, one time in four, at most once every 1.5 seconds. The flash and the stamina are each their own roll. As built the flash comes by chance; a blow pressed at the exact moment the last one lands does not yet make it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One chance in four of a flash: every blow critical for 0.8 seconds. One chance in four of 6 stamina back. Each can come at most once every 1.5 seconds.

**Works with.** Sharp Light (critical blows strike 15% harder), Bowed Head (critical blows break balance 20% sooner), Weighed, and quick weapons such as the Twin Knives, which fit more blows into the moment.

**Example.** In a flurry on a duellist, one cut flashes and the two after it land critical without a mark.

## light.dazzle
**What it does.** A perfect dodge flashes in the attacker's eyes. It, and every enemy standing close to it, is Marked and held still for a moment.

**When it happens.** A perfect dodge: a blow passes through the very start of your roll.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Everything within 2 metres of the attacker is Marked and held still for 1 second (shorter on elites and gods, and not at all on what cannot be held).

**Works with.** Narrow Escape (the perfect moment 23% longer), Bright Evasion (perfect dodges give 42% more mana), Clarity (dodges safe half again as long), Step Into Light, and the root's three critical blows.

**Example.** Roll through a brute's swing at the last moment: it stands frozen and Marked while you turn and land three critical blows.

## light.step
**What it does.** You step through the enemy you face in a line of light and come out on its far side, turned back toward it, cutting and Marking everything on the line. If the cut lands, your next heavy blow in the moment after is critical. Nothing can strike you in the step; a wall, or something too big to shove, stops you short.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). With no enemy faced you step ahead anyway, and the slot still waits.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 8 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 7 metres: to 1.8 metres past the enemy faced, or about 5 metres ahead with none. The cut is 10 damage, 10 stagger and Marked on all on the line. The heavy blow after it is critical if it lands within 1.5 seconds.

**Works with.** The root (the mark it lays gives the heavy blow and two more their critical), Sharp Light, Long Rays (the step reaches 25% further), Dazzle, and the charged blade: the cut is a spell, so it charges your weapon with Light for the next three moves.

**Example.** Step through an archer behind its shield-bearer, turn, and land a critical heavy blow in its back.

## light.unblemished
**What it does.** Fight a while without a wound and every weapon blow you land is critical, and gives more mana. A blow that lands on you ends it, and until you have gone a while unwounded again you take more from every blow.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. You are unwounded once no blow has landed on you for 8 seconds; the gift lasts until the next one does.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Unwounded: every weapon blow critical, and 25% more mana from blows. After a wound, for 8 seconds or longer, you take 15% more damage. A blocked blow, a perfect guard or a perfect dodge is not a wound.

**Works with.** Sharp Light, Weighed (critical blows 30% harder), Bowed Head, Clarity's longer perfect moments, Measured Guard and Narrow Escape: every blow you turn keeps you clean.

**Example.** Guard and dodge a duellist's whole chain without a scratch, and every answer you give it lands critical.

## light.weighed
**What it does.** Marks deepen, up to three. When an affliction on an enemy reaches its full depth, a mark at three among them, your critical blows strike harder for a while.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The deeper marks need Light to be your first element; with Light second, a mark stays one deep. The harder critical comes each time any affliction you lay fills on an enemy.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Marked up to three deep, each mark laid adding one. A full affliction makes critical blows deal 1.8 times their damage, where they deal 1.5, for 6 seconds.

**Works with.** The root, Sentence (which needs three marks), Plain Guilt, Lasting Mark, Brimming and Two Suns (more marks laid), Unblemished and Just Cut (more critical blows to spend it on).

**Example.** End three chains on a brute: it is three deep, and for six seconds every critical blow hits for 1.8 times.

## light.sentence
**What it does.** A heavy blow on an enemy marked three deep passes sentence. Its marks are spent at once, and a moment later a blade of light falls on it, striking everything where it lands.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy carrying Marked at full depth: three, with Weighed and Light as your first element; with Light second, any mark will do.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The blade falls 0.8 seconds later, warned by a ring, 1.8 metres in radius: 36 damage and 45 stagger. At most once a second.

**Works with.** Weighed, the root, Ring of Spears and Brimming to lay the marks fast, Bowed Head (the heavy blow itself is critical), and finishers: a finisher that passes sentence lands the blade on a kneeling enemy.

**Example.** Mark a captain three deep, land a heavy blow, and step back as the blade falls on it and the soldier at its side.

## light.spears
**What it does.** Six spears of light stand in the air round the enemy you face, wait a breath, and drive in together, Marking it.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). With no enemy faced they close on a spot ahead of you, and the slot still waits.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It finds an enemy within 10 metres. The ring is 3 metres in radius and closes after 0.6 seconds. Each spear deals 6 damage and 5 stagger and Marks: 36 damage and 30 stagger in all. As built the spears do not hold the enemy still while they wait.

**Works with.** Sentence and Weighed (each spear lays a mark), Returning Light and Two Suns (six spell hits), Heavy Verdict, Hard Light, and the charged blade, which it charges with Light.

**Example.** Cast on a brute, land your three critical blows while the spears are still closing, then a heavy blow for Sentence.

## light.whatitowes
**What it does.** An enemy that wounds you is Marked.

**When it happens.** Each time a blow lands on you; the mark goes on the enemy that struck.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One mark on the one who struck.

**Works with.** The root (the next three blows you return are critical), Weighed and Sentence, Reckoning, and Unblemished's price: a wound marks your answer.

**Example.** A soldier's spear catches you: it is Marked, and the three blows you give back are all critical.

## light.reckoning
**What it does.** You set a seal on the enemy you face. Each of your next six weapon blows on it sets off a burst of light round it that strikes and Marks everything near. With no enemy faced nothing is sealed and the slot still waits.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then land weapon blows on that enemy. As built it is a burst with each blow; the seal that counts every wound and pays it back at once is still to come.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 10 metres; the seal lasts 6 seconds or six bursts. Each burst is 2 metres in radius: 12 damage, 7 stagger and Marked.

**Works with.** The root and Lasting Mark (each burst marks again after the blow that spent it), Weighed and Sentence, Heavy Verdict and Hard Light (the bursts are spells), Returning Light, Two Suns, Condemned.

**Example.** Seal a captain in its guard and cut it six times: every burst marks it and the soldiers beside it, and every cut after the first lands critical.

## light.condemned
**What it does.** When an affliction on an enemy reaches its full depth, its balance takes a great blow. The price: your weapon deals less to an enemy that carries nothing, though blows and spells on any afflicted enemy strike a little harder.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The blow to balance comes each time any affliction you lay fills on an enemy, a third mark among them. As built it breaks balance; finishing a marked enemy below a quarter of its health as if it were staggered is still to come.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. A burst 1.2 metres round the enemy: no damage, 60 stagger, at most once every 4 seconds. Weapon damage 15% less; damage on an afflicted enemy 15% more, so against those the two nearly cancel.

**Works with.** Weighed, Sentence, Reckoning and Ring of Spears to fill marks, and a second element's affliction, which fills on its own.

**Example.** Bring a brute's mark to three: the burst all but staggers it, and a heavy blow finishes the work.

## light.brimming
**What it does.** While your mana is full, every heavy blow throws a wave of light ahead of it that strikes and Marks everything in front of you.

**When it happens.** Each heavy blow or finisher that lands while your mana bar is full.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A cone 5 metres long and 70 degrees wide: 12 damage, 12 stagger and Marked.

**Works with.** Gleaning (blows give 10% more mana), Rich Mark, Struck Flint and perfect guards to fill the bar, Weighed and Sentence, and the root. Spending mana on Daybreak or the surge ends it until the bar is full again.

**Example.** Full of mana before a pack: every heavy blow marks the three in front of you.

## light.returninglight
**What it does.** A spell that strikes a Marked enemy gives you mana back.

**When it happens.** Each time a spell from your slots strikes an enemy carrying Marked.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 9 mana, at most once a second.

**Works with.** Glare and Ring of Spears (cast on a marked enemy), Standing Light (its glow marks, then strikes again), Reckoning, Two Suns. Basic spells cost no mana now, so the mana goes to Daybreak, Clarity or a deep strike.

**Example.** Mark a soldier with a chain, then Glare it: 9 mana back toward your Daybreak.

## light.lantern
**What it does.** You plant a lantern of light a little ahead of you. Enemies in its glow are struck lightly and Marked again and again while it stands.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Planted 2.5 metres ahead; the glow is 5 metres in radius and lasts 10 seconds. It strikes 0.2 seconds after planting and then every 2.5 seconds: 2 damage and Marked each time. As built a perfect guard inside the glow does nothing more.

**Works with.** The root (a fresh mark, and three fresh critical blows, every 2.5 seconds), Returning Light and Two Suns (each strike is a spell hit), Weighed and Sentence, Condemned.

**Example.** Plant it in a doorway and fight on its edge: everything that comes through arrives Marked, and is Marked again as you spend it.

## light.daybreak
**What it does.** You brace and loose a beam of light down one long line. It bites everything in it four times and Marks it. The stick, or the cursor, turns it slowly while it burns.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). As built the beam is loosed at once; gathering it blow by blow before you loose it is still to come.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 45 mana. A 0.3-second brace, then a beam 12 metres long and 1.6 metres wide for 0.4 seconds. It bites every 0.1 seconds: 12 damage, 9 stagger and Marked each time, 48 damage on what stays in it.

**Works with.** Long Rays (25% further), Hard Light and Heavy Verdict, Returning Light, Two Suns, Weighed and Sentence, and the charged blade, which it charges with Light.

**Example.** A line of spearmen coming up a stair: one beam down the stair marks them all and leaves them reeling.

## light.twosuns
**What it does.** Each enemy your spells strike is struck again by a burst of light a moment later, and Marked. The price: spells paid in mana cost a quarter more.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: each time a spell from your slots strikes an enemy. As built it is a burst on each enemy struck; the second casting of your spell from the far side is still to come.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. A burst 2.5 metres in radius on the enemy struck, 0.4 seconds later: 12 damage, 10 stagger and Marked; at most five times a second. Daybreak costs 56 mana where it cost 45. Spells on a cooldown still cost nothing.

**Works with.** Ring of Spears, Reckoning, Standing Light, Daybreak and Glare, Weighed and Sentence, Condemned.

**Example.** Glare a pack: the ray strikes three, and a burst follows on each, marking the ones beside them too.

## light.scales
**What it does.** While your surge lasts, the wounds you deal with your weapon and the wounds you take are weighed. If yours are the heavier when it ends, a sword of light falls on every Marked enemy near you. If not, the light leaves you, and your mana with it.

**When it happens.** Light your surge with the surge button (Clarity, or the other element's surge if you wear that); the scales are read when it ends.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Each sword deals 60% of how much more you dealt than you took, 120 at most, with 80 stagger, as a heavy blow, to each Marked enemy within 12 metres. Taken more than you dealt, or as much: your mana empties.

**Works with.** Clarity (it marks with every blow and makes the perfect moments half again as long, so you take less), Unblemished, the root, Standing Light and Brimming to have them Marked when it ends.

**Example.** Light Clarity, guard and cut for eight seconds without being touched: 200 dealt to none taken, and a sword falls for 120 on every Marked enemy.
