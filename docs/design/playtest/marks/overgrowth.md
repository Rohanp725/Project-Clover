# Overgrowth: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Overgrowth, the branch Life and Earth share.

## overgrowth.rootgrip
**What it does.** A heavy blow on an enemy that carries both Tremor and Seeded sends roots up its legs and holds it where it stands. Your heavy blows also lay Tremor and Seeded together, whichever of the two you carry first.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands. The hold comes when the enemy already carries both, at most once every 3 seconds.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A heavy blow lays one stack of Tremor and one of Seeded. Your first element's own is not laid twice: once its root is learned, the root's stack is the one a heavy blow lays. Held still for 1.2 seconds (elites and gods for less; a few great forms cannot be held).

**Works with.** Seeded or Tremor, Briar Patch or Stone Rise for more of either, the charged blade, Living Rampart (its perfect guard lays Tremor), Ripening, The Ground Wakes.

**Example.** Life first: a heavy blow seeds and shakes a brute, and the next one roots it for a light chain that mends you with every cut.

## overgrowth.moss
**What it does.** Stand your ground and the ground feeds you. While you fight without rolling, you mend slowly, and thorns grow on you that cut and seed whoever strikes you.

**When it happens.** While you have not begun a dodge for 3 seconds. The mending comes every 3 seconds in a fight (an enemy within 12 metres); the thorns with each blow that lands on you, at most once a second.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 2 health every 3 seconds. The thorns: 8 damage and one stack of Seeded, 1 metre round the attacker.

**Works with.** Root Grip, Living Rampart (guard instead of rolling), Briar Coat, Seeded, Shade.

**Example.** Hold a doorway with your guard and never roll: the soldiers that get a blow through are seeded for your answer.

## overgrowth.briarpatch
**What it does.** The thorns of a Seeded enemy's death take root where they fall. The patch shakes the ground and seeds whatever walks in it. Not to be confused with Life's own spell of the same name.

**When it happens.** Whenever an enemy carrying Seeded dies, at most once a second.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A patch 3 metres in radius for 6 seconds. Every second it bites for 3 damage and one stack of Seeded; every 2 seconds it lays a stack of Tremor, which slows by 10% a stack and stops balance draining.

**Works with.** Seeded (its death burst comes first), Fallen Seeds (a thornbush in the same place), Root Grip (what walks through carries both), Living Rampart.

**Example.** Kill a seeded soldier in the middle of the pack: the rest wade through briar, slowed and seeded, and their balance stops draining.

## overgrowth.thornwall
**What it does.** You raise a hedge of stone and briar before you. Nothing walks through it, and no blow from its far side reaches you. When its time is up, or sooner if you strike it with a heavy blow, it bursts into thorns.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Swing a heavy blow close to it to burst it early.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The hedge stands 2.5 metres ahead, 5 metres wide, rises 0.2 seconds after the cast and stands 8 seconds. The burst reaches 5 metres from its middle: 20 damage, 25 stagger and one stack of Seeded; burst by your heavy blow it is a heavy blow. One hedge at a time. As built, touching the hedge does not cut.

**Works with.** Seeded, Ripening, Root Grip, Briar Patch, and the charged blade: its burst charges your weapon with Life.

**Example.** Wall off the archers while you deal with the spearman, then burst it with a heavy blow as they come round.

## overgrowth.stoneoak
**What it does.** You plant a stone oak a little ahead of you. For twenty seconds its roots strike everything in a wide circle round it, again and again, seeding what they strike.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards, 45 mana. Planted 2 metres ahead; the circle is 5 metres in radius. It first strikes half a second after planting, then every 2 seconds, for 20 seconds: 10 damage, 15 stagger and one stack of Seeded each time. As built it strikes as a grown tree from the start; it does not grow with blows, mend in its shade or block the way, and it is not one of your grown things for Shade or Reap What Is Sown.

**Works with.** Seeded, Ripening, Root Grip, Paid in Blood, and the charged blade: its first strike charges your weapon with Life.

**Example.** Plant it in the middle of a room and fight inside its circle: everything there is seeded every two seconds, and every cut mends you.

## overgrowth.livingrampart
**What it does.** Your guard is alive. Every blow held on it mends you a little and seeds the one who struck. A perfect guard roots the attacker where it stands and shakes the ground under it. The price: guarding costs more stamina.

**When it happens.** Each blow you block with a plain guard, and each perfect guard.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. A blocked blow: 2 health and one stack of Seeded on the attacker. A perfect guard: held still for 1 second (elites and gods for less) and two stacks of Tremor (one, with Earth first and its root learned). Blocking costs 15% more stamina.

**Works with.** Moss on Stone, Root Grip (the perfect guard lays the Tremor it needs), Bitter Rind, Briar Coat, Green Wood.

**Example.** Block a soldier's three-blow chain: it is seeded, you are 6 health better off, and your answer mends you more.

## overgrowth.groundwakes
**What it does.** When your surge begins, the ground rises round you in a ring of stone and root and holds every enemy inside it, shaken and seeded. Then it flowers: a burst, and you are mended for each one it held.

**When it happens.** Each time you light the surge with the surge button, whichever element's surge you wear.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Everything within 7 metres of you is held for 2 seconds (elites and gods for less) and takes three stacks of Tremor and one of Seeded. When the hold ends, everything within 7 metres takes 15 damage and 20 stagger, and you mend 5 for each enemy held.

**Works with.** Regrowth or Bedrock, Root Grip, Seeded, Ripening, Living Rampart.

**Example.** Light Regrowth with four soldiers round you: all four stand held and shaken for two seconds, then you mend 20.
