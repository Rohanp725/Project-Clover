# Overgrowth: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Overgrowth, the duo branch Life and Earth share. Reshaped in
Phase 4 (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Life and Earth") and drawn in the heart of the chart while Life and Earth
are both carried. Its reaction is Split Rock (which does what Root Grip did); its Shape mark lets you choose what Split
Rock does.

## overgrowth.shape
**What it does.** Unlocks two new shapes for Split Rock. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Split Rock uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Taking Root**: a short hold, and the floor cracks around the enemy right away, shaking everything nearby. **Root Web**: the roots keep spreading underground and catch every enemy nearby for a moment.

**When it happens.** Every time Split Rock goes off while you carry both Life and Earth. Split Rock goes off when Tremor lands on a seeded enemy, or when Seeded lands on a shaken one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Split Rock. The plain one holds the enemy for 2 seconds, and a heavy blow while it is held splits the floor.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Taking Root: the enemy is held for 1 second, and every other enemy within 2.5 metres gets one Tremor. Root Web: every Seeded enemy within 6 metres is held for 0.5 seconds and gets one Tremor. After Split Rock goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Briar Patch and Stone Rise to add the seed and the shaking, Living Rampart, Thorns Take Root, Stone Oak.

**Example.** A pack closing in: Root Web holds them all for half a second while you roll away. One brute: use the plain Split Rock, and land a heavy blow while it is held.

## overgrowth.moss
**What it does.** Hold your ground, and the ground feeds you. While you fight without rolling, you heal slowly, and thorns grow on you that cut and Seed anyone who hits you.

**When it happens.** While you haven't started a dodge for 3 seconds. The healing comes every 3 seconds during a fight (when an enemy is within 12 metres). The thorns come with each blow that lands on you, at most once a second.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 2 health every 3 seconds. The thorns deal 8 damage and one stack of Seeded within 1 metre of the attacker.

**Works with.** Split Rock's shapes, Living Rampart (guard instead of rolling), Seeded.

**Example.** Hold a doorway with your guard and never roll. Any soldier that gets a blow through is Seeded, ready for your answer.

## overgrowth.briarpatch
**What it does.** The thorns from a Seeded enemy's death take root where they fall. The patch shakes the ground and Seeds whatever walks in it. It used to be called Briar Patch, until Life's own spell took that name.

**When it happens.** Whenever an enemy with Seeded dies, at most once a second.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A patch with a 3-metre radius that lasts 6 seconds. Every second, it deals 3 damage and one stack of Seeded. Every 2 seconds, it adds a stack of Tremor, which slows by 10% per stack and stops the stagger meter from going down.

**Works with.** Seeded (its death burst comes first), Fallen Seeds (a thornbush in the same spot), Split Rock (enemies that walk through get both afflictions), Living Rampart.

**Example.** Kill a seeded soldier in the middle of the pack. The rest have to wade through briars, slowed and Seeded, and their stagger stops going down.

## overgrowth.stoneoak
**What it does.** You plant a stone oak a little in front of you. For twenty seconds, its roots hit everything in a wide circle around it, again and again, Seeding what they hit.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards, 45 mana. It is planted 2 metres in front of you; the circle has a 5-metre radius. It first hits half a second after planting, then every 2 seconds for 20 seconds, for 10 damage, 15 stagger and one stack of Seeded each time. For now, it hits like a fully grown tree from the start. It doesn't grow with blows, heal you in its shade, or block the way, and it doesn't count as something you grew for Shade or Reap What Is Sown.

**Works with.** Seeded, Moss on Stone or Thorns Take Root (it comes after either on the chart), Split Rock, and the charged blade: its first hit charges your weapon with Life.

**Example.** Plant it in the middle of a room and fight inside its circle. Everything there is Seeded every two seconds, and every cut heals you.

## overgrowth.livingrampart
**What it does.** Your guard is alive. Every blow you block heals you a little and Seeds the enemy that hit you. A perfect guard roots the attacker in place and shakes the ground under it. The cost: guarding costs more stamina.

**When it happens.** Each blow you block with a normal guard, and each perfect guard.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. A blocked blow: you heal 2, and the attacker gets one stack of Seeded. A perfect guard: the attacker is held still for 1 second (less for elites and gods) and gets two stacks of Tremor (one, if Earth is your first element and you've learned its root mark). Blocking costs 15% more stamina.

**Works with.** Moss on Stone or Thorns Take Root (it comes after either on the chart), Split Rock (the perfect guard adds the Tremor it needs), Seeded.

**Example.** Block a soldier's string of three blows. It is Seeded, you have 6 more health, and your answer heals you even more.

## overgrowth.groundwakes
**What it does.** When your surge starts, the ground rises around you in a ring of stone and roots and holds every enemy inside it, shaken and Seeded. Then it flowers: a burst goes off, and you heal for each enemy it held.

**When it happens.** Each time you light the surge with the surge button, whichever element's surge you have.

**The numbers.** 5 Shards. You can buy it once you carry both elements; no god gives it. Everything within 7 metres of you is held for 2 seconds (less for elites and gods) and gets three stacks of Tremor and one of Seeded. When the hold ends, everything within 7 metres takes 15 damage and 20 stagger, and you heal 5 for each enemy held.

**Works with.** Split Rock, Seeded, Living Rampart.

**Example.** Light Regrowth with four soldiers around you. All four are held and shaken for two seconds, then you heal 20.
