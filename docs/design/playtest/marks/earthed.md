# Earthed: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Lightning and Earth share, named for its
reaction, Earthed (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Lightning and Earth"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while
Lightning and Earth are both carried.

## earthed.shape
**What it does.** Ground Strike unlocks two new shapes for Earthed. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Earthed uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Thunderstone**: the charge doesn't spread. It is held in a stone of glassy rock until your heavy blow throws it out as bolts at everything nearby. **Step Current**: the fern-shaped burn mark on the ground spreads wider and stays live, so enemies who come at you pay for every step.

**When it happens.** Every time Earthed goes off while you carry both Lightning and Earth. Earthed goes off when Static lands on a shaken enemy, or when Tremor lands on a charged one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Earthed.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Thunderstone: nothing is used up. The enemy's Static is held for 6 seconds; it doesn't fade, and blows don't use it up. The enemy is rooted in place for 1 second. Your first heavy blow on it sets off all of the Static at once, for 7 damage per stack to everything within 6 metres, and puts a Static on each of them. Step Current: the enemy's Static runs out into everything within 6 metres, for 5 damage and 3 stagger per stack, plus a Tremor. The burn mark stays for 3 seconds and deals 4 damage and 6 stagger every half second. The enemy is rooted for 1 second. After Earthed goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** The Watch Kept (charge it again with a perfect guard), Deep Earth, The Ground Answers, The Capstone.

**Example.** A pack around one big enemy: use Thunderstone, build up lots of charge, and choose your moment. A rush of enemies coming at you: use Step Current.

**Not built yet.** Thunderstone's stone doesn't yet stand on its own where the enemy stood. For now, the enemy holds the charge, only your heavy blow throws it, and a burst of Static nearby doesn't. Step Current's burn mark hurts everything on it, standing still or not, but not yet the enemy it started from.

## earthed.watchkept
**What it does.** A perfect guard against an enemy that has Tremor puts two Static on it.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard against a shaken enemy, while you carry both Lightning and Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Two Static, at most once every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Earthed (a shaken enemy that gets charged is Earthed right away), Thunderstone, The Ground Answers.

**Example.** Shake the brute and block its swing perfectly. The ground takes its charge.

## earthed.deepearth
**What it does.** Earthed's burn mark is 1 metre wider, and the Earthed enemy stays rooted 0.5 seconds longer.

**When it happens.** Each time Earthed goes off.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor mark, 1 Shard. The burn mark reaches 5 metres instead of 4, and the enemy is rooted for 1.5 seconds instead of 1. With Thunderstone and Step Current, the 6-metre reach becomes 7 metres, and the root lasts 1.5 seconds.

**Works with.** Earthed and its shapes, The Watch Kept.

**Example.** The fern-shaped mark reaches the archer at the back.

## earthed.groundanswers
**What it does.** Every rock you raise holds charge. When Static goes off within 4 metres of one of your rocks, it runs into that rock and out of every other rock you have standing, dealing 7 damage per stack within 2.5 metres of each.

**When it happens.** Each time Static goes off, while you carry both Lightning and Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. For now: when Static goes off on a shaken enemy, it bursts again around that enemy for 7 damage and 5 stagger per stack within 2.5 metres, at most once every half second.

**Works with.** Earthed, The Watch Kept, The Capstone, Earth's raised stones.

**Example.** Raise stones around the room, then set off Static next to any of them.

**Not built yet.** Your rocks don't hold charge yet. For now, the second burst comes from the shaken enemy itself, not out of every rock you have standing.

## earthed.capstone
**What it does.** A round-topped stone falls on the enemy you face. It deals 18 damage and 40 stagger, fills its Tremor all the way, and pins it in place for 2 seconds. While the stone stands, each weapon blow near it calls a bolt of lightning down it into the ground, for 6 damage, 12 stagger and a Static within 2 metres.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Lightning and Earth. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Lightning.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 12 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. It lands on an enemy up to 7 metres away, after 0.4 seconds. It deals 18 damage, 40 stagger, a Static, fills Tremor all the way, and holds the enemy for 2 seconds (0.8 for elites and gods). For 5 seconds, it calls a bolt at most once every half second.

**Works with.** Earthed (the stone charges a shaken enemy, so it is Earthed), The Watch Kept, When the Watch Sleeps.

**Example.** Pin the captain and keep hitting it. Every blow calls a bolt down around it.

**Not built yet.** For now, the stone looks like a big sword falling and cracking the ground. The bolts come from your blows on the pinned enemy itself, not from blows on anything within 2 metres of the stone.

## earthed.watchsleeps
**What it does.** A duo surge. While it is lit, the god looks away and the ground is loose. Every heavy blow shakes the floor within 3 metres of the enemy you hit. Any enemy shaken while it has Static is Earthed right away. Heavy blows also deal 8 more stagger.

**When it happens.** At a rest fire, pick it for your surge slot in the Hand while you carry both Lightning and Earth. Light it with the surge button.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. 40 mana, lasts 10 seconds. For now: each heavy blow puts a Tremor on everything within 3 metres and 8 more stagger on the enemy you hit. A charged enemy you hit is Earthed right away, at most once every half second.

**Works with.** Earthed, The Capstone, The Watch Kept.

**Example.** Charge up a crowd, light your surge, and every heavy blow sets off Earthed on them.

**Not built yet.** Only the enemy you hit is Earthed right away, not every charged enemy the shaking reaches.
