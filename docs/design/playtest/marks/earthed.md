# Earthed: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Lightning and Earth share, named for its
reaction, Earthed (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Lightning and Earth"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while
Lightning and Earth are both carried.

## earthed.shape
**What it does.** Ground Strike: opens Earthed's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Earthed wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Thunderstone**: the charge does not spread; it is held in a stone of glassy rock until your heavy blow throws it as bolts at everything near. **Step Current**: the burnt figure spreads wider and stays live, so enemies that close on you pay for every step.

**When it happens.** Every time Earthed fires (Static landing on a shaken enemy, or Tremor on a charged one) while Lightning and Earth are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Earthed runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Thunderstone: nothing is spent; its Static is held, neither fading nor spent by blows, for 6 seconds and it is rooted 1 second; your first heavy blow on it discharges all of it at 7 damage a stack to everything within 6 metres and lays a Static on each. Step Current: its Static runs out at 5 damage and 3 stagger a stack, and a Tremor, to all within 6 metres; the figure stays 3 seconds, biting 4 damage and 6 stagger every half second; it is rooted 1 second. Earthed rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** The Watch Kept (charge it again with a perfect guard), Deep Earth, The Ground Answers, The Capstone.

**Example.** A pack around one big enemy: Thunderstone, charge it deep and choose your moment. A rush coming at you: Step Current.

**Not built yet.** Thunderstone's stone does not yet stand by itself where the enemy stood: the enemy holds the charge, only your heavy blow throws it, and a discharge near it does not. Step Current's figure bites everything on it, standing still or not, and not yet the enemy it started from.

## earthed.watchkept
**What it does.** A perfect guard against an enemy that carries Tremor lays two Static on it.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard against a shaken enemy, while Lightning and Earth are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Two Static, at most every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Earthed (a shaken enemy charged grounds at once), Thunderstone, The Ground Answers.

**Example.** Shake the brute, turn its swing, and the ground takes its charge.

## earthed.deepearth
**What it does.** Earthed's figure is 1 metre wider, and the grounded enemy is rooted 0.5 seconds longer.

**When it happens.** Each time Earthed fires.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. The figure reaches 5 metres, not 4, and the grounded enemy is rooted 1.5 seconds, not 1. In Thunderstone and Step Current the 6-metre reach becomes 7 metres, and the root lasts 1.5 seconds.

**Works with.** Earthed and its shapes, The Watch Kept.

**Example.** The fern reaches the archer at the back.

## earthed.groundanswers
**What it does.** Every rock you raise holds charge. Static discharged within 4 metres of one runs into it and out of every other rock you have standing: 7 a stack in 2.5 metres round each.

**When it happens.** Each discharge of Static, while Lightning and Earth are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: Static discharged from a shaken enemy bursts again round it, 7 damage and 5 stagger a stack in 2.5 metres, at most every half second.

**Works with.** Earthed, The Watch Kept, The Capstone, Earth's raised stones.

**Example.** Raise stones round the room, then discharge by any of them.

**Not built yet.** Your rocks do not yet hold charge: the second burst comes from the shaken enemy itself, not out of every rock you have standing.

## earthed.capstone
**What it does.** A round-headed stone falls on the enemy you face: 18 damage and 40 stagger, Tremor to full, and it is pinned 2 seconds. While the stone stands, each weapon blow near it calls a bolt down it into the ground: 6 damage, 12 stagger and a Static in 2 metres.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Lightning and Earth are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Lightning.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 7 metres away, after 0.4 seconds: 18 damage, 40 stagger, a Static, Tremor to full and a hold of 2 seconds (elites and gods 0.8). For 5 seconds a bolt at most every half second.

**Works with.** Earthed (the stone charges a shaken enemy, so it grounds), The Watch Kept, When the Watch Sleeps.

**Example.** Pin the captain and work it over; every blow calls a bolt round it.

**Not built yet.** The stone is drawn as a great sword's fall and crack; the bolts answer your blows on the pinned enemy itself, not on anything within 2 metres of the stone.

## earthed.watchsleeps
**What it does.** A duo surge. While your surge lasts the god looks away and the ground is loose: every heavy blow shakes the floor in 3 metres round the enemy struck, any enemy shaken while it carries Static is Earthed at once, and heavy blows deal 8 more stagger.

**When it happens.** While your surge lasts, with Lightning and Earth both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. As built: each heavy blow lays a Tremor on everything within 3 metres and 8 more stagger on the enemy struck; a charged enemy struck is Earthed at once, at most every half second.

**Works with.** Earthed, The Capstone, The Watch Kept.

**Example.** Charge a crowd, light your surge, and every heavy blow grounds them.

**Not built yet.** It is not yet a surge of its own (40 mana, 10 seconds) in the Hand's surge slot; it rides whatever surge you light, and only the enemy struck is Earthed at once, not every charged enemy the shake reaches.
