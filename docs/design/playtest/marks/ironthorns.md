# Iron Thorns: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Life and Metal share, named for its reaction, Iron
Thorns (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Life and Metal"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Life and Metal are
both carried.

## ironthorns.shape
**What it does.** Graft: opens Iron Thorns' two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Iron Thorns wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Pruning Hook**: your blows prune the thorns rather than throw them, and each one cut heals you. **Iron Harvest**: none is thrown; the thorns all burst out together at the end.

**When it happens.** Every time Iron Thorns fires (Sundered landing on a seeded enemy, or Seeded on a notched one) while Life and Metal are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Iron Thorns runs (three thorns, each weapon blow throwing one at the nearest other enemy).

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Pruning Hook: thorns for 6 seconds; each of your first three weapon blows on it heals you 4. Iron Harvest: when it dies or the 6 seconds end, a ring of blades to 3 metres, 8 damage, 8 stagger and a notch on each enemy it reaches. Iron Thorns rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Briar Patch and Flung Blade to bring the two, Sap of Iron, Grafted Edge, The Sown Men.

**Example.** Low on health against one brute: Pruning Hook, and three blows heal you 12. A pack: Iron Harvest on the one in the middle.

**Not built yet.** Iron Harvest's five thorns burst as one ring rather than five blades.

## ironthorns.sap
**What it does.** An enemy that dies with thorns in it gives its iron to the ground and the ground to you: a quarter of a flask fills at once, as plants draw metal up through the root.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy dies carrying both Seeded and Sundered, while Life and Metal are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A quarter of a flask, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Iron Thorns, Grafted Edge, The Sown Men.

**Example.** A pack of seeded, notched soldiers falls and a flask comes back full.

**Not built yet.** It answers any death seeded and notched, not only one with thorns still in it.

## ironthorns.ore
**What it does.** Things you grow draw iron from the ground: each grown thing lays a notch of Sundered when it bites.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Life and Metal are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. As built: while you stand near something you grew, your blows lay a notch, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Briar Patch, Sapling, Elder Tree, Iron Thorns (a notch on the seeded starts it).

**Example.** Fight beside your sapling and every soldier you strike there is notched and ready for Iron Thorns.

**Not built yet.** Your grown things do not yet lay the notch themselves: your blows near them do.

## ironthorns.grafted
**What it does.** A notch laid on a Seeded enemy lays two.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Life and Metal are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. As built: each blow on an enemy carrying both Seeded and Sundered adds one more notch, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Sap of Iron, which it grows from, Iron Thorns (more notches, more thorns), Flung Blade.

**Example.** Two blows on a seeded soldier and its armour is three notches broken.

**Not built yet.** It answers blows, not the laying of each notch.

## ironthorns.sownmen
**What it does.** You throw five teeth ahead; they sink, and warriors of bronze and root rise from the soil. Each goes to the nearest enemy and strikes it again and again, seeding it, and every third strike notches. After 6 seconds they crumble to rust and leaves.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Life and Metal are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Life.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards, 45 mana: the pair's larger spell. Five warriors for 6 seconds, each striking about every 0.7 seconds for 8 damage, 4 stagger and a Seeded; about one strike in three also notches.

**Works with.** Iron Thorns (a seeded, notched enemy grows thorns), Sap of Iron, Grafted Edge.

**Example.** Sow them into a pack and fight beside them.

**Not built yet.** The teeth are not thrown in a fan nor do the warriors rise from the floor yet, the notch comes by chance rather than on every third strike, and a heavy blow of yours does not yet call every standing warrior to strike the same enemy.
