# Steam: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Fire and Water share, named for its reaction,
Steam (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Fire and Water"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Fire and Water are
both carried.

## steam.shape
**What it does.** Two Sisters: opens Steam's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Steam wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Blind Steam**: the plume does not lift but lies low round the enemy, a white cloud the enemies in it cannot see out of. **Scald**: no cloud; the water flashes off in a harder burst that throws everything near, and the Burn is not spent but boils on.

**When it happens.** Every time Steam fires (Fire landing on a Soaked enemy, or Water on a burning one) while Fire and Water are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Steam runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Blind Steam: the Soaked is spent and half the Burn with it, as plain; the cloud lies 3 metres round it for 4 seconds and stings 3 a second; enemies in it as it forms see only 2 metres for those 4 seconds and their swings come a fifth slower. Scald: the Soaked is spent and the Burn kept; a burst of 14, and 6 more for each Burn it carries, with 30 stagger, in 2.5 metres, and everything in it thrown 1.5 metres. Steam rests 3 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Hissing Blade to bring the two, Strike Through Steam, Kettle, The Sisters' Quarrel.

**Example.** A crowd closing on you: Blind Steam, and they lose you in the cloud. One deep-burning brute: Scald, again and again, since its Burn is never spent.

**Not built yet.** An enemy that walks into Blind Steam's cloud after it forms is stung but not blinded.

## steam.hissingblade
**What it does.** Your heavy blows lay Burn and Soaked together.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, while Fire and Water are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Burn and one Soaked.

**Works with.** Steam (one heavy blow on a fresh enemy brings both), Strike Through Steam.

**Example.** Heavy, and the struck enemy steams at once.

## steam.strikethrough
**What it does.** A weapon blow on an enemy within a second of its steam is a scald: half again the damage and stagger.

**When it happens.** For one second after Steam fires on an enemy, while Fire and Water are both carried; the first weapon blow on it in that second.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The blow deals 1.5 times its damage and stagger. In that second every stagger it takes is raised the same way, a spell's too.

**Works with.** Hissing Blade (the heavy that steams it, then a quick light blow), Steam.

**Example.** Heavy to steam it, then cut through the plume at once.

## steam.kettle
**What it does.** A Soaked enemy that dies burning goes up as steam at full strength, with no rest.

**When it happens.** Each time a Soaked, burning enemy dies, while Fire and Water are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. A burst of 8, and 4 more for each Burn it carried, with 15 stagger, to everything within 2.5 metres.

**Works with.** Hissing Blade, Steam, The Sisters' Quarrel.

**Example.** Kill a soaked, burning soldier in the middle of its pack and the pack is scalded.

**Not built yet.** It bursts as the plain Steam does whatever shape you wear, not in your chosen shape.

## steam.quarrel
**What it does.** Flame from one hand and a wave from the other close like pincers on the enemy you face, 7 metres off at most. Where they meet steam bursts, 30 and 55 stagger in 3.5 metres, and Steam goes off at full. For 3 seconds your weapon blows on those caught strike harder and burn.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Fire and Water are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Fire.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The two strikes close in 0.45 seconds, 8 and 10 stagger each, one laying Burn and Soaked; the blast deals 30 and 55 stagger in 3.5 metres; Steam is forced on the enemy even while it rests. For 3 seconds your weapon blows on it deal a quarter more and each lays a Burn.

**Works with.** Steam and its shapes, Strike Through Steam, Kettle.

**Example.** Pin the captain between flame and wave, then cut it while it is raw.

**Not built yet.** The flame and the wave do not yet run along two curved lanes striking what they cross: two strikes close on the enemy from a ring round it.
