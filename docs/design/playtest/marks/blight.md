# Blight: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Blight, the branch Dark and Poison share.

## blight.wither
**What it does.** Poison bites a quarter harder on a Cursed enemy, and does not fade from it while the curse lasts. Your heavy blows also lay a Curse and poison, but a weapon blow lays only your first element's affliction, so they lay the one of the two you carry first.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while you carry both Dark and Poison. The heavy-blow laying comes with every heavy blow or finisher that lands.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Poison on a Cursed enemy bites 25% harder and keeps its doses for as long as the curse holds. A heavy blow lays a Curse (Dark first) or two doses of poison (Poison first); with your first element's root learned, it adds only what goes beyond the root's own stack, so a Curse, one deep at most, adds nothing and the poison adds one dose. The other affliction comes from your spells, the charged blade and your strike.

**Works with.** Curse or Venom, Feeding Rot, Carrion Flies, The Reaping, Grasp and Miasma, and the infused strikes (R2 lays the second element).

**Example.** Poison first: lay eight doses with blade and heavy blows, then Grasp the soldier. While it stays Cursed the poison never runs out and bites for 20 at a time.

## blight.feedingrot
**What it does.** Each time poison bites a Cursed enemy, a little of it comes back to you as health.

**When it happens.** Every bite of poison, on its slow beat, on an enemy that is Cursed.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Half a point of health for each dose that bit: 4 at eight doses, every 2 seconds.

**Works with.** Wither (the poison does not fade while the curse holds), Curse, Long Curse, One Death Feeds, Wasting Grip.

**Example.** Three Cursed soldiers at eight doses each: every two seconds you heal 12 while you guard.

## blight.wastinggrip
**What it does.** You brace and pour a short stream of rot at what stands before you, biting again and again and laying Curse. While you hold this mark, any spell of yours that strikes a poisoned enemy also feeds you.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). While it pours, the stick or the cursor turns it slowly. A dodge lets it go, and a blow that lands on you breaks it. The feeding comes whenever a spell of yours strikes a poisoned enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards, 40 mana. A bar 2.5 metres long and 1.2 metres wide, after a 0.3-second brace, for 2.5 seconds. It bites every half second for 8 damage, 6 stagger and a Curse: five bites on what stays in it. A spell striking a poisoned enemy heals 5, at most once a second. As built it does not yet take hold of an enemy, drink its poison or throw it.

**Works with.** Wither, Feeding Rot, Blood Price (pay in health when short of mana), Miasma and Grasp (both feed it when they strike the poisoned), and the charged blade (it charges with Dark).

**Example.** A poisoned brute in front of you: pour the rot over it and it is Cursed through the bites while every spell you land on it heals 5.

## blight.carrionflies
**What it does.** An enemy that dies both Cursed and poisoned bursts a moment later. Everyone near takes the doses it carried, and a curse.

**When it happens.** The moment an enemy carrying both Curse and poison dies; the burst follows 0.8 seconds later.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Everything within 5 metres of where it fell is Cursed at once. Then the burst, 5 metres round: 3 damage and as many doses of poison as the dead one carried, up to eight. At most twice a second.

**Works with.** Wither, Feeding Rot, One Death Feeds, Dread, The Reaping.

**Example.** Kill a Cursed soldier at eight doses in a crowd, and the others are eight deep and Cursed before they reach you.

## blight.gravesoil
**What it does.** You foul the ground round your feet. For a while, whatever stands on it is bitten every two seconds and Cursed.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. A circle 5 metres in radius, half a metre ahead of you, for 8 seconds. It bites 0.3 seconds after the cast and then every 2 seconds, four bites in all: 3 damage and a Curse each, with no stagger. It always lays Curse, whichever element you carry first, and does not hurry the poison's beat.

**Works with.** Wither (poison never fades from what stands in it), Feeding Rot, Carrion Flies, One Death Feeds, Long Shadows.

**Example.** Plant it where you mean to fight a pack: everyone who steps in is Cursed, so Wither keeps their poison running.

## blight.onedeathfeeds
**What it does.** When a Cursed enemy dies, the poison in every other enemy near it bites at once, out of turn, and you are fed for each one bitten.

**When it happens.** The moment a Cursed enemy dies, whatever killed it; at most twice a second.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. Every poisoned enemy within 12 metres of where it fell bites at once for its doses, and you heal 4 for each one bitten. Each poison keeps its own beat afterward.

**Works with.** Wither, Feeding Rot (each extra bite on a Cursed one feeds again), Carrion Flies, Dread, Curse.

**Example.** Five soldiers at eight doses: kill one Cursed, and the other four bite for 16 each at once while you heal 16.

## blight.reaping
**What it does.** Your finisher on an enemy both Cursed and poisoned reaps the field. A blade as long as the field sweeps once round it, time slows, and all the poison in every enemy near lands at once. Half of all it deals comes back to you as health.

**When it happens.** Every finisher (a heavy blow on a staggered enemy) that lands on an enemy carrying both Curse and poison.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. The sweep takes 0.42 seconds and reaches 9 metres round the finished enemy. Each poisoned enemy in it has its poison set off and spent: 9 damage a dose (72 at eight) and 4 stagger a dose. Half the total heals you.

**Works with.** Wither, Carrion Flies (spread the doses first), Feast, Miasma, Grasp, Long Shadows.

**Example.** Four soldiers at eight doses round a Cursed brute: finish the brute and each takes 72 while you heal half of it.
