# Caught Light: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Water and Light share, named for its reaction,
Caught Light (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Water and Light"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Water and
Light are both carried.

## caughtlight.shape
**What it does.** Net or Eye: opens Caught Light's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Caught Light wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Thrown Wide**: the net is cast wider, and when its time ends it is hauled in, dragging everything on it toward the enemy that bore it. **The Fish's Eye**: no net; the water on the body gathers the light into one white eye, and your next weapon blow on it is a critical at three times the damage that passes through to the one behind.

**When it happens.** Every time Caught Light fires (Water landing on a Marked enemy, or a Mark on a Soaked one) while Water and Light are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Caught Light runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Thrown Wide: a net 5 metres across for 4 seconds that Marks what stands in it twice a second; those in it as it is cast are a quarter slower; at its end everything within 2.5 metres of its enemy is dragged 2 metres toward it. The Fish's Eye: for 6 seconds your next weapon blow on it is critical and deals twice as much again (three times in all), its Mark is spent, and a strike of 12 and 10 stagger lands on the line 2 metres beyond it. Caught Light rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Bright Water to bring the two, Sun on the Spray, Seen in the Water, The Gods' Road.

**Example.** A scattered pack: Thrown Wide, and haul them into one heap. A captain behind its shield-bearer: The Fish's Eye, and strike through one into the other.

**Not built yet.** Those who walk onto the net later are Marked but not slowed or hauled. The Fish's Eye's blow does not yet pass through with its own damage: a strike of 12 lands behind instead.

## caughtlight.brightwater
**What it does.** The blow that ends a chain lays Soaked and a Mark together.

**When it happens.** The last blow of a light chain, or the heavy blow that ends one, while Water and Light are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Soaked and one Mark.

**Works with.** Caught Light (one chain on a fresh enemy brings both), Sun on the Spray.

**Example.** Finish each chain and the net opens under the enemy.

## caughtlight.spray
**What it does.** A perfect dodge Soaks and Marks every enemy within 3 m of where it ends.

**When it happens.** Each perfect dodge, while Water and Light are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Soaked and one Mark on every enemy within 3 metres, at most every half second.

**Works with.** Caught Light (both at once, so the net opens), Bright Water, The Gods' Road.

**Example.** Dodge through a pack at the last moment and a net of light opens under each.

**Not built yet.** The spray is round you at the moment of the perfect dodge, not where the dodge ends.

## caughtlight.seen
**What it does.** While an enemy stands on the net, your blows on any other enemy on it are critical as if it were Marked, without spending a mark.

**When it happens.** For the 4 seconds after each Caught Light, while Water and Light are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Every enemy within 3 metres of the one that bore the net as it opens takes critical blows for 4 seconds, and no Mark is spent by them.

**Works with.** Caught Light (its net), Bright Water, Sun on the Spray.

**Example.** Net a pack and cut them all critically, keeping their marks for later.

**Not built yet.** It opens those within 3 metres as the net forms, not those who walk onto it later, and it counts with any shape, The Fish's Eye's too, which lays no net.

## caughtlight.road
**What it does.** A rainbow of falling spray arches from you to the enemy you face, within 10 metres. You run it, untouchable, and come down on it with a falling cut, 18 and 30 stagger, Soaked and Marked. The road stands 3 seconds and Soaks and Marks what stands in it.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Water and Light are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Water.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, movement, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The cut deals 18 and 30 stagger and lays Soaked and a Mark, so Caught Light opens under the enemy; your next heavy blow within 1.5 seconds is critical. The road lies 8 metres ahead of where you cast it for 3 seconds, Soaking and Marking once a second what stands in it.

**Works with.** Caught Light and its shapes, Seen in the Water, Sun on the Spray.

**Example.** Run the road to the archer and net it where it stands.

**Not built yet.** The road does not yet seek a Soaked or Marked enemy before the one you face, and pressing light as you land does not yet make the cut critical: the heavy blow after it is.
