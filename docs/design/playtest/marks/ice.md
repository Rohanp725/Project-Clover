# Ice: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Ice tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## ice.chill
**What it does.** Every blow of your weapon that lands chills the enemy, a stack at a time, up to five. Each stack slows it. At five it is Frozen: held still in ice until it thaws, or until a heavy blow shatters it for a burst of damage.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every weapon blow that lands, light or heavy, while Ice is your first element (or all in). With Ice second, your weapon lays the first element's affliction and Chill comes from your Ice spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One stack a blow, five at most, lasting 6 seconds. Each stack slows the enemy by 7%. At five it freezes for 2.5 seconds; elites and gods thaw after 1 second, and some great forms cannot be frozen. A heavy blow on a frozen enemy shatters it for 45 more damage, and the Chill ends. Nothing chills a frozen enemy further.

**Works with.** Glaze (one more Chill a blow), Hoarfrost, Cold Read, Flying Shards, Brittle, Clean Break, Long Winter (Chill lasts 50% longer), Held Fast, Sharp Break, and Soaked, which carries Chill to everyone within 4 metres.

**Example.** Four quick light blows slow a soldier to a crawl; the fifth freezes it, and a heavy blow breaks it for 45 on top of the cut.

## ice.coldread
**What it does.** A perfect guard chills the one who struck, two stacks at once.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Two stacks of Chill on the attacker.

**Works with.** Chill (three blows after the guard freeze it), Patient Guard (the perfect moment 17% longer), Gathering Frost, Unmoved, Flash Freeze on the Glacier branch.

**Example.** A soldier already three deep swings at you: guard it perfectly and it freezes on the spot, ready for a heavy blow.

## ice.gather
**What it does.** Catching blows makes your own blows harder for a while. A blow held on your guard leaves your weapon a fifth harder; a perfect guard leaves it two fifths harder, and every affliction you lay in that time is one stack deeper.

**When it happens.** Each blow you block with the guard button, and each perfect guard. Each gift lasts four seconds.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A blocked blow: 20% more weapon damage for 4 seconds. A perfect guard: 40% more weapon damage and one more stack of every affliction you lay, for 4 seconds. As built there are no rings of frost gathering while you wait: it is the guard alone that feeds it.

**Works with.** Cold Read, Braced (guarding costs 17% less stamina), Slow Pulse, Heavy Snow, Unmoved, Following Draft on the Wind tree (every timed gift lasts longer).

**Example.** Perfect-guard a soldier's thrust and answer with two light blows: each lands two fifths harder and chills two deep.

## ice.statue
**What it does.** You raise a block of ice just ahead of you. Nothing walks through it, and blows from its far side do not reach you. When its time ends, or when you strike it with a heavy blow, it bursts and chills everything near.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). A heavy blow swung within reach of it bursts it early.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The block stands 1.5 metres ahead of you, 2 metres wide, and lasts 6 seconds. The burst reaches 3.5 metres: 20 damage, 30 stagger and three stacks of Chill; burst by your heavy blow, it lands as a heavy blow. As built it is a small wall of ice: it does not yet draw enemies to it or burst when they strike it. One wall of ice stands at a time: raising another, this or Winter Wall, takes the first away without its burst.

**Works with.** Chill (three stacks from the burst, two more blows to freeze), Gathering Frost, Unmoved, Bitter Cold (spells strike chilled enemies 20% harder), Flying Shards.

**Example.** Raise it in a doorway, let the soldiers bunch against it, then cut it with a heavy blow: all of them take the burst and three Chill.

## ice.unmoved
**What it does.** Nothing breaks your heavy blows. While you wind up or swing one, a blow that lands on you still hurts, but at half, and your swing goes on, red attacks included. The price: your dodge is short and costs more.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The shield holds from the start of a heavy blow's wind-up to the end of its swing.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Blows taken during a heavy blow's wind-up and swing do 50% of their damage and do not make you flinch. Dodges go 30% less far and cost 25% more stamina (about 31 where they cost 25).

**Works with.** Chill and its shatter (every heavy blow lands), Gathering Frost, Heavy Snow (heavy blows break balance 15% harder), Hollow Statue, Heavy Hand on the Earth tree, which does the same for the swing.

**Example.** A brute brings down a red overhead as you start a heavy blow on its frozen friend: you take half, and the shatter lands anyway.

## ice.shards
**What it does.** A shattered enemy throws its ice wide. Everyone the shards reach is struck and chilled two deep, the one that shattered among them.

**When it happens.** Each time a frozen enemy is shattered, by your heavy blow or anything else that shatters.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A burst 4.5 metres round the shattered enemy: 14 damage, 10 stagger and two stacks of Chill to each.

**Works with.** Chill, Brittle, Clean Break, Sharp Break, All at Once, Icebreaker on the Glacier branch, and The World Holds Still, whose chain of shatters throws the shards again at each link.

**Example.** Shatter one frozen soldier in a pack of four: the other three are two deep at once, three light blows from freezing.

## ice.winterdraw
**What it does.** When the Iaido Slash lands on a chilled enemy, a blade of ice flies on ahead and strikes and chills everything in its path.

**When it happens.** Each time the Iaido Slash, the Katana's weapon skill, strikes an enemy that already carries Chill. It needs the Katana.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The blade flies at 16 metres a second for half a second, about 8 metres, 1 metre in radius. Each enemy it meets takes 18 damage, 20 stagger and two stacks of Chill. Once a second at most. The skill costs its usual mana. As built any chilled enemy sets it off; it does not need to be Frozen.

**Works with.** Chill, Cold Read and Hoarfrost to lay the first stack, Flying Shards (the Slash is a heavy blow, so on a frozen enemy it shatters too).

**Example.** Slash a chilled spearman at the front of a line: the ice flies on and the two behind it are two deep before they reach you.

## ice.brittle
**What it does.** An enemy whose ice is gone, by a thaw or a shatter, stays brittle for a while. Your weapon cuts deeper into it and its balance breaks sooner.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, on every enemy that has been Frozen and is no longer.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. For 5 seconds after its ice is gone the enemy takes 20% more damage from weapon blows and 20% more stagger from everything. Spells do not cut deeper.

**Works with.** Chill, Held Fast, Sharp Break, Clean Break, Heavy Snow, Unmoved, and the finisher: a shatter and a few brittle blows often stagger it.

**Example.** Shatter a brute, then keep cutting: for five seconds every blow bites a fifth harder and its balance bar fills fast.

## ice.cleanbreak
**What it does.** Shatter an enemy that has less than a fifth of its health left and nothing is left of it: it dies outright. The gods are spared.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy is shattered while under a fifth of its health.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Below 20% of its health when it shatters, it is ended a moment after the blow. Elites are not spared; gods are.

**Works with.** Chill, Flying Shards, Brittle, Sharp Break, All at Once, Eight Points, The World Holds Still.

**Example.** An elite knight is down to a sliver: freeze it, shatter it, and it is gone without the slow finish.

## ice.hanging
**What it does.** You hang a great blade of ice over the enemy you face. It follows that enemy, then falls as a heavy blow that chills what it strikes, and leaves cracked ground that bites for a moment.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 7 metres; with none, 7 metres ahead. It falls after 3 seconds, following its enemy until a moment before. The fall strikes everything within 2 metres for 30 damage, 40 stagger and three stacks of Chill, as a heavy blow, so a frozen enemy under it shatters. The crack, 1.5 metres round, bites every half second for 2 seconds for 2 damage and a stack of Chill. With no enemy faced the cast is still spent and the slot waits.

**Works with.** Chill (freeze it during the three seconds and the blade shatters it), Eight Points, Flying Shards, Brittle, Clean Break, Bitter Cold.

**Example.** Hang it over a brute, chill it to five with your blade as it falls: the blade comes down on a frozen body and shatters it.

## ice.eight
**What it does.** You set eight shards in a ring round the enemy you face. They turn with it, then close on it together, each laying a stack of Chill: enough to freeze it from nothing.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 11 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 6 metres. The ring is 3.5 metres round it and closes after 1.2 seconds. Each shard deals 5 damage, 4 stagger and one stack of Chill: 40 damage and 32 stagger in all, and the fifth freezes it. With no enemy faced the shards close on a spot 6 metres ahead and strike what stands within 1.2 metres of it; the cast is spent either way.

**Works with.** Chill, Hanging Ice, All at Once, Clean Break, and the charged blade: Ice's charged blows shatter a Frozen enemy with a light blow as well as a heavy one.

**Example.** Cast it on an archer at the back: it freezes where it stands, and you have 2.5 seconds to reach it with a heavy blow.

## ice.allatonce
**What it does.** Shattering strikes three times as hard. The price: Chill no longer slows anyone. It still freezes at five.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. A shatter deals 135 where it dealt 45 (146 with Sharp Break). Chill's slowing is gone, at every depth.

**Works with.** Eight Points (a freeze from nothing), Hanging Ice, Sharp Break, Brittle, Clean Break, Flying Shards, Flash Freeze on the Glacier branch.

**Example.** Freeze an elite with Eight Points and shatter it with one heavy blow: 135 from the ice alone, on top of the blow.

## ice.blackice
**What it does.** Hoarfrost leaves a sheet of ice on the ground ahead of you. Enemies standing on it grow colder.

**When it happens.** Each time you cast Hoarfrost, at most once a second. As built every element's own spell lays it: carrying two elements, the other element's own spell lays the sheet too.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Four patches in a line 4.5 metres long toward the enemy you face, each 1.1 metres in radius, for 4 seconds. Each bites once a second for 1 damage and one stack of Chill.

**Works with.** Hoarfrost (Long Frost sends Hoarfrost itself 33% further, not the sheet), Chill, Long Winter, Bitter Cold, and Hollow Statue or Winter Wall to keep enemies standing on the ice.

**Example.** Cast Hoarfrost down a corridor: the soldiers who walk in arrive two or three deep before you have swung.

## ice.splinter
**What it does.** When a ward turns a blow, it bursts round you, striking and chilling everything near, and gives you the mana of a perfect guard.

**When it happens.** Each time a ward is spent: Glaze's ward, or any other.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A burst 3.5 metres round you: 12 damage, 30 stagger and three stacks of Chill. 15 mana. As built the turned blow does not count as a perfect guard for other marks.

**Works with.** Glaze (one ward when lit), Thick Glaze (Glaze lasts 50% longer), Clear Ice (Glaze costs 20% less), Second Skin (a ward every 12 seconds), Chill.

**Example.** Light Glaze and let a brute's red attack land on the ward: everything round you is three deep and staggered, and the mana pays toward the next Glaze.

## ice.wall
**What it does.** You raise a wall of ice before you. Nothing walks through it and blows from its far side do not reach you. When its time ends, or when you strike it with a heavy blow, it bursts and chills everything near it.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). A heavy blow swung within reach of it bursts it early.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 35 mana. The wall rises 2 metres ahead, 5 metres wide, and stands 6 seconds. The burst reaches 4 metres round it: 20 damage, 30 stagger and three stacks of Chill; burst by your heavy blow, it lands as a heavy blow. As built it chills only when it bursts, not on a touch, and it bursts where it stands rather than forward. One wall of ice stands at a time: raising another, this or Hollow Statue, takes the first away without its burst.

**Works with.** Splinter Ward, Second Skin, Black Ice, Bitter Cold, Chill, Unmoved.

**Example.** Archers loose at you across a yard: raise the wall, wait behind it for the soldiers to come round, then cut it as they gather.

## ice.skin
**What it does.** While an enemy is near, a ward of ice forms on you every twelve seconds at no cost, up to the three you can hold. The price: you have less health.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The count runs only while an enemy is within 12 metres.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. One ward every 12 seconds, three at most. 25 less health (75 where you had 100). As built the shell does not regrow by itself between blows and cannot be broken by choice: it is a ward on a clock.

**Works with.** Splinter Ward (each ward spent bursts), Glaze, Winter Wall, Unmoved.

**Example.** Stand your ground against a long fight: every twelve seconds another blow you could not avoid is turned.

## ice.stillworld
**What it does.** Lighting Glaze raises a shell of ice round you. Press the surge button again while Glaze lasts and the shell breaks outward as a ring that freezes everything it crosses, while the world is slowed. And whenever you shatter a frozen enemy, a line of breaking ice runs to the nearest one still Frozen and shatters that one too, which sends the line on again.

**When it happens.** The shell comes when you light the surge, and stands while it lasts; the break comes when you press the surge button again, once each time you light it. As built it answers whichever surge you light. The chain of shatters comes with every shatter, Glaze or not.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. The ring runs out to 9 metres in half a second: 10 damage, 30 stagger and Chill to full depth, so it freezes what can be frozen. Enemies are slowed to a fifth of their speed for 1.5 seconds. Glaze and its ward stay. The chain looks for a Frozen enemy within 7 metres of the one that shattered and strikes it 0.2 seconds later for 20 damage and 30 stagger as a heavy blow, which shatters it for 45 more.

**Works with.** Glaze, Flying Shards (the shards fly at every link), Clean Break, All at Once, Sharp Break, Brittle.

**Example.** Light Glaze in a crowd, break it outward, and shatter the nearest: the ice runs from one to the next until every frozen body has broken.
