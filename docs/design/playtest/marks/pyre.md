# Pyre: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Pyre, the branch Poison and Fire share.

## pyre.fuel
**What it does.** Heavy blows lay more of your first element's affliction. Your weapon lays only your first element's affliction, so of the two this mark lays only that one: poison with Poison first, Burn with Fire first. The other comes from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. With Poison first: two doses a heavy blow, or one once Venom is learned (Venom's own heavy-blow dose counts against it). With Fire first: one stack of Burn a heavy blow, and nothing once Cinder is learned. Poison holds up to eight doses for 6 seconds and bites for 2 a dose every 2 seconds; set off, it deals 6 a dose.

**Works with.** Venom or Cinder, Miasma or Flame Wave in a slot (the other element's affliction), the charged blade and the R2 strike, Goes Up, Funeral Fire, Light the Pyres.

**Example.** Carrying Poison first and Fire second: a chain ending in a heavy blow leaves a soldier deep in poison, and a Flame Wave sets it burning beside it.

## pyre.flashpoint
**What it does.** When a burning enemy's poison reaches its full depth, it goes up. The poison explodes and sets alight everyone near.

**When it happens.** The moment an affliction fills on an enemy that is burning and carries a full eight doses of poison.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards. The poison is set off: 6 damage a dose to everything within 3 metres, 48 at eight, with 4 stagger a dose. Everything within 3 metres also takes two stacks of Burn. At most twice a second.

**Works with.** Fuel, Cinder, Funeral Fire, Slow Burn, Searing Edge.

**Example.** Keep a burning brute poisoned; the blow that brings it to eight doses makes it go up among its friends.

## pyre.fireeater
**What it does.** You brace and breathe out a stream of green fire for two seconds, turning slowly as it burns. It poisons everything it sweeps across. A dodge lets it go.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). While it pours, the stick or the cursor turns it.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. A bar 5 metres long and 1.2 metres to each side, after a 0.3-second brace, for 2 seconds. It bites every quarter second for 4 damage, 3 stagger and one dose of poison: eight bites on what stays in it. The branch's spells are made of Poison whichever element is first, and one that lands charges the blade with Poison.

**Works with.** Goes Up (eight doses fast), Fuel, Funeral Fire, Light the Pyres.

**Example.** Sweep it across a line of soldiers as they come up the stair.

## pyre.funeralfire
**What it does.** An enemy that dies poisoned and burning explodes, and leaves the ground burning where it fell.

**When it happens.** The moment an enemy dies carrying both poison and Burn.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A burst 3.5 metres in radius: 20 damage, 20 stagger and two doses of poison, and one stack of Burn on all within it. The ground burns 1.5 metres round for 4 seconds, biting for 5 every half second with a stack of Burn. At most twice a second.

**Works with.** Fuel, Goes Up, Catching Fire, Wildfire.

**Example.** Kill one poisoned, burning soldier in a crowd and the rest catch both.

## pyre.slowfuse
**What it does.** You set a fuse on the enemy you face. Your next weapon blow on it within five seconds sets off a great blast round it. With no enemy faced nothing is set, and the slot waits all the same.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then land a blow on that enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 8 metres. The blast is 3.5 metres in radius: 40 damage, 40 stagger and two doses of poison.

**Works with.** Fuel (make that blow a heavy one), Goes Up, Funeral Fire.

**Example.** Set the fuse on the brute in the middle and cut it once: the whole group takes 40.

## pyre.slowburn
**What it does.** Every affliction you lay works harder, Burn and poison among them: each bite, and each set-off.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. 30% stronger afflictions.

**Works with.** Fuel, Goes Up, Funeral Fire, Searing Edge, Light the Pyres.

**Example.** A five-deep burn that dealt 12.5 a second now deals over 16.

## pyre.lightthepyres
**What it does.** You kneel and drive your weapon into the ground. A line of fire runs to every poisoned or burning enemy near and follows it. Stand through three breaths, and a pillar of flame rises under each, setting off its burn and its poison. A dodge or a blow taken breaks it. With no one alight, the mana comes back.

**When it happens.** Cast it from its spell slot (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4) and stay still.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. 60 mana. Up to six enemies within 9 metres. The three breaths take 2.2 seconds. Each pillar is 2 metres in radius: 40 damage and 40 stagger, a heavy blow, two doses of poison, plus 6 a dose of poison and 10 and 10 a stack of Burn set off.

**Works with.** Fuel, Fire Eater, Goes Up, Cinder, Slow Burn.

**Example.** Fight until five enemies are burning, roll clear, and light the pyres.
