# Kindling Point: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Light and Fire share, named for its reaction,
Kindling Point (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d3.md`, "Light and Fire"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Light and Fire
are both carried.

## kindlingpoint.shape
**What it does.** Unlocks two new shapes for Kindling Point. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Kindling Point uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. Both shapes still focus the light on one point, like before: each of your blows adds to its Burn, and the third one sets it on fire. **Burning Glass**: the point stays after the burst, like a magnifying glass held still, adding Burn every second. **The Fire Is Hungry**: the flame jumps to the next enemy, so the point moves through a crowd.

**When it happens.** Every time Kindling Point goes off while you carry both Light and Fire. Kindling Point goes off when Burn lands on a Marked enemy, or when a Mark lands on a burning one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Kindling Point.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both shapes: for up to 8 seconds, each of your critical hits on the enemy adds a Burn, and the third critical hit uses up all its Burn. Blows that aren't critical don't count. Burning Glass: the third blow bursts for 10 damage per Burn and leaves the enemy with two stacks of Burn. Then, for 4 seconds, it gains one more Burn every second. The Fire Is Hungry: the third blow jumps to the nearest other enemy within 6 metres for 10 damage per Burn, Marking it and giving it two stacks of Burn. This is on purpose: it may start a new point there. After Kindling Point goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 6 seconds.

**Works with.** Glare and Flame Wave to set it up, The Sun Points, Noon Guard, Hands of the Sun.

**Example.** One brute: use Burning Glass, and keep it burning. A crowd: use The Fire Is Hungry, and move the point through it.

## kindlingpoint.sunpoints
**What it does.** The blow that ends a chain of blows on a burning enemy Marks it.

**When it happens.** The last blow of each chain that lands on a burning enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Mark.

**Works with.** Cinder, Kindling Point, Clear Sky.

**Example.** End each chain on a burning soldier, and the point starts to gather.

## kindlingpoint.noonguard
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a Marked, burning enemy sets the point on fire right away.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy that has both a Mark and Burn.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. All its Burn is used up, for 10 damage each, and it keeps burning with two stacks.

**Works with.** Kindling Point, The Sun Points, Kiln Stance.

**Example.** A Marked, burning brute swings. Guard it perfectly, and it bursts into flame.

## kindlingpoint.clearsky
**What it does.** A Mark lasts one blow longer on a burning enemy.

**When it happens.** Always on once you learn it, while you carry both Light and Fire.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor mark, 1 Shard. A Mark that would be used up by a blow on a burning enemy stays for one blow more, once each time it is laid, whether the Mark or the Burn came first.

**Works with.** The Sun Points, which it comes after on the chart, Kindling Point.

**Example.** One more critical hit before the Mark is used up.

## kindlingpoint.feast
**What it does.** When a Kindling Point burst kills an enemy, every burn within 8 metres gains a stack, and you get mana.

**When it happens.** Each time a blow of Kindling Point itself kills an enemy, in whichever shape it goes off, while you carry both Light and Fire. An enemy your weapon or a spell finishes off doesn't count.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. One Burn on everything within 8 metres, and 10 mana, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Kindling Point, Hands of the Sun, and Daybreak and Meteor Leap to spend the mana.

**Example.** Burn down a Marked pack, and every death feeds the fire.

## kindlingpoint.hands
**What it does.** A burning sun rises over you for a moment, and long rays reach down to the four nearest enemies. Each ray ends in a hand of flame that grabs its enemy. Each one is held, gets two stacks of Burn and a Mark, so every one of them has a Kindling Point.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Light and Fire. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Light.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 12 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. It reaches up to four enemies within 9 metres. Each takes 6 damage, 10 stagger, a Mark and two Burn, and is held for 1 second. The hand lets go at your first weapon blow on that enemy, within 4 seconds: that blow is a critical hit and adds two more Burn.

**Works with.** Kindling Point and its shapes, The Feast, The Sun Points.

**Example.** Start a fight with it on a pack: four points start gathering at once.

**Not built yet.** For now, the rays jump from one enemy to the next instead of coming down from one sun.
