# Kindling Point: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Light and Fire share, named for its reaction,
Kindling Point (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d3.md`, "Light and Fire"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Light and Fire
are both carried.

## kindlingpoint.shape
**What it does.** Opens Kindling Point's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Kindling Point wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. Both gather the point as before: each of your blows deepens its Burn, and the third sets it alight. **Burning Glass**: the point stays on after the burst, a lens held still, deepening the Burn each second. **The Fire Is Hungry**: the tongue of flame leaps on to the next enemy, so the point moves on through a crowd.

**When it happens.** Every time Kindling Point fires (Burn landing on a marked enemy, or a Mark on a burning one) while Light and Fire are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Kindling Point runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both: for up to 8 seconds each of your weapon blows on it adds a Burn, and the third spends all its Burn. Burning Glass: the third bursts for 10 damage a Burn and leaves it burning two deep, then for 4 seconds its Burn deepens by one each second. The Fire Is Hungry: the third leaps to the nearest other enemy within 6 metres for 10 damage a Burn, Marking it and burning it two deep, which on purpose may start a point there. Kindling Point rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Glare and Flame Wave to bring the two, The Sun Points, Noon Guard, Hands of the Sun.

**Example.** One brute: Burning Glass and keep it burning. A crowd: The Fire Is Hungry and walk the point through it.

**Not built yet.** Each weapon blow counts toward the three, not only critical ones.

## kindlingpoint.sunpoints
**What it does.** The blow that ends a chain on a burning enemy Marks it.

**When it happens.** The last blow of each chain that lands on a burning enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Mark.

**Works with.** Cinder, Kindling Point, Clear Sky.

**Example.** End each chain on a burning soldier and the point gathers.

## kindlingpoint.noonguard
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a Marked, burning enemy sets it alight at the point at once.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy carrying both a Mark and Burn.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. All its Burn is spent: 10 damage for each, and it burns on two deep.

**Works with.** Kindling Point, The Sun Points, Kiln Stance.

**Example.** A marked, burning brute swings: guard it perfectly and it goes up in flame.

## kindlingpoint.clearsky
**What it does.** A Mark lasts one blow longer on a burning enemy.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Light and Fire are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** The Sun Points, which it grows from, Kindling Point.

**Example.** One more critical before the Mark is spent.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet.

## kindlingpoint.feast
**What it does.** When a Kindling Point burst kills, every burn within 8 metres deepens by one and you gain mana.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy carrying both a Mark and Burn dies, while Light and Fire are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. One Burn on everything within 8 metres, and 10 mana, at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Kindling Point, Hands of the Sun, Daybreak and Meteor Leap to spend the mana.

**Example.** Burn down a marked pack and every death feeds the fire.

**Not built yet.** It answers any marked, burning enemy that dies, not only one killed by the burst.

## kindlingpoint.hands
**What it does.** A burning sun rises over you for a breath, and long rays reach down to the four nearest enemies. Each ray ends in a hand of flame that grips: held, burned two deep and Marked, so every one of them carries a Kindling Point.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Light and Fire are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Light.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to four enemies within 9 metres: 6 damage, 10 stagger, a Mark and two Burn, held 1 second.

**Works with.** Kindling Point and its shapes, The Feast, The Sun Points.

**Example.** Open on a pack: four points gathering at once.

**Not built yet.** The rays leap from one enemy to the next rather than falling from one sun, and the hand does not yet let go at your first blow with that blow critical.
