# Bloom: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Bloom, the duo branch Water and Life share. Reshaped in Phase 4
(`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Water and Life") and drawn in the heart of the chart while Water and Life are both
carried. Its reaction is Quickening (which does what Flowering did, with no wait); its Shape mark lets you choose what
Quickening does.

## bloom.shape
**What it does.** Opens Quickening's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Quickening wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Floating Flower**: the flower drops to the ground under the enemy as a lily that mends you and soaks what stands on it. **Rootlets**: as the flower opens, rootlets run through the wet and seed every enemy near to full; a heavy blow still bursts the flower for a heal.

**When it happens.** Every time Quickening fires (Seeded landing on a soaked enemy, or Soaked on a seeded one) while Water and Life are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Quickening runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both seed the enemy to full. Floating Flower: you mend 8 as it opens; the lily is 2 metres across for 5 seconds and soaks whatever stands on it once a second. Rootlets: every enemy within 5 metres is seeded to full; a heavy blow on the flowering one within 4 seconds mends you 10. Quickening rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Riptide and Briar Patch to bring the water and the seed, Drowned Roots, Strangling Vine, Seeded's own burst of thorns when a seeded enemy dies.

**Example.** A wet pack with one seeded soldier: Rootlets seeds them all to full, and each that dies bursts into thorns.

**Not built yet.** The lily mends you once as it opens rather than 3 a second while you stand on it, and the rootlets reach every enemy within 5 metres, not only the soaked.

## bloom.roots
**What it does.** A perfect guard against an enemy that is both Soaked and Seeded binds it in roots, held where it stands for a moment.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy carrying both Soaked and Seeded.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Held still for 1.2 seconds.

**Works with.** Riptide and Briar Patch (one affliction each), Quickening's shapes, Great Lotus, every Water and Life mark that answers a perfect guard.

**Example.** Soak and seed a duellist, then guard its next thrust perfectly: it stands rooted while you land two cuts.

## bloom.dew
**What it does.** Drinking a flask gives you a ward that turns aside one blow. And your blows throw a short blade of water toward the enemy struck that soaks what it passes, now and then. As built the dew does not yet gather in your health bar.

**When it happens.** The ward with every flask you drink; the blade of water with a weapon blow that lands, at most once every 0.8 seconds.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One ward a flask; you hold three at most. The blade flies 3.5 metres at 14 metres a second, 0.8 metres wide: 4 damage, no stagger, and Soaked.

**Works with.** Wellspring, Deep Well, Drowned Roots.

**Example.** Drink before the brute's red overhead: the ward takes it, and your answering cuts soak it.

## bloom.vine
**What it does.** A great vine bursts up under the enemy nearest you. It strikes hard and throws that enemy and anything beside it into the air for a moment. If the one it lifts is soaked and seeded, the vine drinks the water (the Soaked is spent) and the seed flowers at once: Quickening, whatever its rest. As built the vine rises under the nearest enemy, not the nearest soaked one, and the twice-as-great heal from a heavy blow on it while it is lifted is still to come.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It marks the spot under the enemy and follows it until the vine rises.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards, 45 mana. The nearest enemy within 5 metres, or 5 metres ahead with none. It rises 0.5 seconds after the cast, 3 metres in radius: 20 damage and 50 stagger, a heavy blow, and Soaked. What it strikes is thrown up for 0.8 seconds. If it lands, it charges your blade with Water.

**Works with.** Quickening (a soaked and seeded enemy it lifts flowers at once, whatever its rest), Drowned Roots or Morning Dew (it grows from either), Riptide.

**Example.** A brute and two soldiers close in: the vine throws all three up, and you cut the brute as it comes down.

## bloom.great
**What it does.** A flask drunk while your surge burns opens a great lotus under you. Inside it you mend, and enemies are Soaked and Seeded. Each perfect guard you make inside closes a petal on the attacker and holds it. When the last petal closes, or its time is done, the lotus bursts, harder for every petal closed.

**When it happens.** Choose it for your surge slot in the Hand at a rest fire, while Water and Life are both carried, and light it with the surge button. Drink a flask while it burns to open the lotus.

**The numbers.** 40 mana, 10 seconds. 5 Shards, bought once both elements are carried; no god gives it. The lotus is 3 metres in radius and lasts 10 seconds. Each second, Soaked and a stack of Seeded on every enemy in it, and 2 health to you while you stand in it. Five petals; each holds its enemy 3 seconds. The burst is about 5 metres in radius: 30 damage and 30% more for each petal closed (75 at five), 40 stagger and a stack of Seeded.

**Works with.** Wellspring (the flask heals half again as much), Morning Dew (the flask's ward), Drowned Roots.

**Example.** Light Wellspring, drink in the middle of a pack, then perfect-guard five blows in the lotus: it bursts for 75 on everyone held.
