# Bloom: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Bloom, the branch Water and Life share.

## bloom.wetseed
**What it does.** Meant to make your blows lay Soaked and Seeded together. As built, a weapon blow lays only your first element's affliction, so Wet Seed lays just that one: Soaked with Water first, Seeded with Life first. If you have already learned that element's root mark, it adds nothing to your blows. The other affliction comes from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike.

**When it happens.** Every weapon blow that lands, while you carry both Water and Life.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One stack of your first element's affliction a blow. Soaked lasts 8 seconds; Seeded holds up to three stacks for 8 seconds, and each of your weapon blows on it heals you 1 a stack.

**Works with.** Flowering and Drowned Roots (they need Seeded, and Soaked as well), Riptide and Briar Patch to lay the other one, Strangling Vine, Great Lotus.

**Example.** Carrying Life first without Seeded learned, every blow seeds; Riptide then soaks the same enemies for Drowned Roots.

## bloom.flower
**What it does.** A heavy blow on a Seeded enemy bursts its seed: you are mended, and everyone near it takes a burst of thorns and is Seeded. With Water as your first element, they are also Soaked. It can happen once every seven seconds. As built no flower is shown and the burst comes at once.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy carrying Seeded, at most once every 7 seconds.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. You mend 12. Everything within 3.5 metres of the one struck takes 12 damage, no stagger, and a stack of Seeded. Soaked on everything within 3.5 metres too, but only while Water is your first element. The enemy's own Seeded stays.

**Works with.** Wet Seed, Briar Patch, Seeded's own burst of thorns when a seeded enemy dies, Drowned Roots, Strangling Vine.

**Example.** Seed a soldier in a crowd and land a heavy blow: you mend 12 and the soldiers round it are seeded for your next cuts.

## bloom.roots
**What it does.** A perfect guard against an enemy that is both Soaked and Seeded binds it in roots, held where it stands for a moment.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy carrying both Soaked and Seeded.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Held still for 1.2 seconds.

**Works with.** Wet Seed, Riptide and Briar Patch (one affliction each), Flowering, Lotus Step, Great Lotus, every Water and Life mark that answers a perfect guard.

**Example.** Soak and seed a duellist, then guard its next thrust perfectly: it stands rooted while you land two cuts.

## bloom.dew
**What it does.** Drinking a flask gives you a ward that turns aside one blow. And your blows throw a short blade of water toward the enemy struck that soaks what it passes, now and then. As built the dew does not yet gather in your health bar.

**When it happens.** The ward with every flask you drink; the blade of water with a weapon blow that lands, at most once every 0.8 seconds.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards. One ward a flask; you hold three at most. The blade flies 3.5 metres at 14 metres a second, 0.8 metres wide: 4 damage, no stagger, and Soaked.

**Works with.** Spilled Water, Wellspring, Clear Water, Deep Well, Catch the Rain, Drowned Roots.

**Example.** Drink before the brute's red overhead: the ward takes it, and your answering cuts soak it.

## bloom.lotus
**What it does.** You open a lotus on the ground just ahead of you. For twelve seconds it cuts every enemy standing on it and keeps it Soaked. As built it is one patch; the lotus left by each dodge, and its mending, are still to come.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It opens under an enemy within 2 metres, or 2 metres ahead.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. 1.5 metres in radius, for 12 seconds. It bites once a second for 10 damage, with no stagger, and lays Soaked. If it lands, it charges your blade with Water.

**Works with.** Drowned Roots (hold them on it), Wet Seed, Undertow (pull them onto it), Heavy Water, Great Lotus.

**Example.** Open it at your feet in a doorway and hold the doorway: every enemy that reaches you is bitten and Soaked.

## bloom.vine
**What it does.** A great vine bursts up under the enemy nearest you. It strikes hard and throws that enemy and anything beside it into the air for a moment. As built the three-second hold and the greater mending are still to come.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It marks the spot under the enemy and follows it until the vine rises.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards, 45 mana. The nearest enemy within 5 metres, or 5 metres ahead with none. It rises 0.5 seconds after the cast, 3 metres in radius: 20 damage and 50 stagger, a heavy blow, and Soaked. What it strikes is thrown up for 0.8 seconds. If it lands, it charges your blade with Water.

**Works with.** Spring Tide (it is paid in mana, so it grows), Flowering, Heavy Water, Undertow, Wet Seed.

**Example.** A brute and two soldiers close in: the vine throws all three up, and you cut the brute as it comes down.

## bloom.great
**What it does.** A flask drunk while your surge burns opens a great lotus under you. Inside it you mend, and enemies are Soaked and Seeded. Each perfect guard you make inside closes a petal on the attacker and holds it. When the last petal closes, or its time is done, the lotus bursts, harder for every petal closed.

**When it happens.** Drink a flask while Wellspring or Regrowth burns.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. The lotus is 3 metres in radius and lasts 10 seconds. Each second, Soaked and a stack of Seeded on every enemy in it, and 2 health to you while you stand in it. Five petals; each holds its enemy 3 seconds. The burst is about 5 metres in radius: 30 damage and 30% more for each petal closed (75 at five), 40 stagger and a stack of Seeded.

**Works with.** Wellspring (the flask heals half again as much), Regrowth, Morning Dew (the flask's ward), Drowned Roots, Spilled Water, Catch the Rain.

**Example.** Light Wellspring, drink in the middle of a pack, then perfect-guard five blows in the lotus: it bursts for 75 on everyone held.
