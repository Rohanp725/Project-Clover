# Thermal Shock: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Fire and Ice share, named for its reaction,
Thermal Shock (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d3.md`, "Fire and Ice"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Fire and Ice are
both carried.

## thermalshock.shape
**What it does.** Unlocks two new shapes for Thermal Shock. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Thermal Shock uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Hot Shards**: when the enemy splits, it throws shards of hot stone and ice that make the crowd crack too. **Crazing**: the crack doesn't wait for a heavy blow. While the enemy has both afflictions, it splits again on its own.

**When it happens.** Every time Thermal Shock goes off while you carry both Fire and Ice. Thermal Shock goes off when Chill lands on a burning enemy, or when Burn lands on a chilled one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Thermal Shock.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both shapes crack the enemy's body for 14 damage. Hot Shards: your next heavy blow within 5 seconds splits it for 30 damage and 40 stagger, and shards hit everything within 3 metres for 8 damage, a Burn and a Chill. This is on purpose: the shards may crack those enemies too. Crazing: while the enemy has both afflictions, it splits every 1.5 seconds for 10 damage and 12 stagger. After Thermal Shock goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 6 seconds.

**Works with.** Spark and Rime, Fire-Setting, and Flame Wave and Hoarfrost to set it up.

**Example.** A crowd: use Hot Shards, and one heavy blow cracks the pack. A brute: use Crazing, and keep both afflictions on it.

## thermalshock.sparkrime
**What it does.** Heavy blows add one Burn and one Chill.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, while you carry both Fire and Ice.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One stack of each. For your first element's affliction, it adds nothing beyond what that element's root mark already adds.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock (one heavy blow starts it), Neither Wins, Fire Under the Ice.

**Example.** Every heavy blow on a fresh enemy cracks it.

## thermalshock.hotstone
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a cracked enemy splits it right away.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against a Cracked enemy, in any shape of Thermal Shock.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The crack splits just as a heavy blow would split it: 30 damage and 40 stagger, and with Hot Shards the shards fly too. The crack is used up. With Crazing, the crack splits once more right away: 10 damage and 12 stagger.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock, Kiln Stance, Spark and Rime.

**Example.** A cracked brute swings. Guard it perfectly, and it splits without your heavy blow.

## thermalshock.neitherwins
**What it does.** On an enemy that has both, neither the Burn nor the Chill fades.

**When it happens.** Always on once you learn it, while you carry both Fire and Ice.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor mark, 1 Shard. Each one is kept while the other is there. Once one of them is gone, the other starts to fade as usual.

**Works with.** Crazing (both afflictions must last), Spark and Rime, which it comes after on the chart.

**Example.** A burning, chilled enemy stays both for the whole fight.

## thermalshock.underice
**What it does.** A Frozen enemy that is burning shatters on any blow, not only a heavy one, and its shatter sets everything within 3 metres on fire with two Burns.

**When it happens.** Each time a burning enemy shatters, while you carry both Fire and Ice.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Two Burns on everything within 3 metres, at most five times a second.

**Works with.** Hoarfrost and Winter Wall (to freeze enemies), Spark and Rime, Thermal Shock.

**Example.** Freeze a burning soldier in a pack and shatter it. The pack catches fire.

**Not built yet.** The shatter still needs a heavy blow for now. The spreading fire is built.

## thermalshock.firesetting
**What it does.** A fire is lit against the enemy you face. When it is put out with a splash of frost, the heated body splits: a hard hit and three Chill, and the enemies next to it take half.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Fire and Ice. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Fire.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 12 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. It reaches an enemy within 6 metres. Putting it out deals 20 damage, 60 stagger, two Burns and three Chill within 2.5 metres.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock and its shapes, Spark and Rime.

**Example.** Set it on a brute, hit the brute, and putting out the fire splits it.

**Not built yet.** A second press doesn't put it out yet; for now, your weapon blow sets it off, like a sign on the enemy. Its Burn doesn't grow while the fire waits, and blows before you put it out don't make it stronger yet.

## thermalshock.gap
**What it does.** A duo surge. While it is lit, your weapon blows take turns burning and chilling, so Thermal Shock keeps going off, and each split drops melted water that heals you.

**When it happens.** At a rest fire, pick it for your surge slot in the Hand while you carry both Fire and Ice. Light it with the surge button.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. 40 mana, lasts 10 seconds. For now: each blow adds a Chill, and each Thermal Shock heals you 2.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock, Spark and Rime, Cinder.

**Example.** Light your surge in a crowd, and every second blow cracks something.

**Not built yet.** Your blows don't really take turns yet; for now, each blow adds a Chill. Thermal Shock still has to wait 6 seconds on each enemy, instead of 2.
