# Thermal Shock: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Fire and Ice share, named for its reaction,
Thermal Shock (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d3.md`, "Fire and Ice"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Fire and Ice are
both carried.

## thermalshock.shape
**What it does.** Opens Thermal Shock's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Thermal Shock wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Hot Shards**: the split throws shards of hot stone and ice that set the crowd cracking too. **Crazing**: the crack does not wait for a heavy blow; while the enemy carries both afflictions it splits again by itself.

**When it happens.** Every time Thermal Shock fires (Chill landing on a burning enemy, or Burn on a chilled one) while Fire and Ice are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Thermal Shock runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both crack the body for 14. Hot Shards: the next heavy blow within 5 seconds splits it, 30 damage and 40 stagger, and shards strike everything within 3 metres for 8, a Burn and a Chill; on purpose these may crack them too. Crazing: while it carries both, it splits every 1.5 seconds for 10 damage and 12 stagger. Thermal Shock rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Spark and Rime, Fire-Setting, Flame Wave and Hoarfrost to bring the two.

**Example.** A crowd: Hot Shards and one heavy blow cracks the pack. A brute: Crazing, and keep both afflictions on it.

## thermalshock.sparkrime
**What it does.** Heavy blows lay one Burn and one Chill.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, while Fire and Ice are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One stack of each; of your first element's, none beyond its root's.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock (one heavy blow starts it), Neither Wins, Fire Under the Ice.

**Example.** Every heavy blow on a fresh enemy cracks it.

## thermalshock.hotstone
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a Cracked enemy splits it at once.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy carrying both Burn and Chill.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 30 damage and 40 stagger.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock, Kiln Stance, Spark and Rime.

**Example.** A cracked brute swings: guard it perfectly and it splits without your heavy blow.

**Not built yet.** It answers any attacker carrying both afflictions, not only a Cracked one.

## thermalshock.neitherwins
**What it does.** On an enemy that carries both, neither the Burn nor the Chill fades.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Fire and Ice are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** Crazing (both must last), Spark and Rime, which it grows from.

**Example.** A burning, chilled enemy stays both for as long as the fight lasts.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: an affliction kept while another lasts is still to come.

## thermalshock.underice
**What it does.** A Frozen enemy that is burning shatters on any blow, not only a heavy, and its shatter sets everything within 3 metres burning two deep.

**When it happens.** Each time a burning enemy shatters, while Fire and Ice are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Two Burn on everything within 3 metres, at most five times a second.

**Works with.** Hoarfrost and Winter Wall (to freeze), Spark and Rime, Thermal Shock.

**Example.** Freeze a burning soldier in a pack and shatter it: the pack catches.

**Not built yet.** The shatter still needs a heavy blow; the burning spread is built.

## thermalshock.firesetting
**What it does.** A fire is laid against the enemy you face. When it is quenched with a dash of rime, the heated body splits: a hard blow, Chill three deep, and its neighbours take half.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Fire and Ice are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Fire.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Within 6 metres; the quench: 20 damage, 60 stagger, two Burn and three Chill within 2.5 metres.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock and its shapes, Spark and Rime.

**Example.** Set it on a brute, hit it, and the quench splits it.

**Not built yet.** A second press does not quench it yet (your weapon blow sets it off, as a brand); its Burn does not deepen while the fire is banked, and blows before the quench do not make it harder.

## thermalshock.gap
**What it does.** A duo surge. While it burns your weapon blows take turns, burning and chilling, so Thermal Shock keeps setting off, and each split lets fall drops of melt that heal you.

**When it happens.** Choose it for your surge slot in the Hand at a rest fire, while Fire and Ice are both carried, and light it with the surge button.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. 40 mana, 10 seconds. As built: each blow lays a Chill; each Thermal Shock heals you 2.

**Works with.** Thermal Shock, Spark and Rime, Cinder.

**Example.** Light your surge in a crowd and every second blow cracks something.

**Not built yet.** Your blows do not truly take turns yet: each blow lays a Chill. Thermal Shock still rests 6 seconds rather than 2.
