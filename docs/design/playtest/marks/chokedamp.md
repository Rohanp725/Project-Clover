# Chokedamp: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Earth and Poison share, named for its reaction,
Chokedamp (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Earth and Poison"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Earth and Poison
are both carried.

## chokedamp.shape
**What it does.** Damp or Ore: opens Chokedamp's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Chokedamp wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Blackdamp**: the damp does not run to the others but lies still and deep under the one, a place to hold. **Red Ore**: no mist; the poison sets in the cracks as veins of red ore, and your next heavy blow on it bursts them.

**When it happens.** Every time Chokedamp fires (a dose landing on a shaken enemy, or Tremor on a poisoned one) while Earth and Poison are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Chokedamp runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Blackdamp: a pool 3.5 metres wide for 7 seconds that doses once a second all who stand in it; those in it as it pools move a fifth slower for the 7 seconds. Red Ore: for 4 seconds your next heavy blow on it bursts the ore in 3 metres, 14 damage and 3 more for each dose it carries, 30 stagger and three doses to each enemy reached. Chokedamp rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Foul Ground to bring the two, The Lamp Goes Out, Seam of Ore, The Clay Rank.

**Example.** A doorway they must come through: Blackdamp. A deep-dosed brute in a crowd: Red Ore, and heavy it.

**Not built yet.** Blackdamp's pool doses whoever stands in it, but slows only those in it as it forms.

## chokedamp.foulground
**What it does.** Your heavy blows lay a Tremor and a dose together.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, while Earth and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Tremor and one dose.

**Works with.** Chokedamp (one heavy blow on a fresh enemy brings both), The Lamp Goes Out.

**Example.** Heavy into the pack and the ground breathes out under them.

## chokedamp.lamp
**What it does.** Your weapon breaks the balance of enemies standing in the damp a quarter harder.

**When it happens.** For 5 seconds after each Chokedamp, on the enemy it fired on and the shaken enemies near it, while Earth and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A quarter more stagger on the enemy that reacted and up to three shaken enemies within 7 metres, for 5 seconds.

**Works with.** Chokedamp, Foul Ground, Seam of Ore.

**Example.** Damp a pack and break them one after another.

**Not built yet.** It keeps its own 5 seconds from each Chokedamp rather than reading who stands in the damp, and it raises every stagger they take, a spell's too, not only your weapon's.

## chokedamp.seam
**What it does.** Each time poison bites a shaken enemy, the ground shakes under it: a Tremor to every enemy within 2 m.

**When it happens.** Each bite of poison on an enemy carrying Tremor, while Earth and Poison are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. One Tremor on everything within 2 metres of it, at most four times a second; those it shakes that are poisoned may start Chokedamp.

**Works with.** Foul Ground, Chokedamp, The Lamp Goes Out.

**Example.** One shaken, poisoned soldier shakes the ground for all its neighbours with every bite.

## chokedamp.clayrank
**What it does.** Three clay soldiers rise in a rank before you, grey men with cracked faces leaking damp, and go at the enemy for 6 seconds. Each strike shakes and doses what it hits.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Earth and Poison are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 14 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Three soldiers for 6 seconds, each striking about every 1.2 seconds for 6 damage, 10 stagger, a Tremor and a dose, so Chokedamp starts under what they strike.

**Works with.** Chokedamp and its shapes, Seam of Ore, Foul Ground.

**Example.** Send the rank into the pack and follow it in.

**Not built yet.** The soldiers do not yet march in a rank 7 metres, push, take the enemies' blows and break after two, nor burst in damp and clay (12, a Tremor and three doses in 2.5 metres, twice as hard broken by your heavy blow): they run at the enemies and strike them.
