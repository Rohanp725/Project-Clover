# Stolen Warmth: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Dark and Ice share, named for its reaction,
Stolen Warmth (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Dark and Ice"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Dark and Ice
are both carried.

## stolenwarmth.shape
**What it does.** Let the Cold In: opens Stolen Warmth's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Stolen Warmth wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Cold Shade**: the warmth is drawn into you as before, and a shattered enemy leaves its shadow behind as a figure of black ice that chills all near. **The Long Night**: no health is drawn; the warmth goes to the dark, and while it is frozen the cold spreads to everything near it.

**When it happens.** Every time Stolen Warmth fires (a Curse landing on a chilled enemy, or Chill on a Cursed one) while Dark and Ice are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Stolen Warmth runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both: while it carries both its Chill does not fade, and each of your weapon blows on it lays a Chill. Cold Shade: each blow also heals you 2 (a god 1), at most four times a second; when it dies a patch 5 metres across stays 4 seconds where it fell, chilling once a second what stands in it. The Long Night: no healing; while it is frozen, once a second everything within 3 metres takes a Chill. Stolen Warmth rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Hel's Half, Deep Veins, No Summer, Ice in the Blood, Three Winters.

**Example.** Fighting alone and hurt: Cold Shade, and heal as you strike. A pack round a big enemy: The Long Night, freeze the big one and let the cold take the rest.

**Not built yet.** Cold Shade's shade is left when the enemy dies rather than when it is shattered, and is a chilling patch: it cannot yet be struck by a heavy blow to shatter it a second time.

## stolenwarmth.helshalf
**What it does.** Killing an enemy whose warmth is stolen Curses every enemy within 4 metres and gives each two Chill.

**When it happens.** Each kill, while Dark and Ice are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. As built: any enemy that dies both Cursed and chilled lays a Curse and two Chill on everything within 4 metres, at most every half second; laid by the mark, they start Stolen Warmth on each.

**Works with.** Stolen Warmth (it spreads from body to body), Three Winters, No Summer.

**Example.** Kill one in the pack and the cold walks into the rest.

**Not built yet.** It answers any enemy that dies Cursed and chilled, whether its warmth was being stolen or not.

## stolenwarmth.deepveins
**What it does.** Each blow on an enemy whose warmth is stolen draws 3 health, not 2, and 12 a second at most.

**When it happens.** Each weapon blow on such an enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** Stolen Warmth and Cold Shade, Hel's Half.

**Example.** A long fight with one brute keeps you whole.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: a mark that changes the reaction's own numbers is still to come.

## stolenwarmth.nosummer
**What it does.** Hunger costs no health while any enemy within 8 metres is Frozen.

**When it happens.** While you carry Dark's Hunger as your surge, with Dark and Ice both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: for 4 seconds after each shatter, prices paid in health are set aside.

**Works with.** Stolen Warmth (its Chill does not fade, so freezing comes easily), The Long Night, Three Winters, Dark's Hunger.

**Example.** Keep one enemy frozen near you and feed Hunger for nothing.

**Not built yet.** It does not yet read whether an enemy near you is Frozen: the price is set aside for 4 seconds after each shatter, and for every price paid in health, not only Hunger's.

## stolenwarmth.iceinblood
**What it does.** A sign of black frost is set on the enemy you face for 6 seconds. Each of your next four weapon blows on it freezes a vein, 8 and two Chill, and the fourth breaks it from within: 35, 50 stagger, and a Curse and two Chill on everything within 3 metres.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Dark and Ice are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Dark.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 8 metres; set off by your weapon blows, 35 damage, 50 stagger, a Curse and two Chill to everything within 3 metres.

**Works with.** Stolen Warmth (all it strikes carries both), Hel's Half, Three Winters.

**Example.** Sign the captain, four blows, and its guard breaks in frost.

**Not built yet.** The veins are not yet frozen one by one: the brand's own set-off by your blows stands in, and it does not wait for the fourth.

## stolenwarmth.threewinters
**What it does.** A duo surge. While your surge lasts frost creeps over the floor round you, and every enemy that dies freezes where it falls as a statue of black ice for 5 seconds, chilling a stack a second within 2.5 metres; a heavy blow on a statue shatters it, 25, two Chill and a Curse in 3 metres.

**When it happens.** While your surge lasts, with Dark and Ice both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. As built: each enemy that dies leaves a patch 5 metres across for 5 seconds, chilling once a second what stands in it.

**Works with.** Stolen Warmth, Hel's Half, No Summer, Ice in the Blood.

**Example.** Light your surge in a crowd and each death freezes the floor round it.

**Not built yet.** It is not yet a surge of its own (40 mana, 10 seconds) in the Hand's surge slot; it rides whatever surge you light. The frost on the floor is not drawn, and no statue stands to be shattered: each death leaves a chilling patch.
