# Eclipse: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Eclipse, the duo branch Light and Dark share. Reshaped in
Phase 4 (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Light and Dark") and drawn in the heart of the chart while Light and Dark
are both carried. Its reaction is Diamond Ring; its Shape mark lets you choose what Diamond Ring does.

## eclipse.shape
**What it does.** Opens Diamond Ring's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Diamond Ring wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Shadow Bands**: the bead of light breaks as before, and bands of shadow ripple out across the floor and Curse every enemy they cross. **Through the Throat**: the light slips out of its far side as a line that cuts and Marks all on it.

**When it happens.** Every time Diamond Ring fires (Cursed landing on a marked enemy, or Marked on a cursed one) while Light and Dark are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Diamond Ring runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Shadow Bands: the bead, 16 damage and 30 stagger, half a second on, and a Curse on everything within 5 metres. Through the Throat: a line 6 metres long beyond it, 12 damage and a Mark on everything on it. Both keep its Mark from being spent by blows for 4 seconds. Diamond Ring rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Dusk and Dawn (its cut sets Diamond Ring off whatever the rest), Grasp and Glare to bring the two, The Dividing Line.

**Example.** A pack round a marked soldier: Shadow Bands curses them all. A line of them: stand at its end and Through the Throat marks the whole line.

## eclipse.daynight
**What it does.** A perfect dodge heals you a little. For a while after a perfect dodge, a perfect guard gives double mana; for a while after a perfect guard, a perfect dodge gives double mana. Weaving the two pays twice.

**When it happens.** Every perfect dodge heals. Each perfect dodge opens a time in which perfect guards pay double; each perfect guard opens a time in which perfect dodges pay double.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 5 health for each perfect dodge. Each window lasts 6 seconds. A perfect guard gives 30 mana in it, where it gives 15; a perfect dodge 24, where it gives 12. As built the dodge heals always, not only after a guard.

**Works with.** Clarity (perfect moments half again as long), Glare's Master (a perfect dodge that holds and Marks), Low Sun, Daybreak and the deep strike to spend it.

**Example.** Dodge a duellist's lunge at the last moment, then perfect-guard its next cut: 5 health and 42 mana from two blows.

## eclipse.duskdawn
**What it does.** You step through the enemy you face and cut it in passing, coming out on its far side turned back toward it. If the cut lands, your next heavy blow in the moment after is critical. Nothing can strike you in the step; a wall, or something too big to shove, stops you short.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). As built it is the step and its critical heavy blow; the stored cut of light that should land with that heavy blow is still to come.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, 5 Shards, 45 mana. Up to 5 metres: to 1.8 metres past the enemy faced, or about 4 metres ahead with none. The cut is 10 damage, 10 stagger and Marked on all on the line. The heavy blow after it is critical if it lands within 3 seconds.

**Works with.** Diamond Ring (on a Marked and Cursed enemy its cut sets it off at once, whatever the rest), Light's root, Reckoning's Spoken Sentence, Weighed, and the charged blade, which it charges with Light.

**Example.** Step through a captain's guard, turn, and land a critical heavy blow in its back.

## eclipse.lowsun
**What it does.** Your weapon blows give more mana. Each time your health falls below a third, they give far more for a while.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned; the greater share comes each time your health drops below a third.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Weapon blows give 20% more mana. Falling below a third adds 60% more for 12 seconds, 80% in all. As built it does not yet rise steadily as your health falls.

**Works with.** The Dividing Line, Day and Night, Dark's marks that are paid in health, Brimming (mana kept full has a use), and the mana spells it pays for: Dusk and Dawn, Daybreak.

**Example.** A brute's blow leaves you at a quarter of your health: for twelve seconds every cut fills your mana nearly twice as fast, enough for Dusk and Dawn and a way out.

## eclipse.dividingline
**What it does.** Your health sets how you fight. At half your health or more, your critical blows strike harder and your blows give more mana. Below half, every blow Curses and feeds you. Each time your health falls below a third, a burst round you Curses everything near.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The day half holds at half health or more; the night half below half; the burst each time your health drops below a third.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. At half or more: critical blows deal 1.7 times their damage, where they deal 1.5, and blows give 20% more mana. Below half: each weapon blow Curses and heals you 8% of its damage. Below a third: a burst 3.5 metres round you, 16 damage, 25 stagger and Cursed, at most once every 4 seconds. As built the burst does not come when you cross half the other way.

**Works with.** Day and Night or Low Sun (it grows from either), Light's root and Unblemished on the day side, Dark's Cursed kills on the night side, Totality.

**Example.** A brute drives you under half: each cut now curses and heals you, and when the next blow drops you below a third the burst curses the pack round you.

## eclipse.totality
**What it does.** When your surge begins, the sun is covered. While the dark lasts every enemy near you is kept Marked and Cursed, and every weapon blow you land is critical and heals you. What each enemy loses to your weapon in the dark is remembered. When the surge ends the light returns, and a column of it strikes every enemy you wounded for a share of what it lost.

**When it happens.** Light your surge with the surge button (either element's surge you wear); the light returns when it ends.

**The numbers.** 5 Shards, bought once both elements are carried; no god gives it. Each second, Marked and Cursed on every enemy within 14 metres. Every weapon blow critical; each heals 1, at most five times a second. The returning column deals half of what that enemy lost to your weapon, 80 at most, with 30 stagger.

**Works with.** Clarity (eight seconds of dark that also marks with every blow) or Hunger (feeding you while it drains), Diamond Ring's shapes, Weighed, Reckoning's Spoken Sentence, The Dividing Line.

**Example.** Light the surge among four soldiers, cut each of them hard while the dark lasts, and watch the light come back down on all four as it ends.
