# Eclipse: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Eclipse, the duo branch Light and Dark share. Reshaped in
Phase 4 (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Light and Dark") and drawn in the heart of the chart while Light and Dark
are both carried. Its reaction is Diamond Ring; its Shape mark lets you choose what Diamond Ring does.

## eclipse.shape
**What it does.** Unlocks two new shapes for Diamond Ring. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Diamond Ring uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Shadow Bands**: the bead of light breaks like before, and bands of shadow ripple out across the floor, Cursing every enemy they cross. **Through the Throat**: the light slips out of the enemy's far side as a line that cuts and Marks everything on it.

**When it happens.** Every time Diamond Ring goes off while you carry both Light and Dark. Diamond Ring goes off when a Curse lands on a Marked enemy, or when a Mark lands on a Cursed one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Diamond Ring.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Shadow Bands: half a second later, the bead deals 16 damage and 30 stagger, and puts a Curse on everything within 5 metres. Through the Throat: a line 6 metres long past the enemy deals 12 damage and a Mark to everything on it. With both shapes, your blows don't use up the enemy's Mark for 4 seconds. After Diamond Ring goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Dusk and Dawn (its cut sets off Diamond Ring even if it would normally have to wait), Grasp and Glare to set it up, The Dividing Line.

**Example.** A pack around a Marked soldier: Shadow Bands Curses them all. A line of enemies: stand at one end, and Through the Throat Marks the whole line.

## eclipse.daynight
**What it does.** A perfect dodge heals you a little. For a while after a perfect dodge, a perfect guard gives double mana. For a while after a perfect guard, a perfect dodge gives double mana. Switching between the two pays double.

**When it happens.** Every perfect dodge heals you. Each perfect dodge starts a window when perfect guards give double mana. Each perfect guard starts a window when perfect dodges give double mana.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. You heal 5 for each perfect dodge. Each window lasts 6 seconds. During it, a perfect guard gives 30 mana instead of 15, and a perfect dodge gives 24 instead of 12. For now, the dodge always heals you, not only after a guard.

**Works with.** Clarity (perfect timing windows last 50% longer), Glare's Master mark (a perfect dodge that holds and Marks), Low Sun, and Daybreak and the deep strike to spend the mana.

**Example.** Dodge a duellist's lunge at the last moment, then perfect-guard its next cut. You get 5 health and 42 mana from two blows.

## eclipse.duskdawn
**What it does.** You step through the enemy you face and cut it as you pass, coming out on its far side turned back toward it. If the cut lands, your next heavy blow right after is a critical hit. Nothing can hit you during the step. A wall, or an enemy too big to push past, stops you short.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). For now, it is the step and its critical heavy blow. The saved-up cut of light that should land with that heavy blow is not built yet.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, 5 Shards, 45 mana. It goes up to 5 metres: to 1.8 metres past the enemy you face, or about 4 metres ahead if there is none. The cut deals 10 damage, 10 stagger and a Mark to everything on the line. The heavy blow after it is a critical hit if it lands within 3 seconds.

**Works with.** Diamond Ring (on an enemy that is Marked or Cursed, even with only one of the two, its cut sets off Diamond Ring right away, even if it would normally have to wait), Light's root mark, Reckoning's Spoken Sentence shape, Weighed, and the charged blade, which it charges with Light.

**Example.** Step through a captain's guard, turn around, and land a critical heavy blow on its back.

## eclipse.lowsun
**What it does.** Your weapon blows give more mana. Each time your health drops below a third, they give much more for a while.

**When it happens.** Always on once you learn it. The bigger bonus comes each time your health drops below a third.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Weapon blows give 20% more mana. Dropping below a third adds 60% more for 12 seconds, for 80% in total. For now, it doesn't yet rise smoothly as your health drops.

**Works with.** The Dividing Line, Day and Night, Dark's marks that you pay for with health, Brimming (gives a use for keeping your mana full), and the mana spells it pays for: Dusk and Dawn, Daybreak.

**Example.** A brute's blow leaves you at a quarter of your health. For twelve seconds, every cut fills your mana almost twice as fast, enough for Dusk and Dawn and a way out.

## eclipse.dividingline
**What it does.** Your health decides how you fight. At half your health or more, your critical hits hit harder and your blows give more mana. Below half, every blow Curses and heals you. Each time your health drops below a third, a burst around you Curses everything nearby.

**When it happens.** Always on once you learn it. The day side works at half health or more. The night side works below half. The burst comes each time your health drops below a third.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. At half health or more: critical hits deal 1.7 times their damage instead of 1.5, and blows give 20% more mana. Below half: each weapon blow Curses and heals you 8% of its damage. Below a third: a burst with a 3.5-metre radius around you deals 16 damage, 25 stagger and a Curse, at most once every 4 seconds. For now, the burst doesn't come when your health goes back up past half.

**Works with.** Day and Night or Low Sun (it comes after either on the chart), Light's root mark and Unblemished on the day side, Dark's Cursed kills on the night side, Totality.

**Example.** A brute knocks you below half. Each cut now Curses and heals you. When the next blow drops you below a third, the burst Curses the pack around you.

## eclipse.totality
**What it does.** When your surge starts, the sun is covered. While it is dark, every enemy near you stays Marked and Cursed, and every weapon blow you land is a critical hit and heals you. The game remembers how much damage your weapon did to each enemy in the dark. When the surge ends, the light comes back, and a column of light hits every enemy you hurt for a share of that damage.

**When it happens.** Light your surge with the surge button (whichever element's surge you have). The light comes back when it ends.

**The numbers.** 5 Shards. You can buy it once you carry both elements; no god gives it. Every second, every enemy within 14 metres is Marked and Cursed. Every weapon blow is a critical hit and heals you 1, at most five times a second. The column of light deals half the damage your weapon did to that enemy, up to 80, plus 30 stagger.

**Works with.** Clarity (eight seconds of dark, and every blow also Marks) or Hunger (it heals you while it drains), Diamond Ring's shapes, Weighed, Reckoning's Spoken Sentence shape, The Dividing Line.

**Example.** Light the surge among four soldiers. Cut each of them hard while it is dark, and watch the light come back down on all four when it ends.
