# Logan Stone: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Wind and Earth share, named for its reaction,
Logan Stone (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Wind and Earth"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Wind and Earth
are both carried.

## loganstone.shape
**What it does.** Grit or Drift unlocks two new shapes for Logan Stone. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Logan Stone uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. Both shapes still make the enemy rock back and forth like before, and your third blow knocks it down. **Sandblast**: the dust from the fall doesn't settle. It blows on, away from you, as stinging grit, which may set the next enemy rocking. **Drifted Over**: the wind piles sand over the enemy where it falls. It stays down longer, and enemies near it have to wade through the sand.

**When it happens.** Every time Logan Stone goes off while you carry both Wind and Earth. Logan Stone goes off when Unsteady lands on a shaken enemy, or when Tremor lands on an Unsteady one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Logan Stone.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both shapes: the enemy rocks for 3 seconds. Your first weapon blow breaks its balance 1.25 times as fast, the next 1.5 times, and the third knocks it flat. Its fall deals 20 damage, 30 stagger and a Tremor within 3 metres. An elite only staggers, taking 40 stagger. Sandblast: the enemy is down for 1.8 seconds, and the grit flies out of its far side, away from you, as a cone 5 metres long and 60 degrees wide: 6 damage, 15 stagger and an Unsteady to every other enemy in it. This is on purpose: the Unsteady may start a new Logan Stone. Drifted Over: the enemy is down for 2.8 seconds, and every enemy within 2 metres of it moves at 60% speed for those 2.8 seconds. After Logan Stone goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 6 seconds.

**Works with.** Grit in the Wind to set it up, New Face, The Prophecy Broken, Walking Dune.

**Example.** A line of soldiers: use Sandblast, and knock them down in a row. A pack around you: use Drifted Over, and finish the buried one while the rest wade through the sand.

**Not built yet.** Drifted Over slows enemies who are near when it falls, but not enemies who wade in later.

## loganstone.grit
**What it does.** Your light blows on a shaken enemy make it Unsteady. Your heavy blows on an Unsteady enemy shake it.

**When it happens.** Every light blow on an enemy that has Tremor, and every heavy blow or finisher on one that has Unsteady, while you carry both Wind and Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Unsteady, or one Tremor.

**Works with.** Logan Stone (mixing light and heavy blows adds both), New Face.

**Example.** Shake the enemy with a heavy blow, then a light blow sets it rocking.

## loganstone.newface
**What it does.** Your first blow on an enemy that has been knocked down is a critical hit and gives you 6 mana back.

**When it happens.** After Logan Stone knocks an enemy down, while you carry both Wind and Earth: your first weapon blow on it while it is on the ground.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The blow is a critical hit and gives you 6 mana back, if it lands while the enemy is still down: 1.8 seconds, or 2.8 with Drifted Over. Elites, which only stagger, give nothing.

**Works with.** Logan Stone and its shapes, Grit in the Wind.

**Example.** The third blow knocks it down. Hit it on the ground for a critical hit and the mana for your next spell.

## loganstone.prophecy
**What it does.** Elites and gods can be knocked down. After three Logan Stones have only made one stagger in a fight, the fourth knocks it flat for 1 second.

**When it happens.** Each Logan Stone on an elite, while you carry both Wind and Earth.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Every fourth Logan Stone on an elite knocks it flat for 1 second. Each elite keeps its own count.

**Works with.** Logan Stone, Grit in the Wind, Walking Dune.

**Example.** Keep an elite rocking, and every fourth stone puts it down.

**Not built yet.** No god is knocked flat yet.

## loganstone.dune
**What it does.** A ridge of sand piles up in front of you, and the wind drives it 7 metres forward. Each enemy it rolls over takes 16 damage, 35 stagger, two Unsteady and a Tremor, so it rocks as the dune passes. It has to wade through sand for 4 seconds after.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Wind and Earth. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Wind.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 12 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. It is a cone 7 metres long and 45 degrees wide. Each enemy in it takes 16 damage, 35 stagger, two Unsteady and a Tremor, then moves at 60% speed for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Logan Stone (everything it hits rocks), New Face, The Prophecy Broken.

**Example.** Roll the dune over a pack, then knock them down one by one as they rock.

**Not built yet.** The ridge doesn't roll forward over them yet and stay as a dune where it stops. A heavy blow into it doesn't drive it on another 5 metres yet.
