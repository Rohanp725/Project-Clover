# Logan Stone: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Wind and Earth share, named for its reaction,
Logan Stone (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Wind and Earth"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Wind and Earth
are both carried.

## loganstone.shape
**What it does.** Grit or Drift: opens Logan Stone's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Logan Stone wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. Both rock it as before, and your third blow throws it down. **Sandblast**: the dust of the fall is not let settle but thrown on away from you as stinging grit, which may set the next one rocking. **Drifted Over**: the wind heaps sand over it where it falls; it lies longer, and those near it wade.

**When it happens.** Every time Logan Stone fires (Unsteady landing on a shaken enemy, or Tremor on an Unsteady one) while Wind and Earth are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Logan Stone runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both: it rocks 3 seconds; your first weapon blow breaks its balance at 1.25 times, the next at 1.5, and the third throws it flat (its fall strikes 20, 30 stagger and a Tremor in 3 metres); an elite only reels, 40 stagger. Sandblast: down 1.8 seconds, and the grit strikes 6, 15 stagger and an Unsteady in three bursts on the line beyond it, away from you; on purpose, the Unsteady may start a Logan Stone of its own. Drifted Over: down 2.8 seconds, and every enemy within 2 metres of it moves at 60% speed for those 2.8 seconds. Logan Stone rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Grit in the Wind to bring the two, New Face, The Prophecy Broken, Walking Dune.

**Example.** A line of soldiers: Sandblast, and fell them in a row. A pack round you: Drifted Over, and finish the buried one while the rest wade.

**Not built yet.** Sandblast's grit is three bursts in a line rather than a cone 5 metres long and 60 degrees wide. Drifted Over slows those near as it falls, not those who wade in later.

## loganstone.grit
**What it does.** Your light blows on a shaken enemy leave it Unsteady; your heavy blows on an Unsteady enemy shake it.

**When it happens.** Every light blow on an enemy carrying Tremor, and every heavy blow or finisher on one carrying Unsteady, while Wind and Earth are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Unsteady, or one Tremor.

**Works with.** Logan Stone (the weave of light and heavy brings both), New Face.

**Example.** Shake it with a heavy, then a light blow sets it rocking.

## loganstone.newface
**What it does.** Your first blow on a felled enemy is critical and gives you 6 mana back.

**When it happens.** After Logan Stone throws an enemy down, while Wind and Earth are both carried: your first weapon blow on it while it lies.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The blow is critical and gives you 6 mana back, within 1.8 seconds of the fall. Elites, which only reel, give nothing.

**Works with.** Logan Stone and its shapes, Grit in the Wind.

**Example.** Third blow, it falls; strike it on the ground for a critical and the mana for your next spell.

**Not built yet.** It counts the rocking's blows itself from each Logan Stone rather than reading the fall, so it stays open 1.8 seconds after the third blow even when Drifted Over buries it longer.

## loganstone.prophecy
**What it does.** Elites and gods can be felled: after three Logan Stones have only made one reel in a fight, the fourth throws it flat for 1 s.

**When it happens.** Each Logan Stone on an elite, while Wind and Earth are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: one Logan Stone in three on an elite throws it flat for 1 second at once, by chance, at most once in 6 seconds.

**Works with.** Logan Stone, Grit in the Wind, Walking Dune.

**Example.** Keep an elite rocking and now and then it goes down.

**Not built yet.** Logan Stones are not yet counted on one enemy over a fight, so the fall comes by chance rather than on the fourth; and no god is knocked flat yet.

## loganstone.dune
**What it does.** A ridge of sand heaps up before you and the wind drives it 7 metres on. Each enemy it rolls over takes 16 and 35 stagger, two Unsteady and a Tremor, so it rocks as the dune passes, and wades for 4 seconds after.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Wind and Earth are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Wind.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. A cone 7 metres long and 45 degrees wide: 16 damage, 35 stagger, two Unsteady and a Tremor to each enemy in it, which then moves at 60% speed for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Logan Stone (everything struck rocks), New Face, The Prophecy Broken.

**Example.** Roll the dune over a pack, then fell them one by one as they rock.

**Not built yet.** The ridge does not yet roll forward over them and lie as a dune where it stops, and a heavy blow struck into it does not yet drive it on 5 metres.
