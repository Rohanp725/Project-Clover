# Ill Wind: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Poison and Wind share, named for its reaction,
Ill Wind (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d3.md`, "Poison and Wind"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Poison and Wind
are both carried.

## illwind.shape
**What it does.** Opens Ill Wind's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Ill Wind wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. Both loosen its poison as before. **Dust on the Blade**: no trail; the grit coats your edge, and your next blows each lay a dose and an Unsteady. **Dust Bank**: the trail does not settle but runs on the way the enemy was thrown, a low bank of dust that makes Unsteady what it passes.

**When it happens.** Every time Ill Wind fires (poison landing on an Unsteady enemy, or Unsteady on a poisoned one) while Poison and Wind are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Ill Wind runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both: the poison stays loose while it carries both, and your heavy blow on it sheds all of it. Dust on the Blade: no plume; for 6 seconds your next three weapon blows on it each lay a dose and an Unsteady. Dust Bank: the plume as plain (3 seconds, a dose a second), and a line of dust 8 metres long downwind, 1.2 metres wide, that lies 4 seconds and makes Unsteady once a second what stands in it. Ill Wind rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Spring Dust to bring the two, Gust at the Heel, No Still Air, The Yellow Gale.

**Example.** One brute: Dust on the Blade and keep at it. A line of soldiers: Dust Bank, and heavy the first one down the line.

**Not built yet.** Only a heavy blow sheds it, not every shove and throw. Dust on the Blade's coat stays on the enemy shed rather than on your edge, and counts any weapon blow, not only light ones. Dust Bank's trail is laid at once rather than drifting 2 metres a second.

## illwind.springdust
**What it does.** Heavy blows dose what they shove, and make it Unsteady.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, while Poison and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One dose and one Unsteady; of your first element's, none beyond its root's.

**Works with.** Ill Wind (one heavy blow brings both), Fine Dust, No Still Air.

**Example.** Each heavy blow on a fresh enemy loosens its poison.

## illwind.gustheel
**What it does.** A perfect dodge leaves a gust behind you that throws the attacker 3 metres, so a poisoned attacker trails at once.

**When it happens.** Each perfect dodge, while Poison and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Everything within 2.5 metres of you is thrown 3 metres, at most every half second.

**Works with.** Ill Wind, No Still Air, Spring Dust.

**Example.** Dodge a poisoned brute's swing at the last moment and it is blown away from you.

**Not built yet.** It throws everything near you, not only the attacker, and the throw does not yet shed the dust.

## illwind.finedust
**What it does.** Ill Wind's trails last 2 seconds longer.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Poison and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** Spring Dust, which it grows from, Ill Wind and its shapes.

**Example.** The plume still lies when the next soldier steps in.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet.

## illwind.nostillair
**What it does.** A poisoned, Unsteady enemy cannot stand still: every 2 seconds the wind shoves it 1 metre away from you, and it sheds a trail each time.

**When it happens.** From each Ill Wind, for as long as that enemy carries both, while Poison and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Every 2 seconds: a shove of 1 metre straight away from you, all its poison shed in a plume behind it that lies 2 seconds and doses once a second all who stand in it.

**Works with.** Ill Wind, Spring Dust (which keeps it Unsteady and dosed), The Yellow Gale.

**Example.** A poisoned, unsteady brute is blown back step by step, laying dust on its own pack.

## illwind.yellowgale
**What it does.** The wind turns round you and goes out as a gale of yellow grit, a cone 9 metres long and 60 degrees wide. Everything in it is thrown 4 metres, made Unsteady three deep, dosed, and its poison bites at once.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Poison and Wind are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Poison.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. 8 damage and 20 stagger, two doses, three Unsteady, a throw of 4 metres and a bite.

**Works with.** Ill Wind (everything struck carries both), No Still Air, Spring Dust.

**Example.** Gather a pack in front of you and blow it away in yellow dust.

**Not built yet.** The button is not yet held to drink for 1.5 seconds: clouds, trails and half of each poisoned enemy's doses within 6 metres are not yet drawn in and shared out, and weapon blows before the cast do not yet add doses.
