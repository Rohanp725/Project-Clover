# Carried Embers: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Fire and Wind share, named for its reaction,
Carried Embers (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Fire and Wind"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Fire and Wind
are both carried.

## carriedembers.shape
**What it does.** Opens Carried Embers' two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Carried Embers wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Fire Whirl**: the flame stands up on the enemy as a turning column that draws the enemies near into it and lights them. **Hot Wind**: one long gust downwind, away from you, instead of embers.

**When it happens.** Every time Carried Embers fires (Unsteady landing on a burning enemy, or Burn on an unsteady one) while Fire and Wind are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Carried Embers runs (a column of fire running 5 metres downwind, lighting what it reaches).

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Fire Whirl: 3 seconds; once a second every enemy within 3 metres is drawn 1 metre toward it and everything within 2 metres takes a Burn. Hot Wind: a lane 8 metres long and 2 metres wide beyond it lays a Burn on all in it, and the enemy is shoved 2 metres. Carried Embers rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Flame Wave and Gust to bring the two, Fan the Flame, The Fire Breathes In.

**Example.** A ring of soldiers: Fire Whirl gathers them round the burning one. A corridor: stand at its end and Hot Wind burns down it.

**Not built yet.** The whirl stands on the enemy rather than moving and pulling as a hazard of its own, and Hot Wind shoves only the enemy, not all in the lane.

## carriedembers.fan
**What it does.** A perfect dodge fans the flame on the attacker: it burns one deeper, and if it is unsteady too, Carried Embers fires again at once, whatever its rest.

**When it happens.** Each perfect dodge (roll just as a blow would land) against a burning enemy.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Burn on the enemy you face; Carried Embers forced past its 4-second rest.

**Works with.** Gust and Updraft (Unsteady), Long Embers, The Wind Carries It.

**Example.** A burning, unsteady brute swings: dodge it perfectly and its embers fly again into the pack behind it.

## carriedembers.long
**What it does.** When Carried Embers fires, one more ember flies 2 metres further than the rest.

**When it happens.** Each time Carried Embers fires, plain or shaped, while Fire and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. Four embers fly, not three, and they land 2 metres further downwind: the farthest about 8.5 metres away.

**Works with.** Carried Embers, The Wind Carries It.

**Example.** Embers land among the archers at the back as well as the soldiers in front.

## carriedembers.carries
**What it does.** Your heavy blows on a burning enemy throw its embers downwind as if it had reacted, with no Unsteady needed and no rest. Each throw costs it one Burn. Where you stand when you strike aims the fire, so every heavy blow is a choice of where it lands.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands on a burning enemy, while Fire and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. Three small fires 5 metres downwind of it, 1 metre apart: each 1 metre across for 3 seconds, 3 damage and a Burn every half second. One of its Burn stacks is spent.

**Works with.** Cinder (the burn to spend), Flame Wave, Long Embers, Fan the Flame.

**Example.** A burning soldier between you and a pack: three heavy blows and the pack stands in fire.

## carriedembers.breathesin
**What it does.** A ring of flame stands at the aim point and the fire draws its own wind: every enemy near is pulled toward it, unsteadied as it comes, and the fire bursts over them.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Fire and Wind are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Fire.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 16 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 6 metres ahead; for 3 seconds it draws in everything within 6 metres, then bursts: 20 damage, 20 stagger, two Burn and an Unsteady.

**Works with.** Carried Embers (they come out burning and unsteady), The Wind Carries It, Fan the Flame.

**Example.** Cast it into a scattered fight and fight the knot it makes.

**Not built yet.** The ring does not stand for 6 seconds burning what is inside, the draw does not reach 8 metres and unsteady them every 2 seconds, and a heavy blow does not yet throw an enemy toward it.
