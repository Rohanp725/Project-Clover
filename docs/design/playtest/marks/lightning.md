# Lightning: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Lightning tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## lightning.static
**What it does.** Light blows build Static on an enemy, a stack at a time, up to five. Static does nothing by itself: a heavy blow or a finisher discharges it all at once, into the one struck and everyone standing near it.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. Every light blow that lands adds a stack; a heavy blow or a finisher on an enemy carrying Static discharges it.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One stack a light blow, five at most, lasting 6 seconds. A discharge deals 7 damage and 5 stagger for each stack to everything within 4 metres: 35 damage and 25 stagger at five.

**Works with.** Long Arc (the discharge reaches 5.5 metres), Heavy Air, Full Discharge, Single Stroke, Conductor, Overdrive's quicker swings, and Arc Fire or Thunder Lift on the mixed branches.

**Example.** Four light blows on a soldier, then a heavy: 28 damage leaps out of it into the two beside it as well.

## lightning.charge
**What it does.** A perfect guard banks a charge. The next blow you land spends it for double damage and double stagger.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) banks it; your next weapon blow that lands spends it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The charged blow deals twice its damage and twice its stagger.

**Works with.** Hard Charge (the charged blow breaks balance 2.5 times as hard), Deep Reserve, a heavy blow that also discharges Static, Grounding, and Overclock's slowed breath.

**Example.** Perfect-guard a soldier's thrust and answer with a heavy blow: it lands for double, often enough to stagger it at once.

## lightning.reserve
**What it does.** Perfect guards bank up to three charges instead of one. Each blow you land spends one, and the next is made ready at once. The count shows on the screen as Charge.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard adds a charge; each weapon blow that lands spends one.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Three charges at most. Each charged blow deals twice its damage and twice its stagger.

**Works with.** Stored Charge (it needs it), Hard Charge, Keen Edge, Open Seam, Full Discharge.

**Example.** Perfect-guard all three blows of a duellist's flurry, then answer with three light blows that each land for double.

## lightning.cuts
**What it does.** The Iaido Slash cuts twice. A moment after the slash, when the blade is home, its cut lands again all at once across the ground it covered.

**When it happens.** Each time the Iaido Slash, the Katana's weapon skill (hold the heavy attack to full), strikes at least one enemy. It needs the Katana.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The second cut lands 0.55 seconds later, over a circle in front of you as wide as about two thirds of the slash's reach, for 60% of the slash's damage and 22 stagger.

**Works with.** Static (the slash discharges it), Stored Charge on the first cut, Grounding.

**Example.** Slash through a pair of soldiers and sheathe: both are struck again before they can turn.

## lightning.fulldischarge
**What it does.** A discharge of five Static or more holds its enemy still for a moment and gives you mana.

**When it happens.** Whenever a discharge spends five stacks or more: a heavy blow or finisher on a full enemy, or Conductor setting one off.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Held still for 0.8 seconds (elites and bosses for less, a god not at all). 10 mana.

**Works with.** Static, Single Stroke (ten stacks still counts), Waiting Thunder, Stored Charge, Thunder Lift on the Tempest branch.

**Example.** Build five Static on a brute and discharge it: it freezes mid-swing for a breath, long enough for two more cuts.

## lightning.grounding
**What it does.** A perfect guard sends an arc of lightning through the one who struck and on to two more enemies near it.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 12 damage and 20 stagger to each, leaping up to 5 metres from one to the next.

**Works with.** Stored Charge, Deep Reserve, Overclock, Capacitor.

**Example.** Three soldiers close in and the first swings: guard it perfectly and all three are jolted.

## lightning.waiting
**What it does.** Five bolts hang at your shoulder, crackling. Each light blow you land sends one down onto the enemy struck. A heavy blow sends every one that is left. Those not sent when the time runs out go out.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button. Then your blows send the bolts.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 35 mana. Five bolts, for 10 seconds. Each deals 8 damage and 6 stagger and lays one stack of Static.

**Works with.** Static (the bolts build it as you cut), Full Discharge, Single Stroke, Overdrive.

**Example.** Cast, land two light blows, then a heavy: the last three bolts fall with it and the heavy discharges what they laid.

## lightning.stroke
**What it does.** Static builds to ten. A discharge no longer arcs to anyone else: all of it lands on the one struck, twice as hard.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while you hold Lightning.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Static up to ten stacks. A discharge deals 14 damage and 10 stagger for each stack to the one struck alone: 140 damage and 100 stagger at ten.

**Works with.** Static, Heavy Air, Waiting Thunder, Full Discharge, Stored Charge (a charged heavy blow that also discharges).

**Example.** Against the Gate Captain, keep cutting until it carries ten Static, then land one heavy blow for 140.

## lightning.conductor
**What it does.** Thunderclap leaves Static two deep on everything it strikes. An enemy already at full Static when it is struck is set off at once.

**When it happens.** When Thunderclap strikes. As built it answers every spell of yours that strikes an enemy, not only Thunderclap.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Two stacks of Static on each enemy struck. Setting off a full enemy deals 7 damage a stack to everything within 4 metres (35 at five), with no stagger.

**Works with.** Third Leap (Thunderclap leaps to one more), Quick Clap, Static, Full Discharge, Live Wire, Chain Lance.

**Example.** Thunderclap a group twice: the first leaves each two deep, and each struck at five by the second goes off.

## lightning.rod
**What it does.** You plant a rod a little ahead of you. For eight seconds bolts fall inside a circle round it, again and again, striking whatever stands there and laying Static.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 40 mana. Planted 2 metres ahead; the circle is 3.5 metres in radius. It strikes 0.2 seconds after planting and then every 0.9 seconds for 8 seconds: 12 damage, 12 stagger and one stack of Static each time.

**Works with.** Static (heavy blows on enemies inside discharge it), Lodestone, Live Wire, Capacitor.

**Example.** Plant it in a doorway and fight on its edge: everything that comes through it arrives already carrying Static.

## lightning.wire
**What it does.** Casting any spell lays a line of lightning on the ground ahead of you. Enemies standing on it are shocked again and again and gather Static.

**When it happens.** Each time you cast a spell, Thunderclap or the worn spell, at most once every six seconds.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Five patches along a line 8 metres ahead, each 0.9 metres in radius, for 4 seconds. Each bites every half second for 5 damage and one stack of Static.

**Works with.** Static, Lightning Rod, Lodestone (drag them onto the line), Long Overdrive, Running Current.

**Example.** Thunderclap down a corridor, and the soldiers who follow walk onto the wire and are three deep before they reach you.

## lightning.lodestone
**What it does.** A line of lightning leaps from your hand to every enemy near, and each is dragged toward you, further for every Static it carries. Then everything close to you is struck, and all the Static on it is set off at once and added to the blow.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 45 mana. Reaches enemies within 7 metres, eight at most. Each is dragged 1 metre, and 0.8 metres more for each stack of Static, never nearer than 1.3 metres. About 0.3 seconds later everything within 3.5 metres of you takes 14 damage and 30 stagger, plus 7 for each stack of Static it carried.

**Works with.** Static, Lightning Rod, Live Wire, Conductor, Seven Flashes.

**Example.** Five soldiers spread round you at five Static: one cast pulls them all into sword reach and each takes 49.

## lightning.overclock
**What it does.** Overdrive opens with a breath in which everything but you is slowed. Each perfect guard or perfect dodge while Overdrive lasts opens another such breath. The price: Overdrive costs more mana.

**When it happens.** Pressing the surge button to light Overdrive; then every perfect guard or perfect dodge until it ends.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. The opening breath slows enemies to 35% of their speed for 2.5 seconds; each later one for 1 second. Overdrive costs 55 mana where it cost 40.

**Works with.** Storm Step, Long Overdrive, Stored Charge, Afterimage, Never There.

**Example.** Light Overdrive as a brute begins its combo: you get two and a half slowed seconds to cut and step clear.

## lightning.lance
**What it does.** You throw a bolt at the enemy you face that leaps on through four more, leaving Static on each.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 35 mana. Reaches 12 metres to the first enemy, then leaps up to 5 metres from one to the next, five enemies in all. 26 damage, 30 stagger and two stacks of Static to each.

**Works with.** Conductor, Static, Live Wire, Lodestone.

**Example.** Open on a pack of five archers from range: each takes 26 and two Static before they draw.

## lightning.blink
**What it does.** Each dodge sends a line of lightning down its path that shocks whatever it crosses.

**When it happens.** Every dodge.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The shock is 1.1 metres in radius and travels with you for the length of the dodge, striking each enemy once for 10 damage and 14 stagger.

**Works with.** Forked Step (80% wider), Biting Step (40% harder), Afterimage, Never There, Storm Step.

**Example.** Roll through a gap between two soldiers and both are shocked as you pass.

## lightning.afterimage
**What it does.** A dodge at the last moment leaves a copy of you that strikes where you stood a moment later.

**When it happens.** A perfect dodge: a blow passes through the very start of your roll.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The strike lands 0.3 seconds later where your dodge began, 2.3 metres in radius: 26 damage and 28 stagger.

**Works with.** Bright Echo (30% harder), Blink, Seven Flashes, Slipstream, Charged Step, Overclock.

**Example.** A brute's overhead: roll through it at the last moment, and the copy left behind hits it as the blow lands.

## lightning.flashes
**What it does.** You vanish and cut from enemy to enemy in one breath, then stand again where you began with your back to the fight. The cuts land together once you are still, and any enemy left at full Static discharges. You cannot be struck while gone; if you are struck while standing still, the cuts land at half and nothing discharges.

**When it happens.** When worn: hold the spell button.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 45 mana. Seven cuts among enemies within 8 metres (against one enemy, seven from all sides), 0.1 seconds apart, then half a second still. Each cut deals 9 damage, 8 stagger and one stack of Static. A full enemy discharges for 7 damage and 5 stagger a stack within 4 metres.

**Works with.** Static, Afterimage, Never There, Lodestone.

**Example.** Against one duellist at three Static: seven cuts bring it to full and it discharges as you turn.

## lightning.neverthere
**What it does.** Every dodge carries you further and a perfect dodge costs nothing. The price: a blow held on your guard costs twice the stamina.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The free dodge comes with each perfect dodge; the price with each blow you block.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Dodges go 30% further. A perfect dodge gives back 25 stamina, the dodge's usual cost. A blocked blow costs twice the stamina for each point caught.

**Works with.** Blink, Afterimage, Slipstream, Charged Step, Seven Flashes, Overclock.

**Example.** Fight a fast duellist by dodging every swing at the last moment and never raising your guard.

## lightning.slipstream
**What it does.** A dodge at the last moment quickens your feet and your weapon for a while.

**When it happens.** A perfect dodge: a blow passes through the very start of your roll.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 25% faster on your feet and 15% faster swings, for 6 seconds.

**Works with.** Afterimage, Never There, Overdrive, Charged Step, Quick Hands.

**Example.** Roll through a spear thrust at the last moment and close the gap before it can draw back.

## lightning.stormstep
**What it does.** While Overdrive lasts, every dodge costs nothing and shocks what it passes through, whether or not you learned Blink. A shape of light is left where you stood.

**When it happens.** Every dodge while Overdrive, lit with the surge button, is running.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Each dodge gives back its whole stamina cost. The shock is Blink's: 10 damage and 14 stagger, 1.1 metres in radius along the dodge.

**Works with.** Blink's minors (Forked Step, Biting Step), Overclock, Long Overdrive, Afterimage, Never There.

**Example.** Light Overdrive in a crowd and roll back and forth through them: no stamina spent, and every pass shocks.
