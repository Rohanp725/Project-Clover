# Breath Taken: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Dark and Wind share, named for its reaction,
Breath Taken (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Dark and Wind"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Dark and Wind are
both carried.

## breathtaken.shape
**What it does.** Storm or Breath: opens Breath Taken's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Breath Taken wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. Both wind it as before. **Black Storm**: the taken breath does not scatter but runs on as a streak of black dust, winding the next enemies too. **Second Wind**: the breath is blown to you, and you draw it in: stamina, mana and free dodges.

**When it happens.** Every time Breath Taken fires (a Curse landing on an Unsteady enemy, or Unsteady on a cursed one) while Dark and Wind are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Breath Taken runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both, as plain: winded 1.5 seconds (an elite 0.6), beginning no attack and taking 30% more stagger. Black Storm: the two nearest other enemies within 6 metres are winded 1.5 seconds too, and each takes a Curse and an Unsteady. Second Wind: 25 stamina and 8 mana to you, and your dodges cost no stamina for 3 seconds. Breath Taken rests 6 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Ill Omen to bring the two, Short of Breath, The Last Breath, Blown Through, Wind From the Grave.

**Example.** A pack about to swing: Black Storm, and three of them double over. A long fight on your own: Second Wind, and dodge without counting.

**Not built yet.** Black Storm's streak does not yet run downwind from body to body: the two nearest are winded at once. Second Wind frees every dodge in its 3 seconds rather than only the next one.

## breathtaken.illomen
**What it does.** A perfect dodge Curses the one dodged and leaves it two deeper in Unsteady.

**When it happens.** Each perfect dodge, while Dark and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A Curse and two Unsteady, at most once a second.

**Works with.** Breath Taken (one perfect dodge brings both), Short of Breath, Blown Through.

**Example.** Slip the swing at the last moment and its breath is taken.

**Not built yet.** It reaches everything within 2.5 metres of you, not only the one dodged.

## breathtaken.short
**What it does.** Your heavy blows on a winded enemy strike half again as hard, and each takes 1 s off every running spell cooldown.

**When it happens.** While an enemy is winded by Breath Taken (1.5 seconds; an elite 0.6), while Dark and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 1.5 times the damage of your weapon on it; each heavy blow takes 1 second off every running spell clock, at most every 0.3 seconds.

**Works with.** Breath Taken and its shapes, Ill Omen.

**Example.** Wind it, then heavy while it gasps.

**Not built yet.** It keeps its own winding from each Breath Taken, and every weapon blow in it strikes harder, not only the heavy ones.

## breathtaken.lastbreath
**What it does.** A winded enemy that dies lets its last breath out as a black gust: everything within 4 m is pushed 3 m and Cursed.

**When it happens.** The death of an enemy within its winding after Breath Taken (1.5 seconds; an elite 0.6), while Dark and Wind are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. A push of 3 metres and a Curse on everything within 4 metres.

**Works with.** Ill Omen, Short of Breath, Breath Taken.

**Example.** Kill one as it gasps and its pack is thrown back cursed, ready to be winded in turn.

## breathtaken.blownthrough
**What it does.** You stand in a black gust for 1 second. The first blow that comes finds no one: the wind carries you through the attacker and sets you down 2 metres behind it, its breath taken. Your first blow from its back within a second is critical and gives back half the wait.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Dark and Wind are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Dark.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, counter, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The answer: 6 damage, 15 stagger, a Curse and an Unsteady, so Breath Taken fires; you step to 2 metres beyond it; your first weapon blow on it within 1 second is critical and takes 4.5 seconds off every running spell clock.

**Works with.** Breath Taken and its shapes, Short of Breath, Ill Omen.

**Example.** Wait for the brute's swing, pass through it, and cut its back.

**Not built yet.** The wind does not yet carry you through it untouchable and set you down facing it: you step past it. The blow from its back cuts every clock 4.5 seconds rather than giving back half of this one's, and when nothing comes the gust does not yet break in a small push.

## breathtaken.grave
**What it does.** A duo surge. While it burns a black wind blows from you the way you face, 9 metres long and 100 degrees wide: enemies in it are pushed away half a metre a second and made Unsteady each second, and a Curse laid on any in the wind is carried downwind.

**When it happens.** Choose it for your surge slot in the Hand at a rest fire, while Dark and Wind are both carried, and light it with the surge button.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. 35 mana, 10 seconds. As built: once a second a cone 9 metres long and 100 degrees wide lays an Unsteady on what is in it, and everything within 6 metres of you is pushed half a metre away.

**Works with.** Breath Taken, Ill Omen, Blown Through.

**Example.** Light your surge, face the pack, and Curse one: the wind does the rest.

**Not built yet.** There is no streaming field turning with you yet, the push is all round you, and a Curse is not yet carried downwind.
