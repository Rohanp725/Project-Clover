# Earth: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Earth tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## earth.tremor
**What it does.** Your heavy blows shake the ground under an enemy and lay Tremor, and a finisher lays all of it at once. A shaken enemy is slowed, and its balance does not come back while the shaking lasts.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow that lands adds a stack; every finisher fills it. Like every root, it lays with the weapon only while Earth is your first element; carried second, Earth's Tremor comes from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One stack a heavy blow, three on a finisher, three at most. Tremor lasts 6 seconds; each stack slows the enemy by 10%, and while it lasts its stagger meter does not drain.

**Works with.** Landslide, Long Tremor (Tremor lasts 50% longer), Shaken Ground, Bedrock (one more stack with every blow), Buckled on the Forge branch, and The Ground Gives when Soaked meets it.

**Example.** A heavy blow on a brute leaves its balance bar stuck where you left it: step back, guard its swing, and come in again with the bar still waiting.

## earth.rooted
**What it does.** Fight for a few breaths without rolling and you are Rooted. Rooted, you take less from every blow and holding your guard costs less stamina. Starting a roll ends it, and the count begins again.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. You are Rooted once no dodge has begun for 3 seconds.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Rooted: 8% less damage taken, and a blocked blow costs 30% less stamina. Deep Footing makes it come a second sooner; Thick Hide takes 8% more off.

**Works with.** Deep Footing, Thick Hide, Stone Skin, Heavy Hand, Mountain Stance, and Bedrock (20% less damage again while it burns). As built, nothing yet stops you being pushed back while Rooted.

**Example.** Plant your feet in front of a duellist and block its chain: each blocked blow costs less stamina and the grey it leaves is smaller.

## earth.stoneskin
**What it does.** A perfect guard grows a plate of stone on you. A plate takes the whole of one blow and breaks. As built, a plate is a ward.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One ward a perfect guard, three at most. A ward takes a blow you did not guard, a blow that would have broken your guard, or a red attack. Wards last until spent, until you rest, or until you change element.

**Works with.** Aftershock (a plate that breaks bursts), Glaze's ward if you carry Ice, Steady Arm, Old Granite, Heavy Hand.

**Example.** Perfect-guard three blows of a soldier's chain and you carry three plates: the archer's arrow and the brute's red overhead that follow are both turned.

## earth.aftershock
**What it does.** When a plate (a ward) breaks, it bursts outward and shakes everything round you.

**When it happens.** Each time a ward turns a blow, whatever gave the ward: Stone Skin or Glaze.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A burst 3 metres round you: 14 damage, 24 stagger and one stack of Tremor.

**Works with.** Stone Skin, Glaze on the Ice tree, Tremor, Buckled on the Forge branch.

**Example.** Surrounded with two plates on: the first two blows that reach you each throw stone back at the whole ring.

## earth.stance
**What it does.** You stand as stone for a moment. The first blow that reaches you, from any side, red or not, is turned for nothing, and you turn and answer it with a cone of rock that shakes everything in it. A dodge lets the stance go. If no blow comes, nothing happens and the slot still waits.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4), then any blow in the next 1.5 seconds.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Ready for 1.5 seconds. The answer is a cone 4 metres long and 90 degrees wide toward the one who struck: 30 damage, 60 stagger, a heavy blow, two stacks of Tremor. The blow you catch takes 2 seconds off your waiting slots, as a perfect guard does, but unlike Fire's Kiln Stance it gives no mana and sets off no mark that waits on a perfect guard.

**Works with.** Tremor, Rooted, Buckled on the Forge branch. A cone that lands charges the blade with Earth.

**Example.** A brute winds up a red overhead you cannot block: cast just before it falls, and the rock throws it off balance and leaves it two deep in Tremor.

## earth.immovable
**What it does.** You stop rolling away. Your roll hardly moves you, but every blow takes less of you, guarding and dodging cost less stamina, and the moment of a perfect guard is longer.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. The roll goes a tenth as far (about half a metre). 16% less damage taken. Dodges and blocked blows cost 30% less stamina. The perfect guard's moment is 30% longer. As built the roll is still a roll: it still lets blows pass through it, and it still ends Rooted.

**Works with.** Stone Skin, Rooted (do not roll, and both count), Heavy Hand, Steady Arm, Toppling and Struck Ore (more perfect guards), Bedrock.

**Example.** Fight a duellist toe to toe: guard everything, perfect-guard what you can, and roll in place through the one red thrust.

## earth.heavyhand
**What it does.** Nothing stops your heavy blows. Struck while winding one up or swinging it, you still take the blow, but less of it, and yours still lands. Red attacks cannot stop it either.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: any blow that lands on you during a heavy blow's wind-up or swing.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. You take 35% less from a blow that lands then, and you do not flinch.

**Works with.** Tremor, The Slow Hand, Hand of the Mountain, Hammer and Anvil on the Forge branch, Breaking Weight and Full Swing.

**Example.** Two soldiers swing at you as you wind up: you take both, lightly, and your heavy blow lands on the first anyway.

## earth.landslide
**What it does.** Each light blow makes your blows land harder and break balance sooner for a moment. The blow that ends a chain shakes the ground round its enemy.

**When it happens.** Every light blow that lands starts or renews the boost. The last blow of a chain that lands makes the burst.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Each light blow: 16% more weapon damage and 16% more stagger for 2 seconds. It does not grow with more light blows; each one starts its 2 seconds again. The burst at a chain's end: 2.5 metres round the enemy struck, 10 damage, 15 stagger and one stack of Tremor, at most once a second.

**Works with.** Tremor, Shaken Ground (10% more against an afflicted enemy), Long Tremor, Heavy Hand, The Slow Hand, Following Draft from the Wind tree (the boost lasts longer).

**Example.** Two light blows and a heavy on a soldier: the heavy lands 16% harder and shakes the two beside it.

## earth.quakestep
**What it does.** You raise a foot and bring it down. A moment later the ground heaves all round you and shakes everything it strikes.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It lands 0.45 seconds after the cast, 5 metres round you: 16 damage, 30 stagger and two stacks of Tremor on each enemy. As built it does not yet strike harder against an enemy that is winding up, nor break its attack.

**Works with.** Tremor, Standing Stones (a pillar on the first enemy it strikes), Buckled on the Forge branch, Easy Burden. Landing it charges the blade with Earth.

**Example.** Four soldiers close round you: stamp, and all four are two deep in Tremor with their balance bars stuck.

## earth.cleaver
**What it does.** A great blade of stone forms over the enemy you face and follows it for a moment, then falls on it as a heavy blow and leaves a crack in the ground that keeps shaking whatever stands in it. As built it is one great falling blade; holding the button to charge it is to come.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). With no enemy faced it falls 5 metres ahead, and the slot still waits.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 5 metres. It falls 1.2 seconds after the cast, 3 metres in radius: 55 damage, 70 stagger, a heavy blow, two stacks of Tremor. The crack is about 2.2 metres in radius and lasts 3 seconds, biting for 4 every half second with a stack of Tremor.

**Works with.** Tremor, Standing Stones, Hard Stone and Old Stones, The Slow Hand. Earth's deep strike is also called Mountain Cleaver: that one is your weapon's, this one is a spell.

**Example.** Freeze a brute's balance with Tremor, then drop the blade on it: 70 stagger from a meter that is not draining will often open it for a finisher.

## earth.slowhand
**What it does.** Every swing is slower and costs more stamina, but your light blows hit like heavy ones, and every blow shakes its enemy.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The Tremor on every blow comes only while Earth is your first element.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Swings 22% slower; blows cost 20% more stamina. Light blows deal 35% more damage and 60% more stagger. Every weapon blow lays one stack of Tremor. As built, light blows do not yet count as heavy: they pass no guard, force no flinch, and set off nothing that waits on a heavy blow.

**Works with.** Tremor, Landslide, Heavy Hand, Easy Burden, Shaken Ground, Bedrock.

**Example.** Three light blows on a soldier leave it three deep in Tremor and close to staggered, without a heavy blow thrown.

## earth.standingstones
**What it does.** When a spell of yours strikes an enemy, a pillar of stone comes down on it a moment later and shakes it. As built the pillar strikes and is gone; pillars that stand on the field are to come.

**When it happens.** Whenever a spell of yours strikes an enemy, Stone Rise or any spell in your slots. At most once every second and a half.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The pillar falls on a warning ring 0.5 seconds later, 1.5 metres in radius: 20 damage, 35 stagger and one stack of Tremor.

**Works with.** Stone Rise, Quake Step, Fissure, Mountain Cleaver, Hard Stone, Tremor.

**Example.** Stone Rise under an archer, and half a second later a pillar lands on it as well.

## earth.toppling
**What it does.** A perfect guard brings a pillar of stone down on the one who struck, and shakes it. As built it needs no standing pillar nearby: every perfect guard brings one.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The pillar falls 0.3 seconds later on a warning ring, 1.5 metres in radius: 26 damage, 45 stagger and two stacks of Tremor.

**Works with.** Stone Skin (a plate with the same guard), Immovable (a longer moment to guard in), Tremor, Struck Ore on the Forge branch, Far Roots.

**Example.** Perfect-guard a soldier's thrust: on top of the guard's own stagger, the pillar's 45 often staggers it outright.

## earth.fissure
**What it does.** You drive your weapon into the ground. A crack runs ahead toward the enemy you face and breaks open in a row of spikes that strike and shake what they catch. As built the spikes strike and fall; they do not yet hold what they catch, nor stand as a wall.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The crack runs 8 metres at 20 metres a second, a spike every 1.5 metres from 1.3 metres out, each 1 metre in radius. Each enemy is struck once: 28 damage, 35 stagger and one stack of Tremor.

**Works with.** Standing Stones, Hard Stone, Old Stones, Far Roots (it runs further), Tremor. Landing it charges the blade with Earth.

**Example.** Three spearmen in a line down a stair: one crack catches all three.

## earth.rampart
**What it does.** You raise a wall of stone before you. Nothing walks through it, and no blow from its far side reaches you. When its time runs out it bursts; strike it with a heavy blow and it bursts at once, harder.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then a heavy blow near it, or its time, bursts it.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It rises 2.5 metres ahead, 5 metres wide, and stands 8 seconds. One wall at a time. The burst strikes everything within 4 metres of its middle and along its length: 34 damage, 50 stagger and two stacks of Tremor; burst by your heavy blow, it is a heavy blow itself.

**Works with.** Tremor, Hard Stone, Old Stones, Far Roots, The Closed Ring, Buckled on the Forge branch.

**Example.** Wall off the archers, fight the soldiers who come round its ends, then break it with a heavy blow as they bunch beside it.

## earth.closedring
**What it does.** Lighting Bedrock raises eight stones in a ring round you. They bite and shake whatever stands among them, and while Bedrock burns you take less harm still. The price: Bedrock costs more. As built the stones bite what touches them; they do not yet stop anything crossing the ring.

**When it happens.** Each time you light Bedrock with the surge button.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Eight stones 6 metres from where you lit it, each 1 metre in radius, for 10 seconds. Each bites once a second for 8 damage, 20 stagger and one stack of Tremor. 8% less damage taken for 10 seconds, on top of Bedrock's 20%. Bedrock costs 50 mana where it cost 40.

**Works with.** Bedrock, Deep Bedrock, Rooted, Immovable, Hand of the Mountain, Tremor.

**Example.** Light Bedrock in the middle of a pack: those who come for you walk through the stones, already shaken when they arrive.

## earth.hand
**What it does.** While Bedrock burns, a hand of stone as tall as a tower hangs over your shoulder. Each heavy blow or finisher you land, it rises over that enemy and comes down on it a moment later, striking everything round it.

**When it happens.** Light Bedrock with the surge button. The hand stays until Bedrock ends.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. The hand falls 0.5 seconds after the blow, 3 metres in radius: 70% of the blow's damage again, 40 stagger, a heavy blow, one stack of Tremor. It answers one blow at a time: a heavy blow landed while it is still falling is not answered.

**Works with.** Bedrock, Deep Bedrock, Heavy Hand, The Closed Ring, Tremor, finishers.

**Example.** Light Bedrock and finish a staggered soldier: the hand comes down on the finisher's spot and staggers the two behind it.
