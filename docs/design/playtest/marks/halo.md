# Halo: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Halo, the duo branch Metal and Light share. Reshaped in Phase 4
(`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Metal and Light") and drawn in the heart of the chart while Metal and Light are both
carried. Its reaction is Glint; its Shape mark lets you choose what Glint does. Answering Draw has moved to the Katana's
infusion marks on its Way.

## halo.shape
**What it does.** Unlocks two new shapes for Glint. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Glint uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. **Mirror to Mirror**: the ray of light keeps bouncing from enemy to enemy, drawing a zigzag of light across the room. **Burning Mirror**: there is no ray. Instead, the light gathers into the cracks of the enemy's armour as one white-hot point, and your next weapon blow on it hits hard and adds two notches.

**When it happens.** Every time Glint goes off while you carry both Metal and Light. Glint goes off when a Mark lands on a notched enemy, or when Sundered lands on a Marked one. If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Glint. The plain one sends one ray to the nearest other enemy within 6 metres.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Mirror to Mirror: up to four enemies, each within 6 metres of the last one, take 10 damage, 15 stagger and a Mark, and the enemy's notch is passed on. Burning Mirror: the point lasts 5 seconds. Your next weapon blow on the enemy adds two notches and 10 more damage. After Glint goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Bright Steel (a perfect guard adds both afflictions), Halo of Blades, Counter Cut, Open Line.

**Example.** A crowd of Marked soldiers: Mirror to Mirror sends one flash through four of them. A one-on-one fight: use Burning Mirror, then a heavy blow.

**Not built yet.** Mirror to Mirror's ray bounces on from every enemy it hits, not only enemies that have Sundered. Burning Mirror's blow hits for 10 more damage instead of being a critical hit.

## halo.brightsteel
**What it does.** A perfect guard makes your blade flash. The attacker is Marked, and its armour gets a notch.

**When it happens.** Every perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One stack of Marked and one notch of Sundered on the attacker. If your first element's root mark already adds its own affliction on a perfect guard (Sunder's notch, or Marked), that one isn't added twice. The guard leaves one of each.

**Works with.** Sunder, Halo of Blades (the three blows on a Marked enemy that follow are critical hits, and each makes a blade), Counter Cut, Open Line, Edge Guard, Mirror Edge.

**Example.** Perfect-guard a soldier's swing, then answer with three light blows. All three are critical hits, and they land on notched armour.

## halo.haloofblades
**What it does.** Every critical hit you land makes a blade of light in a ring behind your shoulders, up to six. A perfect guard sends them all flying at the enemy that hit you.

**When it happens.** Each critical weapon blow makes one blade. A perfect guard sends off every blade you have. The count shows on the screen as Blades.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Up to six blades. Each flies at 16 metres a second, turning to follow the attacker, and hits for 9 damage, 8 stagger and one notch of Sundered.

**Works with.** Bright Steel and Marked enemies (every blow on them is a critical hit), Counter Cut, Edge Guard, Blinding Guard.

**Example.** Land three critical hits on a Marked soldier, then perfect-guard the brute behind it. Three blades fly into the brute.

## halo.mirroredge
**What it does.** A perfect guard throws a bolt of light straight back at the attacker. It hits hard and Marks what it hits.

**When it happens.** Every perfect guard. For now, it works on any perfect guard, not only against a thrown attack, and the bolt deals its own damage, not the damage of the blow you blocked.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The bolt flies straight at 18 metres a second for 1.5 seconds and passes through enemies. Each enemy it hits takes 20 damage, 30 stagger and one stack of Marked.

**Works with.** Bright Steel, Halo of Blades, Blinding Guard, Open Line, Edge Guard.

**Example.** An archer shoots, and you perfect-guard the arrow. The bolt flies back down the line and Marks the archer.

## halo.blindingguard
**What it does.** A perfect guard blinds the enemies in front of you. They are Marked, and they are knocked off balance. Everyone near you, on any side, is frozen in place for a moment. The cost: blocking a blow with a normal guard costs more stamina.

**When it happens.** Always on once you learn it. The flash comes with every perfect guard. The cost comes with every blocked blow.

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards. You turn to face the attacker. A cone 6 metres long and 120 degrees wide gives 15 stagger and one stack of Marked to everything in it. Everyone within 6 metres is held for 1.5 seconds (less for elites; enemies that can't be frozen aren't held at all). A blocked blow costs a fifth more stamina: 1.08 for each point of damage instead of 0.9.

**Works with.** Bright Steel, Halo of Blades, Mirror Edge, Open Line, Edge Guard.

**Example.** Three soldiers close in on you. Perfect-guard the first one. All three stand still and Marked, and your next blows are critical hits.

## halo.sixmirrors
**What it does.** Six steel mirrors stand in a ring around the enemy you face. You vanish and dash across the ring from each mirror in turn, too fast to follow. Then you stand again where you started, and every cut lands at once. Nothing can hit you from the moment you vanish until the cuts land.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 5 Shards. You can buy it once you carry both elements; no god gives it. 60 mana. It targets an enemy within 8 metres; the ring has a 3-metre radius. There are six dashes, 0.13 seconds apart, then 0.3 seconds of standing still. Each cut deals 14 damage and 12 stagger to the enemy (84 damage and 72 stagger in total), and half that to anything else its line crosses. Then everything within 1.2 metres of the middle of the ring is Marked and gets three notches.

**Works with.** Bright Steel, Halo of Blades (the blows on a Marked enemy after are critical hits), Sunder, Counter Cut, the charged blade (it charges your weapon with Metal).

**Example.** Against a lone duellist, cast it. It takes 84 damage, is Marked and gets three notches, and your next three blows are critical hits on broken armour.
