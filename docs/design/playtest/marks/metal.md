# Metal: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Metal tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## metal.sunder
**What it does.** Heavy blows and perfect guards notch an enemy's armour, and a finisher notches it three deep at once. Each notch is a stack of Sundered, and each lets your weapon bite deeper.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every heavy blow that lands, every perfect guard (on the one who struck), every finisher. Light blows notch nothing. A weapon blow lays only your first element's affliction, so the heavy blow's and the finisher's notches come only while Metal is your first element (or your only one); the perfect guard's notch comes either way.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One notch a heavy blow or perfect guard, three for a finisher, three at most. Sundered lasts 8 seconds; each notch adds 8% to your weapon's damage on that enemy, 24% at three. Spells are not made stronger by it.

**Works with.** Deep Bite (each notch bites 25% harder), Slow Mending (notches last half again as long), Splinter, Buried Blades, Shatter Point, Counter Cut and critical blows (they multiply each other), Temper (one more notch every blow), Flung Blade, and Live Iron when Static meets the notches.

**Example.** Two heavy blows on a shield-bearer and its armour is two deep: every cut after lands 16% harder for the next 8 seconds.

## metal.forgedanswer
**What it does.** Each perfect guard forges a blade from the blow you turned, and it hangs at your shoulder. Up to three wait there. Your next heavy blow that lands sends every one of them at the enemy it struck.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard forges one; the next heavy blow that lands looses all that hang. The count shows on the screen as Blades.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Three blades at most. Each flies at 16 metres a second, turning after the enemy, and strikes for 10 damage and 12 stagger. As built the blades lay no notch of their own.

**Works with.** Sunder (the heavy blow that sends them notches as well), Practised Wrist and Edge Guard (easier perfect guards), Bright Sparks, Open Line, Buried Blades on the same heavy blow.

**Example.** Perfect-guard a duellist's three-blow flurry, then answer with one heavy blow: three blades follow it in for 30 more damage and 36 stagger.

## metal.openline
**What it does.** A perfect guard throws the attacker's weapon wide. It is held still for a moment, and for a breath after the guard your weapon strikes harder.

**When it happens.** Every perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The attacker is held for 1.2 seconds (elites for less; what cannot be frozen, not at all). Your weapon deals 15% more damage for 1.5 seconds after the guard, on any enemy. As built the hold is the whole of it: blows on the held enemy do not count as from behind.

**Works with.** Sunder (a notch with the same guard), Forged Answer, Hard Return, Braced Frame, Counter Cut, Master's Answer (the free skill comes in the same breath).

**Example.** Perfect-guard a brute's swing and it stands frozen with its arm thrown wide: a heavy blow lands before it can move.

## metal.circle
**What it does.** You stand with your blade raised for a breath. The first blow that reaches you, from any side, red attack or not, is turned for nothing and answered with a cut all round you that notches everything near. If nothing comes, the stance goes out and the cast is spent all the same. A dodge lets it go.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4), then any blow in the next 1.2 seconds.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Ready for 1.2 seconds. The answer strikes everything within 3.5 metres, all the way round, for 18 damage and 40 stagger, a heavy blow, and one notch of Sundered. As built the caught blow is not a perfect guard for your marks: it gives no mana and sets off nothing that waits on one.

**Works with.** Sunder, Splinter (the notches it lays), Buried Blades, the charged blade (the answer is a spell, so it charges your weapon with Metal), Long Temper.

**Example.** Ringed by three soldiers, cast it as the first lunges: the cut turns all three and notches each.

## metal.edgeguard
**What it does.** The perfect guard's moment is half again as long, and every perfect guard cuts the attacker and notches its armour. The price: the dodge and every blow held on a plain guard cost half again as much stamina.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: the cut comes with every perfect guard, the price with every dodge and every blocked blow.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. The perfect moment grows by 55%, from 0.18 seconds to about 0.28. Each perfect guard strikes everything within 1.2 metres of the attacker for 12 damage and 10 stagger, with one notch of Sundered. A dodge costs 37.5 stamina where it cost 25; a blocked blow costs 1.35 stamina a point of damage where it cost 0.9. As built a held guard still blocks as usual.

**Works with.** Sunder (two notches from one guard), Forged Answer, Open Line, Practised Wrist, Master's Answer, Hanging Swords, Bright Steel and Halo of Blades on the Halo branch.

**Example.** Fight a fast duellist with the guard button alone: each of its blows met at the moment is turned, cut and notched.

## metal.countercut
**What it does.** A blow that lands while an enemy is showing its own attack, winding up or swinging, is critical.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: any weapon blow that lands on an enemy in the middle of its attack, or holding you.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A critical blow deals one and a half times its damage. The design's extra stagger is not in it yet.

**Works with.** Sunder (a critical blow and Sundered multiply each other), Clean Edge (critical blows 15% harder), Finishing Edge, Halo of Blades on the Halo branch (each critical blow forges a blade).

**Example.** A brute raises its hammer: step in and land a heavy blow before it falls, for one and a half times the damage.

## metal.splinter
**What it does.** A blow on armour notched three deep throws splinters from you through it, and they cut and notch whoever stands behind.

**When it happens.** Any weapon blow that lands on an enemy carrying three notches of Sundered. As built the splinters fly from you through the one struck, not out of its back.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A cone 5 metres long and 60 degrees wide toward the one struck: 8 damage, 8 stagger and one notch of Sundered to everything in it. At most once every 0.6 seconds.

**Works with.** Sunder, Shatter Point (a finisher notches three deep at once), Circle of Steel, Slow Mending, Deep Bite.

**Example.** Notch the front shield-bearer to three and keep cutting it: each cut sprays splinters into the line behind it.

## metal.shatterpoint
**What it does.** A finisher on notched armour strikes a second time, and notches it three deep.

**When it happens.** Every finisher (a heavy blow on a staggered enemy) on an enemy carrying Sundered.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The second strike is 20 damage, no stagger, to everything within 1.2 metres of the one finished. Three notches of Sundered on it, which Sunder's finisher already lays; like Sunder's, they come only while Metal is your first element.

**Works with.** Sunder, Splinter, Buried Blades, Slow Mending, Counter Cut.

**Example.** Stagger a notched brute and finish it: the finisher lands, then the armour breaks again for 20 more.

## metal.hone
**What it does.** You set a sign on the enemy you face. Each of your next five weapon blows on it sets off a small burst round it that notches. With no enemy faced nothing is set and the cast is spent.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then any weapon blow that lands on that enemy, light or heavy.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 8 metres; the sign lasts 12 seconds or five bursts. Each burst is 1.5 metres in radius: 6 damage, 6 stagger and one notch of Sundered. As built it is a sign on the enemy, not a whetted edge on your blade.

**Works with.** Sunder (the burst notches with light blows as well), Splinter, the charged blade (the first burst charges your weapon with Metal), Balanced Blade, quick weapons.

**Example.** Sign the shield-bearer and land five quick light blows: it is three deep before your heavy blow.

## metal.mastersanswer
**What it does.** For two seconds after a perfect guard your weapon's skill costs no mana and comes at once, with no time to charge. At any other time it costs half again as much.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. A perfect guard opens the two seconds; press the skill then.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Two seconds of a free skill that needs no charging; outside them the skill costs 1.5 times its mana.

**Works with.** Open Line (the attacker is still held when the skill lands), Edge Guard, Practised Wrist, Bright Sparks, Forged Answer, and on the Katana the Iaido Slash and Answering Draw.

**Example.** With the Katana, perfect-guard a thrust and the Iaido Slash leaves the sheath at once, free.

## metal.buriedblades
**What it does.** A heavy blow on notched armour strikes a second time, harder, and notches it again.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow that lands on an enemy carrying Sundered, once a second at most.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The second strike is 24 damage and 30 stagger to everything within 1.2 metres of the one struck, with one notch of Sundered. As built there are no blades left standing in the enemy: the second strike is the whole of it.

**Works with.** Sunder (one heavy blow lays two notches), Forged Answer, Flung Blade, Splinter, Deep Bite.

**Example.** A notched soldier takes one heavy blow and is struck again for 24, two notches deeper.

## metal.ricochet
**What it does.** When a spell of yours strikes an enemy, a blade goes through it and springs on to the next enemy near, and then the next, notching each.

**When it happens.** Each time a spell of yours strikes an enemy, at most about three times a second.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 9 damage, 6 stagger and one notch of Sundered to the one struck, then to two more, leaping up to 6 metres from one to the next.

**Works with.** Flung Blade, Grave of Swords and Hone (spells that strike often), Returning Edge, Sun Wheels on the Halo branch, Sunder.

**Example.** Flung Blade into the front of a pack: the blade springs on through two more and all three carry a notch.

## metal.returningedge
**What it does.** A great head of steel forms at your shoulder and lunges down a line before you. It strikes what it passes and carries it along, and its jaws close hard where the line ends. As built it does not fly back to you.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It forms for 0.3 seconds, then runs 8 metres down a lane 1.5 metres wide. Each enemy passed takes 12 damage, 10 stagger and a notch of Sundered and is pushed along. Where it ends the jaws strike 1.1 metres round for 24 damage and 20 stagger, a heavy blow, and a notch.

**Works with.** Ricochet, Sunder, Long Throw (it runs further), True Flight, the charged blade (it charges your weapon with Metal).

**Example.** Four soldiers come down a corridor: the head runs through them and drags them together at its end.

## metal.graveofswords
**What it does.** Twelve blades hang behind your shoulders. One after another they fly by themselves at the nearest enemy, and a heavy blow that lands sends all that remain at the one it struck.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). It is Metal's larger spell.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 50 mana. Twelve blades for 12 seconds. The first flies 0.6 seconds after the cast, then one every 0.55 seconds at the nearest enemy within 6 metres. Each strikes for 9 damage, 6 stagger and one notch of Sundered. Those not flown when the time is up go out.

**Works with.** Ricochet (each blade that strikes springs on), Sunder, Buried Blades and Forged Answer on the heavy blow that sends the rest, All Blades Home, True Flight.

**Example.** Cast it in a pack and fight on: the blades keep three notches on whoever is nearest, and a heavy blow on the brute sends the last five into it.

## metal.allbladeshome
**What it does.** Lighting Temper throws five blades out from you in a fan, and they come back to you, cutting and notching what they pass both ways. Temper costs more and lasts a little longer.

**When it happens.** Each time you light Temper with the surge button. As built the blades are thrown out fresh; conjured blades do not wait to be called home.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Five blades, 14 degrees apart, flying at 14 metres a second; they turn for home after 0.6 seconds. Each strikes each enemy once going and once coming: 12 damage, 8 stagger and one notch of Sundered. Temper costs 55 mana where it cost 40, and lasts 12 seconds where it lasted 10.

**Works with.** Temper, Long Temper, Hanging Swords, Sunder.

**Example.** Light Temper facing a line of archers: the fan goes through them and back, and each is two notches deep.

## metal.hangingswords
**What it does.** While Temper lasts, every blow you hold on your guard counts as a perfect guard, and a sword is forged over the one who struck, up to eight. Each sword hangs over its enemy and follows it. When Temper ends, they fall.

**When it happens.** Light Temper with the surge button, then guard. The swords fall when Temper ends.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. The perfect moment lasts the whole of Temper, as long as the guard is raised fresh. Each guard gives 5 mana more, at most about three times a second. Eight swords at most; they fall 0.3 seconds after Temper ends, 0.11 seconds apart, each striking 1.5 metres round for 16 damage, 20 stagger and a notch of Sundered.

**Works with.** Temper (30% less damage taken, a notch more every blow), Long Temper, All Blades Home, Sunder, Forged Answer, Open Line, Edge Guard.

**Example.** Light Temper and stand in the middle of a pack with your guard up: every blow turns, and when Temper ends eight swords come down on them.
