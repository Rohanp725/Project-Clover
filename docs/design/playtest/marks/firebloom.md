# Firebloom: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Fire and Life share, named for its reaction,
Firebloom (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Fire and Life"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Fire and Life are
both carried.

## firebloom.shape
**What it does.** Seeds That Wait: opens Firebloom's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Firebloom wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Between Two Fires**: the lilies grow in pairs facing each other across the enemy, and passing between the two of a pair mends you and charges your blade. **Ash Bed**: no lilies; the burn and the seed become one, its Burn holds while it is Seeded, and when it dies a bed of green shoots springs from the ash.

**When it happens.** Every time Firebloom fires (Burn landing on a Seeded enemy, or a seed on a burning one) while Fire and Life are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Firebloom runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Between Two Fires: the Seeded is spent and a pair of lilies grows for each seed it carried, 3 metres apart across the enemy; each lily stands 5 seconds and burns what stands within 1 metre, 3 damage and a Burn every half second, and counts as a thing you grew. Ash Bed: nothing is spent; while it stays Seeded its Burn does not fade, and when it dies a bed of shoots 3 metres across stands 6 seconds where it fell, Seeding once a second every enemy in it. Firebloom rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Kindled Guard (flare a lily with a perfect guard), Dry Pods, The Burning Tree, Need-Fire.

**Example.** A crowd that rushes you: Between Two Fires, and fight in the gap. One big burning brute: Ash Bed, and keep it Seeded so it never stops burning.

**Not built yet.** Between Two Fires' pairs do not yet mend you or charge your blade when you pass between them, and with more than one seed the lilies stand in a ring rather than in facing pairs. Ash Bed's shoots do not yet mend you while you stand in them.

## firebloom.kindledguard
**What it does.** A perfect guard made within 3 metres of a lily makes it flare: 12 damage and two Burn in 2 metres, and it stands 3 seconds longer.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard while something you grew stands near, while Fire and Life are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 12 damage, 10 stagger and two Burn to everything within 2 metres, at most once a second.

**Works with.** Firebloom (its lilies are grown things), Need-Fire, The Burning Tree.

**Example.** Stand by a lily and turn the brute's swing: everything round you catches.

**Not built yet.** The flare bursts round you, not round the lily, whenever anything you grew stands within 4 metres, and the lily does not yet stand longer.

## firebloom.drypods
**What it does.** One more seed is thrown, four at most, and lilies stand 2 seconds longer.

**When it happens.** Each time Firebloom fires.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard.

**Works with.** Firebloom and its shapes, Kindled Guard.

**Example.** A lightly seeded enemy still blooms a full bed.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: a mark that changes the reaction's own numbers is still to come.

## firebloom.burningtree
**What it does.** The first lily of each fight grows into a burning tree that stands 10 seconds and throws a burning seed at the nearest enemy every 2 seconds, Seeded and Burn.

**When it happens.** When Firebloom fires, while Fire and Life are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: five throws over 10 seconds, each 4 damage, 4 stagger and a Burn, at most once in 30 seconds.

**Works with.** Firebloom, Kindled Guard, Need-Fire (which seeds and burns again).

**Example.** Open a fight with one bloom and the tree keeps the pack burning while you work.

**Not built yet.** No tree stands yet: the throws go at the enemy that bloomed, not the nearest, and lay a Burn without a seed; and it comes once in 30 seconds, not once a fight.

## firebloom.needfire
**What it does.** Two fires of green branches spring up on either side of the enemy you face and burn 6 seconds, joined by a line of sparks. Every enemy between them takes a Burn and a Seed each second.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Fire and Life are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Fire.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 7 metres away, for 6 seconds: once a second 2 damage, a Burn and a Seed to every enemy in it, so Firebloom keeps opening on them.

**Works with.** Firebloom (everything in it blooms), Kindled Guard, The Burning Tree.

**Example.** Light it on the pack's leader and fight on the line between the fires.

**Not built yet.** It is one burning patch 5 metres across on the enemy, not two fires with a line between; standing between them does not yet make your blows heal 2 and cut the clocks twice as hard, and a shove across the line does not yet set a Burn to full.
