# Life: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Life tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## life.seed
**What it does.** Every blow of your weapon that lands plants a seed in the enemy: one stack of Seeded, up to three. Each further weapon blow on a Seeded enemy mends you a little, more for each stack. When a Seeded enemy dies, it bursts into thorns that strike everyone near it.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Life is the first element you carry: every weapon blow that lands, light or heavy.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One stack a blow, three at most, lasting 8 seconds. Each weapon blow on a Seeded enemy mends 1 health for each stack, 3 at full depth. Its death throws 12 damage a stack at everyone within 3 metres, 36 at three.

**Works with.** Sweet Sap (30% stronger), Thin Blood, Ripening, Fallen Seeds, Reap What Is Sown, Regrowth (one more stack a blow), Briar Patch, and Rot Roots when Poison meets the seed.

**Example.** With the Twin Knives, three quick cuts seed a soldier three deep; every cut after that gives back 3 health while you keep pressing it.

## life.price
**What it does.** When a spell from your slots costs more mana than you have, the rest is paid in health instead. The health is not lost at once: it turns grey, and you win it back by dealing damage, as after a block.

**When it happens.** Each time you cast a spell that costs mana without the mana to pay for it. Basic spells cost no mana, so this answers your element's larger spell and the duo spells. As built, the surge, the weapon skill and the deep strike still need their mana.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One point of health for every two points of mana missing. It never takes your last point: if the health is not there, the spell is not cast.

**Works with.** Elder Tree (40 mana), the duo spells, Thin Blood (spend health to get below a third), Green Wood (the grey waits longer), Deep Roots (12 more health).

**Example.** With 10 mana and Elder Tree to cast, you plant it anyway: 15 health goes grey, and three good cuts on the next enemy win most of it back.

## life.thin
**What it does.** Below a third of your health you fight harder: your weapon strikes a quarter harder, and the seeds you plant while you are low are twice as strong, so they mend you twice as much and burst twice as hard.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, for as long as your health is below a third.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Weapon damage up 25%. Afflictions you lay while low are 100% stronger: a Seeded enemy three deep mends 6 a blow, not 3, and bursts for 72, not 36. Each seed keeps the strength it was planted with.

**Works with.** Seeded, Paid in Blood and Red Harvest (health spent to get low on purpose), Stubborn (15% less damage taken while low), Sweet Sap.

**Example.** Down to 30 health against a brute, plant three seeds and keep cutting: each blow lands a quarter harder and gives back 6.

## life.harvest
**What it does.** You give 25 health and call a copy of yourself to your side. For eight seconds, every weapon blow you land is landed again by the copy a beat later, at half its strength, and the copy's blow plants a seed.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then fight as usual: the copy follows your blows.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it costs 25 health and waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The health turns grey and can be won back. With 25 health or less it cannot be cast, and the slot does not wait. The copy lasts 8 seconds; each echo lands 0.2 seconds after your blow for 50% of its damage, with no stagger, and lays one stack of Seeded. One copy at a time.

**Works with.** Seeded (the echoes seed for you), Thin Blood (the price takes you lower), Heartwood, and the charged blade: the copy's first blow that lands charges your weapon with Life.

**Example.** Cast it on a brute and land a full chain: every cut lands twice, and the echoes win back the grey health they cost.

## life.heartwood
**What it does.** You have no mana at all. Everything that cost mana is paid in health instead, which turns grey and can be won back. Everything that gave mana mends you instead.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every price in mana (the surge, the weapon skill, the deep strike, larger spells) and every gain (blows, perfect guards, perfect dodges, finishers).

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. One point of health for every two points of mana a thing costs, left grey; never the last point, so with too little health the thing is not done. One point of health mended for every three points of mana you would have earned. The mana bar stays on the screen, empty.

**Works with.** Thin Blood (prices bring you low), Green Wood (grey waits longer), Seeded and Regrowth to mend, Red Harvest, and perfect guards, which now mend 5.

**Example.** Light Regrowth for 20 health, gone grey; it mends 4 a second and turns the grey back at once, so it costs you almost nothing.

## life.fallen
**What it does.** Where a Seeded enemy dies, a thornbush grows and stands for a while. It lashes and seeds whatever stands in it.

**When it happens.** Whenever an enemy carrying Seeded dies, whatever killed it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A bush 2 metres in radius for 8 seconds. It bites every half second for 4 damage and one stack of Seeded. Four stand at once at most; a fifth takes the place of the oldest.

**Works with.** Seeded (its death burst comes too), Long Season (40% longer), Sharp Thorns (25% harder), Shade, Reap What Is Sown (every bush bursts), Wildwood.

**Example.** Kill a seeded soldier in a doorway: the bush where it fell seeds everyone who follows it through.

## life.sapling
**What it does.** A perfect guard plants a sapling at your feet. It stands and lashes every enemy within its reach, again and again, seeding each.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The sapling reaches 3.5 metres and stands 12 seconds. Every 1.5 seconds it strikes everything in reach for 6 damage, 8 stagger and one stack of Seeded. Three stand at once at most; a fourth takes the place of the oldest. As built it strikes every enemy in reach, not only the nearest.

**Works with.** Crowded Bed (one more sapling), Long Season, Sharp Thorns, Shade (mend beside them), Reap What Is Sown, Wildwood, Seeded.

**Example.** Hold your ground against a pack and perfect-guard three blows: three saplings stand round you, seeding everything that comes in.

## life.reap
**What it does.** You call in the harvest. The ground bursts all round you, and at the same moment everything you have grown bursts where it stands and is gone, each one mending you.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The burst round you is 6 metres in radius and lands 0.22 seconds after the cast: 16 damage, 25 stagger and one stack of Seeded. Each thornbush and sapling, wherever it stands, bursts 3 metres round for 16 damage and 25 stagger and mends you 5. As built, the seeds in enemies do not burst with it.

**Works with.** Fallen Seeds and Sapling (the things it bursts), Crowded Bed and Wildwood (more of them), Seeded, and the charged blade: it charges your weapon with Life when it lands.

**Example.** Three saplings stand round you and a pack closes in: reap, and four bursts go off at once while you mend 15.

## life.shade
**What it does.** Standing near anything you have grown, you mend slowly and your stamina comes back faster.

**When it happens.** Every second in a fight (an enemy within 12 metres) while a thornbush or sapling of yours stands within 4 metres of you.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 2 health and 5 stamina each second.

**Works with.** Sapling (it stands at your feet), Fallen Seeds, Long Season, Wildwood, Crowded Bed.

**Example.** Perfect-guard once to plant a sapling, then fight beside it: 2 health a second while you trade blows.

## life.tree
**What it does.** You plant a tree a little ahead of you. For fifteen seconds its roots strike everything in a circle round it, again and again, seeding what they strike.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 40 mana. Planted 2.5 metres ahead; the circle is 3 metres in radius. It first strikes 3 seconds after planting, then every 3 seconds, for 15 seconds: 16 damage, 30 stagger and one stack of Seeded each time. It is not one of your grown things: Shade, Reap What Is Sown and Wildwood do not count it.

**Works with.** Seeded, Paid in Blood (cast it without the mana), Ripening (heavy blows on what it seeded), and the charged blade: its first strike charges your weapon with Life.

**Example.** Plant it where a pack must come to you and fight on its edge: every three seconds the roots stagger and seed them.

## life.wildwood
**What it does.** What you grow stands three times as long, and you may keep three more of each kind. The price: far from your garden, your weapon is a fifth weaker.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The price holds whenever nothing you have grown stands within 7 metres of you.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Thornbushes and saplings stand three times as long (24 and 36 seconds). Seven thornbushes and six saplings may stand at once. Weapon damage down 20% with nothing grown within 7 metres.

**Works with.** Fallen Seeds, Sapling, Shade, Reap What Is Sown, Long Season, Crowded Bed.

**Example.** Plant a ring of saplings at the top of a stair and hold it: the garden lasts the whole fight, and you never leave it.

## life.ripen
**What it does.** A heavy blow on a Seeded enemy bursts its seed at once, without waiting for it to die. The thorns strike everyone near it, and you mend.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands on a Seeded enemy, at most once a second.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The burst is 3 metres in radius: 11 damage and 5 stagger for each stack, 33 and 15 at three. The seed is spent, so it will not burst again at death. You mend 10.

**Works with.** Seeded, Wide Burst (33% wider), Deep Seed, Elder Tree and Thorn Ring (they seed for you), Regrowth.

**Example.** Two light blows and a heavy on a soldier in a crowd: the heavy bursts its seed through the pack and gives you 10.

## life.coat
**What it does.** Thorns grow on you. An enemy that strikes you, or whose blow you block, is cut and seeded.

**When it happens.** Each blow that lands on you, and each blow you block with a plain guard, at most twice a second for each.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. A burst 1.2 metres round the attacker: 8 damage and one stack of Seeded. As built any attacker is cut, even one striking from afar: the thorns land where the blow came from.

**Works with.** Seeded (your next blows on it mend you), Bitter Rind, Bark and Long Regrowth (Regrowth), Green Wood, Shared Roots.

**Example.** Block a soldier's chain of three: it is cut and seeded on the way in, and your answer mends you.

## life.rind
**What it does.** A plain guard seeds the attacker. A perfect guard roots it where it stands for a moment.

**When it happens.** Each blow you block, and each perfect guard.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One stack of Seeded on the attacker for each blocked blow. A perfect guard holds it still for 1 second (elites and gods for less; a few great forms cannot be held).

**Works with.** Briar Coat, Thorn Ring, Seeded, Ripening (a held enemy is an easy heavy blow), Sapling.

**Example.** Perfect-guard a brute's swing: it stands rooted for a second, long enough for a heavy blow that bursts its seed.

## life.ring
**What it does.** A ring of thorns rises round you and the enemy you face. Nothing walks through it until it falls, you included; enemies that touch it are cut. The inside is safe ground.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 5 metres; the ring is set between you, at least 4.5 metres in radius and always wide enough to hold you both. With none faced it closes round you alone. It closes in 0.28 seconds and stands 7 seconds. Every 0.4 seconds its band of thorns cuts what touches it for 8 damage and one stack of Seeded.

**Works with.** Seeded, Ripening, Bitter Rind, Shared Roots, and the charged blade: it charges your weapon with Life when it cuts.

**Example.** Shut yourself in with the archer while its friends are left outside, cut against the thorns.

## life.roots
**What it does.** Blows take a fifth less from you, and each blow that lands on you lashes out at every enemy near. The price: everything you lay works half as well, so Seeded mends you half as much.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The lash comes with each blow that lands on you, at most twice a second.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. 20% less damage taken. The lash: 6 damage to every enemy within 8 metres of you. Afflictions you lay are 50% weaker: a seed three deep mends 1.5 a blow and bursts for 18. As built the wound is not shared out among Seeded enemies; this is what stands in for it.

**Works with.** Briar Coat, Thorn Ring, Stubborn and Bark (less damage still), Ripening (its burst is not weakened).

**Example.** Surrounded by six soldiers, every blow you take cuts all six while you take a fifth less of it.

## life.spring
**What it does.** While your surge burns, press the surge button again and give half your health to the ground. A stand of trees comes up through every Seeded enemy near, strikes it and holds it. Strike what the forest holds, and the health you gave comes back.

**When it happens.** Light the surge (Regrowth, or the other element's surge if you wear that), then press the surge button again while it lasts. Once each time the surge is lit. With nothing Seeded near, it says so and you may press again.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Up to eight Seeded enemies within 12 metres. Each takes 30 damage and 60 stagger and is held for 4 seconds (elites and gods for less). Half your present health is taken and turns grey. Each blow on a held enemy wins back half its damage as health, never more than you gave.

**Works with.** Seeded, Regrowth (4 health a second while it lasts), Long Regrowth, Green Wood (the grey waits longer), Briar Patch, Thin Blood.

**Example.** Seed a pack with Briar Patch, light Regrowth, press again: the whole pack stands held while you cut your health back out of them.
