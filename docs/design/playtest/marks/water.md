# Water: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Water tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## water.soak
**What it does.** Every blow of your weapon that lands leaves the enemy Soaked. A Soaked enemy takes more from your spells, and Static or Chill laid on it is laid on everyone standing near it as well.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned: every weapon blow that lands, while Water is your first element. With Water second, Soaked comes from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike instead.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. Soaked is on or off and lasts 8 seconds; each blow starts it again. Spells deal a Soaked enemy 25% more damage. Static or Chill laid on it is also laid on every other enemy within 4 metres, whether they are Soaked or not.

**Works with.** Riptide, Wellspring, Soaked Through (it lasts longer), Weight of Water, Heavy Water, Lightning's Static and Ice's Chill as your second element, and the reactions Steam (with Fire) and The Ground Gives (with Earth).

**Example.** Carrying Water and Lightning, cut three soldiers in a huddle, then Thunderclap one: the Static runs to all three.

## water.undertow
**What it does.** A heavy blow that lands drags every enemy near you a step toward you.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, at most twice a second. As built it pulls everyone near, Soaked or not.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Every enemy within 6 metres of you is pulled 1.5 metres in. At most once every half second.

**Works with.** Heavy weapons that strike wide, Spilled Water (which pushes them back out), Heavy Water, River Serpent, Whirlpool.

**Example.** Two archers hang back while you fight a brute: each heavy blow on the brute draws them a step closer to your blade.

## water.serpent
**What it does.** You send a serpent of water down a lane ahead of you. It strikes each enemy it passes and carries it along with its head, and at the end of the lane its jaws close in a heavy bite. Everything it strikes is left Soaked.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). With no enemy ahead it still runs its lane, and the slot still waits.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It forms at your shoulder for 0.3 seconds, then runs 8 metres down a lane 1.2 metres to each side in about a third of a second. Passing: 6 damage and 15 stagger, once to each enemy. The jaws close over 1.8 metres at the end: 12 damage and 30 stagger, a heavy blow. Each blow lays Soaked. If it lands, it charges your blade with Water.

**Works with.** Soaked, Undertow, Whirlpool, Heavy Water, a second element's spell to follow it (Static or Chill spreads through the Soaked line).

**Example.** A row of soldiers comes up a corridor: the serpent runs through them and bunches them at the far end, all Soaked.

## water.heavy
**What it does.** Striking a Soaked enemy makes your weapon break balance faster for a few seconds.

**When it happens.** Each time a weapon blow or a spell of yours strikes an enemy that is already Soaked. Each new strike starts the time again.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Your weapon's blows deal 20% more stagger for 3 seconds. It can be set going again at most twice a second.

**Works with.** Soaked, Flowing Guard (30% more stagger after a perfect guard, on top), Ebb and Flood, heavy blows and finishers, Riptide and River Serpent to soak first.

**Example.** Soak a brute, then work it with light blows: its balance bar fills a fifth faster and the finisher comes sooner.

## water.whirlpool
**What it does.** You open a whirlpool where the enemy you face stands. For five seconds it bites everything standing in it and keeps it Soaked. As built it does not draw enemies to its middle.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). With no enemy faced it opens 6 metres ahead of you.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. On an enemy faced within 6 metres. The whirlpool is 4 metres in radius and lasts 5 seconds. It bites every half second for 3 damage, with no stagger, and lays Soaked with each bite.

**Works with.** Soaked, Heavy Water (each bite counts as a spell on a Soaked enemy), Undertow, a second element's Static or Chill, Steam if you carry Fire.

**Example.** Open it under a pack: for five seconds every one of them stays Soaked, ready for one Thunderclap to charge them all.

## water.rain
**What it does.** You call rain over the ground in front of you. For ten seconds drops fall inside its circle, each warned by a ring closing on the spot, and strike and soak what stands there. Every other drop falls where an enemy stands. The circle stays where you cast it.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The circle is 7 metres in radius, just ahead of where you stand. A drop every 0.6 seconds for 10 seconds, landing 0.34 seconds after its ring, 1.2 metres in radius: 3 damage, no stagger, and Soaked.

**Works with.** Soaked, Heavy Water, Whirlpool, Spring Tide's mana for the larger spells after it, and a second element's Static or Chill to spread through the soaked crowd.

**Example.** Cast it in the middle of a room and fight inside it: everything that comes at you arrives Soaked.

## water.spring
**What it does.** A spell paid in mana takes all the mana you hold, and is made greater by what it takes: it strikes harder and reaches further. Basic spells, which cost no mana, are not changed.

**When it happens.** Each time you cast a spell that has a mana price (Poured Blade, or a duo spell such as Strangling Vine), with at least its price in hand.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. The spell's damage is multiplied by the mana you hold divided by its price, up to two and a half times; its reach by the square root of that. All your mana goes. Poured Blade's copy strikes for a share of your own blows, so it does not grow.

**Works with.** Strangling Vine and the other duo spells paid in mana, Catch the Rain and perfect guards to fill mana fast, Standing Rain and Whirlpool cast first while mana builds.

**Example.** At 100 mana, Strangling Vine (45) strikes for over twice its damage, and leaves you with nothing for the surge.

## water.ebbflood
**What it does.** A perfect guard brings the Flood: a splash soaks the attacker and those beside it, and for a while your blows strike harder. A heavy blow that lands brings the Ebb: for a while your guard and dodge cost half the stamina and the moment of your perfect guard is longer.

**When it happens.** The Flood with every perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands). The Ebb with every heavy blow or finisher that lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Flood: a splash 2.5 metres round the attacker, 6 damage, 8 stagger and Soaked; then 15% more weapon damage for 5 seconds. Ebb: dodges and blocked blows cost half the stamina for 4 seconds, and the perfect guard's moment is 22% longer for as long.

**Works with.** Flowing Guard, Unbroken Chain, Long Flood (a longer perfect guard still), Heavy Water, Moon on Water.

**Example.** Land a heavy blow, then guard the answer perfectly with the Ebb's wider moment: the Flood soaks it and your next cuts land 15% harder.

## water.flowing
**What it does.** After a perfect guard, for a moment you swing faster and your blows break balance faster.

**When it happens.** Every perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 30% faster swings and 30% more stagger from your weapon, for 2 seconds.

**Works with.** Ebb and Flood, Unbroken Chain (its quicker swings add to these), Heavy Water, Long Flood, Grey Tide.

**Example.** Perfect-guard a soldier's thrust and answer at once: the quick, heavy cuts often break it before it recovers.

## water.unbroken
**What it does.** A dodge, a blocked blow or a perfect guard quickens your swings for a moment, so you flow from defence straight back into attack. As built it does not yet carry your place in a chain through the dodge or guard.

**When it happens.** When a dodge ends, when you block a blow, and with every perfect guard.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. 20% faster swings for 1.5 seconds. Each new dodge or guard starts the time again; it does not grow.

**Works with.** Flowing Guard (30% more on a perfect guard, on top of this), Ebb and Flood's cheaper guard and dodge, River Without End, Moon on Water.

**Example.** Block a soldier's blow and cut back at once: the first cuts come a fifth faster.

## water.moon
**What it does.** You stand open, ready. The first blow that reaches you, from any side, is turned for nothing and answered with one wide cut that leaves everything it strikes Soaked. If nothing comes, the stance goes out and the cast is spent. A dodge lets it go.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4), then any blow in the next 3 seconds.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Ready for 3 seconds. The answer is a cut 4 metres long and 200 degrees wide: 30 damage and 45 stagger, a heavy blow, and Soaked. If it lands, it charges your blade with Water.

**Works with.** Soaked, Heavy Water, Flowing Guard and Unbroken Chain for the cuts that follow, Ebb and Flood.

**Example.** Surrounded, cast it as the ring closes: the first swing is turned, and the answer catches nearly everyone round you.

## water.river
**What it does.** You swing faster, but each blow strikes softer. As built it is only that: the chain that runs back into its own start is still to come.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. 15% faster swings. Weapon blows deal 12% less damage.

**Works with.** Unbroken Chain and Flowing Guard (their quickness adds to this), Soaked and every mark that answers each blow, the charged blade, Wet Seed.

**Example.** With the Twin Knives, more blows a second means more Soaked, more mana and quicker spells back, for a little less damage each.

## water.spilled
**What it does.** Drinking a flask throws a ring of water round you that soaks everyone near and pushes them back.

**When it happens.** Each time you drink a flask and it heals.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Everything within 3.5 metres of you takes 1 damage, 15 stagger and Soaked, and is pushed 2.5 metres away.

**Works with.** Clear Water and Earned Water, Undertow (pull them back in), Broken Gourd, Wellspring, Morning Dew on the Bloom branch.

**Example.** Cornered and hurt, drink: the ring throws the two soldiers on you back a pace and leaves them Soaked.

## water.gourd
**What it does.** Holding the flask button throws the flask instead of drinking it. Where it breaks it strikes and soaks everyone near and leaves a pool that keeps what stands in it Soaked. The throw also mends you a little at once. A short press still drinks.

**When it happens.** Hold the flask button. Each throw uses a flask.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Hold 0.3 seconds to throw. It flies at the enemy ahead within 9 metres, or 5 metres ahead. The burst is 2.6 metres in radius: 18 damage, 20 stagger and Soaked. The pool is 1.5 metres in radius and lasts 6 seconds, biting once a second for 1 damage and Soaked. The throw mends you 20.

**Works with.** Still Pool (40% longer), Catch the Rain and Earned Water to win flasks back, Heavy Water, Steam if you carry Fire.

**Example.** A pack gathers at a door: break a gourd on the first one and every one of them is Soaked before you reach them.

## water.catch
**What it does.** A perfect guard fills part of a flask you have used.

**When it happens.** Every perfect guard while you carry fewer flasks than your most.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Each perfect guard fills 29% of a flask: four fill one. Unlike earning a flask back with damage, it works whenever a flask is missing, not only when all are gone.

**Works with.** Broken Gourd and Spilled Water (more flasks to spend), Second Gourd, Deep Well, Ebb and Flood's longer perfect guard, Long Flood.

**Example.** Through a long duel, every few perfect guards put a flask back on your belt.

## water.poured
**What it does.** You call a copy of yourself in water to stand at your side. For twelve seconds it lands each of your weapon blows again, a beat late, for a share of its strength. As built it is the copy only; the longer reach, the mending blows and the wave are still to come.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 30 mana: Water's larger spell. The copy lasts 12 seconds. Each weapon blow that lands is struck again 0.2 seconds later for 30% of its damage, with no stagger, and lays Soaked. One copy at a time.

**Works with.** River Without End and Unbroken Chain (more blows, more echoes), Soaked, Heavy Water, Wellspring.

**Example.** Cast it as a brute is staggered: the finisher lands, then lands again for nearly a third.

## water.well
**What it does.** You carry one more flask, but a flask no longer mends you at once. It fills a well instead, and the well mends you only while you are landing blows.

**When it happens.** Drinking fills the well; it pays out while a weapon blow of yours has landed in the last 3 seconds and you are not at full health. The well shows on the screen as Well.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Four flasks. Each flask adds 70 to the well (more with Clear Water or Wellspring), and the well holds 140 at most. It pays 6 health a second.

**Works with.** Clear Water, Wellspring (flasks hold half again as much), Catch the Rain, Spilled Water, Broken Gourd (the throw still mends 20 at once).

**Example.** Drink early in a fight, then keep cutting: the health comes back as you fight, but stop and it waits.

## water.sea
**What it does.** While Wellspring burns, press the surge button again to flood the ground round you, spending a flask. For six seconds everything in the flood is kept Soaked, and every weapon blow you land runs out across the water as a wave. Then the sea draws back to you, dragging everything with it, and breaks where you face.

**When it happens.** Light Wellspring with the surge button, then press the surge button again before it ends. With no flask left, it says so and waits.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. The flood is 9 metres in radius where you stood. Each wave strikes everything within 3.6 metres of the enemy struck for 40% of the blow and 8 stagger, with Soaked. At the end, everything within 9 metres of you is dragged 4 metres in; 0.55 seconds later the sea breaks in a cone 9 metres long and 120 degrees wide: 55 damage and 90 stagger, a heavy blow.

**Works with.** Wellspring, Long Draught, Soaked, Heavy Water, Catch the Rain (for the flask), a second element's Static or Chill.

**Example.** Flood a crowd, cut into the middle of it for six seconds, then roll clear and face them as the sea breaks.
