# Winter Seed: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Ice and Life share, named for its reaction, Winter
Seed (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Ice and Life"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Ice and Life are both
carried.

## winterseed.shape
**What it does.** Thaw unlocks two new shapes for Winter Seed. At a rest fire, you pick which shape your Winter Seed uses. From then on, it uses that shape instead of the plain one. Both shapes keep the sleep: the seed stops fading while the Chill lasts. They change how it wakes up. **Hard Spring**: it wakes up only when the ice is shattered, twice as hard, and fills the Seeded of everything it reaches all the way. **Heart Under Ice**: waking up heals you instead of hurting enemies, and gives you a ward.

**When it happens.** Every time Winter Seed goes off while you carry both Ice and Life. Winter Seed goes off when Seeded lands on a chilled enemy, or when Chill lands on a seeded one. The seed wakes up when its Chill ends (a thaw or a shatter also ends it). If you have not bought this mark, or you carry only one of the two elements, you get the plain Winter Seed.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Hard Spring: only a shatter wakes it; if the cold just melts away, it doesn't wake. Its thorns deal 24 damage per Seeded stack within 2.5 metres, and fill the Seeded of everything they reach all the way. You heal 3 per stack. Heart Under Ice: thorns deal 6 damage per stack within 2.5 metres. You heal 5 per stack and get one ward. After Winter Seed goes off on an enemy, it can't go off on that enemy again for 4 seconds.

**Works with.** Hoarfrost and Briar Patch to set it up, a heavy blow on a frozen enemy (a shatter is the fastest way to wake it), Green Under the Ice, Seed Under Snow.

**Example.** Use Hard Spring on a frozen, seeded soldier in a pack. Shatter it, and the thorns fill the whole pack's Seeded all the way.

## winterseed.undersnow
**What it does.** An enemy that dies chilled and Seeded leaves a frozen seed on the floor. Step on it to crack it open: you heal, and everything nearby is chilled and Seeded.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy dies with both Chill and Seeded on it.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. The seed lies where the enemy fell for 8 seconds. Step within 1 metre of it to crack it: you heal 8, and everything within 2.5 metres of the seed gets a Chill and a Seeded. At most twice a second.

**Works with.** Winter Seed, Late Frost, Hidden in the Tree.

**Example.** Kill a frosted, seeded soldier, and the soldiers next to it start their own Winter Seed.

## winterseed.latefrost
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a Seeded enemy freezes its feet to the ground.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard (press the guard button just before a blow lands) against an enemy that has Seeded.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Two stacks of Chill, and the enemy is held still for 0.8 seconds.

**Works with.** Winter Seed (the Chill puts the seed to sleep), Briar Patch, Seed Under Snow.

**Example.** A seeded brute swings. Guard it perfectly, and it stands frozen to the ankles with its seed asleep.

## winterseed.hidden
**What it does.** The things you grow are covered in frost. While you stand within 4 metres of one, its frozen bark blocks the first blow that would land on you every 6 seconds.

**When it happens.** Always on once you learn it, while you carry both Ice and Life and stand near something you grew.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. You get a ward every 6 seconds while you stand within 4 metres of something you grew. Every time something you grew hits enemies, each enemy it hits also gets a Chill.

**Works with.** Briar Patch, Sapling and Elder Tree (things to grow), Late Frost.

**Example.** Fight next to your sapling, and the first blow of each exchange glances off its bark.

## winterseed.green
**What it does.** You strike the floor, and lines of green run through the frost to every enemy nearby. On each one that has both Chill and Seeded, the frost breaks and its sleeping seed wakes up right away.

**When it happens.** Cast it from a spell slot while you carry both Ice and Life. Press that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or press 1 to 4). The first time it lands, it charges your blade with Ice.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, detonator, 5 Shards. It costs no mana. After you cast it, you must wait 14 seconds to cast it again. Fighting with your weapon makes the wait shorter. The lines reach only enemies within 9 metres that have both Chill and Seeded, eight at most, nearest first. Each one takes 4 damage and 4 stagger. A frozen one is shattered first. Then its Chill is used up, so Winter Seed (plain or with a shape) wakes up. If no enemy has both, nothing is hit and you get half the wait back.

**Works with.** Winter Seed and its shapes, Seed Under Snow, Late Frost.

**Example.** Put five soldiers to sleep under the frost, then wake them all up at once.

**Not built yet.** An enemy your weapon hit in the last 2 seconds doesn't wake up harder yet.
