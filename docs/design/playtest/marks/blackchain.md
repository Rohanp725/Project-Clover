# Black Chain: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Metal and Dark share, named for its reaction, Black
Chain (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d1_d2.md`, "Metal and Dark"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Metal and Dark are
both carried.

## blackchain.shape
**What it does.** Hidden Chain: opens Black Chain's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Black Chain wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Drawn Tight**: the chain snaps taut at once, the two are dragged together and collide, and the chain falls away. **Shared Wound**: no leash, but the chain holds longer and half of every blow on one is shared with the other.

**When it happens.** Every time Black Chain fires (a Curse landing on a notched enemy, or Sundered on a cursed one) while Metal and Dark are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Black Chain runs (a chain to the nearest other enemy within 6 metres for 5 seconds).

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Drawn Tight: it takes 12 damage and 25 stagger, and so does the nearest other enemy within 6 metres. Shared Wound: chained to the nearest other within 6 metres for 8 seconds, half of every blow shared. Black Chain rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Grasp and Flung Blade to bring the two, Dead Weight, One Noose for Two, The Smith's Net.

**Example.** Two shield bearers side by side: Drawn Tight cracks them together. A brute and a small one: Shared Wound, then cut the small one down and half goes to the brute.

**Not built yet.** Drawn Tight strikes both where they stand rather than dragging them together; Shared Wound's chain does not yet pass a curse laid on one to the other.

## blackchain.deadweight
**What it does.** When one of a chained pair dies, the chain drags the other to where it fell, holds it and Curses it again.

**When it happens.** Each time an enemy carrying both a Curse and Sundered dies, while Metal and Dark are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. As built: everything within 6 metres of where it fell is drawn 3 metres toward it, and what stands within 2 metres is held 1 second and Cursed. At most twice a second.

**Works with.** Black Chain, One Noose for Two, Never Sated.

**Example.** Kill a chained soldier and its partner is hauled to the body, held and cursed for your next blow.

**Not built yet.** It answers any death cursed and notched and draws every enemy near, not only the chain-mate.

## blackchain.rust
**What it does.** Notches on a Cursed enemy do not fade while the curse lasts.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Metal and Dark are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards.

**Works with.** Flung Blade and Sunder (notches), Grasp (curses), Black Chain.

**Example.** Curse a brute and its broken armour stays broken.

**Not built yet.** It does nothing yet: an affliction kept while another lasts is still to come.

## blackchain.noose
**What it does.** A finisher on a chained enemy lands on its chain-mate too, at half its damage and stagger.

**When it happens.** Every finisher that lands on an enemy carrying both a Curse and Sundered.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: half the finisher's damage, and 30 stagger, leaps to the nearest other enemy within 4 metres.

**Works with.** Black Chain, Dead Weight, The Smith's Net, Sunder.

**Example.** Finish a chained captain and its guard reels from the same blow.

**Not built yet.** It leaps to the nearest other enemy, not the chain-mate, and its stagger is a fixed 30 rather than half the finisher's.

## blackchain.net
**What it does.** You throw a net of fine black chain; it opens and falls a moment later, seen only as a shimmer and its shadow growing on the floor. Everything under it is Cursed, notched and held, so Black Chain binds them.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Metal and Dark are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Metal.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, opener, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 16 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 7 metres ahead; it falls 0.4 seconds after the cast, 2 metres in radius: 6 damage, 10 stagger, a notch and a Curse, and held 1.5 seconds.

**Works with.** Black Chain and its shapes, One Noose for Two, Dead Weight.

**Example.** Net a pack and cut through it while it stands chained.

**Not built yet.** It does not yet chain everything under it to everything else under it for 6 seconds, keep them in the circle for 3 seconds, or pass a quarter of every blow to the rest.
