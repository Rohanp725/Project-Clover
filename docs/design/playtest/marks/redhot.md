# Red Hot: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Metal and Fire share, named for its reaction,
Red Hot (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d4.md`, "Metal and Fire"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Metal and Fire are
both carried.

## redhot.shape
**What it does.** Slag or Shirt: opens Red Hot's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Red Hot wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Slag**: the armour runs, dripping molten slag that burns where it falls, so walk it through its friends. **The Burning Shirt**: no sparks; the hot plates cling and sear it, and it cannot raise its guard.

**When it happens.** Every time Red Hot fires (a Burn landing on a notched enemy, or a notch on a burning one) while Metal and Fire are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Red Hot runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Slag: for 5 seconds it drips a pool of slag each second, 0.8 metres round, that burns 3 seconds, 3 damage and a Burn each second to all in it but its wearer. The Burning Shirt: for 5 seconds it cannot guard, and every 1.5 seconds it takes a Burn. Red Hot rests 5 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Heated Edge to bring the two, Strike While Hot, Red-Short, Tipped Crucible, Heated Bronze.

**Example.** A brute in a crowd: Slag, and drive it among them. A shield-bearer: The Burning Shirt, and its guard is gone.

**Not built yet.** Slag drips each second where it stands rather than for each metre it moves. The Burning Shirt does not yet answer each attack it begins (a Burn two deeper and a flinch); it burns on a clock instead and cannot guard for the whole 5 seconds, not only while it burns five deep. Its Burn does not yet bite twice as hard, as with the plain Red Hot.

## redhot.heatededge
**What it does.** Your heavy blows lay a Burn and a notch together.

**When it happens.** Every heavy blow or finisher that lands, while Metal and Fire are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One Burn and one notch.

**Works with.** Red Hot (one heavy blow on a fresh enemy brings both), Strike While Hot.

**Example.** Heavy, and the struck plates glow.

## redhot.strikewhilehot
**What it does.** Each heavy blow on a red-hot enemy gives 4 mana and takes 0.5 s off every running spell cooldown.

**When it happens.** For the 5 seconds after each Red Hot, each heavy blow on that enemy, while Metal and Fire are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. 4 mana and 0.5 seconds off every running spell clock a heavy blow.

**Works with.** Red Hot, Heated Edge, Tipped Crucible.

**Example.** Hammer the glowing brute and your spells come back sooner.

**Not built yet.** It keeps its own 5 seconds from each Red Hot rather than reading the glow.

## redhot.redshort
**What it does.** A finisher on a red-hot enemy crumbles its armour: every notch is spent for 15 damage each, and for 10 s it takes 30% more from your weapon.

**When it happens.** The first heavy blow on an enemy in the 5 seconds after its Red Hot, while Metal and Fire are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. 15 damage for each notch spent, and 30% more from your weapon for 10 seconds; once for each Red Hot.

**Works with.** Heated Edge, Red Hot, Strike While Hot.

**Example.** Notch it deep, make it glow, then crack it open.

**Not built yet.** Any heavy blow crumbles it, not only a finisher, and it keeps its own 5 seconds from each Red Hot.

## redhot.crucible
**What it does.** A crucible of molten bronze hangs at your shoulder for 8 seconds, four pours in it. Each pour that lands strikes 14 and 25 stagger, a Burn and a notch, and the ground burns under it while the bronze is red. A heavy blow tips all that are left.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Metal and Fire are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Metal.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, breaker, 5 Shards, 35 mana: the pair's larger spell. Four pours, each 14 damage, 25 stagger, a notch and a Burn, so Red Hot starts; the ground under each enemy struck burns 3 seconds, 3 and a Burn twice a second.

**Works with.** Red Hot and its shapes, Heated Edge, Strike While Hot, Heated Bronze.

**Example.** Raise the crucible, close in and heavy, and the bronze pours over the pack.

**Not built yet.** The pours are not yet tipped one by one along each heavy swing as tongues 4 metres long cooling from white-gold to black: they fly at the nearest by themselves, or all at once on a heavy blow.

## redhot.heatedbronze
**What it does.** A duo surge. While it burns you glow red to the bone like the bronze guardian: every blow that lands on you or your guard burns and notches the one who struck, your guard costs 30% less stamina, and a perfect guard grips the attacker, burns it three deep and throws it 2 metres.

**When it happens.** Choose it for your surge slot in the Hand at a rest fire, while Metal and Fire are both carried, and light it with the surge button.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo surge, 5 Shards. 40 mana, 10 seconds. As built: a Burn and a notch on whoever strikes you or your guard; your guard's stamina 30% less; a perfect guard lays three Burn and throws the attacker 2 metres.

**Works with.** Red Hot, Tipped Crucible, Heated Edge.

**Example.** Light your surge and let the pack break itself on your guard.

**Not built yet.** The perfect guard throws the attacker at once rather than holding it 1 second first.
