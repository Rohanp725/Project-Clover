# Drowned Weight: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on the duo branch Water and Dark share, named for its reaction,
Drowned Weight (`docs/design/steel/pairs_d5.md`, "Water and Dark"). Drawn in the heart of the chart while Water and
Dark are both carried.

## drownedweight.shape
**What it does.** Bound in Debt: opens Drowned Weight's two shapes. At a rest fire you choose which one your Drowned Weight wears, and from then on it runs instead of the plain one. **Drawn Under**: as it swells it sinks knee-deep into a pool of black water, held, and the others are drawn in toward it. **The Pull of Night**: no burst; while it is swollen each of your blows on it draws the wet enemies near toward it, as the night sea draws all things back.

**When it happens.** Every time Drowned Weight fires (a Curse landing on a Soaked enemy, or water on a Cursed one) while Water and Dark are both carried. Before this mark is bought, or with only one of the two carried, the plain Drowned Weight runs.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. Both: it swells 5 seconds, a fifth slower and taking 15% more from weapon blows. Drawn Under: held 1.5 seconds and everything within 3 metres drawn 2 metres toward it; it bursts as plain when it dies or a heavy blow lands (14 damage, 20 stagger, Soaked and Cursed to all within 4 metres). The Pull of Night: no burst; each weapon blow on it draws everything within 5 metres 1 metre toward it, at most every 0.3 seconds. Drowned Weight rests 4 seconds on each enemy.

**Works with.** Held Under, Deep Swell, The Debt Passes, Under the Black Water.

**Example.** Archers hanging back: The Pull of Night, and every blow on the swollen one drags them in. A tight pack: Drawn Under, then a heavy blow for the burst.

**Not built yet.** Drawn Under's pool is not drawn and pulls the others 2 metres at once rather than 1.5 metres a second. The Pull of Night draws every enemy near, not only the Soaked.

## drownedweight.heldunder
**What it does.** A perfect guard against a swollen enemy pushes it down knee-deep: held 1 second, and the attack it was making is lost.

**When it happens.** Each perfect guard against such an enemy, while Water and Dark are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. It flinches (what it had begun is lost) and is held 1 second (elites 0.4), at most once a second.

**Works with.** Drowned Weight, The Debt Passes, Under the Black Water.

**Example.** Turn the swollen brute's swing and it stands sunk for your heavy blow.

**Not built yet.** It answers any enemy that is both Soaked and Cursed, swollen or not.

## drownedweight.deepswell
**What it does.** Drowned Weight stays swollen 2 seconds longer, and its burst reaches 5 metres.

**When it happens.** Each time Drowned Weight fires.

**The numbers.** Mix, a minor, 1 Shard. The enemy stays swollen and slow for 7 seconds, not 5, and its burst reaches 5 metres, not 4. The same in both shapes.

**Works with.** Drowned Weight and its shapes, Held Under.

**Example.** The burst catches the second rank too.

## drownedweight.debtpasses
**What it does.** When a swollen enemy bursts, the nearest enemy the burst reaches swells at once, and killing a swollen enemy heals you 6 more.

**When it happens.** Each death of a swollen enemy, while Water and Dark are both carried.

**The numbers.** Mix, keystone, 5 Shards. As built: when an enemy both Soaked and Cursed dies, every enemy within 4 metres is Cursed (on purpose this may make them swell in turn), at most once a second, and you heal 6.

**Works with.** Drowned Weight (its burst soaks them all first), Held Under, Under the Black Water.

**Example.** Kill the first swollen soldier and the debt runs down the line.

**Not built yet.** It answers any soaked, cursed enemy that dies, and the curse passes to all within 4 metres, not only the nearest.

## drownedweight.blackwater
**What it does.** Black water opens under you and under the enemy you face. Both sink and each rises out of the other's water: you where it stood, it where you stood, Soaked, Cursed and held knee-deep 1 second. The water you rose from stays 3 seconds as a pool that soaks.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot, while Water and Dark are both carried: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Its first landing charges your blade with Water.

**The numbers.** Mix, duo spell, movement, 5 Shards. No mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 9 metres: 8 damage, 15 stagger, Soaked and Cursed (so it swells), held 1 second (elites 0.4). You and the enemy change places: it ends up where you stood when you cast, and you where it stood. An elite or a god is too heavy to move, so you come up 1.5 metres behind it instead. The pool where you come up is 2.5 metres across and lasts 3 seconds; it soaks and curses once a second. Your next weapon blow within 2 seconds hits half again as hard, and your next heavy blow within 2 seconds is critical.

**Works with.** Drowned Weight, Held Under, The Debt Passes.

**Example.** Drop through to the archer and come up swinging.
