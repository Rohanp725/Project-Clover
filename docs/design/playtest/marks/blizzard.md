# Blizzard: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for every mark on Blizzard, the branch Wind and Ice share.

## blizzard.biting
**What it does.** Your weapon blows lay Unsteady and Chill together, whichever of the two you carry first. A mixed branch's marks are the exception to the rule that weapon blows lay only your first element's affliction.

**When it happens.** Every weapon blow that lands, while you carry both Wind and Ice.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One stack of Unsteady and one of Chill a blow. Your first element's own is not laid twice: once its root is learned, the root's stack is the one a blow lays. Unsteady: 6% more stagger, 15% longer shoves and 8% slower turning a stack. Chill: 7% slower a stack, Frozen at five.

**Works with.** Windchill (it needs both, and your blows lay both), Sliding Ice, Gust or Hoarfrost from a slot for more of either, the charged blade.

**Example.** Wind first: four blows leave a soldier four deep in Unsteady and Chill, and with Windchill it goes on growing colder by itself until it freezes.

## blizzard.windchill
**What it does.** The enemy you face, while it is both Unsteady and chilled, grows colder by itself, a stack at a time, until it freezes. As built only the enemy you face grows colder.

**When it happens.** Every 2 seconds in a fight (an enemy within 12 metres), on the enemy you face if it carries both Unsteady and Chill.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. One stack of Chill every 2 seconds. At five it is Frozen for 2.5 seconds (elites and gods 1 second).

**Works with.** Biting Wind, Cold Front, Sliding Ice, Hoarfrost and Gust, Diamond Dust (a frozen enemy to shatter).

**Example.** Gust a soldier and Hoarfrost it, then keep facing it while you guard: eight seconds later it freezes without a blow from you.

## blizzard.front
**What it does.** A perfect dodge leaves a gust of ice behind you. It strikes the attacker in a cone and leaves it two deeper in Chill, while everything round you is knocked two deeper in Unsteady and thrown back.

**When it happens.** A perfect dodge: a blow passes through the very start of your roll. The cone goes at the one who struck, or at the enemy you face when the blow came from afar.

**The numbers.** Mix, 3 Shards. A cone 4 metres long and 90 degrees wide: 10 damage, 20 stagger and two stacks of Chill. Every enemy within 4 metres of you takes two stacks of Unsteady and is pushed 3 metres away.

**Works with.** Windchill (both afflictions at once), Biting Wind, Empty Air, Rushing Wake, Thin Air, Tailwind.

**Example.** Roll through a brute's swing at the last moment: it is two deep in both and being pushed off you before it has recovered.

## blizzard.sliding
**What it does.** A light blow on a chilled enemy throws everything close to you back, and half a second later ice bursts where the one struck stood. As built it does not yet send a Frozen enemy sliding into a wall to shatter; the push and the burst stand in for it.

**When it happens.** A light blow that lands on an enemy carrying Chill, at most once every 1.5 seconds.

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards. Every enemy within 3.5 metres of you is pushed 5 metres away. The burst is 2.5 metres in radius, 0.5 seconds later: 20 damage and 20 stagger. It lays no affliction.

**Works with.** Biting Wind, Windchill, Hoarfrost, Strong Arm (pushes 30% further), Avalanche.

**Example.** Surrounded, cut a chilled soldier: the ring is thrown 5 metres back and you have room to raise your guard.

## blizzard.whiteout
**What it does.** You call driving snow down on a wide circle round you for eight seconds. Shards of ice fall inside it again and again, each warned by a ring closing on its spot, each a small bite. Half of them fall where an enemy stands. As built the snow does not yet hide you or slow the enemies' turning.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** Mix, 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 12 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. The circle is 7 metres in radius, centred just ahead of you, for 8 seconds. A shard every 0.6 seconds, 0.34 seconds after its ring, 1.2 metres in radius: 3 damage, 4 stagger and one stack of Unsteady.

**Works with.** Windchill (it keeps a crowd Unsteady), Against the Wall (a burst on each enemy a shard strikes), Rising Cut, Biting Wind.

**Example.** Call it as a pack pours through a gate and fight on in the middle: they stay Unsteady while you work.

## blizzard.avalanche
**What it does.** A great head of snow, shaped like a dragon, runs down a lane ahead of you. It strikes everything it passes and carries it along, then its jaws close where it stops and bite hard. As built what it carries is not buried Frozen where it stops.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** Mix, 5 Shards, 50 mana. The lane is 9 metres long and 2 metres to each side; the head takes about 1.3 seconds to run it. Passing: 12.5 damage, 15 stagger and four stacks of Unsteady, once each, and shoved along. The bite at the end is 3 metres in radius: 25 damage, 30 stagger, a heavy blow and four stacks of Unsteady.

**Works with.** Rising Cut (it leaves the bitten five deep), Hammer Down, Sliding Ice, Windchill, the charged blade (Wind's shoves).

**Example.** A line of soldiers on a bridge: send it down the bridge, and the whole line is heaped together at its end and bitten.

## blizzard.dust
**What it does.** Shards from an enemy you shatter do not fall. They hang turning round you, and your next dodge throws them all down its path.

**When it happens.** Each time a frozen enemy is shattered, four shards join the ring round you. The next dodge throws them.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. Four shards for each shatter, twelve at most; they wait up to 40 seconds after the last shatter. The dodge throws them down a lane 9 metres long and 1.1 metres to each side, in volleys of four: each volley deals 24 damage, 16 stagger and one stack of Chill to everything in the lane.

**Works with.** Windchill, Cold Front, Biting Wind (Ice first), a heavy blow on a frozen enemy to shatter it, Ice's charged blade (its blows shatter), Long Stride.

**Example.** Shatter three frozen soldiers, then roll toward the archers: twelve shards go with you and strike each archer three times.
