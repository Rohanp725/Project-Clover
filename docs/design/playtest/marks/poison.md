# Poison: detailed explanations

The long explanation the chart shows for each high-level mark of the Poison tree (roots, majors, spells, keystones and the god's gift).

## poison.venom
**What it does.** Your weapon poisons. Every blow that lands adds a dose, a heavy blow one more, a finisher more again. The poison does not tick quietly: on a slow beat it bites for all its doses at once.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while Poison is your first element: your weapon lays only your first element's affliction, so with Poison second this mark does nothing on blows, and its doses come from your spells, the charged blade and the R2 strike instead.

**The numbers.** 2 Shards. One dose a blow; a heavy blow lays two, a finisher four. Up to eight doses, for 6 seconds, and each new dose starts the time again. Every 2 seconds the poison bites for 2 a dose: 16 at eight. Set off, it deals 6 a dose at once: 48 at eight.

**Works with.** Rupture and Volatile to set it off, Catching to spread it, Left to Ripen and In Time on its beat, Deeper Dose (two doses deeper), Bitter Dose (20% harder bites), Fever (one more dose a blow), and Rot Roots when a Seeded enemy is poisoned.

**Example.** Four light blows and a heavy leave a soldier six doses deep; roll away and the next bite takes 12 from it while you are clear.

## poison.intime
**What it does.** Strike as the poison bites and your blow doses twice. For a moment after that timed blow, every blow you land is critical.

**When it happens.** A weapon blow that lands on a poisoned enemy within a quarter of a second of its poison biting, just before or just after. The extra dose comes only while Poison is your first element; the critical moment comes either way.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One dose more on the timed blow. For 1.2 seconds after it, your blows are critical (one and a half times damage). As built the timed blow itself is not made critical, only the ones after it.

**Works with.** Venom, One Sickness (every enemy bites on one beat, so the timing is the same for all), Lance the Wound (it makes the poison bite when you choose), a heavy blow or finisher in the critical moment, Coiled Strike.

**Example.** Watch the bubbles: as they burst, land a light blow, then a heavy in the next breath, and the heavy lands critical.

## poison.ripen
**What it does.** Poison you leave alone ripens. Each bite that comes without a fresh dose since the last is stronger than the one before. Your afflictions also last longer.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. Any fresh dose, from a blow, a spell or a mark, starts the ripening over.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Each ripe bite adds a quarter of the bite: the first bite after a dose is as usual, the next 25% stronger, then 50%, then 75%. Every affliction you lay lasts 34% longer, so poison 8 seconds where it lasted 6.

**Works with.** Lingering and Still Air (longer still), Slow Death (the poison never fades, so it ripens without end), Bitter Dose, Lance the Wound. It works against Venom's every blow, so dose deep, then step back and guard.

**Example.** Put eight doses on a brute and stop hitting it: its four bites come for 16, 20, 24 and 28.

## poison.lance
**What it does.** A perfect guard makes the attacker's poison bite at once, and the poison in every enemy near it with it. Each bites at its own depth.

**When it happens.** A perfect guard: press the guard button just before a blow lands.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Every poisoned enemy within 5 metres of the attacker bites in the next moment, as hard as its beat would. Its own beat goes on as before, so it can bite again soon after.

**Works with.** Venom, Left to Ripen (a ripe bite called early), In Time (strike as it bites), Volatile (it still makes poison bite, though the beat is stilled), Catching (each bite throws a dose on), Waiting Game (more mana from perfect guards), Calm Pulse.

**Example.** Three soldiers at eight doses press you: guard the first swing perfectly and all three take 16 at once, off their beat.

## poison.coiled
**What it does.** You step through the enemy you face in an instant and come out on its far side, facing back, cutting everything on the line and poisoning it deeply. If the cut lands, your next heavy blow soon after is critical. Nothing can strike you in the step; a wall, or something too big to shove, stops you short.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. It goes up to 6 metres, ending 1.8 metres past the enemy faced (with none, 4.5 metres ahead). The cut is 1.1 metres to each side of the line: 24 damage, 25 stagger and four doses. A heavy blow within 3 seconds is critical. As built it is the step alone: the hold that coils it through three fangs is not made yet.

**Works with.** Venom, Rupture (four doses toward full), In Time, Blister, and the charged blade: if it lands it charges your weapon with Poison.

**Example.** Step through a shield-bearer to its back, turn, and the heavy blow you owe it lands critical.

## poison.slowdeath
**What it does.** Your weapon wounds far less. Your poison goes far deeper, and it never fades while the one carrying it lives.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. The greater depth holds while Poison is your first element; the poison stops fading either way.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Weapon damage 30% less. Poison holds twelve more doses, up to twenty (twenty-two with Deeper Dose): at twenty a bite deals 40 every 2 seconds, and set off it deals 120. Poison you lay does not run out.

**Works with.** Venom, Left to Ripen (it ripens without end), Catching, One Sickness, Volatile (it fills at twenty), Rupture (full is twenty now), Plague Bearer, Clean Blade.

**Example.** Dose a Gate Captain twenty deep and spend the rest of the fight guarding: 40 every two seconds, and it never wears off.

## poison.rupture
**What it does.** A heavy blow on an enemy poisoned to its full depth bursts the poison. All of it lands at once, on it and everyone close to it, and the doses are spent.

**When it happens.** A heavy blow or finisher that lands on an enemy carrying a full dose of poison.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. Set off for 6 damage a dose, 48 at eight, with 4 stagger a dose, to everything within 1 metre of the enemy struck. At most about three times a second. Full is eight doses, ten with Deeper Dose and twenty with Slow Death.

**Works with.** Venom (fill it with blows, end with a heavy), Ripe (15% harder), Wider Rupture (25% further), Buckling (40% more stagger), Rotten Fruit, Blister.

**Example.** Eight doses on a soldier, then a heavy blow: 48 lands at once with the blow, and the soldier beside it takes the same.

## poison.rottenfruit
**What it does.** A poisoned enemy that dies bursts, and everyone the burst reaches takes as many doses as it carried.

**When it happens.** The moment an enemy carrying poison dies, whatever killed it.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. The burst is 3.5 metres in radius: 10 damage, 6 stagger, and as many doses as the dead one had, up to eight. At most a few times a second.

**Works with.** Venom, Catching, One Sickness, Rupture and Volatile (spend the poison or pass it on: choose), Slow Death.

**Example.** Kill a soldier at eight doses in the middle of a pack, and the two beside it start at eight.

## poison.blister
**What it does.** You raise a blister on the enemy you face. The next weapon blow you land on it breaks it in a wide burst that poisons everything round it. Left too long, it withers. With no enemy faced, nothing is raised and the slot waits all the same.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4). Then any blow on that enemy, light or heavy, breaks it.

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 10 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. An enemy within 8 metres; one blister at a time; it waits 12 seconds. The burst is 3 metres in radius round the enemy: 40 damage, 30 stagger and four doses.

**Works with.** Venom, Rupture (four doses toward full), Rotten Fruit, Coiled Strike (step in and break it from behind), Spent Venom.

**Example.** Blister the brute in the middle of a group, cut it once, and everyone round it is four doses deep.

## poison.onebyone
**What it does.** A mark closes under each of the nearest enemies, follows it, and a moment later a column bursts up under each in turn, throwing it into the air.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, 50 mana. Up to six enemies within 10 metres of you; with fewer, the spare columns stand round the first. Half a second's warning, then a column every 0.07 seconds, each 2.5 metres in radius: 30 damage and 20 stagger, a heavy blow, and what it strikes is thrown up for 0.8 seconds. As built the columns lay no poison and pass no depth from one enemy to the next.

**Works with.** Venom and Blister to lay the poison first, Rupture and Volatile, and the charged blade: if it lands it charges your weapon with Poison, whose blows dose twice.

**Example.** Six soldiers round you: all six go up at once, and you have a breath to roll clear or cut.

## poison.volatile
**What it does.** Your poison no longer bites on its beat. It builds until it is full, then bursts by itself, spends itself and begins again.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned. It bursts the moment any affliction fills on a poisoned enemy: usually the poison reaching full depth, but another element's filling bursts it too, at whatever depth it has.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. The burst deals 7 a dose, 56 at eight, with 4 stagger a dose, to everything within 2.5 metres of the enemy. At most twice a second. Full is eight doses, ten with Deeper Dose, twenty with Slow Death (140 then).

**Works with.** Venom, Ripe, Wider Rupture, Buckling, Lance the Wound (it still makes the poison bite), Slow Death, Rotten Fruit. Catching, In Time and One Sickness wait on bites, and there are none.

**Example.** Eight light blows on a soldier and it goes up on the eighth, taking the two beside it with it.

## poison.catching
**What it does.** Each time poison bites, one dose leaps from that enemy to the nearest other enemy within reach.

**When it happens.** Every bite of poison on any enemy, however it was laid.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One dose to the nearest enemy within 4 metres (6 with Long Leap).

**Works with.** Venom, One Sickness (they all bite together, so the doses fly together), Lance the Wound (more bites, more leaps), Rotten Fruit, Plague Bearer, Still Air.

**Example.** Poison one soldier deep and keep it near its friends: every two seconds one of them gains a dose without a blow from you.

## poison.onesickness
**What it does.** The poison in every enemy bites on the same beat. Each poisoned enemy beyond the first that bit on the last beat makes every bite of the next one stronger. Blows on poisoned enemies give a little more mana.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned.

**The numbers.** 3 Shards. One beat every 2 seconds for all. Each bite is 15% stronger for every enemy beyond the first that bit on the beat before: four bitten, the next bites are 45% stronger. Weapon blows on a poisoned enemy give 10% more mana.

**Works with.** Catching, Rotten Fruit, Swinging Censer, Share Alike and Plague Bearer to poison many, In Time (one beat to time against), Spent Venom.

**Example.** Five soldiers poisoned eight deep: every two seconds they all bite at once for 16 each, 60% harder, 25.6 each.

## poison.censer
**What it does.** A smoking censer swings round you on its chain, dosing whatever it passes. When its time is up it flies off.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 8 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. One censer, 3 metres out, for 8 seconds, going round about twice in three seconds. It bites every 1.2 seconds whatever is within 1 metre of it: 4 damage, 4 stagger and a dose. One at a time. As built it leaves no smoke behind it.

**Works with.** One Sickness, Catching, Plague Bearer (both poison what stays near you), Venom, and the charged blade: if it strikes it charges your weapon with Poison.

**Example.** Cast it and stand your ground in a doorway: whatever crowds you is dosed every swing as you guard.

## poison.sharealike
**What it does.** You rush straight through everything before you, untouchable, and come out the far side facing back. Every enemy you pass is struck and poisoned, then a second cut lands on everyone on the line.

**When it happens.** From a spell slot: cast it with that slot's button (hold L2 and a face button, or 1 to 4).

**The numbers.** 4 Shards, no mana: it waits 9 seconds after a cast, less with weapon play. Up to 7 metres along a lane 2 metres wide; a wall or something too big to shove stops you. Passing: 10 damage, 10 stagger and two doses. The cut after: the same again, so four doses on each. As built it doses each one alone: it does not yet raise them all to the depth of the sickest.

**Works with.** One Sickness, Catching, Rotten Fruit, Plague Bearer, and the charged blade: if it lands it charges your weapon with Poison.

**Example.** Rush through a line of three archers: each is four doses deep and you stand behind them.

## poison.plaguebearer
**What it does.** You carry the sickness. Enemies near you are poisoned without a blow, a dose at a time, deeper the longer they stay. Your flasks heal less.

**When it happens.** Always on once learned, while an enemy is near.

**The numbers.** 6 Shards. Every 1.5 seconds, one dose on every enemy within 4 metres of you. Flasks heal 30% less: 31.5 where they healed 45.

**Works with.** Swinging Censer, One Sickness, Catching, Slow Death (nothing fades), Lance the Wound, Fever (its healing makes up for the flasks).

**Example.** Guard in the middle of a pack: in twelve seconds each is eight doses deep without your blade moving.

## poison.cleanblade
**What it does.** While your surge lasts the poison waits: it neither bites nor fades, and every bite it would have bitten is owed. Your blows dose once more. When the surge ends time slows, you flick the blade clean, and every bite owed by every poisoned enemy near lands at once.

**When it happens.** Light the surge (a tap of L2, or F); it begins with whichever surge you wear. The flick comes when the surge ends. The extra dose holds while Poison is your first element.

**The numbers.** A god's gift, never sold; in the playtest it can be bought. One more dose a blow, on top of Fever's. When the surge ends, every enemy within 14 metres takes one bite for each whole 2 seconds it waited, at its depth then, with 20 stagger. Fever's 10 seconds owe five bites: 80 on an enemy at eight doses, 100 at ten.

**Works with.** Venom, Fever, Sweet Fever (a longer surge owes more), Deeper Dose and Slow Death (a deeper bite), Plague Bearer. Lance the Wound, In Time and Catching wait on bites, and there are none while the poison waits.

**Example.** Light Fever, dose a whole pack to eight while it waits, and let the surge run out: each takes 80 at once.
