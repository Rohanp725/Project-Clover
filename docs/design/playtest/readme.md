{product}: Combat Playtest {version}, made {date}

A top-down action game of hard fights with weapons and elements. This build is a
testbed for the combat, and it will be updated as the fighting is reworked. It
starts in the Proving Ground, where everything is open to try.

THERE IS NO SOUND YET. Nothing is wrong with your speakers.

TO START
  Run {exe}. It opens on the whole screen at your display's own size.
  Alt+Enter switches between fullscreen and a window you can resize; so does
  "Fullscreen or window" in the pause menu. Any screen shape should work.
  The title shows what is new: press E, Cross (PlayStation) or A (Xbox) to read
  on, Space, Circle or B to begin.

CONTROLS          Keys and mouse       PlayStation   Xbox
  Move            W A S D              Left stick    Left stick
  Light blow      Left mouse  (or J)   R1            RB
  Heavy blow      Right mouse (or K)   R2            RT     (hold: the charged heavy, on weapons that have one)
  Weapon skill    Q                    Triangle      Y      (costs mana)
  Guard           Left Shift  (or L)   L1            LB     (at the last instant: perfect guard)
  Dodge           Space                Circle        B
  Flask (heal)    R                    Square        X
  Four spells     1 2 3 4              hold L2, then Square Triangle Circle Cross
                                                     hold LT, then X Y B A
  Element surge   F                    L2 (tap)      LT (tap)
  Use / read on   E                    Cross         A
  Pause           Esc                  Options       Menu
  With the mouse, blows and spells go toward the cursor. On-screen prompts name
  the buttons of whatever you used last. Every action can be rebound: pause,
  then Controls.

ON THE UPGRADE CHART (rest at a fire)
  Move from mark to mark, E / Cross / A to learn (or, on a spell you have, to
  put it in the next spell slot), R2 or
  right mouse for the whole chart, Q / Triangle / Y for a mark's details,
  Shift / L1 / LB and left mouse / R1 / RB for the tree before and after,
  R / Square / X for the guide to effects, Space / Circle / B to leave.

PLAYTEST RULES
  - Resting gives unlimited Shards: buy any mark, the gods' gifts included.
  - The chart can be walked into every tree and branch. A mark works while you
    hold its element (and, for a few, its weapon).
  - A fall puts you back at the last fire you rested at with nothing lost.
  - Pause, then "Return to the Proving Ground", from any level.
  - Pause, then "Guide to effects", for every affliction, state and resource.

THE PROVING GROUND
  - The rest fire, in the middle. Rest to heal and open the chart.
  - West: the rack of weapons. Use a stand to take a weapon up. The two gods'
    weapons stand at its end; the empty stands are gods' weapons not built yet.
  - The element altars, round the fire: use one to hold its element.
  - East: training dummies. One stands still; one fights back (red blows cannot
    be guarded: roll); a row carries each element's affliction.
  - North: two pens. Use a post to let out an enemy or a group; the last post
    clears the pen. The castle's soldiers on the left, the Fire land's people on
    the right.
  - Far north: the gates to Narukami (the Summit) and Khepharu (the Sun Court),
    and the doors to the Castle and Sun City.

HOW TO GIVE NOTES
  Nothing in the game collects anything. Please type up your notes and send them
  back to whoever gave you the build. The most useful notes say:
  - what you were doing (weapon, element, which marks, which enemy or place);
  - what you expected to happen, and what did happen;
  - what felt good, what felt bad, what you did not understand;
  - anything the guide or a mark's details explained wrongly or not at all.
  If something goes wrong, please send this file with your note:
  {log}
