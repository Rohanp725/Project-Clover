{product}: Combat Playtest {version}, made {date}

A top-down action game of tough fights with weapons and elements. This build is for
testing the combat, and it will be updated as the fighting is reworked. It starts
in the Proving Ground, where everything is open for you to try.

THERE IS NO SOUND YET. Nothing is wrong with your speakers.

TO START
  Run {exe}. It opens full screen at your display's own size.
  Alt+Enter switches between full screen and a window you can resize. So does
  "Fullscreen or window" in the pause menu. Any screen shape should work.
  The title screen shows what's new: press E, Cross (PlayStation) or A (Xbox) to
  read on, or Space, Circle or B to start.

CONTROLS          Keys and mouse       PlayStation   Xbox
  Move            W A S D              Left stick    Left stick
  Light blow      Left mouse  (or J)   R1            RB
  Heavy blow      Right mouse (or K)   R2            RT     (hold: the charged heavy, on weapons that have one)
  Weapon skill    Q                    Triangle      Y      (costs mana)
  Guard           Left Shift  (or L)   L1            LB     (at the last moment: perfect guard)
  Dodge           Space                Circle        B
  Flask (heal)    R                    Square        X
  Four spells     1 2 3 4              hold L2, then Square Triangle Circle Cross
                                                     hold LT, then X Y B A
  Element surge   F                    L2 (tap)      LT (tap)
  First strike    Left Ctrl + left     hold L2, R1   hold LT, RB   (the first element)
  Second strike   Left Ctrl + right    hold L2, R2   hold LT, RT   (the second element;
                                                     with one element or all in, the
                                                     deep strike, 20 mana, once you
                                                     have bought its Devotion)
  Use / read on   E                    Cross         A
  Pause           Esc                  Options       Menu
  With the mouse, blows and spells go toward the cursor. On-screen prompts show
  the buttons of whatever you used last. You can change the button for every
  action: pause, then Controls.

AT A REST FIRE
  Resting opens the rest menu: Set the Hand, The chart, Leave the fire.
  The Hand is your four spells in a diamond laid out like the buttons, with the
  surge in the middle. Choose a slot (E / Cross / A) to see what can go there,
  Q / Triangle / Y for the numbers, and Space / Circle / B to go back. Below are
  the Suggested Hand and three saved Hands: E / Cross / A puts one on, and the
  heavy blow saves the Hand you have on there.

ON THE UPGRADE CHART (rest at a fire, then choose The chart)
  Move from mark to mark. E / Cross / A learns a mark (or, on a spell you
  already have, puts it in the next spell slot). R2 or right mouse shows the
  whole chart. Q / Triangle / Y shows a mark's details. Shift / L1 / LB and
  left mouse / R1 / RB move to the tree before and after. R / Square / X opens
  the guide to effects. Space / Circle / B leaves.

PLAYTEST RULES
  - Resting gives unlimited Shards: buy any mark, including the gods' gifts.
  - You can walk the chart into every tree and branch. A mark works while you
    carry its element (and, for a few, its weapon).
  - Falling in battle puts you back at the last fire you rested at, with
    nothing lost.
  - Pause, then "Return to the Proving Ground", from any level.
  - Pause, then "Guide to effects", for every affliction, state and resource.
  - F8 switches the elements' effects between the new look and the old one, so
    you can compare them. This switch is temporary and will be removed once the
    new look is settled. Every start begins with the new look.

THE PROVING GROUND
  - The rest fire is in the middle. Rest to heal; its menu sets the Hand and
    opens the chart.
  - West: the weapon rack. Use a stand to pick up a weapon. The two gods'
    weapons stand at its end; the empty stands are gods' weapons not built yet.
  - The element altars are around the fire. You arrive carrying Fire. If you
    carry one element, another altar makes its element your second, and the
    altar of the element you carry makes you all in. If you carry two, an
    altar starts you over with only its element. The prompt tells you which
    before you press.
  - East: training dummies. One stands still; one fights back (you can't
    guard its red blows: roll instead); a row of them each has an element's
    affliction on it.
  - North: two pens. Use a post to let out an enemy or a group; the last post
    clears the pen. The castle's soldiers are on the left, and the Fire land's
    people are on the right.
  - Far north: the gates to Narukami (the Summit) and Khepharu (the Sun Court),
    and the doors to the Castle and Sun City.

HOW TO GIVE NOTES
  Nothing in the game collects anything. Please type up your notes and send
  them back to whoever gave you the build. The most useful notes say:
  - what you were doing (weapon, element, which marks, which enemy or place);
  - what you expected to happen, and what actually happened;
  - what felt good, what felt bad, and what you didn't understand;
  - anything the guide or a mark's details explained wrongly or not at all.
  If something goes wrong, please send this file with your note:
  {log}
