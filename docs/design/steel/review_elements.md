# Review: does each element feel like its own substance? (2026-11-18)

**Applied 2026-11-18: the recommended fixes are now in the content files.** (What changed is listed in `README.md`,
"Review fixes applied (2026-11-18)". This page is kept as the record.)

A combat designer's read of every element piece in `elements_a.md`, `elements_b.md` and the element side of
`depth_elements.md`, against `BRIEF.md`, `README.md`, `STEEL_AND_ELEMENT.md`, `WORLD.md`, `LOOKS.md` and `GUIDE.md`
sections 4 to 6. It answers the owner: "going through the elemental tree in its entirety and checking whether the feel
of the spells and upgrades actually feels like that element or not... For ones we think are weak, be creative and come
up with other options that would hit harder." Nothing in the content files was changed. Everything offered here is a
**Proposal**; every number is a first guess in the ranges the built spells use.

**The questions asked of every piece.** (1) Seen from 12 m above with no name on screen, is it that element, behaving as
that substance? (2) Does it fit the god and culture of the land? (3) Do steel and element feed each other, or can the
player stop swinging? (4) Is it distinct inside its tree and across trees? (5) Does it hit, or is it a quiet number?

**Verdicts.** **Strong**: it could only be this element, and it changes what the player does. **OK**: sound and fair,
but generic in shape, a number, or close to something else. **Weak**: wrong substance, wrong culture, a duplicate, an
empty slot, or a quiet number where a fantasy should be. Every Weak piece gets two or three replacements and a
recommendation. A few OK pieces that could be much better get a note in their row.

**What was counted.** 43 pieces an element (42 for Lightning, which has seven passives): each spell's Learn, both Shapes
and Master (24), the six Empowers as one row, the charged blade, the infused strike, the deep strike, the Devotion,
the reaction lift, the passives with the god's gift, the surge, both Heresies and both Evolutions. 472 pieces in all.

The trees are better than the owner may fear. No element reads as another element's colour; the owner's rule that
each is a substance and never coloured light is kept nearly everywhere (Sunspot is the one real slip). The weaknesses

(Masters, counters and pulls built on one pattern), and **heals and numbers standing where a fantasy should**.

## The ten biggest fixes, ranked

1. **Fill the fourteen empty Shapes.** Grave (Metal); Together, Lamp, The Hand Turns, Dawn (Light); Called In, Behind
   It, Changed Places, Pawned (Dark); Creeping, Snake, Lanced, Share Alike, Snap (Poison) are each "as learned". A
   Shape mark (3 Shards) is meant to open two versions; these open one. A third of three trees' Shapes buy nothing.
   Replacements are given under each element: Clashing Bronze, The Drawn Line, Row of Lamps, Witnessed, Red Before
   the Sun, The Black Buffalo, Ash on the Brow, Return to the Shadow, Buried Heart, Low Mist, Centipede's Run, Weeping
   Sore, Carried on the Blade, Eaten in Turn.
2. **Give each element its own Master timing.** In `elements_b.md` 26 of 30 Masters are tied to a perfect guard, a
   perfect dodge or a finisher, and most of those "reset the cooldown". That is right for Metal (meet the blow) and Lightning (the
   instant), and wrong for the rest: a Poison Master should land on the beat, a Life Master on something grown, a Dark
   Master when you are hurt. A table of timings by substance and the Masters to rewrite follows this list.
3. **Life heals in eleven places; make it grow and bind instead.** Root, Ripening, charged blade, Thorn Set, Dodder,
   Reap, Elder Tree, Red Harvest, the lift, Thin Blood and Regrowth all mend. Replace the four quietest: the charged
   blade becomes **Sprout** (blows plant your garden), Thorn Set becomes **Grown Into It** (the struck enemy becomes a
   grown thing), Thin Blood becomes **The Garden Holds You**, Regrowth's half becomes **The Garden Grows**.
4. **Water pulls too much; let it push and carry away.** Riptide, River Serpent, Out to Sea, Undertow Cut, Whirlpool,
   Lash, Reeled In and Undertow all draw enemies to you (and Dark pulls in seven more places). Water that only pulls
   is a magnet. Reeled In becomes **Thrown on the Reef** (the wave throws it into a wall), Standing Rain becomes **Rip
   Current** (a lane that carries them away).
5. **Sunspot becomes Black Pitch.** A swelling sun over the head is the image the owner rejected in Khepharu's fight,
   and its "fed by blows, popped by a heavy" repeats Blister and Reckoning. Black Pitch is bitumen smeared by your
   blows across a crowd, inert until fire touches it, then all at once: fire that must be fed.
6. **Replace the lifts that are a heal or a block.** Every Debt Collected (lifesteal), Bitter Mix (heal), The Hill's
   Weight ("cannot be pushed", which stops the Earth pairs Topple and Frost Heave from throwing). They become **Collected
   Twice**, **The Delayed Touch** and **Left in Rubble**.
7. **Cut the cross-element copies.** Ice's Unmoved is Earth's Heavy Hand; Life's Thin Blood is Dark's Near the Grave;
   Life's Thorn Ring is Earth's Closed Ring; Lightning's Planted Bolt is Lightning Rod in an enemy. They become **Sent
   Sliding** (a frozen body sent down the ice), **The Garden Holds You**, **Burrs** and **Stepped Leader**.
8. **Moon on Water becomes Blowhole.** "Moon on water" is the Japanese sword-mind; the Water land is Polynesian.
   Tonga's Mapu'a 'a Vaea blowholes store the sea's push and throw it up 20 m: the same store-and-release counter, now
   a spout of the land's own sea that lifts the attacker for your blade.
9. **Turn the three quiet charged blades into acts.** Wind's "+1 m reach" is invisible; Life's "mends 3" and Dark's
   "10% back as health" are numbers. They become **Gale Edge** (the blow shoves, the third throws into what is
   behind), **Sprout** and **Shadow Cut Loose** (the third blow cuts the enemy's shadow off its feet, and your blows on
   the shadow strike the body).
10. **Re-aim the three weakest surge halves.** Kindle's "blows cut cooldowns twice" is not fire, Wellspring's flask
    trade still asks to be hoarded, Regrowth's "mend 6 a spell" is more healing. They become **Fed Flame** (Kindle
    lasts while you feed it), **The Flask Breaks** (every flask drunk bursts out of you as a wave) and **The Garden
    Grows**.

Smaller notes that run through every tree, for the reconciler:
- **Three counters are one parry.** Kiln Stance, Mountain Stance and Circle of Steel all "turn the first blow, red ones
  too, and answer from below or around; open 0.4 s if nothing comes". Each has one strong Shape (Shut Door, Grey
  Stone, Ringing Plate); consider making those Shapes the Learn.
- **Three infused strikes throw an arc along the swing** (Burning Edge, Cutting Wind, Ray Along the Edge). Fine as a
  family, but Cutting Wind is the one most like any game's sword beam.
- **"Passes any guard" is spent in eight places** (Metal's charged blade, Red Iron, The Golden Chair, Red Paper, The Sun
  on the Blade, Yama's Noose, Under the Mound's Master, Spoken Sentence's blade). Keep it Metal's and Light's; give the
  others another reward.
- **Decoys**: Left Standing is replaced (Ice, below); Left Behind, Changeling and Shed Skin stay because each is the
  only decoy in its tree.

### Sunspot (Weak): options

**A. Black Pitch.** Detonator, 11 s. Throw a jar of embalmer's pitch at the enemy faced (within 7 m); it is coated, no
damage. For 8 s every weapon blow on a coated enemy splashes pitch onto every enemy within 2 m of it. Pitch takes no
Burn and does nothing, until fire touches any coated enemy (a Burn laid, a burning patch, a reaction): then every
coated enemy goes up at once, 16 damage plus 8 for each blow that spread pitch (70 at most), 35 stagger, 3 Burn each,
and the floor under each burns 3 s. *From above*: glossy black splashes travelling body to body with your swings, then
one sweep of flame racing over all the black, with heavy black smoke. *Steel*: blows spread the fuel and a fire spell,
Cinder or a burning patch lights it; a sweep through a crowd is how you load it. *Myth*: the bitumen of the city of
wicks, where the dead "burn and are not consumed". **New code**: a coat that lights on any Burn. (Uraeus's spit would
then be drawn as burning venom, so pitch is this spell's alone.) Shapes to go with it: **Pitch Pool** (thrown on the
floor, a 3 m pool that coats what walks through) and **The Wick** (one enemy coated deep; lit, it burns 8 s and sets
alight everyone within 2 m each second as it walks). Master: light it with the third charged blow and the blast
doubles.

**B. The Floor Catches.** Detonator, 11 s. A circle of floor 4 m across begins to scorch over 6 s, browning, then
smoking, then catching at the rim. Every Burn on an enemy inside it is counted. At the end, or on your heavy blow inside
it, it flashes over: each enemy inside has its Burn set off (10 a stack) and the circle burns as ground fire for 4 s.
*From above*: a ring of floor going brown, then grey smoke, then a line of flame running round it. *Steel*: the heavy
sets it off early; pull enemies in with Riptide or Grasp. A detonator of a place, not a body, so it repeats nothing.

**Recommendation: Black Pitch.** It is the only fire detonator in any game that is about fuel, it ties to the land's
wicks, and steel spreads it.

### Kindle's new half (Weak): options

**A. Fed Flame.** While Kindle burns, each weapon blow that lands on a burning enemy adds 0.4 s to it, up to twice its
length; each blow you take smothers 2 s. *From above*: the flames on the player swell at each feeding blow and gutter,
smoking, when struck. *Steel*: the weapon is the fuel.

**B. Wildfire.** While it lasts, once a second every burning enemy within 6 m of you passes 1 Burn to the nearest
unburnt enemy within 3 m of it. *From above*: embers hopping from body to body. *Steel*: Cinder lights the first; the
fire does the rest.

**C. The Lioness.** Sekhmet who would not stop. Each kill while it burns adds 2 s and makes your next blow critical; it
cannot be ended early, and when it ends you are left burning two deep. *From above*: a lion's mane of flame on the
shoulders that grows with each kill.

**Recommendation: Fed Flame.** It says "fire must be fed" in one rule, and it is the only surge that lasts as long as
the player earns it with the blade.

### Planted Bolt (Weak): options

**A. Stepped Leader.** The lance is not thrown. A bolt steps out from your blade along the stick, one bright step of
1.5 m every 0.15 s, up to 12 m, and you steer it while you keep moving and striking; each step lays 1 Static on enemies
within 1.5 m. Let go, or land a heavy blow, and the return stroke lights the whole path at once: 14 damage to all on it,
and each discharges. *From above*: a jagged line drawn across the floor step by step, faint forks off each step, then
the whole line white for a frame. *Steel*: the heavy blow calls the return stroke. *Real*: the stepped leader and the
return stroke (NOAA).

**B. Upward Streamers.** The lance is driven into the floor 6 m ahead. Every enemy carrying Static within 8 m sends up a
crackling streamer toward it, and 0.6 s later one stroke joins them all: 12 damage and 6 a Static each, discharging.
*From above*: thin threads rising off charged bodies and meeting in a star. *Steel*: your blows lay the Static that
makes the streamers.

**Recommendation: Stepped Leader.** It is the one Lightning piece where the player draws the path, and it pairs with
Return Stroke as the two halves of a real strike.

**Recommendation: Weeping Sore.** It makes the detonator slow and insidious, and stops it repeating Sunspot's and
Reckoning's swell-and-pop.

### Share Alike, as learned (Weak): options

**A. Carried on the Blade.** Rush through as learned, but take the sickest one's depth onto your blade: your next five
weapon blows each set what they strike to that depth. *From above*: venom dripping heavy from the edge, five beads to
count down. *Steel*: the rush loads the weapon.

**B. Bad Air.** The rush leaves a lane of mist behind you 7 m long for 4 s.

**Recommendation: Carried on the Blade.** It turns a movement spell into a reason to swing.

### Snap (Weak): options

**A. Eaten in Turn.** The line is drawn but nothing bursts. For 8 s, when an enemy on it dies, all its doses pass to the
next on the line, doubled; one pushed past eight bursts at once (6 a dose) and passes on. *From above*: the green line
glowing brighter at each link fed. The gu's rule, where the order of killing matters.

**B. The Slow Line.** The bursts go one a beat, 2 s apart, each larger than the last.

**Recommendation: Eaten in Turn.** It is the land's jar in a spell, and the blade chooses the order.

### Bitter Mix (Weak): options

**A. The Delayed Touch.** Every reaction poison takes part in is held in the body: a thin ring of mist closes on it, and
it lands 2.5 s later at double strength, dosing all it reaches two deep; a heavy blow on that enemy brings it on at
once. *From above*: a closing green ring round the body. *Steel*: the heavy chooses the moment. *Myth*: the legend of the
delayed death touch of the pressure points.

**B. It Will Not Fade.** Poison on anything a reaction struck does not fade while the other affliction stays on it.

**Recommendation: The Delayed Touch.** Poison is paid later; this is that rule, given to every pair.
