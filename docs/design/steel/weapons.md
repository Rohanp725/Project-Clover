Written by Claude on 2026-11-18 for `docs/design/STEEL_AND_ELEMENT.md` sections 7 (the weapon tree) and 8.2 (Pair

## 0. How every Way is laid out

### 0.1 Shape

- **The trunk** works in every aspect: five temper marks, four infusion marks (one per element family), the gated
  technique (once opened), and at the top the Bond mark.
- **Three boughs**, each an aspect: a form of the weapon, chosen at a rest fire, one active at a time (Decided).
  Opening a bough gives its form at once ("What changes"); four marks and a keystone deepen it. Marks bought on a
  bough that is not active are kept and sleep.
- **A god's bough** is the fourth (Decided): the god's weapon itself, its look and its moves. Take opens it for
  nothing; its marks cost as any other. For the three new kinds the god's bough sits beside two plain ones, and
  Consume gives the plain weapon with those two (Decided).
- The moves as built stay the weapon's base. An aspect says only what it changes.

### 0.2 Costs and kinds

| Kind | Memories | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The aspect | 2 | Opens the bough and its form (free for a god's bough after Take) |
| Number | 1 | A plain number |
| Move | 2 | A new move, input or chain step |
| Rule | 2 | Changes how something works |
| Keystone | 4 | The end of the bough; changes how the weapon plays |
| Temper I to V | 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 | Raw damage (0.3) |
| Infusion | 2 each | One per element family (0.4) |
| Technique | 2 | Only after its elite falls (0.5) |

A bough is about 13 Memories; a whole Way about 58. As a scale: an elite should pay about 3, a god 6, each new kind
of enemy 1 the first time.

### 0.3 Temper (Decided: the only way raw damage grows)

Each of Temper I to V gives +6% weapon damage and +5% stagger (+30% and +25% at V). Temper III and V each add one
line of the weapon's own, written in its section. Temper is on the trunk, so it serves every aspect and the god's
bough alike. The god's weapon keeps its built +15% on top (the Storm Katana's), so a god's weapon at Temper V is
about half as strong again as a bare plain one: the reason to Take.

### 0.4 Infusion

**The carrier.** On every Way the infused strike (L2 with R1 or R2, section 3.4) is one of the weapon's own moves,
its **carrier**, played with the element on it. The element writers set the generic effect of each element's
infused strike; the carrier gives it a shape. The cost is one shared rule (`README.md`): each strike button has its own 8 s
cooldown that weapon play shortens, and all in, the deep strike on L2 with R2 also costs 20 mana. Each weapon names its
carrier below.

**The families.** An infusion mark bends the carrier for every element of one family:

| Family | Elements | What the substance does to a strike |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | Fire, Poison, Life | It is left lying on the floor along the strike |
| Line | Lightning, Wind, Light | It carries the strike, or the bearer, further and faster |
| Weight | Earth, Metal, Ice | It sets: the strike holds, stands or pins |
| Draw | Water, Dark | It drags what it touched |

All in on one element (5.6), L2 with R2 is the deeper version: the family mark's length, time or pull is doubled.
The element upgrades already written in `docs/design/weapons.md` (Brand, Glacier Edge, Undertow, Live Wire and the
rest) are kept as extra infusion marks for their element on their weapon, not rewritten here. Two of them share a
name with a built tree mark (Water's Undertow, Lightning's Live Wire) and take new names when they are built. The
five built tree marks that need the Katana come here too (Katana, Infusion).

### 0.5 Gated techniques (Decided: one per elite, its own weapon only, shown in the fight first)

- A technique is a mark on the trunk, sealed until its elite falls, then bought for 2 Memories. It waits if the
  weapon is not owned yet, and works in every aspect (Decided).
- **Input**: every technique is **R1 and R2 pressed together** (keyboard: both mouse buttons). The chord is used by
  nothing else, so a technique never collides with an aspect's own inputs.
- The elite uses the same move against the player, with the same tell, before the player can learn it.

### 0.6 Bond (Decided in 8.1)

Weapon Bond ranks (Proposal): I, recoveries 4% shorter; II, the finisher +10%; III, recoveries 8% shorter; IV, the
finisher +20%; V, the **Bond mark**: the active aspect's keystone in its **true form**. Each aspect below gives its
true form in one line.

## 1. Katana

Built: drawing cut, return cut, stepping cut; overhead; Iaido Slash; a sheathed finisher. **Carrier**: the drawing
cut, made at once (0.18 s wind-up, 22 damage, 170 degrees, carries 1.6 m).

### The Sheath (iai)

From the four parts of a drawn cut in Musō Jikiden Eishin-ryū: the draw, the finishing cut, shaking off the blood,
the return to the sheath. **What changes**: the sword goes home after every chain or 0.6 s idle, and the first blow
of every chain, the draw, deals 1.5 times with +10 stagger. The chain is the draw, the return cut, the sheathe; the
stepping cut is gone.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quick Sheath | Move | 2 | Guard pressed in a cut's recovery sheathes in 0.15 s; the next R1 is a draw |
| Held Draw | Move | 2 | Hold R1 0.4 s: you sink, creeping at a quarter speed. Let go: a draw down a lane 5 m by 1 m that carries you 3 m. 28 damage, 36 stagger |
| Shake the Blood | Move | 2 | R1 just after a kill: one flick of the blade that clears the recovery and sheathes; the next draw +20% |
| Sharp Draw | Number | 1 | The draw's 1.5 becomes 1.7 |
| **Keystone: The Sword Stays Home** | Keystone | 4 | Every blow from the sheath counts as a heavy: it discharges Static, shatters the Frozen, and no guard turns it. The return cut is gone: every chain is one draw and a sheathe, 0.7 s |

True form: the draw cuts twice down the same line, the second 0.12 s later for 60%.

### The Stream (flow)

**What changes**: five cuts in place of three (the built three, then a turning cut, 10, 200 degrees, and a rising
cut, 14). A dodge in any cut's recovery no longer ends the chain; the next R1 carries on from where it was.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| River Bend | Move | 2 | R2 in the middle of the chain is a quick overhead (26) that does not end it |
| Stepping Water | Move | 2 | R1 during a dodge: a cut out of the roll (12, 140 degrees) that counts as the chain's next step |
| Light Wrist | Number | 1 | From the third cut on, cuts are 8% quicker |
| Second Breath | Rule | 2 | Each cut from the fourth on that lands gives back 4 stamina |
| **Keystone: No Last Cut** | Keystone | 4 | After the fifth cut the first follows with no pause. Each loop +8% damage, three loops at most. The chain ends only when you are struck, guard or stop pressing. Mana from light blows is halved |

True form: up to five loops, and the bonus is kept 3 s after the chain ends.

### The Answer (counter)

**What changes**: a perfect guard opens a 0.6 s answer, shown as a white flick down the blade. R1 in it: **Riposte**,
a straight cut, 24, critical. R2 in it: **Through the Gap**, a step through to the attacker's back, 30 and 40
stagger. Light blows deal 10% less.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turned Edge | Number | 1 | Perfect guards throw 25% more stagger |
| Catch the Red | Rule | 2 | After a perfect guard on a red attack the answer lasts 1.0 s, and the Riposte breaks the balance of all but elites and gods (they take 60) |
| Guard Cut | Move | 2 | R1 while guarding: a short cut (8) with the guard still raised. Does not chain |
| Patient Blade | Rule | 2 | Each 0.5 s the guard is held unstruck, the next answer +10%, to +40% |
| **Keystone: Wait for Them** | Keystone | 4 | The answer comes by itself: every perfect guard is followed at once by the Riposte, or by Through the Gap if R2 is pressed within 0.3 s. The perfect window is 0.03 s wider. The light chain is two cuts |

True form: the Riposte cuts every enemy whose blow was turned in the last second, not only the last.

### Narukami's Storm Katana (Lightning)

**What changes**: his own sword, longer by 0.3 m, storm-grey with a crawling scar of lightning down the fuller and
the shrine's paper streamers at the hilt; +15% damage and stagger and the Thunder Draw skill, as built. The chain is
his: across, back, and down (the third a downward cut, 22, in a 3 m lane).

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Red Draw | Move | 2 | Hold R2 past the skill: sheathe, sink, hold 0.6 s, then go through along a 10 m lane to stand behind. 40 damage, 50 stagger, untouchable on the lane, 40 mana. His red draw, given |
| Flank Step | Move | 2 | Dodge, then R1 within 0.2 s: you step through a line of lightning to the faced enemy's side (up to 6 m) and cut as you arrive (18) |
| Seven Drums | Rule | 2 | Each cut that lands beats one of seven drums drawn at the guard; at the seventh the next draw +30% |
| Long Blade | Number | 1 | Reach +0.2 m more |
| **Keystone: Thunder Follows** | Keystone | 4 | Every draw leaves its lane on the floor. 0.5 s later thunder runs down it and strikes all on it again for half the draw and 30 stagger: the flash, then the sound |

True form: the thunder runs down the lane twice, at 0.5 s and 1.0 s.

**Temper.** III: the drawing cut +0.2 m reach. V: the finisher's held pose 0.2 s longer and its blow +15%.

**Infusion** (on the drawing cut). Ground: the draw's arc is left lying alight with the substance for 3 s.
Line: the draw becomes a dash of 5 m through every enemy on its lane. Weight: the draw slows to 0.32 s and cannot
be interrupted, stops in the first enemy and sets the substance in it, +60% stagger. Draw: the return cut follows by
itself and drags all it struck 1.5 m onto the point.

**Element infusion marks** (the built tree marks that need the Katana, moved off the element trees; 2 Memories each,
working while their element is carried): Burning Crescent (Fire), Delayed Cuts (Lightning), Drawing Wind (Wind),
Winter Draw (Ice) and Answering Draw (Light, from Halo). Each keeps its built rule on the Iaido Slash.

**Gated technique: Before the Thunder.** Taught by the Lightning land's elite (Proposal: the Last Retainer, 14).
R1 and R2 together: a draw that is quickest to land of any move (0.12 s). If it strikes an enemy that is winding up
an attack, red ones included, the attack is broken and the enemy takes 50 stagger. If it strikes nothing in a
wind-up it is a plain draw with a long recovery (0.6 s). The Retainer stands sheathed and never moves first: when the
player begins a heavy, a charge or a skill, he cuts it before it lands.

## 2. Massive Sword

Built: three sweeps with poise, the buried overhead, the rising cut, the leaping charged overhead, Elemental Spike
Line, The Headsman. **Carrier**: the rising cut (dragged up off the floor, 120 degrees, 46).

### The Wall

**What changes**: the planted-blade guard can be held as a stance. Held 0.6 s it plants fully: you cannot walk,
blocked blows cost half, and it covers 260 degrees.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barge | Move | 2 | R1 while guarding: the shoulder into the flat, shoving 1.5 m (10, 30 stagger); the guard stays up |
| Uproot | Move | 2 | R2 while guarding: the blade torn out of the floor in a rising cut (34, 50 stagger) |
| Weight of the Door | Rule | 2 | Each blow blocked adds 15% to the next Uproot, four at most |
| Gatepost | Number | 1 | Chip through the planted guard 25% to 15% |
| **Keystone: Stand Like Stone** | Keystone | 4 | Fully planted, no blow but a red one moves you, flinches you or breaks the guard: at no stamina it still holds. A red blow on the planted guard is turned as a perfect guard if the guard was pressed within 0.3 s, not 0.15 |

True form: the planted guard turns a red blow from the front by itself, once every 8 s.

### Half-Sword

From the German longsword's half-swording (the left hand on the blade to thrust) and the murder stroke (the sword
reversed, the cross struck like a hammer). **What changes**: R2 while guarding shifts grip (0.3 s). In the short
grip the light chain is three thrusts down a lane 2.6 m by 0.8 m (0.18 s wind-ups, 16 each) and the guard is a
narrow parry (160 degrees, perfect window 0.19 s). The long grip is the sword as built.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Murder Stroke | Move | 2 | Short grip, R2: the sword reversed, held by the blade, the cross brought down like a hammer. 30, 70 stagger, through any guard |
| Pommel | Move | 2 | The third short thrust is a pommel blow that knocks the enemy back 1 m (12, 30 stagger) |
| Quick Shift | Number | 1 | Grip change 0.3 s to 0.15 s |
| Between the Plates | Rule | 2 | Short thrusts on a guarding enemy go through at half |
| **Keystone: Two Swords in One** | Keystone | 4 | A grip change in the middle of a chain no longer ends it: the next blow is the other grip's next step, and each change in one chain adds 10% to the blows after it, three at most |

True form: the murder stroke strikes twice, the cross and then the pommel.

### The Trade

**What changes**: +10 poise on every swing. A blow taken under poise during a swing's wind-up or swing is a
**trade**: that swing lands +30%, and a red spark runs down the blade to say so.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blow for Blow | Number | 1 | A traded swing +30% to +45% |
| Hard Head | Rule | 2 | The grey health from a traded blow is not wiped by it |
| Into the Teeth | Move | 2 | The charged heavy no longer stops when you are struck (poise 30 through the whole charge) |
| Headsman's Due | Rule | 2 | A finisher makes real the grey health taken in the last 4 s |
| **Keystone: Let Them Strike** | Keystone | 4 | Poise is 40 in every swing; damage taken while swinging is halved; every trade fills the striker's stagger by 25. The dodge costs 40 stamina |

True form: a trade also gives back the stamina its swing cost.

### Brannoch's Standing Stone (Earth)

**What changes**: the giant's sword, a grey slab of standing stone, lichened, its edge knapped sharp, a grip bound
in hide; +15%. The heavy and the charged heavy plant a stone where the blade lands: 1.2 m tall, 8 s, three at most.
Stones are cover, and a wall: an enemy driven into one takes +40% and 30 stagger (decided in WORLD.md: stone
remembering).

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seven Strides | Move | 2 | The charged heavy becomes up to seven strides, each a quake disc of 2 m (14, 20 stagger); let go to end on the overhead |
| Slam Against | Rule | 2 | Light blows shove 1 m toward the nearest stone |
| The Living Wall | Move | 2 | The skill lays the blade flat as a wall 4 m across for 3 s that stops enemies and shots; R2 heaves it up in a cut (44) |
| Long Standing | Number | 1 | Stones stand 12 s, four at most |
| **Keystone: The Giant's Dance** | Keystone | 4 | The finisher wakes every standing stone: they turn once round you like dancers, each at its own distance (7 m at most), striking all they pass for 30 and stopping where the turn ends |

True form: stones stand 2 m tall, and a heavy struck into one topples it onto what lies beyond (40, a 3 m lane).

**Temper.** III: +4 poise in every swing. V: the full turn's ring +0.3 m.

**Infusion** (on the rising cut). Ground: the drag before the rise leaves a line of the substance 4 m behind the
cut. Line: the cut's arc flies on 6 m as an edge (20). Weight: the target is thrown up and down 1 m on, and its
balance does not recover for 3 s. Draw: before rising, the blade sweeps the floor toward you and drags all in the
arc within 5 m to the edge.

**Gated technique: The Return Blow.** Taught by the Bargain (Earth). R1 and R2 together: you lower the blade and
offer the neck, a clear stance held 1.5 s. The next blow that lands in it, red included, lands in full but cannot
kill you (it leaves 1 health), and is returned at once as a downward cut for twice the damage taken plus 60 stagger.
Struck by nothing, it ends in a plain overhead. The churl opens his fight by offering his neck for the player's free
blow and returning it: the beheading game is the lesson.

## 3. Scythe

Built: haft, middle and tip bands; out, back, full turn; the back reap; Crescent Slash; The Wheel. **Carrier**: the
full turn (a circle at 3.7 m with its tip band).

### The Swath

The mower's walk: long, even strokes, a step with each. **What changes**: every light steps 0.6 m forward and the
chain loops out and back as long as R1 is pressed; the full turn moves to R2 after lights. The tip band is 0.3 m
wider.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Even Stroke | Rule | 2 | Each tip blow in a row +6%, five at most; a haft blow or a miss ends the row |
| Whetstone | Move | 2 | Guard tapped twice: strop the edge for 0.5 s; the next five tip blows are critical |
| Long Pull | Number | 1 | Light arcs 200 to 230 degrees |
| Windrow | Rule | 2 | Enemies struck at the tip are pushed 0.8 m to the side the swing ends, so they lie in a row for the next |
| **Keystone: Mow the Field** | Keystone | 4 | While you walk the chain, blows of 15 or less do not flinch you, and every fifth tip blow in a row cuts the whole tip ring round you |

True form: the row survives one haft blow.

### The Hook

From Mair's sickle plays: reach to the forward leg and pull back. **What changes**: R2 after a light is the
**Hamstring**: the blade goes behind the leg at the tip and pulls; a light enemy falls (knocked down 1.2 s), an
elite stumbles, a god is only turned. 16, 30 stagger. The back reap stays on the plain R2.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reel In | Move | 2 | Hold R1: hook a light enemy up to 4 m off and drag it to exactly the tip band |
| Over the Haft | Rule | 2 | Every blow on a fallen enemy is a tip blow |
| Catch the Arm | Rule | 2 | A perfect guard hooks the attacker's arm: its next attack is 0.5 s late |
| Long Curve | Number | 1 | Hamstring reach +0.6 m |
| **Keystone: Nothing Leaves the Curve** | Keystone | 4 | An enemy in the tip band that moves away from you is hooked back 1 m, once every 2 s each. You hold them at the tip |

True form: every hook is also a Hamstring on light enemies.

### The Harvest

**What changes**: each tip blow binds a sheaf, drawn as stalks tied on the haft, five at most. The heavy spends them:
+12% damage and +10 degrees of arc a sheaf.

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glean | Move | 2 | Hold the flask button 0.4 s with sheaves: spend them for 4 health each (grey made real first). No flask is used |
| Thresh | Move | 2 | R2 after the full turn: the haft beaten on the floor, the sheaves thrown out as chaff: a disc of 3 m, 6 damage and 8 stagger a sheaf |
| Good Year | Number | 1 | Six sheaves at most |
| Seed Corn | Rule | 2 | One sheaf is always kept back after spending |
| **Keystone: The Last Sheaf** | Keystone | 4 | With five sheaves the next kill is the last sheaf: the body is bound standing as a straw figure for 3 s, and every enemy within 6 m turns to strike it. Then it falls apart: 20 a sheaf to all within 3 m (the old custom of the last sheaf, dressed as the Hag) |

True form: the straw figure stands 5 s and draws even a god's next blow.

### Carnwyn's Golden Scythe (Life)

**What changes**: a gold blade grown long from the druids' gold sickle, on an oak haft with mistletoe tied under the
blade; +15%. A kill sprouts a healing bloom (8 health, walked over, stands 6 s); the full turn grows a ring of
thicket at the tip's distance that stands 1.5 s and slows what crosses it by half (both decided in WORLD.md).

| Mark | Kind | Memories | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harvest Sweep | Move | 2 | A charged heavy (new for the scythe): hold R2 0.5 s for a 300 degree sweep at 4.2 m that stops at nothing (30 at the tip) |
| Cauldron Call | Move | 2 | The skill: two antlered beasts of the cauldron's plate run out through the faced enemy and back (three strikes of 10 each) |
| Thicket Line | Move | 2 | R2 while guarding: thorns rise in a line 4 m across ahead for 3 s; nothing walks through |
| Deep Root | Number | 1 | Blooms heal 12 |
| **Keystone: Every Cut Grows Back** | Keystone | 4 | A bloom left unpicked for 4 s grows into a thorn stand 2 m across for 4 s, rooting what is inside for 1 s in each second. The ground grows where you fight |

True form: blooms heal 16 and give 10 stamina.

**Temper.** III: the tip band begins 0.2 m nearer (2.3 m). V: tip stagger 1.6 to 1.8 times.

**Infusion** (on the full turn). Ground: the tip's ring is left lying 1 m wide for 3 s. Line: the ring flies outward

drawn into the tip band before the turn arrives.

**Gated technique: Over the Shoulder.** Taught by the Wicker Man (Life; Proposal: it carries a great reaping hook).

behind you at the tip's distance (26, 50 stagger), where your next swing's tip will be. Gods and fixed enemies are
not thrown but turned, back to you. The Wicker Man's grab is this move: it scoops the player over its shoulder into
an empty cage.

## 4. Great Spear

Built: two thrusts and a sweep; the guard thrust; the slap; the vault; the couched charge; Flying Spear; The

## 15. Pair Bond

### 15.1 How it grows (Decided in 8.2; numbers Proposal)

A weapon and an element carried together grow a shared Bond: blows the weapon lands while the element is carried
count once; blows carrying the element (the charged blade, the infused strike) count twice; an elite beaten with the
pair counts 50 blows, a god 200. Three ranks: I and II each +5% to the pair's infused strike; at III the infused
strike of that element on that weapon becomes the pair's own strike. Splitting the pair loses the strike, not either
Bond (Decided). Twelve kinds and eleven elements make 132 pairs (8.2's "about 110" counted ten weapons).

### 15.2 The rule (Decided: built from the weapon's infused strike and the element's substance)

The pair's strike is **the weapon's carrier, as infused** (its generic element effect and any infusion mark bought),
at **1.3 times its damage and stagger**, plus **the element's verb** acting on the carrier's **footprint**.

**The footprint** is the ground the carrier's blow covered, recorded when it strikes: a lane (length L, width W), an
arc (radius R, angle A), a disc (radius R), a ring (radius R, width 1 m), or a path (a shot's or a dash's line, length
L). Every carrier above is one of these: the Katana's draw an arc, the Massive Sword's rising cut an arc, the
Scythe's turn a ring, the Spear's thrust a lane, the Hammer's drop a disc, the Knives' crossing a path, the Bow's shot
a path, the Chain's snap a lane, the Fists' kick an arc, the Fan's sweep an arc, the Hook's cast a lane, the Hook
Swords' whip an arc. **The struck** are the enemies it hit.

**The verbs** (one per element, from its substance in `docs/design/LOOKS.md`; each built from actions the schema has,
`docs/UPGRADE_SCHEMA.md` section 7):

| Element | Verb | What it does to the footprint and the struck | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | spreads | The footprint burns 3 s (6 every 0.5 s, Burn). At 1.5 s the fire leaps once to every enemy within 2 m of it (Burn 2) | `Hazard` along the shape; `Afflict` with `Radius` |
| Lightning | jumps | Each struck enemy throws the strike on to the nearest enemy not struck within 6 m, at 60%, two jumps at most, drawn as a bolt | `Arc` with `Count` 2 |
| Wind | carries | You travel the footprint during the strike (a lane or path, its length; an arc, a half turn; a disc or ring, its rim), and the struck are thrown 3 m along the strike | `Step`; `Push` |
| Ice | holds | The footprint freezes into a crust for 3 s (Chill 2 on what stands in it); your next heavy on the crust shatters it and all in it, 30 and 40 stagger | `Hazard`, and Hoarfrost's shatter on a heavy blow |
| Water | returns | 0.4 s later the footprint is struck again from its far edge toward you at 50%, and the struck are dragged 2 m toward you | `Echo` reversed; `Pull` |
| Life | grows | 0.6 s later thorns rise over the footprint: what is inside is held 1.2 s and Seeded | `Hazard` with a hold; `Afflict` |
| Earth | rises | Stone rises along the footprint's far edge, one pillar for each 1.5 m of edge, four at most, standing 4 s: they block, and a struck enemy driven into one takes +30% | Stone Rise's wall |
| Metal | forges | Blades are forged in the air over the footprint, one for each 2 square metres, six at most, and fall 0.5 s later at 40% each | `Falling` with `Count` |
| Light | mirrors | The strike is struck again from the far side of the first enemy back toward you, at 60%: its reflection | New: `Echo` at a point with the facing turned |
| Dark | swallows | The footprint is a pit for 1.5 s that draws in at 2 m a second, then closes: 20, plus 4 for each enemy inside | `Pull`, then `Burst` |
| Poison | seeps | The footprint leaves mist for 5 s: one dose a second on what is inside, and the next infused strike that crosses the mist sets off every dose in it | `Hazard`; `SetOff` |

**The name** is the element's substance and the carrier's name: "Burning Draw", "Thunder Drop", "Backwash Turn". A
standout (15.4) replaces the rule's strike for its pair and has its own name.

**As data**: `PairStrike { Weapon, Element, Footprint (from the carrier), Verb (from the element), Standout (optional,
overrides) }`. The footprint is read from the carrier's `AttackStep` (`Reach`, `ArcDegrees`, `LineWidth`, `Radius`,
`Travel`, `Shot`). One new piece: the strike must record its footprint and its struck when it lands.

### 15.3 Three worked examples

**Katana and Fire: Burning Draw.** Carrier: the drawing cut, an arc of 170 degrees and 2.6 m. Infused it is 22
damage with the generic Fire effect; at 1.3 times, 29. Verb, spreads: the 170 degree arc burns on the floor for 3 s
(6 every 0.5 s, Burn), and at 1.5 s the flame jumps to every enemy within 2 m of the arc's edge. Seen from above: a
crescent of fire drawn on the floor in one stroke, smoke rising from it, and a moment later tongues of flame running
off its rim onto the ones who stood just outside. With the Line infusion mark bought the draw is a 5 m dash, so the
footprint is the lane: a corridor of fire behind the bearer instead of a crescent in front.

**Great Hammer and Lightning: Thunder Drop.** Carrier: the drop, a disc of 1.5 m. Infused, the generic Lightning
effect; damage 26 at 1.3 times is 34, 48 stagger at 1.3 times is 62. Verb, jumps: each enemy under the disc throws the
blow on to the nearest enemy not struck within 6 m, at 60% (20 damage, 37 stagger), twice at most, so one drop on one
enemy reaches three. Seen from above: the hammer lands, and a bolt leaps from the dent to the next body and the next,
a small disc of scorched stone under each. With the Weight infusion the disc is a pit that holds for 0.5 s; the pit
is struck, not the jumps.

**Scythe and Water: Backwash Turn.** Carrier: the full turn, a ring at the tip's distance (3.7 m, 1 m wide). Infused
20 becomes 26 before the tip's 1.5. Verb, returns: 0.4 s after the turn, the ring is struck again from its outer edge
inward at 50%, and everything struck is dragged 2 m toward you: which leaves it at 1.7 m, inside the haft. So the
pair's strike fights the scythe's own rule, and a player learns to step back 1 m as the water returns to put them at
the tip again. Seen from above: a ring of spray thrown out by the blade, then the foam running back in like a wave
off a beach. This is why the verbs are rules and not numbers: each one meets each weapon's own habit.

dies, all its doses pass to the nearest enemy inside, plus three, as in the gu jar the last creature alive takes the
others' poison.

**Fists and Fire: Flint and Tinder.** The pair every player starts with: fists, then Fire from the first god. The
knuckles are struck together before the infused kick, throwing a shower of sparks in a cone of 3 m (Burn 1 to all in
it); then the kick lands on the nearest, and every burning enemy in the cone takes one more Burn for each blow you
landed in the last 2 s. A quick chain is the tinder.

**Great Bow and Light: Arrow at Noon.** The infused shot is loosed straight up. 0.8 s later it comes down as a column
of sunlight on the faced enemy (a disc of 2 m, 40, Marked), and the light is thrown back off every arrow standing in
the floor within 8 m, each casting a ray to the nearest enemy (12, Marked). The planted arrows are the mirrors. The
champion's bow taught at last, without his thousand.

**Katana and Wind: The Air Closes.** A draw so fast it cuts the air itself. The infused draw leaves an empty lane
8 m long behind the blade, drawn as a thin clear line with dust standing still at its edges. 0.5 s later the air
rushes back into it: every enemy within 3 m of the lane is drawn onto the line and struck as it closes (30, 40
stagger), and they are left in a row along it, ready for the next draw. The cut, then the clap of air. (The
whirlwind of three, trip, cut and close, is the Wind and Metal duo spell Three Weasels.)

## 16. What to cut or merge

- **Merge the Bone Hook into the chain family.** A weighted line that is thrown, catches, binds and pulls is what the
  Chain Sickle already is; the Hook's Lure and Line aspects are its best new ideas and fit the chain family as well.
  Making the fisher's Jawbone a god's bough on the Chain Sickle's Way would leave eleven kinds, give the chain a god,
  and give every kind a technique (the hook brother would teach the chain family's). The owner decided the hook is
  its own kind, so it is written whole above; this is the one place two Ways risk feeling alike.
- **Fold Full Body Armament into the Fists** as their third aspect (section 9), and take its mark off the eleven
  element trees.
- **Watch the Twin Knives and the Hook Swords**: both are two short blades. Kept apart here by what each asks (knives
  are about being behind; hooks about catching and reach), but if one must go after play, it is the plain Hook Swords'
  Catch aspect, not the god's bough.
- No other cut: the Massive Sword and the Great Hammer were built far enough apart (poise and trading against placed
  discs and the late guard).

## 17. New engine work, in one place

- The technique chord: R1 and R2 pressed together, as an input action.
- Enemy states: **knocked down** (a timed fall, its own clip), **Hooked** (moves, but the line is the player's),
  **disarmed** (no weapon attacks), **pinned** (held, like Bound, but by a staked point).
- A grip or stance flag on the weapon that swaps its combo (Half-Sword, the War Fan shut and open, Linked).
- Counters a weapon keeps: Heat, sheaves, nicks, weights, drums, layers of the coat.
- An answer window after a perfect guard (the Katana's Answer, the Hammer's ring) as a timed state that changes what R1
  and R2 start.
- Anchors in the scenery the Bone Hook can catch (a tag on colliders).
- For Pair Bond: the strike records its footprint and its struck; one new action, a mirrored `Echo`.
