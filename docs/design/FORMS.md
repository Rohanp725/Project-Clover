# Spell forms: the fixed list of ids

Tree files name a form by id in `Upgrade.Form`; the forms are built by one worker while the trees are written by
others, so the ids are fixed here first. Every form takes the caster's element colour and affliction and a
`SpellSpec` (see `docs/UPGRADE_SCHEMA.md` section 10). The fields each form reads are listed; others are ignored.

Existing: `wave`, `bolt`, `leap`, `rod`.

bespoke work later.

## Bespoke

Moves that belong to one mark: the gods' gifts and the crowns of the mixed branches. Each is a form of its own under
`Runtime/Player/Forms/`. Two are worn spells (held spell button); the rest are begun by their mark with
`UpgradeAction.Form` on a trigger (docs/UPGRADE_SCHEMA.md, 16) and cost nothing. They have no stone on the yard's rack:
the GOD GIFT and CONSUME NEXT shrines give them. Storm Step (Lightning) and Plasma were already written by hand in
`PlayerUpgrades` and have no form.

`ElementSubstance` is the table every form reaches without knowing it. `FormFx` asks it what a plain piece becomes in
the colour of the element the player holds (`PlayerElement.Take` tells it): a **Shard** becomes a fireball, a jagged
bolt, a streak of air, an ice splinter, a drop, a leaf, a pebble, a blade, a ray, a wisp of smoke or a bubble; a
**Spike** becomes flame tongues, a crystal cluster, a spout, a vine or a rock; a **Pillar** becomes a rock or a
crystal. It also gives the stuff its own motion wherever a form puts it (flame flickers, bolts jump to a new shape 18
times a second, smoke swells, water and bubbles wobble), its shine (`_Sheen`) and its thinness (`_Thin`: smoke and
air are stippled, so they are seen through), and `FormKit.Impact` calls its `Burst`, which throws the element's
pieces out and leaves what that element leaves (flames, crystals, a spout and ripples, vines and a flower, rocks,
blades stuck in the ground, crossed flares, sinking smoke, rising bubbles; lightning nothing). Sixteen new meshes in
`FormMeshes` (Flame, Fireball, Bolt, Crystal, Crest, Foam, Droplet, Spout, Rock, Pebble, Leaf, Vine, Flower, Cloud,
Bubble, Ray, Gust). A form that wants a particular shape asks for it by name and is left alone.

`SurgeLook` holds six solids on the player while a surge lasts, placed every frame by element. `AfflictionLook`
throws two small pieces of an affliction's stuff on the body that carries it a few times a second (from `EnemyBars`,
taking turns when a body carries several).

Two shapes were added to `FormMesh` (at the end, so no number moved): **Scarab** and **Falcon**. **Crest** is no
longer cut from facets: `FormMeshes.Wave` sweeps a side profile (a long back, a crest, a lip thrown forward, a

to nothing at both ends, and paints it per vertex. **Foam** is twenty-four flecks scattered over a square metre.

`Clover/Spell` now reads vertex colour in its main pass: rgb multiplies the object's colour (the wave is deep at
its foot and green toward its top) and alpha below a half is drawn as foam (white where lit, blue-grey in shade)
whatever the object's colour. Every mesh `FormMeshes` makes is painted white with alpha one, which changes nothing.
